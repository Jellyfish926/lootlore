How these guides are made

The same standard applies to every game on Lootlore, and it is what separates a guide you can trust from a page that merely ranks.

Facts come first

Nothing on this site is written from memory or guesswork. Boss names, item stats, achievement rates and patch numbers are pulled from official store pages, developer announcements and platform data, then cross-checked in the running game.

When two official sources disagree, we show the discrepancy instead of quietly picking one. When a number cannot be verified, the page says so.

Kept alive after launch

A guide written at release and never touched again is worse than no guide at all. Each hub tracks patches and revisits its pages as the game changes — difficulty rebalances, new content, fixed exploits.

Pages carry the date they were last reviewed and the game version they were checked against, so you always know how fresh the advice is.

Depth over volume

We would rather cover fewer games completely than many games thinly. A hub only joins Lootlore once it can answer the questions players actually search for — from “is it worth buying” to the last hidden achievement.

Independent and unofficial

Lootlore is a fan-made resource with no affiliation to any developer or publisher. Screenshots and artwork remain the property of their studios and are credited on every page where they appear. Read more in our editorial policy.
