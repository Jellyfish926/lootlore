3:T1ff9,<p>Most roguelites express progress as <em>going deeper</em>. Sephiria expresses it as <em>the same place getting different</em>. The tower's floors do not change — Mole Stronghold is always floor 1 — but as chapters advance, the <a href="/bosses/chapters-and-bosses/">bosses</a> occupying those floors are replaced, and the run extends further.</p>
<p>Understanding that is the difference between "why am I fighting a different boss on floor 4" and knowing exactly what the story just did to your run.</p>

<h2>How far each chapter goes</h2>

<p>Chapter 6 shipped with the 1.0 release and contains the game's ending and credits. Before that, Sephiria had no ending — it was in Early Access from April 2025 to July 2026.</p>

<h2>What swaps in when</h2>
<p>The clearest way to see the structure is per-floor:</p>
<p><strong>Floor 1 (Mole Stronghold)</strong> — Mogdi, the Black Hand from Chapter 1. From Chapter 2 it becomes <strong>Odner, the Blasting Expert</strong>. From Chapter 4, <strong>Haz, the Mind-Eater Root</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Floor 2 (Woodlands)</strong> — Rataka from Chapter 1, then <strong>Lerid, the Shadow Blade</strong> from Chapter 2. Lerid was originally the Woodlands <em>mini</em>-boss before being promoted.</p>
<p><strong>Floor 3 (Library)</strong> — Erma, the Mad Scientist from Chapter 1, then <strong>Pentaxis, Guardian of the Library</strong> from Chapter 2. Pentaxis was added in patch 0.7.50, mid-Early-Access.</p>
<p><strong>Floor 4 (Wasteland)</strong> — this is the one that matters most. <strong>Askard, the Cult Leader</strong> in Chapter 1 only; <strong>Mutated Askard</strong> in Chapter 2 only; <strong>Askard's Phantom</strong> from Chapter 3 onward.</p>
<p><strong>Floor 5 and beyond</strong> — the Guardian Demons of Qliphoth. <strong>Kovess</strong> on floor 5 from Chapter 3, <strong>Kraz</strong> on floor 5.5 from Chapter 4. Floor 6 (Deep Cave) holds Qliphoth from Chapter 5. Floor 7 (Qliphoth's Temple) is Chapter 6 only, and holds the three-form final fight.</p>
<p>In Chapter 6 the Guardian pattern changes: <strong>only Kovess or Kraz appears on floor 5, chosen at random.</strong> You no longer fight both.</p>

<h2>The achievements that expire</h2>
<p>Because bosses are chapter-locked, several achievements have windows:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Enlightened Scholar</strong> — defeat Askard. Must be done in <strong>Chapter 1</strong>. 72.4% of players have it.</li><li><strong>Qliphoth Contractor</strong> — defeat Mutated Askard. <strong>Chapter 2 only.</strong> 63.1%.</li></ul>
<p>If you push the story forward before taking these, that specific boss stops spawning. This is the single most common reason players end up with a gap in an otherwise complete achievement list.</p>
<p>The chapter-completion achievements themselves are all hidden, and their descriptions are deliberately oblique — <em>Chapter I</em> is "Meet the Seed," <em>Chapter II</em> is "Meet the Seed again," <em>Chapter III</em> is "Meet three animals," <em>Chapter IV</em> is "Meet the last Hero," <em>Chapter V</em> is "Rescue three animals."</p>
<h2>The difficulty curve, in numbers</h2>
<p>The stage table's base enemy DEF is the cleanest measure available:</p>
<p>Mole Stronghold 0 → Woodlands 18 → Library 46 → Wasteland 66 → Rooted Cave 79 → Rooted Cave (5.5) 110 → Deep Cave 110 → Qliphoth's Temple 120.</p>
<p>The jump from Woodlands to Library is where most builds first fail — DEF more than doubles, and Library is also where Pentaxis waits from Chapter 2 onward. If your run keeps ending on floor 3, the answer is usually that your <a href="/artifacts/artifacts-and-tablets/">artifacts</a> are not at level, not that you need a different weapon.</p>
<p>Steam's completion rates trace the same curve: 89.7% have beaten floor 1's boss, 76.3% floor 3's, 50.6% Pentaxis, 34.1% Haz, and <strong>14.5% have finished the game</strong>.</p>
<h2>Where the story happens</h2>
<p>Outside the tower there is <strong>Bunnyville</strong>, the <strong>Ark</strong>, the <strong>Tree</strong>, and from patch 0.11.5 the <strong>Comprehensive Training Grounds</strong>. Chapter 5 adds a <strong>Place of Recollection</strong> version of Bunnyville.</p>
<p>The Obelisk event has a chapter-specific behaviour worth knowing: in <strong>Chapter 6</strong>, any Obelisk you interact with shows the <strong>Old Records</strong> lore sequence instead of its usual random buff. If you are chasing story rather than stats in your final chapter, take Obelisks.</p>
<h2>Preparing for a chapter transition</h2>
<p>The practical preparation is not about the boss. A chapter transition raises the <em>floor</em> on what your build needs to be doing, and builds fail at chapter boundaries mostly because they are half-committed in two directions.</p>
<p>Before pushing a new chapter, check three things: your weapon is at max tier for its tree (rush every anvil), your core artifacts are actually at max level rather than merely present, and you have at least two <a href="/guides/combo-effects/">combos</a> past threshold. A build that satisfies all three usually clears the boundary; one that satisfies two usually does not.</p>
<h2>One correction</h2>
<p>Chapters in Sephiria have <strong>numbers and nothing else.</strong> No first-party source gives them titles, themes or subtitles. If you find a page listing "Chapter 5: The Rooted Cave" or similar, that title was invented by whoever wrote the page — the Rooted Cave is a floor name, not a chapter name.</p>
<h2>Checked against 1.0.30</h2>
<p>Route and floor structure are unchanged. Two chapter-specific fixes: <strong>1.0.28</strong> repaired corrupted UI on some Chapter 4 story objects, and <strong>1.0.26</strong> added a quest guide message when you cannot depart in Chapter 5, which was the most common "stuck" report after launch. Chapter 6 multiplayer freezes were fixed in 1.0.29.</p>
