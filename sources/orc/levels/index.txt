3:Tb22,<h2>Levels are numbered, and the numbers matter</h2>
<p>Levels use an <code>X.Y</code> format — 1.1, 2.1, 3.1 and so on up through 6.2. That numbering is what players search for, and it is how the community organises its layouts: a guide titled "6.1 4-Tower only" is telling you both the level and the constraint.</p>
<p>The game has <strong>11 achievements</strong> — with official names like <em>Four-tified</em> and <em>Fine, I'll Do It Myself</em> — and Steam publishes the global unlock rate for each one. That rate is the most honest difficulty curve available, because it is measured rather than opinion. Three of the eleven are progression markers; the other eight are self-imposed constraints.</p>
<h2>The difficulty curve, measured</h2>
<p>Surviving the first level sits at <strong>97.2%</strong> — effectively everyone who launches the game. Surviving the third is at <strong>88.4%</strong>. Then it falls off a cliff: surviving the sixth is at <strong>29.8%</strong>.</p>
<p>The constraint achievements sit lower still. Clearing a level with only four turret types is the rarest in the game at <strong>12.1%</strong>, below even the three-type version at 17.1% (rates re-read from Steam on 1 September 2026). That inversion is worth pausing on — using <em>more</em> turret types is harder, because the achievement forbids abilities and forces you to hold every lane with turret placement alone.</p>
<h2>Which level each challenge is done on</h2>
<p>A guide by the Steam user <em>itsuno</em>, posted 29 July 2026 and read by roughly 4,900 people, maps each constraint achievement to the level they cleared it on: single turret on 1.1, no turrets at all on 2.1, one turret type on 3.1, the flamethrower/tesla/cryo combination on 4.1, three types on 5.1 and four types on 6.1.</p>
<p>We are repeating that as <em>where one player did it</em>, not as a requirement the game states. Whether the achievement is level-locked or simply easiest there is not documented anywhere we can point at.</p>
<h2>The two hardest ones have published layouts</h2>
<p>Level 6.1 with only four turret types has at least three player-written layouts, and they disagree in an instructive way. One build leans on slow-down grenades; another player, <em>Григорий</em>, explicitly wrote his because every walkthrough he found required grenades he had not unlocked, and solved it with freeze-beam discipline and constant pausing instead.</p>
<p>If you are stuck on a layout, the useful question is usually which upgrade branch it silently assumes you have.</p>
