# Southern Mudding Game Passes: All 13 with Robux Prices

Southern Mudding has 13 game passes on sale, priced from 80 to 490 Robux. The most expensive is Spawn 4 Vehicles at 490; the cheapest are Rock Lights Customization and Portable Customization at 80 each. Buying all 13 costs 3,685 Robux. Twelve have a gift product and a thirteenth probably does; seven gifts are listed below the pass price.

Names, prices and descriptions below are copied from the [Roblox game pass API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/8719555347/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100), checked on 2 October 2026. None carried a discount. The API also returns a fourteenth record named "Placeholder" that is not for sale and has no price; we leave it out.

## Are the gift versions cheaper?

Often, yes. Twelve passes have a developer product named as their gift, and one more product, "Deluxe Trailer Pack", is probably the gift for the pass of the same name. Seven are listed below the pass price, five match it, and one costs more:

Two notes on this table. First, the product called "Deluxe Trailer Pack" has no "Gift" in its name. It was created within the same hour as the first seven gift products on 23 September 2025, so it is likely the gift version, but the data does not say so. Second, we cannot tell why the prices differ. All 13 pass records were last updated between 9 and 14 August 2026, later than most gift products, so the passes may have been repriced and the gifts left alone. That is an inference; Roblox does not record what an update changed. Check the price shown in game before you buy either version.

## Which Southern Mudding pass should you buy first?

Our suggestions, based only on what each pass says it does:

- **You keep running out of vehicle slots:** Spawn 4 Vehicles (490). It doubles the limit from 2 to 4, and it is the dearest pass, so be sure the limit is what holds you back.
- **You tow a lot:** Spawn 4 Trailers (300) doubles trailers; Portable Trailer Spawner (325) lets you spawn them "from anywhere in the map". The [spawning guide](/southern-mudding/spawning/) compares the four spawning passes.
- **You want boats or jet skis on a trailer:** Deluxe Trailer Pack (200) is the only pass that names its contents: the Boat Trailer and the Double Jet Ski Trailer.
- **You want more to drive:** Classic Car Pack (290) has the lowest price per vehicle; Deluxe Mudder Pack (320) is the only pack described as mudding trucks. The [vehicle overview](/southern-mudding/vehicles/) sets out every route to a new vehicle.
- **You tune constantly:** Portable Customization (80) lets you customize "from anywhere" instead of driving back to the garage.
