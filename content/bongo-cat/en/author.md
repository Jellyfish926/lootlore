# Author: Jellyfi, Bongo Cat Guide Editor Profile

Jellyfi is the editorial byline on {{BRAND}}, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the [Bongo Cat guides](/bongo-cat/) on this site. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

## How these guides are researched

The game changes through small, frequent patches, so the most reliable record is the developer's own. Mechanics are taken first from the Steam store page and the official announcements on Steam, then from replies the developer posted in the Steam discussions under the [developer] badge. Steam's public achievement stats supply names and unlock rates. Every guide ends with numbered sources (starting at S001) and an access date, so you can open the original and check it yourself.

![An orange cat skin in a yellow hood tapping above the taskbar counter](ss04 "Every guide on this site is published under one editorial byline")

Fan wikis and other guide sites are used only to cross-check, never as the only source for a fact. Where two official sources disagree, or where a number was only ever stated in an older patch, the page says so and gives the date. Advice such as which achievement to chase first is labelled as a suggestion, not a rule.

## How to read the dates on a page

Under the heading of each article, "Published" is the date the page went live on this site and "Last checked" is the date the text was checked against its sources. The game has no public version number, so pages are dated rather than tied to a game version.

## Reporting an error

If a rule, a date or an item name does not match what you see in the game, email us through the [Contact](/contact) page and include the page link and the date you noticed it. The site-wide writing and correction rules are on the [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy) page.
