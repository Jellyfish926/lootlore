# All 13 Deep Fishing Badges and How Many Players Own Them

Deep Fishing has 13 Roblox badges: Welcome!, four first-catch badges (Legendary, Mythical, Secret and Mutation) and eight catch-count milestones from 50 to 500,000 fish. The easy ones come in your first session; 10K Catches and above belong to a tiny group — on 10 October 2026, 500K Catches had been awarded 85 times.

Everything on this page comes from the [Roblox badge API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/10526853622/badges?limit=100), the same data the Roblox experience page shows. The unlock text is copied exactly, including the developer's own "1.000" spelling.

## What are all the badges, and how many people have each?

"Share" is our own calculation: the badge's total awards divided by the 3,051,522 awards of the Welcome! badge, both read on 10 October 2026. Think of it as "out of every 100 players with the entry badge".

The totals move daily — First Mutation alone was awarded 57,380 times in the day before our read on 10 October 2026 — so treat the numbers as a snapshot rather than a record.

## Which badges will you get on day one?

Welcome! is automatic. First Mutation and 50 Catches are the next easiest; they were held by about 85 and 65 of every 100 Welcome! badge holders on 10 October 2026.

First Legendary is the first real milestone. About 42 in 100 Welcome! badge holders had it on 10 October 2026. If you are not there yet, the [how-to-play guide](/deep-fishing/how-to-play/) explains why we put distance ahead of luck.

## Why do some totals look low?

Two reasons. The badges were created on 28 July and 1 August, while the game record was created on 18 July. Players from those first days only earned badges if they returned afterwards. And the Welcome! count is badge awards, not unique people who ever opened the game — Roblox counted 10,311,806 visits on 10 October 2026, which include repeat sessions.

So use the shares to compare badges with each other, not as exact odds.

## How hard are the catch milestones?

The jump after 1,000 fish is steep:

- 1,000 Catches: about 17 in 100 Welcome! badge holders.
- 10K Catches: about 2 in 100.
- 50K Catches: 7,944 awards in total.
- 500K Catches: 85 awards in total.

Those are the 10 October 2026 totals.

At that level, the paid helpers the developer sells matter more than for anything else. **Fish Magnet** ("Pull Fish close to the Bait") and **Auto Sell** are the two passes aimed at catching and clearing volume; our [game pass guide](/deep-fishing/gamepasses/) compares all nine. How many fish count per cast for these milestones is not stated officially.

## How do you get the rarity badges?

First Legendary, First Mythical and First Secret need you to land a fish of that tier. The developer does not publish odds, but the badge totals give a clear ladder: Legendary was roughly 4.7 times as common as Secret on 10 October 2026. Our [rarity and mutations guide](/deep-fishing/rarity/) covers what we know about each tier and the boosts that target them.
