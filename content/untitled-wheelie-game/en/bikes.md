# Untitled Wheelie Game Bikes, Parts and Backfire Guide

The store records name Tuttiro Bike, Eblox Dragster and the bike groups "Ebikes" and "Emotos"; the developer's event announcements, read on 10 October 2026, add Ridstar, Super73, Macfox, Jetson Ebike, Normal Moped and Junkyard Moped. Three products carry BACKFIRE in their names. The full bike list and prices are not in those records.

The official description promises you can "Buy bikes, upgrades, and parts" and "Paint and customize your bike". This page collects the bike-related names in the store records and in the event announcements, both read on 10 October 2026; the announcements are printed in full on the [patch notes page](/untitled-wheelie-game/patch-notes/). All three bike passes were off sale on that date.

## Which bikes has the developer named?

| Name | How it was sold | Official description | On sale on 10 October 2026? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EBike Pack ⚡ | Game pass | Gives you all Ebikes in the game. (Not Emotos) | No |
| Tuttiro Bike | Game pass | None | No |
| Eblox Dragster | Game pass | None | No |

The EBike Pack line tells us something useful: the game has at least two bike groups, "Ebikes" and "Emotos", and the pack covered only the first. The Tuttiro Bike and Eblox Dragster are named but not described, so we cannot say what kind of bike either is.

The developer's event announcements say more names. The table copies them as written:

| Name in the announcement | Announcement | What the announcement says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ridstar, Super73, Macfox | EBIKE UPDATE | Subtitle "Ridstar, Super73, Macfox"; the one-line description adds no bike names or numbers |
| Jetson Ebike | MAP REVAMP | "5. Jetson Ebike" |
| Normal Moped | Mopeds + Rain 🛵🌧️ | "Normal Moped, 40MPH stock, 80MPH modded 🛵" |
| Junkyard Moped | Mopeds + Rain 🛵🌧️ | "Junkyard Moped, 35MPH stock, 160MPH modded 🛠️" |

An announcement is what the developer said was coming; it is not a store record, and it gives no price.

None of these passes had a price listed on 10 October 2026, because Roblox's store data shows no price for a pass that is off sale.

## What is the best bike in Untitled Wheelie Game?

We cannot tell you, and neither can anyone quoting official numbers. The developer's Mopeds + Rain announcement lists "40MPH stock, 80MPH modded" for Normal Moped and "35MPH stock, 160MPH modded" for Junkyard Moped; we found no handling or price data, and no speed for any other bike, in the records we read on 10 October 2026. Rankings on other sites are opinions. Our advice: compare bikes in the in-game shop, and pick the one whose wheelie you can hold longest — the developer stresses balance, throttle and brake, as the [how-to-play guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/how-to-play/) explains.

## What does Backfire do?

Three products carry BACKFIRE in their names; all three records were created on 4 September 2026:

| Product | Robux |
| --- | --- |
| INSTALL BACKFIRE | 25 |
| LEVEL 2 BACKFIRE | 30 |
| LEVEL 3 BACKFIRE | 40 |

Buying all three costs 95 Robux in total (our sum). The products carry no description. The developer's MAP REVAMP announcement lists "4. Backfire Mod for Gas Bikes" among the things coming with that update; what the mod changes is not confirmed.

## Can you make your bike faster?

The developer once sold a pass called Extra Bike Speed ("Adds speed to your bike"). It was off sale on 10 October 2026. The description also lists "upgrades" among the things you can buy; which upgrades exist and what they cost is not in the records we read. If you are saving up, the [money guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/money/) covers the fastest official ways to earn.

## How do you paint and customize a bike?

"Paint and customize your bike" is one of the official activities. A Bike Customization pass ("Customize the color of your bike.") has a record created on 5 June 2026 and was off sale on 10 October 2026. We could not confirm whether painting is free for everyone or needs something else, so check the customization options in the game.

The developer also sells a pass named More Helmets 🪖 for 99 Robux. Its description is empty.
