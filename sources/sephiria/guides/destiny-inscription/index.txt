3:T1eef,<p>Destiny Inscription is Sephiria's meta-progression tree, reached by talking to the Tree. It is where the Sapphires you earn by dying go, and it is the reason <a href="/guides/beginner/">early runs</a> matter even when they end on floor 2.</p>
<p><strong>Full completion costs 383 Sapphires</strong> across twelve tiers, distributed 0 / 3 / 2 / 7 / 34 / 55 / 57 / 35 / 54 / 77 / 31 / 28. That ninth-tier spike is real — it is the single most expensive band in the tree.</p>
<figure class="fig"><img src="/images/sephiria-bunnyville-village-1200.webp" srcset="/images/sephiria-bunnyville-village-800.webp 800w, /images/sephiria-bunnyville-village-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, 720px" alt="Bunnyville village hub in Sephiria with NPC dialogue" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Bunnyville is the hub you return to between runs. Official screenshot — Sephiria, TEAM HORAY</figcaption></figure>
<h2>How it unlocks</h2>
<p>Nodes connect by lines. You must own <strong>at least one rank</strong> of a prerequisite node before the next along the line becomes purchasable. Some nodes are free; the rest cost Sapphires.</p>
<p>Sapphires come from <strong>dying</strong>, scaled to how deep you got and how many <a href="/bosses/chapters-and-bosses/">bosses</a> you killed, and they are also spent in-run — at the Dimensional Rift, at Double Doors, at the Chest of Possibilities, and on costume appearance variants (30 for an alternate look, 100 for a second).</p>
<h2>The buy order that matters</h2>
<p><strong>Tablet</strong> is free and first. Tablets are gated content: until you buy this, tablet nodes and tablet objects do not appear in runs at all. Everything about the build system is downstream of it.</p>
<p><strong>Home Repairs</strong> is second. It unlocks Talent Point assignment — without it your talent points have nowhere to go — and every Tier-3 node requires it. It also grants the <em>DIY</em> achievement, which 91.5% of players have, making it the most-unlocked achievement in the game.</p>
<p>After that:</p>
<ol><li><strong>Talent Points ×5</strong> — five nodes granting +1 / +2 / +3 / +4 / +5, for <strong>25 points</strong>.</li><li><strong>Expanding Pouch (1) and (2)</strong> — three ranks each of +1/+2/+3, for <strong>+6 inventory slots</strong>, exactly one extra row on the 6-wide grid.</li><li><strong>A second weapon</strong> — see below.</li><li><strong>Seed Money</strong> — start runs with 200, then 400 Leaf.</li><li><strong>Dice (1)(2)(3)</strong> — up to <strong>6 starting dice</strong>, plus <em>Dice Acquisition</em> (+2 on boss kill) and <em>Dice Transformation</em> (+1 when you skip a reward).</li></ol>
<h2>The five weapon unlocks</h2>
<p>This is the part guides most often get half-right. Buying the node is not enough — <strong>each weapon requires completing a training quest with Moro</strong> in the village training grounds afterwards.</p>
<div class="tw"><table><thead><tr><th>Node</th><th>Unlocks</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Heavy Weapon Training</td><td>Great Sword</td></tr><tr><td>Nimble Weapon Training</td><td>Dagger</td></tr><tr><td>Ranged Weapon Training</td><td>Crossbow</td></tr><tr><td>Sharp Weapon Training</td><td>Blade</td></tr><tr><td>Graceful Weapon Training</td><td>Staff</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>With the starting Sword &amp; Shield that accounts for all six classes. Older guides list only Longsword, Dagger and Crossbow because they predate the Blade (0.8.11) and Staff (0.11.0). Once every weapon is unlocked, a <strong>random weapon</strong> option becomes available at run start.</p>
<h2>The quality-of-life nodes</h2>
<p>Several nodes are side quests rather than stat purchases, and they unlock systems rather than numbers:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Terrain Recovery</strong> — opens the <strong>Willpower (Luck)</strong> talent tree. Involves a hole in the Ark and a puzzle.</li><li><strong>Magic Workshop</strong> — adds the <strong>Potion Bag</strong>, two slots reserved for potions only.</li><li><strong>Basement</strong> — unlocks <strong>alternate appearances</strong> for every costume.</li><li><strong>Fruit Skewer</strong> — lets you bias which combos' items appear, set before a run.</li><li><strong>Wishing Fountain</strong> — carry chosen items into a run; base capacity 4, extendable to 8 via the Willpower talent.</li><li><strong>Chest of Possibilities</strong> — spend Sapphires to restock merchant stock. In multiplayer each player's rolls and costs are private, doubling 2 → 4 → 8 → 16.</li><li><strong>Rylie's Gift</strong> — start every run with a free Restoration Potion.</li><li><strong>Side Bag (1) and (2)</strong> — added in 1.0. Storage for spare items. Remember that <strong>items in the Side Bag are inactive</strong>.</li><li><strong>Hide and Seek</strong> — a village quest node.</li></ul>
<h2>The stat nodes</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Increase HP (1) and (2)</strong> — +5/+10 each, <strong>+20 Max HP</strong> total</li><li><strong>MP Regeneration</strong> — +3 / +6</li><li><strong>Extra Experience Drop</strong> — +10%</li><li><strong>Level Up Restoration</strong> — +3% / +6% / +9% / +12% HP restored on level-up</li><li><strong>New Artifacts (1)–(8)</strong> and <strong>Bond Artifact</strong> — pool expansions. New Artifacts (1) adds 21 magic artifacts; the Bond Artifact node adds 23 Bond Artifacts.</li></ul>
<h2>Hard Mode sits here too</h2>
<p>The <strong>Hard Mode</strong> node is free and lives in the roots of the tree, but it requires <strong>clearing Chapter 2</strong> before it becomes available. It moved here in 0.7.55 — it used to be a pure Destiny Inscription unlock, and became a dialogue keyword with the Tree instead. If that dialogue stops appearing after you clear Chapter 4, a later patch added the ability to unlock it from the Destiny Inscription menu directly.</p>
<h2>Why you will never see 100%</h2>
<p>Multiple players with every purchasable node at maximum rank report the completion counter reading <strong>99%</strong>. The top of the tree — roughly eleven hexagons — is currently blank and unimplemented.</p>
<p>So the practical definition of "done" is 383 Sapphires spent, not a percentage. Do not spend runs hunting the last 1%; it is not there yet.</p>
<h2>What this buys you in total</h2>
<p>Fully completed, Destiny Inscription hands you: all six <a href="/weapons/all-weapons/">weapons</a>, 25 Talent Points, +6 inventory slots, +2 potion slots, +20 Max HP, 400 starting Leaf, 6 starting dice plus two dice-generation effects, the Side Bag, the Wishing Fountain, the Fruit Skewer, costume variants, and several hundred additional artifacts in the drop pool.</p>
<p>Combined with the 20 Talent Points available from Hard Mode tier rewards, that is <strong>45 Talent Points</strong> — enough to take three talents to 10 and one to 15, or to push two to the level-20 capstones.</p>
<h2>Caveats</h2>
<p>The node list above comes from a community compendium maintained against version 1.0.25, cross-checked against patch notes where possible. TEAM HORAY has never published the tree as a document. Two specific ambiguities: the Side Bag nodes are recorded as "+1/+1/+2 Side Bag Slot(s)" without clarifying whether those are cumulative, and the exact Sapphire cost of individual nodes is not published — only the tier totals are.</p>
<h2>Checked against 1.0.30</h2>
<p>Tree contents are unchanged through 1.0.30. Two fixes touch how the tree interacts with the rest of the game (<strong>1.0.26</strong>): tablets no longer show in the Subspace Shop's unlock list before you have made a Destiny Inscription, and one NPC's Destiny Quest no longer blocks another's from progressing while it is waiting to be started. Preset slots grew from 7 to 15 with a lock function in the same patch — useful once you have several inscription-dependent loadouts.</p>5:["category","guides","d"]
