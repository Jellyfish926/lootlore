# Author: Jellyfi, Editor of the Southern Mudding Guides

Jellyfi is the editorial byline on {{BRAND}}, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the [Southern Mudding guides](/southern-mudding/) on this site. The byline covers research, fact checks against official Roblox data and updates when the game changes. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

## How these guides are researched

Southern Mudding is a Roblox game, so the most reliable facts are the ones the developer registers on Roblox itself: the game description, the four badges, the 13 game passes, the 61 developer products, the event listings and the group page. Those are read directly from Roblox's public data and quoted word for word, brackets and exclamation marks included, with the date we checked them.

![Promotional art: a red pickup truck tows a multi-axle trailer carrying a large red and white speedboat with four outboard engines to the water's edge](th5 "Every guide in this section is published under one editorial byline")

Fan wikis, code sites and videos are used only as leads. If one of them is the only source for a fact, such as a code, a vehicle's speed, a map location or a way to earn money, that fact stays off our published pages until something official or our own check confirms it. That is why there is no codes page, no map page and no tier list yet.

Vehicles are named exactly as the developer names them: Pickup Truck, UTV, Monster Truck and so on. We do not attach real-world makes or models to them.

Buying orders and "what to do first" advice are editorial suggestions, not rules of the game, and each page says so where it matters. Ratios such as "per 100 Welcome badges" and "Robux per vehicle" are our own arithmetic on official numbers.

## How to read the dates on a page

"Published" is when the page first went live. "Last checked" is when the text was compared against its sources. Roblox numbers such as badge totals, visits, member counts and prices are snapshots from that day. This game updates every Friday, so a page checked before a Friday update describes the previous week's version until we review it again.

## Found a mistake?

If a price, name or menu no longer matches the game, use the [Contact](/contact) page and include the page link and a screenshot from the game. Screenshots of the nitrous controls, the spawn slot menu, the house claim prompt or an official codes box are especially welcome, because those are gaps we could not fill from official data.
