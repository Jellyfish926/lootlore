<h2>Corrections come first</h2>
<p>If a claim on this site is wrong, that is the message we want most. Include the page URL and what the correct information is; if you can say where you confirmed it — a patch note, the store page, a reproducible in-game test — we can act on it same-day rather than re-verifying from scratch.</p>
<p>Corrections are handled before anything else in this list. A page that is confidently wrong costs a reader a run; a page that is missing costs them a search.</p>
<h2>Everything else worth sending</h2>
<p><strong>A guide we have not written.</strong> If you searched for something about Sephiria and found no straight answer anywhere, that is useful signal. Requests have a real influence on what gets written next — better than us guessing.</p>
<p><strong>Something broken.</strong> Dead links, missing images, layout that falls apart on your phone. Your device and browser help.</p>
<p><strong>Disputed information.</strong> If you have seen two sources contradict each other on a mechanic, send both. We would rather publish "these disagree" than pick one.</p>
<p><strong>Reuse and quoting.</strong> Ask before republishing material at length; short quotes with a link back are fine.</p>
<h2>What we cannot do</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Support for the game itself.</strong> Account problems, refunds, crashes, billing — we have no access to any of that. TEAM HORAY handles it through the <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2436940/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Steam store page</a>.</li><li><strong>Cheats, exploits, piracy.</strong> Not hosted, not discussed.</li><li><strong>Pre-release information.</strong> We have no early access and nothing embargoed.</li></ul>
<h2>How long we take</h2>
<p>Usually a few days. Corrections are faster. If something is urgent and factual — a page actively telling people the wrong thing — say so in the subject line and it moves to the front.</p>
