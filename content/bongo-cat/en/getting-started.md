# Bongo Cat Getting Started: First-Week Guides

The app looks simple, but most first-week questions are the same four: how chests actually arrive, what the Steam Error popup means, whether an app that watches your keyboard is safe, and how to get friends' cats on screen. Each has its own guide below, written from the developer's announcements and replies.

What you need to install it, how taps pay for chests on the timer the developer set, the first settings worth changing, and why you should keep your duplicates.

## Quick answers before you pick a page

- Is it free? Yes, it is free to play on Steam; paid supporter items are optional and not needed for chest drops. The full breakdown is in [how to play](/bongo-cat/how-to-play/).
- Popup says Steam Error? Check Inventory History in Steam before retrying; during the February–March 2026 errors the developer said drops still went through. Steps are on the [Steam Error page](/bongo-cat/steam-error/).
- Does it read what you type? The developer says it only counts presses; the quotes and dates are on the [safety page](/bongo-cat/is-it-safe/).
- Can friends see your cat? Only in a shared lobby, and everyone needs the latest version; see the [multiplayer guide](/bongo-cat/multiplayer/).

## Where to go after your first week

Once chests are coming in steadily, the questions shift from "how does it work" to "how do I get the one hat I want". That is the job of the [Hats, Skins & Achievements](/bongo-cat/collection/) section: the rarity tiers and every item source, the exchange and trading rules, the event timeline and the full achievement table.

## Scope

These guides cover the Steam version for Windows, with notes where macOS or the Linux test branch differ. Timings such as the chest timer come from the developer's 2025 patch notes; if the game shows something different after an update, trust the game and tell us through the author page.
