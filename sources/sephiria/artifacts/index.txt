3:Tc63,<h2>The <a href="/guides/inventory-grid/">inventory grid</a> is the build</h2>
<p>Most games keep the build and the bag separate: you choose a build, then you carry things. Sephiria collapses the two into one screen.</p>
<p>Every cell in your inventory carries a number. That number is the level of whatever artifact is sitting in it. <a href="/tablets/all-tablets/">Tablets</a> are emitters that push numbers onto specific cells. Arranging the bag is arranging those numbers — and moving an artifact one cell can change its level.</p>
<p>This is the system that most often decides whether a run survives chapter 3, and it is also the one that reads as fiddly inventory management right up until it clicks.</p>
<h2>Four maxed beats twelve found</h2>
<p>The practical consequence is worth stating plainly, because it is counter-intuitive: <strong>a bag full of level-0 artifacts loses to a bag with four maxed ones.</strong></p>
<p>Artifacts scale in discrete levels with real stat arrays behind them — one runs 3/4/6/8 Ice Damage across its levels, another 6/12/18/24 HP. Max level varies per artifact, so a cell buffed to +5 is partly wasted on an artifact that caps at 3.</p>
<p>The advice experienced players give reduces to one rule: once you hold three or more useful artifacts, take the tablet over another artifact.</p>
<h2>Where artifacts actually come from</h2>
<p>More places than most players notice, and several are finite pools worth farming deliberately.</p>
<p>The obvious sources are Artifact nodes, Level Up rewards, mini-boss drops and shops. The ones people miss: the <strong><a href="/guides/destiny-inscription/">Dimensional Rift</a></strong> permanently unlocks 8 artifacts and 1 tablet into your pool for Sapphires and nowhere else; the <strong>Wandering Witch</strong> sells 11 artifacts and 2 tablets exclusive to her, at unusually low prices; <strong>Blood Donation</strong> trades 33% of your current HP for an item; <strong>Collin</strong> sells a Companion-combo artifact for 500 Leaf; and the <strong>Wishing Fountain</strong> lets you carry chosen items into a run.</p>
<h2>Two conversion devices</h2>
<p>The <strong>Mystic Pot</strong> turns 1 artifact into a different one of the same rarity, or 2 artifacts into one of higher rarity. The <strong>Tablet Combiner</strong> merges two tablets into one carrying both effects, for 200 Leaf, once per combiner.</p>
<p>Both matter because they turn dead picks into live ones. A run that offers you nothing you want is not lost — it is raw material.</p>
<h2>On completeness</h2>
<p>There is no full artifact table in this section, and the reason is not effort.</p>
<p>TEAM HORAY says the game has "about 300 artifacts." The most careful community audit counts 236. Those figures do not reconcile, and neither side has published a methodology. Any site showing you a complete, confident table has resolved that contradiction silently.</p>
