# Get Your Driver's License! Line: Every Skip Product Priced

You can skip the line in Get Your Driver's License! with five Robux products. Skip Line [SALE] moves you up 5 places for 9 Robux, Skip Time [SALE] cuts 2:30 from the waiting room for 9, Skip Test passes the written test for 29, Skip To End goes straight to the driving test for 48, and Retake Test costs 19.

Names, prices and descriptions below come from the [Roblox developer product API](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/10768565603/developerproducts?limit=100), read on 9 October 2026. All five were on sale. The game is free to play and no description says a purchase is required, but we have not played a full run without paying.

## How does the line work?

The official description gives it two lines: "Wait in line and get your Driver's License!" and "Take a number and wait your turn". That is all the developer says about it directly.

The store listings use two different words for the wait. Skip Line [SALE] talks about "the line", measured in places. Skip Time [SALE] talks about "the waiting room", measured in minutes and seconds. Whether those are one queue seen two ways or two separate stages is not stated. We read it as a queue you move up plus a timer you sit through, and we have not confirmed that in the game.

One number is on record: Roblox lists the game at 25 players per server. The developer does not say how the line is filled, so we do not turn that into a line length.

## What does each skip cost?

| Product | Robux | Official description | What it gets you past |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Skip Line [SALE] | 9 | Jump 5 places ahead in the line. | Five places in the line |
| Skip Time [SALE] | 9 | Skip 2:30 of the wait in the waiting room. | Two and a half minutes of the timer |
| Retake Test | 19 | Failed your driving test? Take it again right away, no line. | The whole line, after a failed drive |
| Skip Test | 29 | Pass the written test straight away. | The written test |
| Skip To End | 48 | Go straight to your driving test. | Everything before the driving test |

The last column is our reading of each description. Skip To End does not list what it skips. Its name and the phrase "straight to your driving test" suggest the line, the waiting room and the written test all go, and that is the reading used below.

## Which skip is the best value?

That depends on where you are stuck. The sums are ours.

| Situation | Cheapest product that fits | Robux | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One to five people ahead | Skip Line [SALE] | 9 | 1.8 Robux per place when you use all five |
| Many places to make up | Skip To End | 48 | Five Skip Line [SALE] purchases cost 45 and cover 25 places; a sixth brings the total to 54 |
| Stuck in the waiting room | Skip Time [SALE] | 9 | Removes 2:30 per purchase |
| Only the written test bothers you | Skip Test | 29 | Passes it "straight away" |
| You just failed the drive | Retake Test | 19 | 29 Robux cheaper than Skip To End |

One comparison is worth spelling out. Skip Line [SALE], Skip Time [SALE] and Skip Test bought together cost 47 Robux. Skip To End costs 48 and, on our reading, covers all three stages in full. If you were going to buy two of the small skips anyway, the single product is the tidier purchase.

## Is the [SALE] price a real discount?

Not according to Roblox's price record. Both products have "[SALE]" in their name, but the record for each shows a base price of 9 Robux, a current price of 9 Robux and an empty list of discounts. The tag is part of the name. Treat 9 Robux as the normal price.

## What do Kill and Kill All say about the line?

Two more product descriptions mention the line. Kill costs 9 Robux: "The person ahead of you in line goes poof." Kill All costs 160 Robux: "Everyone waiting in line goes poof."

Neither description says what going poof does to a player, or whether Kill All includes the buyer. If a poof ahead of you moves you up one place, Kill is five times the price per place of Skip Line [SALE]. Kill All costs more than three Skip To End purchases. On price alone both look like pranks first and shortcuts second. That is our view, not a description from the developer.

## Can you skip the line after failing the test?

Yes. Retake Test was added to the store on 8 October 2026, nine days after the game was created. Its description is the clearest statement we found of what a fail costs you: "Failed your driving test? Take it again right away, no line." The words "no line" imply that without it, a failed driver queues again. The [driving test guide](/get-your-drivers-license/driving-test/) covers what is known about passing.

## Can you buy the same skip twice?

All five are developer products, which Roblox defines as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once". Skip Line [SALE] and Skip Time [SALE] are worded as fixed amounts, 5 places and 2:30, so adding them up would take repeat purchases. We have not tested whether the game limits how often you can buy one in a single wait.

## Do you need to pay at all?

Nothing official says you do. Get Your Driver's License! is free to play, and no pass or product description says a purchase is required. We have not played a full run without paying, so we cannot confirm it from experience. The description's first words are "Wait in line". Our advice is to sit through the wait once before spending anything, because only then do you know how long it is in your server. The [how-to-play guide](/get-your-drivers-license/how-to-play/) sets out a plan for that first run, and the [Robux shop guide](/get-your-drivers-license/shop/) lists the other eight products.

## What is not published?

- How many places the line has and how numbers are handed out.
- How long the waiting room timer runs in total.
- Whether Skip To End also skips the written test. Its description names only the destination.
- What "goes poof" means for the player it happens to.
- Whether VIP Ticket 🎟️, described as "Ticket #001", changes your place in the line. Its description mentions only your next car.
