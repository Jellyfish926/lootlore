    Southern Mudding (listed on Roblox as "[🚀Nitrous!🚀] Southern Mudding 🚜 OffRoading") is an off-road driving game by the verified group of the same name. You spawn pickup trucks, semi trucks, trailers, classic cars and muscle cars on a map built for mud, then tow, haul, go boating, claim a house and customize what you drive.

When we checked at 11:19 UTC on 2 October 2026 it had 428 million visits, 625,025 favourites, 221,349 likes against 17,627 dislikes, and 14,526 people playing.     What can you actually do in the game?

    The description names four reasons to play: "off-road, play with friends, roleplay, or customize your vehicles". It also lists the activities the developer builds around: "heavy towing and deep mud runs", "hauling, boating, and dialing in your perfect truck setup". The four badges trace the basic loop: join, spawn a vehicle, spawn a trailer, claim a house.

    What the public data does not show is how you earn or spend in-game money, what the map's locations are called, or how any vehicle performs. We mark those gaps on each page instead of guessing.

    When does the game update?

    On Fridays. The description ends with "Updates every Friday!", and the developer's Roblox event listings back that up: "This Week's Update!" starts on Friday 2 October 2026 at 17:00 UTC, and "Next Week's Update!" on Friday 9 October at 17:00 UTC. The previous one, "🚀Nitrous Update!🚀", started at 17:15 UTC on 25 September. The update schedule page converts those times and lists all 43 weekly event titles since December 2025.

    What did the 25 September update add?

    Four lines, quoted from the description as it stood on 2 October 2026, before that day's update: "New Nitrous Customization for Pickup Trucks! Add it in the customization garage.", "New Pickup Truck!", "New UTV!" and "New LED Whip Lights!". The nitrous line is the only one with an instruction attached. Our nitrous and customization guide sets out what that confirms and what still needs an in-game check.

    What can you buy with Robux?

    Thirteen game passes, from 80 to 490 Robux, which together cost 3,685 Robux. They fall into three groups: vehicle packs, spawning and trailer tools, and customization. The store also holds 61 developer products. Twenty-two of those are single vehicles priced from 200 to 325 Robux, most with "Limited" in the name, and each has a gift version at the same price. The rest are gift products for the passes (twelve named as gifts and one probable), three paid tornado spawns and one event unlock.

    The Robux vehicle list has every name, price and creation date, and the vehicle overview explains which vehicle types sit behind which purchase.

    Why is there no codes page?

    Because no official source we could read lists a code. The game description and the group description carry none, and the group shout was empty. The group wall endpoint returned an error when we asked for it, the game's social links need an age-verified sign-in, and we found no Discord invite we could trace to an official page, so we could not read them. The one free reward the developer states publicly is "Join the group for a free pickup truck!". Our community page covers the group and what we could not confirm.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who made Southern Mudding?
    The Roblox group Southern Mudding, which carries Roblox's verified badge and is owned by the account SouthernMudHold. The experience was created on 15 September 2025 and is the group's only public game.
    When does Southern Mudding update?
    The game description says "Updates every Friday!". The developer's Roblox event listings for 2 and 9 October 2026 both start at 17:00 UTC. See the update schedule.
    Are there Southern Mudding codes?
    We found no code in the game description, the group description or the group shout, so we list none. The one free reward the developer does publish is on the group page: "Join the group for a free pickup truck!". Details are in codes, group and Discord.
    How many players fit in a server?
    Ten, according to the Roblox listing. Private servers were not enabled when we checked on 2 October 2026.
    Is Southern Mudding free?
    Yes. The listing has no entry price. Robux purchases are optional: 13 game passes and 61 developer products, covered in the game pass guide and the Robux vehicle list.
