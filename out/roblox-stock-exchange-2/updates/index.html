    Roblox Stock Exchange 2 Updates: Official Event History

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-08

    Scope: All 12 events returned by the Roblox virtual-events listing for the game on 2026-10-08 (the listing returned only the last 2 events on one request that day and all 12 on later requests), official event art, and creation dates of passes, products and badges; an event listing is an announcement and does not prove when a feature went live; we found no official patch notes

    • The Custom Offices event is listed to start on Saturday 10 October 2026 at 20:00 UTC, and its listing ends on 16 October 2026 at 23:00 UTC.

    Roblox Stock Exchange 2 has 12 official update events on Roblox. The first was listed to start on 17 August 2026. The twelfth, Custom Offices, is listed to start on Saturday 10 October 2026 at 20:00 UTC and reads "Your own 3d office you can walk inside of and upgrade". The other eleven include hedge funds, seasons and bonds.

    We found no patch notes for Roblox Stock Exchange 2 from Summit Productions Development. What exists is a run of official event listings, each with a title, a short description and an image. This page reads all of them.

    What time is the Custom Offices start in your time zone?

    The listing runs from 20:00 UTC on 10 October to 23:00 UTC on 16 October (the exact timestamps are 20:00:18 and 23:00:18). Converted for seven zones:

    Time zoneListed startListing ends
    UTCSat 10 Oct, 20:00Fri 16 Oct, 23:00
    US Pacific (PDT)Sat 10 Oct, 13:00Fri 16 Oct, 16:00
    US Eastern (EDT)Sat 10 Oct, 16:00Fri 16 Oct, 19:00
    Brazil (BRT)Sat 10 Oct, 17:00Fri 16 Oct, 20:00
    UK (BST)Sat 10 Oct, 21:00Sat 17 Oct, 00:00
    Central Europe (CEST)Sat 10 Oct, 22:00Sat 17 Oct, 01:00
    UTC+8 (Manila, Singapore)Sun 11 Oct, 04:00Sat 17 Oct, 07:00

    The listed start is the time to note. Roblox's event system lets players "opt into notifications that they'll receive when your event begins", so you can ask for a reminder from the event on the game's Roblox page.

    What has the developer said about Custom Offices?

    One sentence and one picture. The listing's title is Custom Offices and its subtitle is "New Update". The whole description reads "Your own 3d office you can walk inside of and upgrade". The host is Summit Productions Development, the category is new content, and the listing was created on 3 October 2026 at 09:08 UTC.

    How you get an office, what upgrades cost and whether an office changes your trading are not stated. The event art shows a single room, and no trading screen.

None of these events came with a new Roblox badge: the newest badge was created on 15 August, two days before the first event, and all eleven are in the badge list.

    What does the official event art add?

    Each event has a piece of art, and several of them carry more detail than the one-line descriptions. The art is made to promote an update, so numbers in it are illustrations and the live game can differ.

    EventWhat the art shows

    NEW FUNCTIONSA script editor with a MACD example, labelled 48 functions and 10 price sources

    One mismatch is worth knowing. The REAL ESTATE description says "a global map", while its art shows a United States map and says 50 states. We have not checked which one the live game matches.

    How often are updates announced?

    Official events have been listed with a start about once a week, usually on a Saturday. Eight of the twelve events have a Saturday start, and every Saturday from 22 August to 10 October 2026 has one. The other four started on a Monday or a Wednesday: HEDGE FUNDS, NEW FUNCTIONS, CHALLENGES and NEWS OVERHAUL, all between 17 August and 2 September.

    Start hours vary. The Saturday events of 12, 19 and 26 September are listed at 20:00 UTC, the interface overhaul of 3 October at 06:00 UTC, and Custom Offices on 10 October at 20:00 UTC.

    Why not use the "updated" date on the game page?

    Because it does not mark updates. We read the game's Roblox record three times within 24 minutes on 8 October 2026, and its "updated" field showed a different time on each read: 03:36:09, 03:44:15 and 03:56:24 UTC. So we do not use it as a patch date; this page dates updates by event listings and store records instead.

    What do the store records show?

    Every pass, product and badge carries a creation date. Read together they show when the shop grew:

    Date (2026, UTC)Created that day
    13 JulyThe experience itself; 8 game passes; 11 developer products, including the four algo bot products and the three time skips; 5 badges
    14 JulyMore Player Companies pass; Double Earnings and Trader Starter Pack; 5 more badges
    19 JulyOvernight Desk pass; 10 products with pass-style names
    8 and 9 AugustCustom Timeframes and Unlimited AI Usage passes; three AI Token packs
    15 AugustAll Gamepasses Bundle pass; Instant Acension and three products with pass names; the Met the game owners! badge
    2 OctoberTrader Pass - Season 1

    A creation date proves the item existed by then. It does not prove it was on sale in the game that day. Prices for all of these are in the Robux shop guide, and the code that arrived with the 10K-likes milestone is on the codes page.

    How do we keep this page current?

    On each review we re-read the Roblox Stock Exchange 2 event listing, both store lists and the badge list. After the Custom Offices listing's end time (16 October 2026, 23:00 UTC) we will re-read all four and add whatever the store shows.
