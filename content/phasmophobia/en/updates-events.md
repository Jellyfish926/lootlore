## How to read dates on these pages

Three kinds of date show up, and they are not interchangeable.

- Announced dates are the developer's stated plan, such as the Crimson Eye window. We write "announced for" until there is a post confirming it happened.
- Steam post dates are the UTC timestamps on the official Steam announcements. We use them for every patch in the timeline.
- Official site dates come from the news list on kineticgames.co.uk. They sometimes sit a day or more away from the Steam timestamp for the same post, and we mention it when they do.

The same care applies to wording. The Steam post and the site version of an announcement are usually identical, but not always: the Crimson Eye announcement gained an edit on the site moving its patch from October 6 to October 7, and the December 2025 notes list three new ghost types on the site that the Steam version leaves out.

## What this section does not do

It does not predict. If the developer has not dated something, the page says "not announced". It also does not cover data-mined or leaked content, test branch builds or anything from community Discord servers.
