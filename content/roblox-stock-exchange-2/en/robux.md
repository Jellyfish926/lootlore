# Roblox Stock Exchange 2 Passes and Robux Shop Guides

Roblox Stock Exchange 2 is free to play, and everything it sells for Robux falls into two groups: 13 game passes that you buy once, and 31 developer products that you can buy again. These three guides list every one with its official name and price, and are careful about what the developer has and has not explained.

One thing helps. Twelve of the thirteen passes come with precise descriptions, such as a fee rate, a leverage cap and an offline window in hours. The products mostly come with a name and nothing else. So the pass guide can tell you what you get, and the shop guide often can only tell you what something is called.

## Pick a guide by purchase

### [Game Passes: All 13 and Prices](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/gamepasses/)

Every pass with the developer's full description, from Custom Timeframes at 39 Robux to the All Gamepasses Bundle at 999. Includes the bundle maths, the limits each pass raises, and the four passes whose same-named product has a different price.

### [Robux Shop: All 31 Products](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/shop/)

Time skips from 9 Robux, three AI Token packs, Trader Spotlight by the minute, hour or day, and the Season 1 Trader Pass, which the developer describes as cosmetic only.

### [Algo Bots: Costs and Settings](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/algo-bots/)

The four bot products, the First Algo Bot badge count, the hedge fund strategy that lowers bot commission, and a straight answer on why nobody can hand you official best settings.

## How do the purchase types compare?

| Type | How many | Robux range | Buy more than once? | Official descriptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single game pass | 12 | 39 to 299 | No | All 12 |
| All Gamepasses Bundle | 1 | 999 | No | One line |
| Developer product | 31 | 9 to 999 | Yes | 1 of 31 |

The "buy more than once" column follows Roblox's own definitions. A pass is "a one-time Robux fee to access special privileges inside your game", and a developer product is "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once".

## What should you read before spending anything?

Start outside this section. The [how-to-play guide](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/how-to-play/) shows what the pass descriptions imply about the base game: a default of 8 offline hours ("instead of 8") and two player-founded companies ("instead of 2"). Several passes raise limits whose base values are not published.

Then look at the code. One code is printed in the game description, and the [codes page](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/codes/) tracks it; we have not tested it in the game.

If you do buy, our general order is passes before products. A pass with a clear description is a known quantity. A product with no description is a guess, and a guess you can pay for more than once.

## What is not in these guides?

In-game cash prices, the standard trading fee, leverage caps by level and bot performance. None of those is published in an official source we could read, and we do not fill gaps with numbers from videos or other sites.

## Scope

Prices were read from Roblox on 8 October 2026. The newest product in the list was created on 2 October 2026. Everything in the game is simulated, and nothing here is financial advice.
