    What Shift At Midnight actually is

    Shift At Midnight is a co-op survival horror game built by solo developer Bun Muen and published by Kwalee. It released on 22 July 2026 for Steam, Xbox Series X|S and the Microsoft Store, and it landed on Xbox Game Pass on day one. It is an Xbox Play Anywhere title, which matters more than it sounds — that single detail is why the crossplay answer is messy.

    The loop is a shift. You clock in at a gas station, and customers arrive. Your job is to serve them, keep the shelves stocked and hit a quota. The complication is that some customers are doppelgangers — entities that copy a human's appearance, voice, mannerisms and backstory well enough to walk through the door and ask for cigarettes. You have an ID verification computer and your own judgement, and you have to decide who gets served and who gets dealt with.

    Get it wrong in the generous direction and something comes through the door that shouldn't have. Get it wrong in the other direction and you have killed a paying customer, which the game is happy to let you do — the most common achievement in the entire game, held by 96.9% of players, is called First Blood and it unlocks for killing your first customer. That statistic is a design statement.

    Every named threat

    Most of what kills you has a name, and the names matter because the counterplay is completely different for each one. There is no universal answer — the tool that saves you from one of these gets you killed by another.

    Marionette Boss. Tied to a wind-up music box. Only 41.6% of players have killed one. The Dentist Eight feet tall, invisible to the ID computer. No weapon, trap or barricade works. You run. Jack-in-the-Box Wind it or it springs. The same box decides whether the Marionette fight is survivable. Shrieking Doll Crawler that screams to pull your attention, then follows you. Demented Killing one unlocks Freed — held by 79.8% of players. Norbert Not a threat. A gnome with a fake ID who shows up on Night 2 to cause chaos.

    Doppelgangers are the actual game

    The monsters get the attention, but the thing you spend most of a shift doing is deciding whether the person at the counter is human. Doppelgangers mimic look, voice, mannerisms and background. The ID verification computer is your primary tool, and it has a specific, narrow function: it tells you whether the document is authentic. It does not tell you whether the holder is human.

    That gap is where the game lives. Norbert is the cleanest illustration — his ID scans as fake, he is openly not what he claims to be, and he will not hurt you. Meanwhile the Dentist does not register on the computer at all. A player who has learned "fake ID equals kill" will kill Norbert and get ambushed by something the scanner never flagged.

    Content note

    Steam labels this game as containing "plenty of gore and blood". It is a horror game about deciding whether to kill the person in front of you, and it does not soften that.

    One person, and a rule about guessing

    This is an independent fan wiki, not an official Kwalee or Bun Muen site. It is written and maintained by one person who plays the game and checks the numbers, not generated in bulk.

    The rule that shapes every page here: facts come from the official Steam store listing and public Steam global achievement stats. Inference is labelled as inference.     Common questions

    Is Shift At Midnight on Game Pass?

    Yes. It launched day one on Xbox Game Pass for both console and PC, and it is an Xbox Play Anywhere title — one Microsoft Store purchase covers the Xbox and Windows versions. Full detail on the Game Pass page.

    Is Shift At Midnight crossplay?

    Partially, and the distinction costs people money. Xbox console players and PC Game Pass players share one pool through Play Anywhere. The Steam version connects only to other Steam players. If half your group owns it on Steam and half plays it on Game Pass, you cannot play together. See the crossplay page.

    How many people can play together?

    Up to three, in online co-op with proximity chat. It also has a full single-player mode. See multiplayer.

    How much does it cost?

    $9.99 USD. The 10% launch discount ended on 29 July 2026. It is also included with Game Pass, which for a lot of players makes the purchase question moot. See price & editions.

    Is Shift At Midnight on PS5 or mobile?

    No to both. It is PC (Steam, Microsoft Store) and Xbox Series X|S only — no PlayStation, Switch, iOS or Android version has been announced. See platforms.

    Is Shift At Midnight free?

    The game is $9.99, but it is included with Xbox Game Pass at no extra cost on console and PC. See price.

    Does it have mods?

    There is no official mod support or Steam Workshop integration announced as of 28 July 2026. See the mods page for what that means in practice.
