# Get Your Driver's License! Driving Test and Endings

In the official description of Get Your Driver's License!, the driving test is listed after the line and the written test. You drive a test course with cows, ducks, ramps "and more". The description also names two endings, Honor Roll and Towed. Fail the drive, and Retake Test (19 Robux) offers another try with "no line".

This page collects every official statement about the test, the endings and the examiner. It is short on step-by-step advice for one reason: we found no published route, scoring rules or ending list, and we have not driven the course ourselves yet.

## What is on the test course?

One line of the official description covers it: "Drive the test course: cows, ducks, ramps and more". So the course has animals and ramps, and the list is not complete.

The game's two official images add a little set dressing. Both show a sign reading DRIVING SCHOOL and orange traffic cones. The icon shows a small red car with an L plate on its roof. These are promotional drawings. They do not show the course layout, and nothing in them tells you where a cow or a ramp sits.

## Which endings does the developer name?

| Ending | Where the name appears | What is published about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Honor Roll | Game description | The name only |
| Towed | Game description | The name only |

The full line is "Unlock every ending, from Honor Roll to Towed". The phrase "from ... to ..." reads like a range with a best and a worst end, and the names fit that reading: an honor roll is a list of top students, and a towed car has stopped moving. That is our interpretation. The description does not say how many endings there are, what triggers each one, or which order they fall in.

"Unlock every ending" suggests endings are kept once earned. Roblox lists no badges for the game, and where endings are recorded is not stated.

## What does failing look like?

The promotional image on the game's Roblox page shows it from inside the car. An examiner with a moustache and a red shirt shouts and points at the driver, holding a clipboard marked with a large red F in a circle. The driver, in a blue hoodie, is in tears with both hands on their head. The icon shows the other outcome: a character in the same blue hoodie grinning and holding up a card headed DRIVER LICENSE with a green tick.

Art is not a rulebook, but it matches the store. One product exists only for drivers who fail.

![Store icon: a grey steering wheel with a red centre and an orange circular retry arrow in the corner](prod-retake-test "Official store icon of the Retake Test product, 19 Robux")

## What happens if you fail the driving test?

Retake Test costs 19 Robux and its description reads: "Failed your driving test? Take it again right away, no line." Three things follow from that sentence.

- The driving test can be failed.
- A fail normally costs you time. "Right away, no line" is the selling point, which implies the free route is to queue again.
- A retake is cheaper than Skip To End, which costs 48 Robux.

Retake Test is the newest listing in the store, created on 8 October 2026. Whether a failed run still awards a car or an ending is not stated. The [line guide](/get-your-drivers-license/waiting-line/) compares it with the other four skips.

## Who is the Examiner, and how do you become one?

The description's last feature line is "Be the Examiner and judge other players' driving". The examiner is a role a player takes, not only a character in the art.

The store sells time in that role. Prices are from Roblox's product record on 9 October 2026, and the totals are our arithmetic.

| Purchase | Robux | Time as examiner | Robux per minute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Be The Examiner 📋 | 29 | 5 minutes | 5.8 |
| +1 Minute | 9 | 1 more minute | 9 |
| Be The Examiner 📋 plus one +1 Minute | 38 | 6 minutes | about 6.3 |
| Be The Examiner 📋 plus five +1 Minute | 74 | 10 minutes | 7.4 |

Both are developer products, which Roblox defines as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once". On those prices a second Be The Examiner 📋 is better value than five single minutes: 29 Robux against 45 for the same five minutes. We have not checked whether the game lets you stack them.

Two questions stay open. The description does not say whether the role can also be reached without Robux. And no source we read says what the examiner's judgement decides: it may set the ending, the pass or fail, or neither.

## What does the description say you get?

Two things are named in the description. One is a car: "Win 1 of 18 cars, from rusty hatchbacks to secret supercars". The other is the ending. Neither line ties a particular car to a particular ending, so we do not claim that a better drive wins a better car. What is published about car tiers, including the one tier name the store gives away, is in the [car guide](/get-your-drivers-license/cars/).

## How can you track endings yourself?

Until a full list is confirmed, the practical method is a notebook. After each run of Get Your Driver's License!, write down the ending name exactly as the game shows it, if it shows one, and what you did differently on the course. Two names are confirmed so far: Honor Roll and Towed. If you are new to the game, the [how-to-play guide](/get-your-drivers-license/how-to-play/) covers the line and the written test.
