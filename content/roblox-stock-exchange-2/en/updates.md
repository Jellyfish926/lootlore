# Roblox Stock Exchange 2 Updates: Official Event History

Roblox Stock Exchange 2 has 12 official update events on Roblox. The first was listed to start on 17 August 2026. The twelfth, Custom Offices, is listed to start on Saturday 10 October 2026 at 20:00 UTC and reads "Your own 3d office you can walk inside of and upgrade". The other eleven include hedge funds, seasons and bonds.

We found no patch notes for Roblox Stock Exchange 2 from Summit Productions Development. What exists is a run of official event listings, each with a title, a short description and an image. This page reads all of them.

## What time is the Custom Offices start in your time zone?

The listing runs from 20:00 UTC on 10 October to 23:00 UTC on 16 October (the exact timestamps are 20:00:18 and 23:00:18). Converted for seven zones:

| Time zone | Listed start | Listing ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UTC | Sat 10 Oct, 20:00 | Fri 16 Oct, 23:00 |
| US Pacific (PDT) | Sat 10 Oct, 13:00 | Fri 16 Oct, 16:00 |
| US Eastern (EDT) | Sat 10 Oct, 16:00 | Fri 16 Oct, 19:00 |
| Brazil (BRT) | Sat 10 Oct, 17:00 | Fri 16 Oct, 20:00 |
| UK (BST) | Sat 10 Oct, 21:00 | Sat 17 Oct, 00:00 |
| Central Europe (CEST) | Sat 10 Oct, 22:00 | Sat 17 Oct, 01:00 |
| UTC+8 (Manila, Singapore) | Sun 11 Oct, 04:00 | Sat 17 Oct, 07:00 |

The listed start is the time to note. Roblox's event system lets players "opt into notifications that they'll receive when your event begins", so you can ask for a reminder from the event on the game's Roblox page.

## What has the developer said about Custom Offices?

One sentence and one picture. The listing's title is Custom Offices and its subtitle is "New Update". The whole description reads "Your own 3d office you can walk inside of and upgrade". The host is Summit Productions Development, the category is new content, and the listing was created on 3 October 2026 at 09:08 UTC.

How you get an office, what upgrades cost and whether an office changes your trading are not stated. The event art shows a single room, and no trading screen.

## How often are updates announced?

Official events have been listed with a start about once a week, usually on a Saturday. Eight of the twelve events have a Saturday start, and every Saturday from 22 August to 10 October 2026 has one. The other four started on a Monday or a Wednesday: HEDGE FUNDS, NEW FUNCTIONS, CHALLENGES and NEWS OVERHAUL, all between 17 August and 2 September.

Start hours vary. The Saturday events of 12, 19 and 26 September are listed at 20:00 UTC, the interface overhaul of 3 October at 06:00 UTC, and Custom Offices on 10 October at 20:00 UTC.

## Why not use the "updated" date on the game page?

Because it does not mark updates. We read the game's Roblox record three times within 24 minutes on 8 October 2026, and its "updated" field showed a different time on each read: 03:36:09, 03:44:15 and 03:56:24 UTC. So we do not use it as a patch date; this page dates updates by event listings and store records instead.
