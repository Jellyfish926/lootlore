About Sephiria Wiki

Last updated 2026-08-10

Sephiria Wiki is an independent reference for Sephiria, written by players who are still working through the tower themselves. This page covers the three things worth knowing before you trust anything here: who writes it, how it gets checked, and who pays for it.

The problem this site is trying to solve

Sephiria released on 31 July 2026. That is recent enough that most of what circulates about it — item effects, boss behaviour, which weapon class is strongest — is reconstruction rather than published fact, and a lot of it is repeated between sites without anyone checking it once.

We are trying to be the version that says which is which. Where a mechanic is confirmed, we say what confirmed it. Where it is not, we say so and leave the gap visible instead of filling it with something plausible.

How a page gets published

Every guide here ends with a Sources checked box naming exactly what that page was verified against — the Steam listing, TEAM HORAY's own material, community documentation, or our own play. If a claim cannot be traced to one of those, it does not go on the page.

That rule has a visible cost, and we would rather you see it than not: there is no complete artifact table on this site, and no per-boss phase breakdown. Both would be popular. Neither can be sourced properly yet, and a boss guide with a wrong tell sends people into a fight prepared for the wrong thing.

Who writes it

A small independent team. We are not affiliated with TEAM HORAY, we do not receive review copies or early access, and no one from the studio reviews anything before it goes up.

What this site is not

  • Not editable by readers — corrections come through us so they can be checked first.
  • Not a news site. We cover systems, not announcements.
  • Not official, and not endorsed by TEAM HORAY.

Corrections

Corrections are the single most useful thing you can send. If something here is wrong, email hello@sephiriawiki.site or use the contact page — tell us the page and, if you have it, where the right answer comes from. Fixes go live with an updated verification date.

How the site is funded

Sephiria Wiki is free to read and paid for by advertising, plus affiliate links to game retailers where they are relevant. Neither changes what we write: guides are drafted before any commercial consideration, and pages carrying affiliate links say so on the page. Details of what advertising sets on your device are in the privacy policy.

The authoritative source

For anything official — pricing, patch notes, platform support — TEAM HORAY is the source, not us. See the Steam store page.
