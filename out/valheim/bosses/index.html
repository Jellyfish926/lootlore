    Valheim Boss Guides: All Eight Fights

    Author Jellyfi · Published 2026-09-17 · Last checked 2026-09-17 · Checked on version 1.0.12

    Language: English中文

    Key points

    • Eight boss guides ordered by main progression stage, from Eikthyr to the final fight.
    • Each page starts from the summon or entry condition, then gear, food and an exit.
    • The plans are first-playthrough thinking; they do not rely on terrain exploits or one recorded kill.

    These are ordered by main progression stage. Confirm the summon or entry conditions first, then sort out gear, food and an exit. The guides give first-playthrough thinking, do not rely on terrain exploits, and do not treat one recorded kill as something any setup can repeat.

    Pick a guide by problem

    • Valheim Eikthyr: Summon Items and Fight Plan

      Bring two deer trophies, learn to read the melee and lightning attacks, and turn the drops from Eikthyr into your first mining trip in the Black Forest.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim The Elder: Seeds and Swamp Key

      Prepare The Elder fight in the Black Forest: where ancient seeds come from, how to move under vines and roots, and how to get ready for the Swamp after.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Bonemass: Blunt Hits, Poison Resist

      Summon Bonemass with ten withered bones, pick a damage type that actually works, handle the poison cloud and adds, then use the Wishbone in the Mountains.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Moder: Dragon Eggs and Safe Windows

      How to summon Moder and set up the battlefield, what changes between the flying and grounded phases, and why the dragon tear matters for the next stage.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Yagluth: Totems and Fire Resistance

      Summon Yagluth in the Plains, tell the melee burst, ranged breath and meteor pressure apart, and turn the fight's drop into your Mistlands preparation.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim The Queen: Sealbreaker and Arena

      What you need to enter the Queen's stronghold, how heavy knockback, adds and multi-level terrain stack up, and what melee and magic builds should prepare.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Fader: Bells, Ground Tells, Retreats

      How to prepare the Fader summon, read the ground spikes, fire and meteors that reshape the arena, and what the win opens up toward the Deep North.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Kall Fimbulbringer: Three-Phase Plan

      A framework for the three-phase final boss: direct attacks, an invulnerable ice block with seven summoned boss avatars, then a stronger elemental return.

      v1.0.12

    Scope

    These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
