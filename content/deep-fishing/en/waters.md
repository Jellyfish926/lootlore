# Deep Fishing Waters: Fernshore, The Pond, Dune Haven

The only official statement about waters in Deep Fishing is in the game description: "Reach new waters with rarer fish." Cast distance is how you reach them, which is why Strength and rods matter. The names and unlock costs of individual waters have not been published by LazyGames.

## What does the developer confirm about waters?

| Official line | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Reach new waters with rarer fish | Roblox game description |
| Upgrade your Strength to throw farther | Roblox game description |
| Reach the water to start fishing | Roblox game description |

That is enough to plan around distance — see the [how-to-play guide](/deep-fishing/how-to-play/) and the [rod list](/deep-fishing/rods/) — but not enough to name each water.

## Which waters do fan guides report? (unverified)

| Water | Reported unlock | Reported by | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fernshore | Starting water, no gate | deep-fishing.wiki | Unverified |
| The Pond | Level 5 and 25,000 coins | deep-fishing.wiki, citing a gameplay video | Unverified |
| Dune Haven | 2,500,000 coins; linked to enchanting | deep-fishing.wiki, citing a gameplay video | Unverified |
| Deep ocean | Casts past about 1,014 m with Tide Bait | deep-fishing.wiki | Unverified |

The same fan wiki says a full official water order "is not currently documented". Video titles also mention a "Frost Key", a rod that needs a "frozen key", and a "Toxic Kraken" update, which may be tied to later waters; we have not watched those videos in full.

## What we need before publishing

- An in-game capture of the travel or map menu showing each water's name and cost.
- Confirmation of the level requirement for The Pond.
- Whether Dune Haven is a fishing water or only an enchanting location — related to the Enchant Stones in the [Robux shop](/deep-fishing/shop/).
