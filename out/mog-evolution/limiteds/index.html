    +1 Mog Evolution Gigachad, Claviculars Hammer and Smoothie

    Key points

    • Five store items cost 399 Robux each: Gigachad [LIMITED], Claviculars Hammer, Smoothie, LOOKSMEOWXER [W2 LIMITED] and MANGO MOGGER [W3 LIMITED].
    • Gigachad is a body with a x4 Appeal multiplier; Claviculars Hammer gives x1024 Appeal; Smoothie gives x2048 Charisma. All work 'while equipped'.
    • Gigachad's 1,500-copy counter is 'informational': you can still buy it after it reaches zero.
    • The two World-limited items have no description, so we cannot say what they do.

    Five unlocks in +1 Mog Evolution cost exactly 399 Robux each. Three are described: the Gigachad body (x4 Appeal), the Claviculars Hammer (x1024 Appeal) and the Smoothie (x2048 Charisma). Two World-limited items, LOOKSMEOWXER and MANGO MOGGER, have no description at all.

    Each described item is a permanent, one-per-player unlock that works "while equipped". Here is what the developer says about each, and how we would choose.

    What does each 399-Robux unlock do?

    ItemAddedTypeEffect, in the developer's words
    Gigachad [LIMITED]10 Sep 2026Body"Gives a x4 body Appeal multiplier while equipped."
    Claviculars Hammer10 Sep 2026Hammer"Gives x1024 Appeal while equipped."
    Smoothie24 Sep 2026Not stated"Gives x2048 Charisma while equipped."
    LOOKSMEOWXER [W2 LIMITED]26 Sep 2026Not statedNo description
    MANGO MOGGER [W3 LIMITED]26 Sep 2026Not statedNo description

    All five are developer products, not game passes, and all were on sale on 1 October 2026.

    Is the Gigachad body really limited?

    Not in the usual sense. Its full description reads: "Permanently unlock the Gigachad body in +1 Mog Evolution. Gives a x4 body Appeal multiplier while equipped. One unlock per player. The 1,500-copy counter is informational; purchases remain available after it reaches zero."

    So the counter you may see in the game does not stop sales. You do not need to rush to buy it before the number runs out. The "x4 body Appeal multiplier" wording suggests other bodies have their own multipliers too, but the developer has not published them. The body system is explained in the progression guide.

    How strong is the Claviculars Hammer?

    On paper, very. "Permanently unlock Claviculars Hammer in +1 Mog Evolution. Gives x1024 Appeal while equipped. Unlimited availability; one permanent unlock per player."

    x1024 is the largest Appeal multiplier in the store's wording, ahead of the x999 treadmill belt. But the two are not alike: the hammer works while equipped, the belt only while you stand on it. Whether the hammer's x1024 replaces your normal hammer's bonus or adds to it is not stated. Bonesmash, the hammer upgrade named in the game description, is covered in how to play.

    What is the Smoothie, and what is Charisma?

    "Permanently unlock Smoothie in +1 Mog Evolution. Gives x2048 Charisma while equipped. Unlimited availability; one permanent unlock per player."

    "Charisma" appears nowhere else in the game's official text. It may be another name for Appeal, as "Power" seems to be in the Appeal packs, but we could not confirm it. Until we can, we treat x2048 Charisma as an unknown stat, not as double the Claviculars Hammer.

    What are LOOKSMEOWXER and MANGO MOGGER?

    Both were added to the store on 26 September 2026, and both cost 399 Robux. The tags in their names, "[W2 LIMITED]" and "[W3 LIMITED]", point to World 2 and World 3. Neither has a description, so we do not know whether they are bodies, hammers or something else, or whether they are limited by time or copies. When they appeared is on the updates page.

    How do they compare with cheaper boosts?

    A 399-Robux unlock is a big spend in this store. Here is how the official multipliers line up against the cheaper permanent boosts, sorted by price. The wording differs ("while equipped", "while standing on it", "permanently earn"), so these numbers are not directly comparable.

    ItemRobuxMultiplier, as wordedWhen it works

    x256 Power199256x PowerAlways (permanent)
    x99 Treadmill24999x AppealWhile standing on the belt
    Gigachad [LIMITED]399x4 body AppealWhile the body is equipped

    If the boosts multiply together, the cheap permanent ones make every 399 unlock stronger. If only the highest counts, the Claviculars Hammer is the biggest confirmed Appeal number in the game.

    Which one should you buy?

    Our advice, based only on the official wording:

    If you want…PickWhy
    The biggest confirmed Appeal multiplierClaviculars Hammerx1024 Appeal, while equipped
    A body upgradeGigachadx4 body Appeal; no rush, the counter does not end sales
    To gamble on an unknown statSmoothiex2048, but "Charisma" is unexplained
    A World-limited collectibleWaitNo description yet; we would not buy blind

    None of the five is mentioned in the game description, which describes the whole loop without them. Before any of these, check the cheaper boosts in the Appeal guide. VIP (10x) and the first Power multipliers cost a fraction of 399 Robux. For the full price list, see all 43 Robux products.
