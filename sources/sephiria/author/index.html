How this site is written

Every page starts from a primary source — a TEAM HORAY patch note, the Steam store page, or a named community guide with real play time behind it — and says which one at the bottom under Sources checked for this page. Where two sources disagree, both are printed. Numbers that nobody has published are marked as unpublished rather than guessed, and pages carry the date they were last checked against the live game and the version they were checked on. The full rules are in the editorial policy.
