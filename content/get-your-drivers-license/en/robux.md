# Get Your Driver's License! Cars and Robux Shop Guides

Get Your Driver's License! sells 18 things for Robux: 5 game passes and 13 developer products. The passes include an airhorn, a gravity gun and a ban hammer. The product descriptions cover skipping the line, retaking the test, examiner time and cars. These three guides cover all of it.

Prices run from 9 Robux to 1,200. Every item has a one-line official description, and the guides quote each one in full, so you can judge a purchase by what the developer actually promises.

## What should you read before spending anything?

Start with the basics. The game is free to play and no description says a purchase is required, though we have not played a full run without paying. The [how-to-play guide](/get-your-drivers-license/how-to-play/) sets out what the description lists, and the [line guide](/get-your-drivers-license/waiting-line/) works out which skip fits which situation. Two points save the most Robux. The two products tagged [SALE] show no discount in Roblox's price record. And Golden Supercar 🏆, described as "The best car in the game", costs 39 Robux, far less than the 149-Robux VIP Ticket 🎟️.

## What is not in these guides?

Car names, tier odds and anything else the store listings do not say. We have not tested purchases in the game, so effects are quoted, not described from experience. No product sells an ending, and no code is listed anywhere on this site, because none appears in the official sources we could read.

## Scope

Prices were read on 9 October 2026 and can change without notice. Check the Robux amount on the Roblox purchase prompt before you confirm. Get Your Driver's License! is free to play, and suggestions about what to buy first are our opinion.
