Terms of Use for the Orc Problem Field Manual

Last updated 2026-09-01

These terms cover your use of the Orc Problem Field Manual at sirwehaveanorcproblem.store. Reading on means you accept them. There is nothing unusual buried in here, but the details matter, so they are written out.

The short version

Read anything, link anywhere, quote a little with credit, do not copy pages wholesale, and treat every number as a dated reading rather than a promise. That is the whole agreement; the sections below only make it precise.

Reading, linking and quoting

Every page is free to read and free to link — deep links to individual guides are welcome. Quoting a table row or a paragraph is fine when it credits the site and links to the page it came from. Copying entire pages, mirroring the site, or feeding it into scrapers that republish the content elsewhere is not permitted. Where our tables themselves cite a named Steam guide, credit belongs to that author first; take the citation with you.

Whose game this is

Sir, We Have an Orc Problem — its name, artwork, screenshots and every orc in it — belongs to Mumpitz Games. This site is an unaffiliated fan reference that uses those materials only to identify and discuss the game. Official screenshots appear with attribution in the caption. If Mumpitz Games ever objects to any use of their material here, it comes down.

No warranty on the numbers

The entire point of this site is that its figures are traced to dated sources, and the flip side of that honesty is a limit: a number that was right when retrieved can be wrong after the next patch. Everything here is provided as-is. Build decisions, achievement attempts and purchases you make based on these pages are your own calls, and a stat sheet that has gone stale is not grounds for a claim against us.

Advertising and external links

Pages carry third-party display advertising, which is what pays the hosting bill, and they link out to Steam pages, community guides and the developer's announcements. We control neither the ads' landing pages nor the linked sites. How advertising interacts with your data is covered in the privacy policy; why it does not influence what we write is covered in the editorial policy.

Changes

When these terms change, the date at the top of this page changes with them. Continued use after a change means the current version applies. Questions about any of this belong on the contact page.
