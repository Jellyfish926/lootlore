    The next update of +1 Mog Evolution is tied to an official event: Admin Abuse & Update 4, from 3 October 2026 at 23:00 UTC to 7 October 2026 at 23:00 UTC. Navoj Mog has not published patch notes, so below we rebuild the update history from the date each store item appeared.

    Treat this page as a timeline of evidence, not a changelog. A new product tells you a system existed by that date; it does not tell you everything the update changed.

    When is Admin Abuse & Update 4?

    Roblox's event listing for the game gives these times. We converted them for a few common time zones.

    Time zoneStartsEnds

    The event is hosted by the Navoj Mog group, filed under "new content", and was created on 28 September. Its title, subtitle and description all read "Admin Abuse & Update 4" and nothing more.

    What will Update 4 add?

    The official listing does not say. On Roblox, "admin abuse" events usually mean the developers join servers and hand out boosts, but that is a habit of the genre, not a promise made in this listing. The name "Update 4" suggests three earlier numbered updates; we found no public labels for them. We will update this page after the event.

    What has been added since launch?

    The experience was created on 30 August 2026. Since then, every store item has a creation date, which gives this timeline. Dates are in UTC, so in the Americas some items appeared on the evening before:

    Most early products were also edited on 8 and 10 September. The items themselves are explained in the Robux products list, and the 399-Robux unlocks in the limited unlocks guide.

    How often does +1 Mog Evolution update?

    Roughly once a week so far. New store items appeared on seven different days in the four weeks after launch: 30 and 31 August, then 9, 10, 18, 24 and 26 September. The pattern is consistent: each wave adds a way to pay for something new in the game, such as Worlds, hammer upgrades or a new limited item. On 1 October the next wave is the event starting on 3 October.

    Products for Rebirth, Ascend, Stages and the Power multipliers were already in the store on launch day. Products for Worlds, the Win Pad, hammer upgrades and the limited items were created later. That dates the products, not the systems: without patch notes we cannot say when each system itself entered the game.

    When did World 2 and World 3 arrive?

    The product dates give the earliest evidence: Skip to World 2 went on sale on 10 September, and Skip to World 3 on 24 September. The game's title now begins with "[W3]". Fan sites report that the title read "[W2]" in mid-September, but we only saw the current title ourselves. How each world unlocks without paying is covered, as far as it is known, in the progression guide.

    Is there a Ranked mode?

    Roblox lists two places inside this experience: the main game and one named "+1 Mog Evolution [RANKED]". So a ranked place exists. Its description is empty, and we found no official rules, rewards or season dates. We will not describe how Ranked works until the developer does.

    What is "+1 Mog evolution [TESTING]"?

    A separate experience owned by the same group, created on 10 September 2026. On 1 October it had 1,287 visits, no players online, no description, and a 50-player server limit. It looks like the developer's test copy; whether players can use it for anything is not stated. More about the group is on the community page.

    How do we keep this page current?

    We re-read the Roblox event listing, the store and the experience timestamps each time we review the page. After 7 October we will rewrite the event section in the past tense and add anything the update put into the store.
