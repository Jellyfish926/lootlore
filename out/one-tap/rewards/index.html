    One Tap Rewards: Levels, Daily, Quests and XP Boosts

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Reward sources named in the official game description, reward lines in six official event listings, and the names and Robux prices of related passes and products on 2026-10-10 (UTC); quest lists, level thresholds, daily reward contents, XP per kill and the size of any boost are not stated by the developer and are not covered; prices can change without notice

    Key points

    • The official description names three reward sources in One Tap: "Earn rewards from levels, daily, and quests". It does not say what any of them pays.
    • Event listings add level reward skins, a leaderboard tag and kill effect for the top 100 ("Given after reset") and a free kill effect for joining during the Valentines listing.
    • Four products are named Xp Boost with 30 Minutes, 1 Hour, 2 Hours and 6 Hours in the name, at 30, 60, 120 and 360 Robux. By our division that is 1 Robux per minute named at every size.
    • Refresh Quest costs 39 Robux. Its record has no description, so what it refreshes is not confirmed.

    One Tap's official description names three reward sources in one line: "Earn rewards from levels, daily, and quests". The developer has not published what each one pays. Event listings add level reward skins and prizes for the top 100 on the leaderboard, and the store has four products named Xp Boost from 30 to 360 Robux.

    Everything below comes from the game description, the six event listings the developer has posted on Roblox and the store records, all read on 10 October 2026. Where a record gives only a name and a price, this page says so and does not fill in the rest.

    Which reward sources does One Tap name?

    Three, all in the description's "Progress & Customization" section.

    SourceWhat is officialNot stated by the developer
    LevelsThe word "levels" in the description; a pass named 2x Level XP; event listings that mention level reward skinsXP per kill, level thresholds, the reward at each level
    DailyThe word "daily" in the descriptionWhat the daily reward is, when it resets, whether a streak matters
    QuestsThe word "quests" in the description; a product named Refresh QuestQuest list, how many run at once, what they pay

    The description puts the three in that order. It is a list of sources, so it does not tell you which pays best or which to chase first. The battle pass is not in the description at all. A product named Premium Battlepass and two listing lines about a season appear elsewhere; what it rewards is not stated. It has its own battle pass page.

    What rewards have One Tap update listings named?

    Four of the six listings mention kill effects, level rewards or leaderboard prizes. The lines are quoted exactly; the dates are the listing windows, which announce an update without proving when it reached players.

    Listing (listed window, 2026, UTC)Reward lines
    Valentines (14 to 15 February)"Obtain a free kill effect by joining."
    Update 2 (13 to 21 March)"New Level Reward Sniper and Knife"; "Leaderboard Tag and Kill Effect reward for top 100 (Given after reset)"
    Update (24 April to 8 May)"4 New Level Reward Skins"; "Leaderboard prize additions"
    Game Revert (15 August)"The new Kill effects and tags are no longer in the game for now but weapons have been re added."

    Two things stand out. Level rewards include weapons or skins: Update 2 names a sniper and a knife, and the April listing adds four skins. And the Valentines listing offered a free kill effect "by joining"; its listed window ended on 15 February 2026. No listing says whether that effect can still be earned.

    How much do One Tap Xp Boost products cost?

    Four One Tap developer products carry the name Xp Boost, each with a duration in the name. The last column is our own division of the price by the minutes in the name.

    ProductRobuxMinutes in the nameRobux per minute (our division)
    Xp Boost 30 Minutes303030 ÷ 30 = 1.0
    Xp Boost 1 Hour606060 ÷ 60 = 1.0
    Xp Boost 2 Hours120120120 ÷ 120 = 1.0
    Xp Boost 6 Hours360360360 ÷ 360 = 1.0

    So, going by the durations in the names, the longer products carry no discount in the records. Xp Boost 6 Hours costs exactly what twelve purchases of Xp Boost 30 Minutes would. None of the four records has a description. The size of the boost, whether each lasts the time in its name, whether any time counts down while you are offline and whether two boosts stack are all not stated by the developer.

    Is 2x Level XP a better buy than a boost?

    It depends on how long you plan to play One Tap. The game pass 2x Level XP costs 149 Robux, and its icon reads "2x XP" and "Grants double XP." Roblox describes a pass as "a one-time Robux fee", so that is a single payment.

    Set against the boosts, the sums are ours:

    OptionRobuxTime in the product names
    Xp Boost 2 Hours + Xp Boost 30 Minutes1502 hours 30 minutes
    2x Level XP pass149A pass, paid once; no time in the name

    The pass costs 1 Robux less than the two boost products whose names add up to two and a half hours. If a boost is also a doubling and lasts the time in its name, the pass pays for itself after that much boosted play. That "if" matters: the boost records do not give a multiplier, so the two may not be equal. Whether a boost stacks on top of the pass is not stated either.

    What does Refresh Quest do?

    Its record gives a name and a price: Refresh Quest, 39 Robux, created on 28 January 2026. There is no description. The name suggests it swaps a quest for a new one, and that is as far as an official source goes. It is a developer product, which Roblox defines as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once". Whether one purchase refreshes a single quest or the whole set is not stated by the developer.

    Our advice, not a rule of the game: read the purchase prompt in the game before you pay, and check first whether quests refresh on their own.

    How do One Tap leaderboard rewards work?

    The Update 2 listing gives the one clear rule: a "Leaderboard Tag and Kill Effect reward for top 100 (Given after reset)". According to the listing, the prize is for the top 100 and is "Given after reset"; the listing does not say what is reset. The same listing says new leaderboards were delayed, and the April listing adds "Leaderboard prize additions" without naming them.

    What the leaderboard ranks, what the reset is and when it happens are not stated in any listing. The description's own warning applies here: farming with alts is named as a reason for a permanent ban, and the how-to-play page quotes it in full.

    Does anything else speed up progress?

    One more pass touches earnings. 2x Money costs 299 Robux and its icon reads "Grants double money from kills." By that line, kills pay money. What money buys is not stated in any official source we read; the game pass guide compares all four passes.

    Still not published anywhere official for One Tap:

    • the XP a kill, a win or a quest gives;
    • how many levels there are and what each unlocks;
    • the contents of the daily reward;
    • whether the Valentines kill effect or the top 100 prizes will return.
