    Deep Fishing Robux Shop: Boosts, Chests, Enchant Stones

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-09-29 · Checked on version 2026-09-28 update

    Scope: Product names, prices and creation dates from the Roblox developer product API on 2026-09-29; effects are only described where the product name states them

    Missing something?

    Tell us which guide you need, report a wrong number or suggest a tool. It lands straight in the editor's inbox.

    Request a guide

    Key points

    • Enchant Stones (from 49 Robux) and Ultra Enchant Stones (from 199) arrived on 6 September 2026.
    • There are four lucky chest types: Element, Pirate, Charm and Hero, from 79 to 149 Robux each.
    • Revive All Fish costs 39 Robux, and Skip Big Mouth Timer costs 79 — the game does not explain either in its listing.
    • One-throw boosts cost as little as 8 Robux and are used up after a single cast.

    Besides rods and passes, Deep Fishing's Robux shop sells enchant stones, four kinds of lucky chest, server-wide luck, a Revive All Fish option, eight paid stat upgrades and cheap one-throw boosts. Enchant Stones start at 49 Robux and Ultra Enchant Stones at 199; both were added on 6 September 2026.

    This page lists the developer products registered for the game on Roblox — 89 of them when we checked — minus the rod unlocks, Exclusive rods and skins (see the rod list), pass gifts (see game passes) and mutation rerolls and scrolls (see the rarity guide). Product listings give a name and a price, not a description, so we only state an effect when the name itself states it.

    What are Enchant Stones in Deep Fishing?

    Enchant Stones are a consumable sold in two grades. They are among the newest additions, and their names confirm that some form of enchanting exists in the game.

    ProductRobux
    x1 Enchant Stone49
    x3 Enchant Stones129
    x10 Enchant Stones399
    x50 Enchant Stones1,499
    x1 Ultra Enchant Stone199
    x3 Ultra Enchant Stones549
    x10 Ultra Enchant Stones1,649
    x50 Ultra Enchant Stones4,499

    What an enchant does, which items take one, and what the Ultra grade adds are not in any official listing. Fan guides link enchanting to rods and to a paid island, but we have not verified that, so we do not describe enchant effects here. If you have a screenshot of the enchant screen, the author page says how to send it.

    Which lucky chests can you buy?

    Four chest types exist, added in two waves.

    ChestSingleBundleAdded
    Element Lucky Chest99x3 for 249, x10 for 79925 Aug 2026
    Pirate Lucky Chest79x5 for 34926 Sep 2026
    Charm Lucky Chest99x5 for 39926 Sep 2026
    Hero Lucky Chest149x5 for 64926 Sep 2026

    Chest contents and drop odds are not published. The Pirate, Charm and Hero chests appeared on the same day as the two Exclusive rods, so they may belong to the same update, but the listing does not say so.

    What is Revive All Fish?

    "Revive All Fish" is a 39-Robux product added on 29 August 2026 and edited on 18 September. The name suggests that fish in the game can be revived; what that means in play, and when you would need it, is not explained in the listing. Players searching for a "revive heart" may be looking for the same system, but we have not confirmed a link, so treat it as an open question.

    A separate product, "Skip Big Mouth Timer" (79 Robux, added 3 September), suggests that something called Big Mouth runs on a timer. What Big Mouth is has not been documented officially.

    Which boosts last for one cast only?

    The "One Throw" products added on 18 September are the cheapest items in the shop.

    BoostRobux
    More Fish (One Throw)8
    Freeze Fish (One Throw)8
    Mutation Luck (One Throw)19
    Collect All (One Throw)24

    Use them on a cast you already expect to go far. A one-throw boost on a short cast is a small price, but still wasted. Mutation-focused players should also read the rarity and mutations guide for rerolls and Ultra Mutation Scrolls.

    Stat Upgrades [7] and [8] were added on 5 September, extending the original six. Which stat each one raises and how many coins each pack holds are not listed. The Skip Gift products suggest a timed gift system; the game's description separately says joining the LazyGames group gives "rewards and updates" — see community links.

    What do the release dates tell you?

    The creation dates double as a rough update log: gifts in early August, Element chests and rod skins on 25 August, Revive All Fish on 29 August, bulk mutation rerolls on 1 September, enchant stones and Ultra Mutation Scrolls on 6 September, one-throw boosts on 18 September, and a new chest-and-rod wave on 26 September. A product's creation date is when it was registered, not always the day its update went live.
