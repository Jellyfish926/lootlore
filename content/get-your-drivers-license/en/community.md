# Get Your Driver's License! Developer, Group and Discord

Get Your Driver's License! is made by Time Will Pass, a Roblox group created on 27 September 2026 and owned by the account DiveAndThrive. The group had 1,146,104 members on 10 October 2026. It has no description and no shout, and we could not confirm an official Discord server, because Roblox hides social links from signed-out visitors.

Everything below was read from Roblox's public records for the group, its owner and its games on 10 October 2026. The [group page](https://www.roblox.com/communities/356677783/Time-Will-Pass) and the [game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/104416416393862/Get-Your-Drivers-License) are the two official places to start.

## Is there an official Discord server?

We could not confirm one, and we will not link one we cannot trace to the developer.

A Roblox game or group can show social links, and that is where an official Discord invite would normally sit. Roblox's own documentation states: "Social media links are only visible to users who have verified their age as at least 16 years old." When we asked Roblox for the links without an account, the request was refused for both the game and the group. So a link may exist that we cannot see.
