# American Plains Mudding Updates: Saturday Times, History

American Plains Mudding update listings start on Saturdays. The developer has published 60 Roblox event listings, nearly one for each Saturday, from 16 August 2025 to 17 October 2026, and every one starts on a Saturday in UTC. Since late March 2026 most of them start between 13:45 and 14:15 UTC.

We read the listings and the game description at 11:13 UTC on Friday 9 October 2026. At that point the newest notes were headed "10/3 Update" and the next listing was a day away. Anything released after that is not covered here.

## What time does the American Plains Mudding update drop?

Usually a little before 14:00 UTC on a Saturday, going by the listings. The current description states no update time, and older versions of it are not in the public data we could read, so the listings are the only schedule we found. Of the last ten, nine start between 13:45 and 14:00 UTC; the exception is 29 August 2026, at 17:00.

Across all 60 listings the spread is wider, and it moves with the seasons:

| Listed starts | Listings | Start times (UTC) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 Aug to 1 Nov 2025 | 10 | All at 13:35 |
| 8 Nov 2025 to 7 Mar 2026 | 18 | 14:35 to 15:30; sixteen of them 14:35 to 14:45 |
| 14 Mar to 21 Mar 2026 | 2 | 14:15 and 14:30 |
| 28 Mar to 17 Oct 2026 | 30 | 27 between 13:45 and 14:15; others 14:50, 15:05 and 17:00 |

The jump from 13:35 to 14:35 UTC falls in the week US clocks went back in November 2025. In US Eastern time both are 9:35 am. So the developer appears to schedule against a US clock, at roughly 9:35 to 10:15 am Eastern all year. That is our inference from the listing times; the developer has not said so.

| Time zone | Local time for 14:00 UTC on 10 October 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| US Pacific (PDT) | 7:00 am |
| US Central (CDT) | 9:00 am |
| US Eastern (EDT) | 10:00 am |
| UK (BST) | 3:00 pm |
| Central Europe (CEST) | 4:00 pm |

UK and European clocks change on 25 October 2026 and US clocks on 1 November. If the pattern of winter 2025-26 repeats, listings may move to about 14:35 UTC in November; that is a possibility, not a schedule.

A listing's start time is the developer's schedule, not a record of when the game changed. [Roblox's event documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-events) says players "can opt into notifications that they'll receive when your event begins", which is what the listings invite: "Get notified right when the update drops!".

## How can you tell an update is live?

Three official signals, none of which needs a login:

- **The update header in the description.** It read "🚨 10/3 Update" when we checked. A new date there means new notes.
- **The game's title prefix.** It was "[🌲FOREST🌲]" at the same check, matching the headline feature.
- **The event listings.** The current one is subtitled "This Weeks Update" or "This Week's Update", and upcoming ones "Next Week's Update".

[Roblox's own documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/update-games) says that when a developer publishes an update, "players aren't immediately removed from old versions of the game", and that without a server restart players move over "as the servers running old versions eventually empty and shut down". If the notes have changed but your game has not, join a fresh server.

## How regular are the American Plains Mudding Saturday listings?

Very. Of the 59 gaps between one listing and the next, 57 are seven days and two are 14 days. The two Saturdays without a listing are 20 September and 18 October 2025. From 25 October 2025 to 17 October 2026 there is a listing for every Saturday, 52 in a row.

A listing does not guarantee a content drop. Roblox's public data does not record what a given Saturday changed, and we have not been in the game on any of those dates.

Not in any public place we could read. Older versions of the description are not available in the public data we could read.
