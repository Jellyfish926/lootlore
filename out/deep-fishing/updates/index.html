    Deep Fishing Updates: Changes Since 28 September 2026

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Game record, developer products, game passes, badges and event listings from Roblox APIs, read on 2026-10-10; all dates and times UTC. Record dates show when a record was created or edited, not when a feature reached players; how seasons work and what the added products do are not confirmed

    Key points

    • Nine developer product records carry a creation date between 29 September and 9 October 2026 (UTC). The store list held 98 products on 10 October, against the 89 this site counted on 29 September.
    • Season Pass costs 399 Robux, Skip 1 costs 79, Skip 10 costs 449 and Reset Season costs 19; how a season works and what it rewards are not confirmed.
    • The records held nine game passes and 13 badges on 10 October 2026, the same counts this site noted on 29 September; no pass or badge record has a creation date after 1 August 2026. All nine passes carry an edit date of 29 September or later.
    • The game record's "updated" timestamp is 9 October 2026 at 18:38 UTC, and an event titled EGGS & PETS are COMING! is listed for 11 to 15 October.

    Deep Fishing's Roblox records show nine store products created between 29 September and 9 October 2026, including Season Pass at 399 Robux and Oni Soulbinder Skin at 1,649. No pass or badge record was created then, the game record was last updated on 9 October, and "EGGS & PETS are COMING!" is listed for 11 to 15 October.

    This update log is built from dated official records, not patch notes: the game record, 98 developer products, nine game passes, 13 badges and eight event listings, all read on 10 October 2026. Every time on this page is UTC. A record date says when a record was created or edited. It does not say when a feature reached players.

Compared with this site's own readings of 29 September 2026, the Robux prices on 10 October 2026 were the same for the nine passes and for all 19 of those products: Starter Pack, the four Server Luck products and the five Coin Packs in our Robux shop guide, and the nine [Gift] products in our game pass guide.

    Which store products were added after 28 September?

    Nine records. This site's shop guide counted 89 developer products on 29 September 2026. The response we read on 10 October 2026 held 98, and nine of them have a creation date in between (89 + 9 = 98, our own sum). We cannot tell from these responses whether any product was deleted in between, so this compares two counts. Four of them form the season group in the next section. The other five are here.

    x1 Leviathan Bottle14930 Sep 2026, 05:3730 Sep 2026, 05:39
    x3 Leviathan Bottles39930 Sep 2026, 05:3830 Sep 2026, 05:39
    x10 Leviathan Bottles1,19930 Sep 2026, 05:3830 Sep 2026, 05:39
    Shop Daily Claimable Ad2930 Sep 2026, 15:279 Oct 2026, 23:53
    Oni Soulbinder Skin1,6499 Oct 2026, 23:53Not edited

    All five had an empty description field and were marked as for sale on 10 October 2026. By our own division, the bottle bundles work out at 133.0 Robux each in the x3 size (399 ÷ 3) and 119.9 each in the x10 size (1,199 ÷ 10), against 149 for one.

    What the names do not tell you is not confirmed. No record we read says what a Leviathan Bottle does or where it is used. "Shop Daily Claimable Ad" is a product name with a 29-Robux price; what a buyer gets for it is not stated. Oni Soulbinder Skin has no "Rod" in its name, unlike the two earlier skin products, "[Skin] Divine King Rod" and "[Skin] Divine Queen Rod", so what it is a skin for is not confirmed either. The rod list covers the two Divine skins.

    This page does not repeat the rest of the store. The full price tables are in the Robux shop guide, and enchant stones have their own enchants page.

    What are Season Pass, Skip 1, Skip 10 and Reset Season?

    They are four developer product records created within five minutes of each other on 29 September 2026. The names and prices are official. How a season works is not in any record we read.

    ProductRobuxCreated (UTC)Edited (UTC)
    Season Pass39929 Sep 2026, 21:26Not edited
    Skip 17929 Sep 2026, 21:274 Oct 2026, 16:14
    Skip 1044929 Sep 2026, 21:274 Oct 2026, 16:15
    Reset Season1929 Sep 2026, 21:31Not edited

    The description field of all four was empty on 10 October 2026. In the records we read that day, the word "season" appears in two product names, Season Pass and Reset Season, and in no game pass, badge, event listing or line of the game description. So the following are not confirmed: how long a season lasts, what the pass unlocks, what the rewards are, what Skip 1 or Skip 10 skips, and what Reset Season resets.

    One piece of arithmetic is safe. Skip 10 costs 449 Robux and Skip 1 costs 79, so the larger product costs about 5.7 times the smaller one (449 ÷ 79 ≈ 5.68). Whether Skip 10 gives ten of whatever Skip 1 gives is a reading of the names, not something the records state.

    Season Pass is a developer product; it is not one of the nine game passes. Our advice, not a rule of the game: read what the game itself says about the pass before paying, because the store record we read gives a name and a price and has an empty description.

    Did any game pass or badge change?

    The counts match: the records held nine passes and 13 badges on 10 October 2026, the same numbers this site noted on 29 September 2026 (nine and 13). We cannot tell from these responses whether any record was deleted. The edit dates did move. Eight passes carry an edit timestamp of 29 September 2026 at 20:23, and Double Coins carries 7 October 2026 at 12:44.

    On 10 October the Double Coins description read "Earn 2x more coins when selling fish! 🪙". This site's own data from 29 September 2026 has the shorter "Earn 2x more coins! 🪙". The pass record does not keep earlier text, so we cannot tie that wording change to the 7 October edit with certainty. The price was 399 Robux in both readings.

    In the badge records we read on 10 October 2026, no badge has a creation or edit date later than 5 August 2026. Award counts differ between our two readings: the Welcome! badge showed 2,104,103 awards on our badge list of 29 September 2026 and 3,034,384 in the response we saved on 10 October 2026; the badge list explains what those counts can and cannot tell you about rarity.

    Which events has LazyGames listed since late September?

    Three listings have a window that touches the period this page covers.

    Event titleStart (UTC)End (UTC)Listing created (UTC)
    Fish Update + Rework27 Sep 2026, 16:0028 Sep 2026, 16:0019 Sep 2026
    NEW WORLD IS COMING4 Oct 2026, 16:008 Oct 2026, 16:0026 Sep 2026
    EGGS & PETS are COMING!11 Oct 2026, 16:0015 Oct 2026, 16:004 Oct 2026

    The first two windows had closed by the read date, and their listings carry the developer's own summary. "Fish Update + Rework" has the subtitle "48 Fish, Many Rods & more". "NEW WORLD IS COMING" has the subtitle "🌋The Emberdeep" and the tagline "Deep Fishing expands with a brand new Volcano World, 30 fish, boats, daily rewards, and much more."

    Those lines are announcements. The second listing was last edited on 27 September 2026, a week before its window opened, so it describes a plan and is not a patch note. This page does not treat either line as proof that each item shipped. In the developer product list we read on 10 October 2026, no product name contains "Emberdeep", "Volcano" or "boat".

    For the third listing we print the title and the window only: 11 October 2026 at 16:00 to 15 October 2026 at 16:00. Its window had not opened on the read date, so this page lists no contents for it.

    Five older listings, from "Update 3 👀" in August to "Toxic + Kraken 🐙☢️" in September, fall outside this page's range. One of them, "Mutation Roll + Rarity💫", is the only listing of the eight whose description field holds a full change list.

    Why is a record date not a release date?

    Because each record logs its own history. Three cases from this page show the gap.

    • Oni Soulbinder Skin was created at 23:53 on 9 October 2026, about five hours after the game record's "updated" timestamp of 18:38 the same day (23:53 minus 18:38 is 5 hours 15 minutes). The product record exists and is marked as for sale; whether the skin could be bought in play on 10 October is not confirmed.
    • Skip 1 and Skip 10 were edited at 16:14 and 16:15 on 4 October 2026, within 15 minutes of the listed start of "NEW WORLD IS COMING" at 16:00. The records do not connect the two.
    • The "EGGS & PETS are COMING!" listing was created on 4 October 2026, a week before its window.

    The game record carries two timestamps, "created" (18 July 2026) and "updated" (9 October 2026 at 18:38), and the second does not say what changed.

    How did we read the records, and what is missing?

    Everything here was read on 10 October 2026 (UTC) from five Roblox endpoints: games, developer products, game passes, badges and virtual events. The product, pass and badge lists each came back complete in one response, with no further page to fetch.

    The events endpoint was inconsistent. Within a few minutes it returned all eight listings on some reads and only the one listing whose window had not closed on others. We used the eight-listing response. If Roblox stops returning closed listings, the two closed events above can no longer be checked there.

    Still not confirmed after this read:

    • how seasons work and what Season Pass rewards;
    • what Leviathan Bottle, Shop Daily Claimable Ad and Oni Soulbinder Skin do;
    • what any of the edits changed, apart from the Double Coins wording noted above;
    • whether the contents named in event listings shipped.

    None of these records held a redemption code; the codes page sets out that check. We will reread the store after the "EGGS & PETS are COMING!" window closes on 15 October 2026 and add any product created by then.
