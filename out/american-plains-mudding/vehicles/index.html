    American Plains Mudding Vehicles and Trailers Overview

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-09 · Checked on version 2026-10-03 update

    Scope: Vehicle, trailer and part types named in the official game description, 60 event listings, game pass descriptions and badge texts on 2026-10-09, plus what ten official promotional images show. Individual vehicle names, prices, unlock conditions and performance are not published and are not covered. Vehicles are described generically; no real-world make or model is implied.

    Like and Favorite vehicle offer

    Quick facts

    The last line of the official game description offers a vehicle for liking and favouriting the game.

    Condition
    Like 👍 and Favorite ⭐ the game
    Outcome
    "to obtain a vehicle!" (which vehicle is not stated)

    Key points

    • American Plains Mudding publishes no vehicle list; this page collects the types its developer has named in the description and event listings.
    • Named so far: ATVs, pickups, cars, an SUV, semis, campers, a combine, amphibious vehicles, dirt bikes, and trailers from tilt deck to gooseneck enclosed.
    • Routes to more vehicles in official text: the like-and-favourite offer, Premium Vehicles (230 Robux), Premium Trailers (195) and limited holiday passes; hidden vehicles earn a badge, and the text does not say you keep the vehicle.
    • No official source gives prices, stats or unlock conditions, so there is no tier list here.

    American Plains Mudding does not publish a vehicle list, so this page collects every vehicle and trailer type the developer has named in official text: the game description, 60 event listings, the game passes and the badges. It covers ATVs, pickups, an SUV, campers, farm machinery and trailers, with no stats or prices.

    That limit is deliberate. Nothing on Roblox's public record says what a vehicle costs, how fast it is or how you unlock it, and we have not checked those in game. What the record does give is a dated trail of what was added.

    Trailers have their own category in the game's slot system: the pass texts split spawn slots into vehicles and trailers, and three of the eight passes on sale are about trailers alone. The spawn slot guide explains how many you can have out at once.

    How do you get more vehicles?

    Official text names four routes to vehicles, and a fifth, hidden vehicles, whose reward is not stated.

    RouteWhat the text saysCost
    Like and favourite"Like 👍 and Favorite ⭐ the game to obtain a vehicle!"Free
    Premium Vehicles pass"Get access to exclusive vehicles!"230 Robux
    Premium Trailers pass"Get access to exclusive trailers!"195 Robux
    Limited holiday passes"Only available near" each holidayOff sale on 9 Oct 2026
    Hidden vehicles"Congrats, you have found a Hidden Vehicle."Not stated; the badge text does not say you receive the vehicle

    Both premium passes add "(Items within this gamepass are subject to change over time)", so their contents are not fixed and not listed; prices for every pass are in the game pass guide. The holiday passes are covered in the limited vehicle guide. For hidden vehicles, the badge text confirms a badge; it does not say the vehicle becomes yours. The badge guide has the counts for all four.

    How ordinary vehicles are unlocked is the biggest gap. The description mentions no in-game currency and no prices. Whether everything outside the passes is free to spawn is something only the game itself can answer.

    Why is there no American Plains Mudding tier list?

    Because a tier list needs numbers, and the developer publishes none. Speed, torque, weight, towing capacity and mud performance are not in the description, the passes or the listings. A November 2025 listing does mention "4WD" among its additions, but it does not say which vehicles have it.

    Ranking vehicles from videos would mean passing on someone else's impressions as fact. We would sooner leave the page without a ranking until the vehicles can be compared in game, on the same mud, with the same setup.
