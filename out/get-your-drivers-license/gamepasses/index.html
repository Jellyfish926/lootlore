    Key points

    • Get Your Driver's License! sells 5 game passes: PRO EMOJI PACK 😈 (9 Robux), Airhorn [ANNOYING] ☠️ (16), Time Out (24), Gravity Gun 🔥 (160) and Ban Hammer (1,200).
    • All five together cost 1,409 Robux. Ban Hammer alone is 85% of that.
    • No pass description mentions the line, the written test, the driving test or cars. Three name other players, one is a loud airhorn and one is an emoji pack.
    • Passes are one-time purchases. The skips and car items are separate developer products.

    Get Your Driver's License! sells five game passes. Four are cheap or mid-priced: PRO EMOJI PACK 😈 at 9 Robux, Airhorn [ANNOYING] ☠️ at 16, Time Out at 24 and Gravity Gun 🔥 at 160. The fifth, Ban Hammer, costs 1,200 Robux. None of the five descriptions mentions passing the test or winning cars.

    Names, prices and descriptions below come from the Roblox game pass API, read on 9 October 2026. All five were on sale and none showed a discount. Names are copied exactly as the store prints them, emoji and brackets included.

    What does each pass do?

    PassRobuxOfficial description
    PRO EMOJI PACK 😈9Unlocks the pro emoji pack.
    Airhorn [ANNOYING] ☠️16A very loud airhorn.
    Time Out24Put someone in time out.
    Gravity Gun 🔥160Pick people up and throw them.
    Ban Hammer1,200Bonk people with the ban hammer.

    Each description is one sentence, and that sentence is all the store listing says. How long a time out lasts, how far the gravity gun throws, and which emoji are in the pack are not stated. We have not tested any of the five.

    Do any passes help you get your license?

    Not according to their descriptions. Read the five sentences again: an emoji pack, a noise, a time out, throwing people and bonking people. Three name other players (Time Out, Gravity Gun 🔥 and Ban Hammer), one is a loud airhorn and one unlocks emoji. None speaks of the line, the written test, the driving test or the 18 cars.

    Everything whose description mentions the line, the tests, the examiner or cars is sold another way, as a developer product. That split is the most useful thing to know before you open the store.

    Is Ban Hammer worth 1,200 Robux?

    That depends on how much a joke is worth to you. The sums are ours.

    MeasureValue
    All five passes1,409 Robux
    Ban Hammer alone1,200 Robux (about 85% of the total)
    The other four passes together209 Robux
    Ban Hammer against Gravity Gun 🔥7.5 times the price

    The description is "Bonk people with the ban hammer." It does not say the hammer removes anyone from the server, despite the name, and it does not say it knocks players out. A separate product, Revenge ⚡, is described as "Get back at whoever knocked you out", so something in the game does knock players out. No listing says the hammer is that something.

    Our view: at 1,200 Robux this is a status item. If the aim is to pick people up and throw them, Gravity Gun 🔥 is described as doing exactly that for 160.

    Do passes run out?

    No. Roblox defines a pass as "a one-time Robux fee to access special privileges inside your game". That is the difference from the skips in the shop, which are developer products. Roblox defines a developer product as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once". So a skip can be bought again and again, while Airhorn [ANNOYING] ☠️ is a one-time fee.

    Which pass is the cheapest way to try one?

    PRO EMOJI PACK 😈 at 9 Robux, then Airhorn [ANNOYING] ☠️ at 16. Those two and Time Out come to 49 Robux in total, about the price of one Skip To End.

    These are our suggestions, not the developer's:

    • You only want more emoji. PRO EMOJI PACK 😈 is the lowest-priced item of any kind in the store, level with five 9-Robux products.
    • You want to be noticed. The airhorn's own name carries the warning: [ANNOYING].
    • You want to move people. Gravity Gun 🔥 is the only pass whose description says you pick players up.
    • You are short on Robux and want your license. Skip all five. No pass description mentions the wait or the tests.

    When were the passes added?

    All five were created on 30 September 2026, the day after Get Your Driver's License! itself. Roblox's record shows them registered within nine seconds of each other, between 06:18:59 and 06:19:08 UTC, and each still shows the same created and updated time. The first 11 developer products were created in the minute before. So the store went up in one batch, and the pass list has not grown since.

    What is not published?

    • The contents of the pro emoji pack, and whether a free set of emoji exists without it.
    • How long a time out lasts and what the player in it can still do.
    • Whether any pass works on a player who is taking the driving test.
    • Whether passes carry over to the developer's other games. Nothing says they do.

    The other 13 store items, with their descriptions and prices, are in the Robux shop guide.
