3:Te64,<h2>First, what kind of game this is</h2>
<p>DragonSword: Awakening is an <strong>open-world action RPG, not a roguelite</strong>. One persistent Continent of Orbis, eight story chapters, nothing resets and no procedural stages. If you arrived expecting run-based progression, that expectation will fight you for the first hour.</p>
<p>The main story runs roughly 10 to 15 hours by the producer's estimate, or about 20 to complete all eight chapters.</p>
<h2>What is in this section</h2>
<p>Pages that explain the systems deciding fights rather than listing what is in the game.</p>
<p>Start with the <strong>beginner guide</strong> if you have just bought it. Go to the <strong>combat page</strong> if your damage feels like it is being ignored — that is usually an element gate rather than a gear problem. Read the <strong>cooking page</strong> as soon as you notice there is no natural HP regeneration, because food is the healing economy and only one hero can heal on demand.</p>
<p>The <strong>chapters and bosses page</strong> maps the eight-chapter story and the three places players wrongly conclude they are stuck. The <strong>co-op page</strong> explains what multiplayer actually covers (2-player Subjugations, 3-player Raids — not the open world) before you buy a second copy. The <strong>FAQ</strong> answers the buying questions, including why this is no longer a gacha game.</p>
<p>The <strong>boss list</strong> reads seventeen boss names and their difficulty gates out of the achievement data, the <strong>achievements page</strong> maps where players actually stop, <strong>familiars</strong> and <strong>gear, runes &amp; karma</strong> cover the two collection systems, and <strong>patch notes &amp; roadmap</strong> tracks every update we can read, in order.</p>
<p>The <strong>system requirements page</strong> is worth reading even if your PC is comfortable: it carries the <code>-dx11</code> workaround for the D3D12 crash Hound13 has acknowledged, and the save-backup path, which several players have needed the hard way.</p>
<h2>Three things that trip people up early</h2>
<p><strong>Basic attacks are not filler.</strong> They do very little damage but chip skill cooldowns and build ailment stacks. If your skills feel permanently unavailable, you are treating basics as a fallback instead of the rhythm.</p>
<p><strong>You can only equip two consumables.</strong> With three party slots and one healer in the entire roster, that constraint shapes every fight. Decide deliberately rather than carrying whatever you cooked last.</p>
<p><strong>Patch 1.0.7 changed recovery substantially.</strong> Goddess Statue usage is now unlimited, and dungeon entry and wipe recovery restore you to full HP with the party revived. Complaints you may read about respawning at 20% health are outdated. What has <em>not</em> landed is a [Retry] button — Hound13 delayed it explicitly.</p>
<h2>How to read these pages</h2>
<p>Every page ends with a <strong>Sources checked</strong> box naming exactly what it was verified against: the Steam store listing, Hound13's own patch notes and announcements, player-written Steam guides, or individually attributed reviews and discussion threads.</p>
<p>Where something is not documented, the page says so rather than filling the gap. That matters here — the game shipped on 22 July 2026, and most of what currently circulates about it is auto-generated from the store page.</p>
