# Untitled Wheelie Game Patch Notes: 7 Dev Announcements

The Untitled Wheelie Game patch notes on this page are the seven developer announcements we read in Roblox's event listings on 10 October 2026; notes posted elsewhere are not confirmed. The Mopeds + Rain announcement lists Normal Moped at "40MPH stock, 80MPH modded" and Junkyard Moped at "35MPH stock, 160MPH modded". We have not tested these in game.

This page prints what the developer's announcements say, in the developer's words, spelling included. Each announcement is the description field of an event listing owned by Untitled Wheelie Group. Times taken from the listings' API fields are written in UTC; where an announcement's own text names a time zone, we copy it as written. An announcement says what an update was planned to contain; it is not proof that each item reached players in that form.

## Which updates has the developer announced on Roblox?

Seven listings came back when we read the event records on 10 October 2026. Titles are copied as written, emoji included. "Start" is the start time on the listing, and "created" is the day the listing itself was made.

| Listing title | Start (UTC) | Listing created (UTC) | What the announcement lists |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FULL GAME RELEASE | 23 Jul 2026, 20:00 | 17 Jul 2026 | A release time for "The full game" |
| BIG UPDATE | 8 Aug 2026, 00:00 | 4 Aug 2026 | "2 new bikes, pedestrians, burnouts, foot brake upgrades for every bike, 1 new job, and bug fixes" |
| EBIKE UPDATE | 15 Aug 2026, 20:00 | 14 Aug 2026 | The subtitle "Ridstar, Super73, Macfox" and a one-line description about bikes |
| MAP REVAMP | 5 Sep 2026, 21:00 | 4 Sep 2026 | A numbered list of seven items, from "New Map" to "Other Small things" |
| AI COPS 👮 | 25 Sep 2026, 22:00 | 21 Sep 2026 | Nine "COP BEHAVIOR" lines and three "WHAT TO EXPECT" lines |
| Mopeds + Rain 🛵🌧️ | 2 Oct 2026, 22:00 | 30 Sep 2026 | Two mopeds with speeds, two lights and a rain setting |
| HALLOWEEN UPDATE 🎃 | 21 Oct 2026, 22:30 | 6 Oct 2026 | "Orange Grass and Trees! 🍂" and three bullet lines |

Each of the seven listings was created before its own start time. The shortest gap is MAP REVAMP, created on 4 September at 22:04 for a start on 5 September at 21:00, which is 22 hours 56 minutes by our subtraction. The longest is HALLOWEEN UPDATE, created on 6 October at 20:52 for a start on 21 October at 22:30, about 15 days. The last-edited time on each listing is also earlier than its start time, so each text was in place before the start time on its own listing. Five of the seven carry the same subtitle, "Be Ready!⌛📢".

## How fast are the two mopeds in the Mopeds + Rain announcement?

The announcement opens with the heading "New Bikes 🏍️:" and two lines: "Normal Moped, 40MPH stock, 80MPH modded 🛵" and "Junkyard Moped, 35MPH stock, 160MPH modded 🛠️". Those four figures are the bike speeds that the records we read on 10 October 2026 give as numbers.

| Bike, as written | Stock | Modded | Gain (our subtraction) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal Moped | 40MPH | 80MPH | 40 MPH (80 − 40) |
| Junkyard Moped | 35MPH | 160MPH | 125 MPH (160 − 35) |

Read side by side, the Normal Moped is listed 5 MPH faster from stock (40 − 35), and the Junkyard Moped's modded figure is twice the Normal Moped's (160 ÷ 80 = 2). Both comparisons are our arithmetic on the developer's numbers.

Three things are not confirmed. The announcement does not say what "modded" means: which upgrades, how many of them, or what they cost. It does not say how either moped is obtained or what it costs. And it does not say whether the figures are top speeds on a flat road. The word "moped" appears in this one listing and in none of the 12 game pass names or 23 developer product names we read the same day, so none of those pass or product names mentions a moped. Our [bikes and parts guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/bikes/) covers the bike passes and the Backfire products.

## What lights and weather does the same announcement list?

Two more headings follow the bikes: "Lights on all bikes 💡:" and "Rain 🌧️:". They hold three lines between them.

| Heading in the announcement | Line as written | What the line leaves open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lights on all bikes 💡: | Bar Light on basically all bikes, some come stock with one 🔦 | Which bikes are excluded, and which ones carry the light from stock |
| Lights on all bikes 💡: | Baja Light for all bikes 🔆 | How the light is fitted and what it costs |
| Rain 🌧️: | A rain setting with a lightning option ⚡🌩️ | Where the setting sits, and whether rain changes grip or is visual |

The wording differs between the two lights: Bar Light is "on basically all bikes", while Baja Light is "for all bikes". We read that as a difference in coverage and nothing more. Rain is described as "A rain setting", which reads like something a player switches on; whether it is per player or per server is not confirmed.

## What did the first four announcements say?

### FULL GAME RELEASE

The description reads: "The full game will be set to release on july 23, 1:00PM MST (4:00 PM EST)!" A second line says "Make sure your notifications are on!⌛". We copy both zone labels as written. The listing's own start field is 23 July 2026 at 20:00 UTC and its end field is 24 July 2026 at 20:00 UTC.

The game record has a creation date of 4 June 2026, which is 49 days before 23 July (26 days left in June plus 23, our count). What state the game was in during those weeks is not stated in the records we read. The [community page](/untitled-wheelie-game/community/) looks at the testing question.

### BIG UPDATE

One sentence: "This update will contain 2 new bikes, pedestrians, burnouts, foot brake upgrades for every bike, 1 new job, and bug fixes. Be ready!" The two bikes and the job are not named. The listing was last edited on 7 August 2026 at 23:53, seven minutes before its start at 00:00 on 8 August. The job that the game description does name, pizza delivery, is in the [money guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/money/).

### EBIKE UPDATE

The subtitle is "Ridstar, Super73, Macfox". The description is a single four-word line saying that all bikes are on the way; we paraphrase it here and quote the subtitle exactly. The listing does not say what the three names are. A game pass record named EBike Pack ⚡ has a creation time of 15 August 2026 at 06:29, the same UTC day as the listing's start; the [game pass list](/untitled-wheelie-game/gamepasses/) has its description.

### MAP REVAMP

The subtitle is "+ New Trick System" and the description is headed "THINGS COMING WITH THE UPDATE:". Its seven numbered items, as written:

1. New Map
2. Infinite Highway System / Tunnel System
3. New Trick System
4. Backfire Mod for Gas Bikes
5. Jetson Ebike
6. Bug Fixes
7. Other Small things

Item 4 is where the seven announcements mention Backfire, and it says which bikes the mod is for: "Gas Bikes". The three Backfire product records were created on 4 September 2026 at 03:02, the day before the listing's start. None of the seven items comes with a place name, a control or a figure such as a speed.

## What does the AI COPS announcement add?

It opens with "AI Cops are being added!" and then gives two headed lists: nine lines under "COP BEHAVIOR:" and three under "WHAT TO EXPECT:". Three of its lines mention "a 3 star system similar to GTA's 5 star system", roadblocks "At 3 stars", and cops that give up if they lose sight of you "for over 60 seconds".

We do not repeat the twelve lines here. The [cop chase rules page](/untitled-wheelie-game/cops-chase-rules/) prints each one with a plain-words note, and the [cops and fines guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/cops-fines/) covers the two fine-related purchases.

## What does the HALLOWEEN UPDATE listing say?

The listing's start field is 21 October 2026 at 22:30 UTC and its end field is 1 November 2026 at 08:00 UTC. It was created on 6 October 2026 and last edited on 9 October 2026 at 22:06. That window had not opened on our read date, so this section prints the text and stops there.

The description opens with "Orange Grass and Trees! 🍂" and then lists three bullets:

- EXCLUSIVE HALLOWEEN BIKE
- Halloween Quest to obtain the limited bike! 🛵
- Other small stuff

It closes with "Be ready 10 days before halloween! 🛵🎃". Ten days before 31 October is 21 October (31 − 10 = 21, our count), which matches the date in the start field. The quest steps, the bike's name and speed, and whether the bike can be obtained after the end time are not confirmed.

## How is this page different from the update history?

The [update history](/untitled-wheelie-game/updates/) is built from the creation dates on game pass and developer product records. It shows when a store record was made. This page is built from announcement text. It shows what the developer said an update would hold.

The two sources sit close in time in three places: the EBike Pack ⚡ pass and the EBIKE UPDATE listing in mid-August, the Backfire products and item 4 of MAP REVAMP in early September, and the AVOID FINES product record (created 21 September 2026) and NEVER PAY FINES pass record (created 26 September 2026) on either side of the AI COPS start time on 25 September. Neither source says what a player saw on a given day.

## What is still not confirmed after this read?

- whether each announced item shipped as written, because we have not tested these in game;
- any price, payout or fine amount: none of the seven announcements states a price or a cash amount;
- what "modded" covers for the two mopeds;
- the names of the "2 new bikes" and the "1 new job" in BIG UPDATE;
- how the Halloween Quest works.

One note on method. On 10 October 2026 the events endpoint did not answer the same way on each read. A request that starts from the first cursor position returned all seven listings on 13 reads out of 13, with no further page after the seventh. A request without a cursor returned seven listings on 10 reads and one listing, HALLOWEEN UPDATE, on 16 others. This page uses the seven-listing response. If Roblox stops returning listings whose end time has passed, the six earlier announcements can no longer be checked there.
