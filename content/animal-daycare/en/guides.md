# Animal Daycare Guides: Shifts, Badges, Shop and Facts

New to Animal Daycare (Anomaly)? Start with the how-to-play guide: it sets out the seven jobs the developer lists for a shift, from inspecting arrivals to surviving the night. Then check the badge guide to see how far most players get, and the shop guide before you spend any Robux.

These four guides stick to what Day Dreams Games has published on Roblox — the game description, the badge list and the Robux store — and say plainly when something is our advice rather than an official rule.

## Pick a guide by question

### [How to Play: What a Shift Asks of You](/animal-daycare/how-to-play/)

The shift explained from the official description: inspect every arrival, spot the visitors who are not real children, feed and play with the kids, keep them calm and survive the night. Includes what the store's item descriptions reveal about Calm, coffee, the Toy Hammer and ghosts.

### [All 5 Badges and How Rare They Are](/animal-daycare/badges/)

Every badge with its exact requirement and total awards. The totals fall steeply after the second night: 61 in 100 first-night badge totals are matched by a second-night badge, but fewer than 5 in 100 reach five shifts (badge totals, not a tracked group). The badges were only created on 16 September 2026.

### [Robux Shop, Lamb Coins and Classes](/animal-daycare/shop/)

All 25 Robux products with official prices, the value of each Lamb Coin pack, what class level unlocks cost at each star tier, and what the store does not explain.

### [Developer, Rating and Servers](/animal-daycare/game-info/)

Who Day Dreams Games is, when the game launched, how many players fit in a server, its Mild maturity rating and how it differs from Animal Hospital (Anomaly).

## What should a new player read first?

Keep it simple for the first session. Read [how to play](/animal-daycare/how-to-play/) to understand the day-and-night structure, then play a couple of shifts before buying anything. When a particular problem keeps ending your shift, the [shop guide](/animal-daycare/shop/) shows which cheap item targets it.

## What these guides do not cover yet

We have no pages yet for codes, a list of impostor clues, the individual night threats or the class list. Codes have not appeared in any official place we can read. The clues and threats appear in gameplay videos, but we have not confirmed them, and the class names are not published anywhere official. We hold those pages back rather than publish guesses.

## Scope

Animal Daycare is a live Roblox game that updates often — the listing showed an update on 30 September 2026. Every guide shows its check date. Numbers such as badge totals and visits are snapshots and grow daily.
