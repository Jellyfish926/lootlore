# One Tap Game Info: Developer, Platforms and Servers

One Tap is made by Stringless Banjo, a Roblox group created on 27 November 2025. The official description says the game supports PC, mobile, tablet and console, with Xbox and PlayStation named. Servers hold 8 players, Roblox rates the content Mild, and the game's record showed 590,415,564 visits as of 10 October 2026.

This page collects the facts about the game itself, as opposed to how to play it. Every figure comes from Roblox's public records, read on 10 October 2026 between 11:39 and 11:44 UTC. Counters such as visits and members move by the minute, so read them as a snapshot.

## Who makes One Tap?

A Roblox group called Stringless Banjo. Its description is one sentence: "Just me and my friend making fun roblox games lol". That is all the group says about who is behind it.

| Members | 207,489 as of 10 October 2026 |
| Group shout | Empty |
| Public games in the group's list | 1: [FPS] One Tap |
| Roblox verified badge | On the owner account; not on the group |

The group was created a week before the game record, which dates from 4 December 2025. Its role list has three ranks above ordinary members, named Developer, Owner and Holder, and each is held by one account. The list also has a Guest role and a second rank-1 role named Cool People. One Tap is the only game in the group's public list, so there is no sister game to compare it with.

## Which platforms does One Tap support?

The description answers under the heading "Platform Support". It lists four device types, PC, Mobile, Tablet and Console, and then adds in brackets: "(Xbox & PlayStation supported)".

| Platform | In the official description? |
| --- | --- |
| PC | Yes |
| Mobile | Yes |
| Tablet | Yes |
| Xbox | Yes, named in brackets |
| PlayStation | Yes, named in brackets |

That is the developer's statement, and we have not tested the game on each device. The description says nothing about controller layouts, touch controls or whether players on different devices share servers. If you want to know how to scope or aim on your device, no official source we read explains it.

## How big is a One Tap server?

Eight players. Roblox's game record sets the maximum at 8, and the game is a free-for-all with no teams, so that is you and up to seven opponents.

Private servers are less clear. The game's public Roblox page carries a private-server price attribute of 49 Robux, but Roblox's own game record for One Tap reports createVipServersAllowed as false. The two disagree, and we could not open a purchase window without an account, so we do not say whether private servers can be bought. Roblox describes a private server as "a subscription-based feature that allows a player to decide who can play a game with them", and says developers can charge "a monthly Robux fee" for it.

## What is One Tap's content rating?

Roblox labels it "Maturity: Mild". The one content descriptor on the label is "Violence (Repeated/Mild)". We read both from Roblox's experience-guidelines API, which answers a POST request for universeId 9294074907; the label is not in the game page HTML. The label lists no other descriptor, and it gives a minimum age of 0.

Roblox also files the game under the genre Shooter, sub-genre Deathmatch Shooter. Both are labels on Roblox's record as of our read, and either can change.

## Which languages does One Tap list?

Roblox's language record for the game lists 18: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Thai, Turkish, Vietnamese, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional) and Hindi. The record does not say whether a translation was written by hand or generated automatically.

## Is there an official One Tap Discord?

We could not confirm one, so this site does not link one. Roblox lets developers add social media links to a game's page, but its documentation says: "Social media links are only visible to users who have verified their age as at least 16 years old." Read without an account, both link lists return an authentication error.

The places we could read contain no invite. The game description has none, the group description has none, the group shout is empty and none of the six event listings on the [updates page](/one-tap/updates/) carries a link. A server that calls itself official may well be real, but we do not publish an invite we have not seen on an official page.

## Does One Tap have badges?

No. Roblox's badge list for the game came back empty on each of three reads on 10 October 2026. The description points to in-game rewards instead: it names levels, daily and quests as reward sources, and the [rewards page](/one-tap/rewards/) covers what is known about them.
