    Beast of Reincarnation: All Endings

    Rewritten post-launch · Ending status re-verified August 31, 2026

    Language: English日本語FrançaisEspañolItalianoDeutsch

    Story · Spoiler-managed

    Now that Beast of Reincarnation is out, the ending question has a real answer: the game runs to a single canonical main-story ending, then unlocks New Game Plus. There is no verified branching, no good/bad split, and no hidden "true ending." Here's how the finale actually plays out, the achievement chain that marks it, and what New Game Plus does next.

    Endings status: Confirmed in-game. Beast of Reincarnation ends with a single main-story ending and then unlocks New Game Plus. Nothing in the finished game exposes multiple endings, a good/bad branch, or a separate "true ending" — the pre-launch speculation about hidden ending routes did not hold up. What the game does have is a fixed three-achievement finale chain and an NG+ loop built for replay.

    How the ending actually works

    Beast of Reincarnation is a linear-story action RPG set in a ruined far-future Japan — the year 4026. You play Emma, a Sealer who fights with a katana and a bow, traveling with Koo, her withered wolf-dog companion. Across roughly 13 chapters plus an epilogue ("Bearing Fruit"), Emma seals a chain of giant Malefacts and, at the story's climax, faces the game's final boss: the Beast of Reincarnation itself.

    When you reach Chapter 13 and defeat the Beast of Reincarnation, the story resolves the same way for every player. There is no choice prompt at the end, no branch to miss, and no alternate finale gated behind optional content. The ending is a fixed, authored sequence — a somber, tearjerker beat about breaking the cycle of reincarnation, built around the bond between Emma and Koo and the theme of letting go.

    The finale achievement chain

    The clearest proof that the ending is a single sequence is the achievement chain that fires around it. Three achievements unlock in order as you close out the story:

    OrderAchievementHow it unlocks
    1The End of ReincarnationDefeat the final boss, the Beast of Reincarnation, at the end of Chapter 13
    2Life After the EndWatch the ending sequence through to completion
    3ReincarnatedBegin New Game Plus after the credits

    Notice what this chain is: a straight line from boss to ending to NG+. There are no "Ending A / Ending B" achievements anywhere in the list — every player earns the same three, in the same order. That is the structure of one canonical ending, not a branching finale.

    Does Beast of Reincarnation have multiple endings?

    No — there is no verified multiple-ending system. Game Freak has not published an official ending count, and nothing in the finished game surfaces a second ending, a good/bad split, or a hidden "true ending" route. Pre-launch, some guides speculated that maxing a rapport stat or clearing optional quests would unlock alternate finales. In the shipped game that did not pan out: rapport with Koo raises her combat strength and unlocks rewards, but it does not branch the ending, and the story runs on a fixed main path rather than gated side-quest routes.

    Four weeks post-launch, that verdict has held: community testing (including Game8's dedicated endings investigation) confirms there are no alternative routes, no choice prompts, and no secret objectives that change the finale — the epilogue concludes the same way on every playthrough.

    New Game Plus after the ending

    Beating the game unlocks New Game Plus, marked by the Reincarnated achievement. NG+ is not an alternate ending — it is a harder replay of the same story, and it is mandatory for completion: the full trophy and achievement set (and a 100% clear) requires finishing the game in NG+. Emma's level cap is 100 and you can respec, so NG+ is where most players finish maxing builds, skills, and collectibles. With a typical run at 25–28 hours and completionist play at 30–35, the NG+ requirement is what pushes a true 100% into a second full pass — see our how long to beat breakdown.

    And to close the last open question from launch week: NG+ has now been confirmed not to alter the ending either — it is the same story at a higher challenge, built for build-completion and the collection grind.

    Spoiler-light story context

    The ending lands as a quiet, melancholy resolution rather than a triumphant one — its subject is the cost of severing a cycle that has defined Emma and Koo's world. At launch, reviewers were split on the storytelling overall: praise for the combat sat alongside criticism of pacing and long cutscenes, and the game settled at Metacritic 72 with players more divided (6.1 user score). The emotional core, though, is consistent: this is a story about bonds, and about letting go.

    For readers who want the actual beats, here is the shape of the finale at one level of abstraction — enough to understand what kind of story this is, without scene-by-scene spoilers. The ending's reveal chain, as documented in Game8's full ending explainer: the Blight's true source traces back to human technological advancement; the Beast of Reincarnation itself is a developed being rather than a natural monster; the “vessels” were created as perfect humans; and the final clash resolves through Emma's sacrifice, with the epilogue following Kagura and Koo afterward. If even that outline is too much, stop at this paragraph — everything above it is spoiler-safe.

    Spoiler policy: the outline above deliberately stops at the structural level — no scene-by-scene dialogue, no epilogue specifics. The linked sources go deeper if you want them.

    FAQ

    How many endings does Beast of Reincarnation have?

    One. The game has a single canonical main-story ending, followed by New Game Plus. There is no verified good/bad split or separate "true ending."

    Is there a hidden or "true" ending I can miss?

    No. Nothing in the finished game gates an alternate finale behind rapport, side quests, or collectibles. Every player reaches the same ending after defeating the Beast of Reincarnation in Chapter 13.

    What is the ending achievement chain?

    Three achievements fire in order: The End of Reincarnation (beat the final boss), Life After the End (watch the ending), and Reincarnated (start New Game Plus).

    Do I need New Game Plus?

    For 100% completion and the full achievement set, yes — one requirement is finishing the game in NG+, so plan on at least two runs. NG+ is a harder replay of the same story, not a new ending.

    Does rapport with Koo change the ending?

    No verified effect on the ending. Building rapport raises Koo's combat strength and unlocks rewards, but it does not branch the finale.
