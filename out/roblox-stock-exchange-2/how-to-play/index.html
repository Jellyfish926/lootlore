    How to Play Roblox Stock Exchange 2: Trades to Rebirth

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-08

    Scope: Systems named in the official game description, badge texts, game pass descriptions and official event listings on 2026-10-08; button positions, fee rates without a pass, level thresholds and rebirth rules are not published and are not covered

    Key points

    • You grow a small simulated account by trading stocks, ETFs, futures and IPOs; the description also lists limit orders, stop losses, take profits, leverage and shorting.
    • The Overnight Desk pass says your market keeps running for 24 hours offline "instead of 8", which implies a default of 8 hours.
    • Badge totals show how many players have reached each step: first trade and first profit are common, shorting much less so, and futures, bots and rebirth are rare.
    • Everything is simulated. The developer says the game does not involve real-money trading or provide financial advice.

    In Roblox Stock Exchange 2 you start with a small simulated account and grow it by trading stocks, ETFs, futures and IPOs. You can go long or short, add leverage, and place limit orders, stop losses and take profits. Algorithmic bots, levels and rebirths are also listed, and your market keeps running for a while after you log off.

    That summary is built from the developer's own description on Roblox. The description is a feature list, not a manual, so this guide also reads the badge texts and game pass descriptions, which give away more detail than the description does.

    What does the developer say you can do?

    The description opens with "BUILD YOUR TRADING EMPIRE!" and tells you to "Study price action, discover leading stocks, manage risk, and grow your portfolio, even while you’re offline!" Seven feature lines follow.

    Official lineWhat it tells you
    Trade stocks, ETFs, futures, and IPOsFour kinds of market are named
    Use advanced indicators and live order flowCharts carry indicators, and there is a view of incoming orders
    Place limit orders, stop losses, and take profitsThree order tools beyond a plain buy or sell
    Unlock and upgrade algorithmic trading botsBots are unlocked first and improved afterwards
    Level up, rebirth, and expand your financial empireThere are levels and a rebirth system
    Track your performance with a P&L calendarResults are recorded day by day
    Use leverage, short the market, and master every market cycleYou can trade with borrowed size and bet on falling prices

    The right-hand column is our plain reading of each line. It adds no numbers, because the description gives none.

    What should your first session look like?

    The badge totals show how many players have reached each step; they do not show the order players do things in. Roblox had awarded the Welcome badge 2,288,320 times by 8 October 2026.

    1. Try the code if you like. TOOLS is printed in the official description; we have not tested it in the game. The codes page has the details.
    2. Make one trade. The First Trade badge reads "You made your first trade!" About 90 in 100 Welcome badge holders have it.
    3. Close a trade in profit. First Profit ("You made your first profit trading!") sits at about 69 in 100.
    4. Try a short once you understand a long. First Short ("You shorted your first stock!") drops to about 27 in 100.
    5. Leave futures for later. First Futures Trade is under 10 in 100.

    This order is our suggestion, drawn from how common each badge is. The game does not prescribe it. Exact counts for all 11 badges are in the badge list.

    How do you buy, sell and close a position?

    The official art above is one public look at the trading screen. It shows a Long and Short toggle above a single "Buy OBBY" button, and an open position row with a red Close button. Tabs under the chart read Positions, Orders, History, P&L, Feed and News. A second official image shows large BUY and SELL buttons beside a chart.

    Treat those labels as a guide, not a map. The art is promotional, and an official event starting 3 October 2026 announced "a brand new interface for the game, reorganized interfaces, and new game features". We have not confirmed where each button sits in the live game.

    What is known about leverage, fees and offline time?

    The description names these systems without numbers. The game pass descriptions fill some of the gaps, because each pass says what it changes.

    SystemWhat an official source saysStill unpublished

    FeesPro Trader pass: "0.02% fees, unlimited drawings, pro stats."The fee without the pass
    Offline marketOvernight Desk pass: "Your market keeps running for 24 hours offline instead of 8."What happens to open positions in that time
    XP and daily rewardsVIP pass: "2x XP, 2x daily rewards, VIP badge, 1 free Sim Day per day."XP needed per level, reward amounts
    Market hoursA 9-Robux product is named "Skip to Market Open"When the market opens and closes
    Player companiesMore Player Companies pass: "Launch up to 5 player-founded stocks instead of 2."How founding a company works
    NewsInsider pass: "Early alerts before news hits + IPO intel."How far ahead the alerts arrive

    Two things follow. The phrase "at any level" suggests the leverage you can use normally depends on your level; that is our reading, not a published rule. And the Overnight Desk description says 24 hours offline "instead of 8", which implies a default of 8 hours; no source states the base value directly. Prices and the full pass list are in the game pass guide.

    What comes after the first million?

    Two badges mark net worth: Millionaire ("Your total net-worth is over $1,000,000!") and $10,000,000 Net Worth. On 8 October 2026 they had been awarded 100,099 and 39,765 times. First Algo Bot stood at 50,910 and First Rebirth at 42,936.

    What a rebirth costs, what it resets and what it gives are not published in any official source we can read. The same goes for the level at which bots unlock. The algo bot guide collects the little that is official about bots, including four Robux products.

    Which markets were announced after launch?

    The developer announces updates as Roblox events. Five of them announced whole new things to trade or own:

    Event start (2026)Event titleDeveloper's words
    17 AugustHEDGE FUNDS"Run a hedge fund"

    5 SeptemberREAL ESTATE"you'll be able to buy real estate across a global map"
    19 SeptemberBONDS"Trade debt, earn interest, weigh the risk."
    26 SeptemberCOMMODITIES EXCHANGE"Prices will be driven by new news events, such as weather reports and more"

    An event listing is an announcement. It does not prove the feature went live at that exact minute. The updates page has every event, including Custom Offices, listed to start on 10 October 2026.

    Is Roblox Stock Exchange 2 realistic?

    It uses real trading vocabulary. Limit orders, stop losses, take profits, leverage, short selling, a Level 2 order book, trailing stops and fib extensions all appear in official text. But the developer states that "All markets and currencies are simulated", the ticker in the official art is OBBY, for a company called Obby Dynamics, and Roblox rates the experience Minimal, "Suitable for everyone". It is a game about trading, and results in it say nothing about real markets.
