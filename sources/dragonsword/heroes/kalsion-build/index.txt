3:T1e61,<p><em>Game version checked: 1.0.10 (19 August 2026 patch). DragonSword patches frequently — if a newer version is live, cross-check tuning-sensitive numbers in-game.</em></p>
<p>The short answer: build Kalsion as a Burn heavy hitter around his signature Karma, <strong>The Sun Rises Again</strong>, a 3-piece Blazing Flame + 2-piece Ruinsbreaker gear split, and Attack Power Up / Critical Hit Chance / Critical DMG as his three main stats. He is one of the few heroes both major English <a href="/heroes/tier-list/">tier lists agree on</a> — S-tier on both rankings, no split — which is rare enough on this roster that it is worth taking seriously.</p>
<h2>Who Kalsion is, and why he ranks where he does</h2>
<p>On our own <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">hero roster page</a>, Kalsion is listed as "Sun of the Kingdom," Roderic royalty, with a [Royal Flame] state — one of the eight heroes handed to you through the story rather than bought with Fate's Invitation tickets. What the roster page does not spell out is his damage identity: build guides consistently classify him as a <strong>Burn</strong> applicator and finisher, grouped with Aria, Kalien and Reina in the game's Blazeflame archetype. That single-element focus is exactly why the two tier lists we tracked down agree on him — a hero whose kit does one thing clearly is easier to rank than one whose value depends on team context.</p>
<h2>The build: Karma, gear, stats</h2>
<p>According to a bylined build guide from AllThings.How (Sehaj Padda, updated 28 July 2026), Kalsion's recommended loadout is:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Karma:</strong> The Sun Rises Again (his Exclusive Karma)</li><li><strong>Gear:</strong> 3-piece Blazing Flame set + 2-piece Ruinsbreaker set</li><li><strong>Main stats:</strong> ATK Power Up, Critical DMG, Critical Hit Chance</li><li><strong>Runes:</strong> Resolve</li></ul>
<p>That 3+2 split follows the general gearing rule our <a href="/guides/gear-runes-and-karma/">Gear, Runes and Karma guide</a> already lays out for this game — a role-defining 3-piece bonus paired with a secondary 2-piece bonus rather than chasing a full 5-piece set. Blazing Flame is the Burn-scaling half of that pairing; Ruinsbreaker's DEF Ignore bonus is the generic DPS half that shows up across most of the roster's best builds, not just Kalsion's.</p>
<p>A second, independently written source — OSLink's character build roundup (Rainee Walker, 9 August 2026) — cross-references Kalsion the same way it does every hero on the list: not as a standalone entry with unique gear text, but as the named Burn partner slotted into Aria's, Kalien's and Eileen's own build recommendations ("Best Team: Reina + Kalsion," "Kalsion + Kalien," "Kalsion + Aria"). That pattern matters for how you should read this build: Kalsion's numbers are validated across at least two outlets, but his <em>role</em> is consistently described in relation to teammates rather than in isolation — he is built to slot into an existing Burn chain, not to run one alone.</p>
<h2>Stat priorities and Karma logic</h2>
<p>Neither source publishes exact substat percentages for Kalsion specifically. What the wider build-fundamentals section of the AllThings.How guide does establish, and what applies to Kalsion the same as anyone else on the roster, is a general damage benchmark: aim for roughly 80% Critical Hit Chance and 150% Critical DMG before over-investing elsewhere, and treat a hero's Exclusive Karma as the default pick over a general-purpose one unless you have checked its actual effect against your build. For Kalsion, The Sun Rises Again is that Exclusive Karma — built around his own kit rather than a shared-pool option — so there is no reason to run a Basic Karma on him once you have unlocked it.</p>
<p>For the underlying Karma system itself — how General Karma differs from Exclusive Karma, what Fragment of Memory and Tower of Trials farming look like, and why the Exclusive Karma recipe is thin on official documentation — see our <a href="/guides/gear-runes-and-karma/">Gear, Runes and Karma page</a>, which sources this in more depth than a single-hero build page can.</p>
<h2>Team pairings that use his Burn kit</h2>
<p>Both sourced tables above point in the same direction for teammates:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Kalsion + Aria</strong> — Aria applies Burn and Airborne from range as a bomb-throwing enabler; Kalsion converts that setup into damage. This pairing shows up as Aria's recommended team <em>and</em> as Kalien's, which tells you it is a core Burn-team slot rather than a one-off suggestion.</li><li><strong>Kalsion + Kalien</strong> — two Burn-focused heroes stacked together for faster Burn application speed, at the cost of redundancy if your third slot doesn't add a different Status Ailment.</li><li><strong>Reina + Kalsion</strong> — pairs Kalsion's Burn damage with Reina's Airborne/Gale kit, useful if you are running a mixed-element team rather than a pure Burn stack.</li></ul>
<p>Our own <a href="/guides/status-ailments-and-signals/">Status Ailments and Signals guide</a> explains the mechanical reason these pairings work: Active Skills apply a Status Ailment, Signal Skills detonate it, and Switching Signals hand the combo to the next hero — so a "Burn team" is really a relay where each hero's job is either to apply, extend, or spend the Burn stack, not to deal damage independently.</p>
<p>For a full breakdown of how a rotation plays out with a Burn or mixed team in practice rather than as a stat table, see our <a href="/guides/best-team-comps/">Best Team Comps guide</a>, which walks through the sequencing logic hero-by-hero.</p>

We are also not able to confirm from official material alone whether Kalsion's Burn application speed changed in the 19 August 1.0.10 patch — our <a href="/guides/patch-notes-and-roadmap/">patch notes and roadmap page</a> does not list a Kalsion-specific balance change in its own record, so treat any claim of a recent Kalsion buff or nerf you see elsewhere as unconfirmed until Hound13's own patch notes name him directly.
