# Valheim Survival & Building Guides

Pick the guide that matches your current bottleneck. Always running out of food? Start with food and farming. Always short on resistance supplies? Read the mead guide. Losing too much time on the road? Read portals and sailing. Base layout and the Rested buff are worth checking together.

## Pick a guide by problem

### [Valheim Base Building: Your First Base](/valheim/first-base/)

Starts from one practical base and explains site choice, roofing, smoke, structural stability, production layout and emergency supplies.

### [Valheim Food Guide: Three Food Slots](/valheim/food/)

Instead of memorising a best-food list that goes stale, learn to combine each trip's food around health, stamina and Eitr needs and what you can actually supply.

### [Valheim Rested Buff: Comfort and Fires](/valheim/rested/)

Tells resting apart from Rested, works through why the buff is not showing up, and builds a supply rhythm that suits long expeditions.

### [Valheim Farming: Seeds and Steady Food](/valheim/farming/)

Builds a farm cycle from your first seeds to reliable restocking, and explains crops that will not grow, running out of seed stock, and the biome requirements of Plains and Mistlands crops.

### [Valheim Mead Guide: Resist and Heal](/valheim/mead/)

Prepares mead bases and fermentation on the current brewing chain, separates environmental resistance from combat healing, and explains why your base is not drinkable yet.

### [Valheim Portals: Pairing and Ore Limits](/valheim/portals/)

How ordinary portals pair, how to check carry restrictions, and how solo and group players build a portal network that will not strand them in the field.

### [Valheim Sailing & Ore Hauling Guide](/valheim/ships/)

From your first practical ship: planning routes and landing spots, handling wind, organising ore runs and cutting your losses when a ship goes down.

### [Traders guide](/valheim/traders/)

What each of the three traders is actually for, how to plan finding and revisiting them, and why the 1.0 inventory expansion and a carry-weight increase are not the same thing.

## Scope

These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
