    How to Get Appeal Fast in +1 Mog Evolution: Every Boost

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-01 · Checked on version 2026-09-30 update

    Scope: Boost names, multipliers and prices from developer product descriptions on 2026-10-01; how multipliers combine and how much Appeal one click gives are not published; buying order is our advice

    Auto Clicker

    VIP

    Key points

    • For free: click constantly, upgrade your hammer towards Bonesmash, and stand on any treadmill belt you have unlocked.
    • The cheapest permanent boosts are the first Power multipliers (x1.5 for 1 Robux, x2 for 2) and the Auto Clicker (39 Robux, 22 clicks per second).
    • VIP gives 10x Appeal for 99 Robux; treadmill belts go from x2 (9 Robux) to x999 (999 Robux).
    • Bigger Appeal packs give far more per Robux: 2,041 Appeal per Robux on the smallest, 200,401 on the largest.

    Click as fast as you can, upgrade your hammer, and stand on the best treadmill belt you have. Those are the free ways to grow Appeal in +1 Mog Evolution. If you spend Robux, start with the cheap Power multipliers and the 39-Robux Auto Clicker, then VIP. Below is every boost with its exact wording and price.

    All prices are Roblox's official figures on 1 October 2026. +1 Mog Evolution sells no game passes, so every boost here is a developer product.

    What can you do without spending Robux?

    Three things raise Appeal for free, going by the official wording:

    1. Click. "Every Click gives you MORE Appeal", says the game description. Faster clicking means more Appeal.
    2. Upgrade the hammer. "Upgrade your hammer to Bonesmash and get even more Appeal". What each upgrade costs is not published.
    3. Use treadmill belts. The paid belts "Earn … Appeal while standing on it, no rebirths needed". That wording suggests belts can also be unlocked through rebirths, which the progression guide explains.

    Which permanent boosts are worth buying first?

    These four boosts are permanent or one-time purchases, according to their descriptions. Sorted by price:

    BoostRobuxOfficial wordingOur take
    x1.5 Power1"Permanently earn 1.5x Power."Cheapest boost in the game
    x2 Power2"Permanently earn 2x Power."Also near-free
    Auto Clicker39"Automatically earns Appeal at 22 clicks per second. Can only be bought once."Best if your hands tire before your session ends
    VIP99"Permanent VIP: earn 10x Appeal, plus a VIP name tag. Can only be bought once."A flat 10x Appeal; we cannot rank it against the Power multipliers until Power is confirmed to mean Appeal

    We read "Power" as the same thing as Appeal, because the "+100K Appeal" pack is described as "100,000 Power". The developer has not confirmed this. We also could not confirm whether VIP, Power multipliers and treadmills multiply together.

    How do the Power multipliers compare?

    Nine Power products, all "Permanently earn" a multiplier. Together they cost 395 Robux.

    ProductRobuxMultiplier per Robux
    x1.5 Power11.50
    x2 Power21.00
    x4 Power50.80
    x8 Power120.67
    x16 Power190.84
    x32 Power291.10
    x64 Power491.31

    x256 Power1991.29

    The last column is simply the multiplier divided by the price, our own maths. If only the highest multiplier you own counts, x128 is the best value per Robux. If they stack, the early cheap ones are an easy win. The store does not say which, and it does not say whether you must buy them in order.

    Which treadmill belt should you buy?

    Treadmill belts multiply Appeal "while standing on it". Only the x9, x99 and x999 belts have descriptions; the six belts added on 18 September have names and prices only.

    BeltRobuxDescription
    x2 Treadmill9None given
    x3 Treadmill19None given
    x5 Treadmill39None given
    x9 Treadmill69"Earn 9x Appeal while standing on it, no rebirths needed."
    x8 Treadmill79None given
    x12 Treadmill99None given
    x18 Treadmill129None given
    x99 Treadmill249"Earn 99x Appeal while standing on it, no rebirths needed."
    x999 Treadmill999"Earn 999x Appeal while standing on it, no rebirths needed."

    Note the odd pair: x9 costs 69 Robux, but the weaker x8 costs 79. Unless x8 does something its name does not show, x9 is the better buy. Per Robux, x999 is the best value among the belts (exactly 1x per Robux), but it is also the most expensive item in the store.

    Are the Appeal packs worth it?

    They are one-off boosts, not permanent. The bigger the pack, the more Appeal per Robux:

    PackRobuxAppeal per Robux
    +100K Appeal492,041
    +1M Appeal1496,711
    +10M Appeal29933,445
    +100M Appeal499200,401

    The Starter Pack (9 Robux, once only) adds "+2,000 Appeal and +50 Wins" plus the LTN body, which makes it the cheapest bundle in the store. It is on the Robux products list.

    Do hammers and bodies boost Appeal too?

    Yes, according to three items in the +1 Mog Evolution store. The Gigachad body gives "a x4 body Appeal multiplier while equipped", and the Claviculars Hammer "Gives x1024 Appeal while equipped". The Smoothie gives "x2048 Charisma", a word the store uses nowhere else. All three cost 399 Robux and are compared on the limited unlocks page.

    Two numbered hammer upgrades are also for sale, at 29 and 79 Robux, with no description.

    Our suggested buying order

    This is our advice, not a rule from +1 Mog Evolution itself. Start with x1.5 and x2 Power (3 Robux together). Add the Auto Clicker if clicking is the bottleneck, then VIP. Buy treadmill belts only once you know where the belts are and how long you will stand on them. Leave the big Appeal packs for last; they are spent once and gone.
