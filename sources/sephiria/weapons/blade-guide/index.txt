3:T1a99,<p>Blade was announced under a different name — "Katana" — before it shipped in patch 0.8.11 as Blade, and the class still plays like the announcement promised: a stance weapon built entirely around alternating between sheathed and unsheathed states. If you're wondering why your Blade run feels like a different weapon depending on whether you just attacked, that alternation is the entire design, not an incidental detail.</p>

<h2>The one mechanic that defines the class</h2>
<p>Everything about Blade reads through one axis: <strong>Sheath</strong> grants an invincibility window, and leaving it changes what your inputs do. <strong>Iaido Slash</strong> is the drawn normal attack — the hit you get from breaking sheath — while <strong>Issen</strong> and <strong>Heavy Slash</strong> are the class's two specials, both keyed to your stance. Unlike <a href="/weapons/great-sword-guide/">Great Sword</a>, where the special is a single charged ability you build a route around, Blade's power comes from timing the sheath/unsheathe cycle correctly rather than from any one button.</p>
<p>One upgrade makes this literal: <strong>Heidi</strong> ties Overheat stacks directly to your Dash Count, and 1.0.19 buffed it meaningfully — Overheat gain on Sheath rose from 2 to 3, and the stack cap from 6 to 9. That's a 50% increase to both the rate and the ceiling of a mechanic that only exists because of the sheath cycle, which tells you how central the developers consider it to the class's identity.</p>
<h2>What 1.0.26 changed</h2>
<p>The most recent substantive Blade change is also the clearest safety buff the class has received: <strong>Lightning Flash's Special Attack: Issen now grants invincibility while sheathing</strong>, added in <a href="/patch-notes/1-0-25-to-1-0-30/">1.0.26</a>. Before this patch, the moment of re-sheathing after Issen was a vulnerability window; now it isn't, for players running that specific upgrade. It's a narrow, named fix rather than a class-wide rework, so it only applies if you've taken the Lightning Flash line specifically — but it's worth checking your current build against, since guides written before 13 August 2026 won't reflect it.</p>
<p>Two smaller items from the same patch window are worth knowing if something on Blade looks broken rather than by design: <strong>1.0.23</strong> fixed Solis Iubar firing a different special attack than intended above 150 MP, and the <strong>Scabbard of Exorcism</strong>'s projectile direction was occasionally wrong in multiplayer, also patched that window per the <a href="/patch-notes/1-0-19-to-1-0-24/">1.0.19 to 1.0.24 record</a>. Neither is a build decision, but both explain behavior that could otherwise read as a bug in your specific run.</p>
<h2>The Solis family and its dependency trap</h2>
<p>Blade's most-documented route by far is the <strong>Solis</strong> upgrade family — Solis Iubar, Solis Missio, Solis Cineris, Solis Fracto, Solis Dekri, and Solis Braca — which Chinese-language build guides refer to as 火太刀 (fire Blade). The dependency worth understanding before you commit to this line: the katana <em>consumes</em> Solar Blade charges, it does not create them. That single fact is why the route needs a dedicated charge-generation piece to function at all rather than running on the weapon alone — the full pickup order, the mandatory artifact, and the Pure White Cloak's 30-second buff window are documented on the dedicated <a href="/builds/fire-blade-solar-blade-build/">Fire Blade build page</a>, which this page treats as the canonical write-up for that specific route rather than duplicating it here.</p>
<p>Note the similarly-spelled name trap here: <strong>Solis Sineris</strong> is not part of this Blade line at all — the <a href="/weapons/great-sword-guide/">Great Sword guide</a> documents it as that weapon's own Solar Blade upgrade, tested by one experienced player alongside The Inquisitor on lower Hard Mode tiers before a different route was chosen for a tier 50 clear. Confusing it with Blade's <strong>Solis Cineris</strong> above is an easy mistake to make from the name alone.</p>
<h2>Blade in a magic-damage build</h2>
<p>Blade also shows up outside its own dedicated route: the "magic-crit Blade" line — the katana variant with magic critical damage — is the highest-ceiling weapon option in the documented <a href="/builds/lightning-boomerang-spellbook-build/">Lightning Boomerang spellbook build</a>, where the build's own author recommends it for players "chasing numbers" over the safer Staff or Sword &amp; Shield alternatives. That's a different use case from the Solis fire route above — a magic-crit Blade build is casting a spellbook continuously and using the weapon's crit scaling as a damage multiplier, not leaning on Sheath timing the way a pure Solis route does. Knowing both uses matters because "Blade build" in community discussion can mean either one depending on context.</p>
<h2>What we're not printing</h2>
<p>TEAM HORAY has never published a numeric i-frame duration for the Sheath state, so exact invincibility windows are <strong>not confirmed</strong> — we can confirm the mechanic exists and that Lightning Flash's Issen now extends it into the re-sheathing moment, but not how many frames either window covers. We also have no verified Blade-specific win-rate or tier-list data; any ranking claim for the class is attributed community sentiment, not something TEAM HORAY has stated.</p>
