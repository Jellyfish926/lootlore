3:T2e05,<p>DragonSword: Awakening has <strong>60 Steam achievements</strong> — <a href="/guides/patch-notes-and-roadmap/">patch 1.0.9</a> (13 August 2026) added the sixtieth, <em>Conqueror of Barduk</em>. <strong>43</strong> carry a public description; the other <strong>17</strong> are hidden, so Steam shows a name and nothing else.</p>
<p>Every percentage below was read off Valve's global stats page on <strong>1 September 2026</strong>. They drift upward as the population plays. Treat them as a snapshot.</p>
<p>Prefer to tick these off as you go? The <a href="/guides/achievement-tracker/">achievement tracker</a> uses the same Steam data, refreshed weekly, and saves your progress in your browser.</p>
<h2>What the distribution looks like</h2>
<p><strong>38 of the 60 sit below 10%.</strong> Ten are above 20%, and two now clear 40%.</p>
<p>The most-unlocked is the hidden <em>Lucky!</em> at <strong>45.9%</strong>; the most-unlocked with a visible description is <em>Anything in Here?</em> — "Opened a Treasure Chest" — at <strong>37.0%</strong>. The rarest described one is <em>We Solve Anything</em>, which wants every Area Quest completed, at <strong>1.2%</strong>.</p>
<p>One caution. We do not know what denominator Valve uses, and Hound13 has not said. So "37.0% have opened a chest" is a fact about the stats page; any conclusion about how far a typical player gets is an inference.</p>

<h2>Names the achievement list confirms and nothing else does</h2>
<p>The stats page is the most useful document about this game that Hound13 did not write as marketing, because it names things.</p>
<p><strong>Seventeen bosses</strong>, split between "Subduer of ~" (defeat three times) and "Conqueror of ~" (defeat on a stated difficulty). They have their own <a href="/guides/bosses/">bosses page</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Five Hero Quests with owners</strong>: Cerese, Kalien, Ornette, Dana and Theresia. The other fourteen of the <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">nineteen heroes</a> have no Hero Quest achievement. That could mean they have no Hero Quest, or that theirs awards nothing — the list cannot distinguish the two, so we claim neither.</p>
<p><strong>Four named side quests</strong>: Marmot King Hog, Anne, Bart and Ninian.</p>
<p><strong>The collectible categories the game tracks</strong>: Treasure Chests, Treasure Maps, Waypoints, Dungeon Gates, Goddess Statues of Organa, Eona's Legacy, the Slide Puzzle, the Platform Puzzle mini-game, Area Quests, Sudden Missions, Hunt Monsters and Raids.</p>
<h2>The band under 2% is all exploration</h2>
<p>Six of the seven rarest described achievements have nothing to do with combat: <em>We Solve Anything</em> (1.2%), <em>Hold On, Stand Right There</em> (1.8%), <em>There's a Marmot in That Burrow!</em> (1.9%), <em>In Search of Hidden Treasure</em> (2.0%), <em>Puzzle Solved by Pushing</em> (2.1%) and <em>Gotta Open That Suspicious Chest</em> (2.4%).</p>
<p>That is a clean signal about the shape of the completion problem. The hard part is not the Nightmare bosses — those sit at 2.8% to 7.6%. The hard part is <strong>finding things</strong>: treasure maps and slide puzzles are unlocked by roughly one player in seventy.</p>
<h2>These numbers move, including ours</h2>
<p>Unlock rates rise over time, so any percentage carries an implicit date. Watch ours drift across three readings:</p>
<ul><li><em>Lucky!</em> — <strong>43.6%</strong> in our early-August check, <strong>44.2%</strong> on 12 August, <strong>45.9%</strong> on 1 September.</li><li><em>Status Ailment Master</em> — <strong>9.5%</strong>, then <strong>9.8%</strong>, now <strong>10.8%</strong>.</li></ul>
<p>An achievement percentage for this game with no date attached is not wrong so much as unusable.</p>
<h2>Where players are actually stuck</h2>
<p>Three Steam forum threads are people asking about specific achievements: <em>"Status Ailment Master Achievement"</em>, <em>"2 Titles / Achievements help"</em> and <em>"Ill Stand in the Sky Achievement with a strong Reina?"</em>. The first has <strong>zero replies</strong>. Nobody has publicly answered it.</p>
<p>Which matches the data: the hidden achievements are where people get stuck, and no source, official or player-made, explains them.</p>
<h2>What is not on this page</h2>
<p><strong>The conditions for the 17 hidden achievements.</strong> Steam does not publish them and Hound13 has not described them in any announcement we have read. Sites listing exact triggers for <em>Status Ailment Master</em> or <em>Gas! Gas! Gas!</em> did not get them from Hound13, because Hound13 has not said. We would rather leave seventeen blanks than guess.</p>
<p><strong>A recommended completion order, or an estimate of how long 100% takes.</strong> Both would require knowing what the hidden achievements ask for.</p>
<p><strong>Per-achievement walkthroughs.</strong> Where a solution exists in a source we can name — Hagen, for instance — it lives on the page for that subject.</p>
