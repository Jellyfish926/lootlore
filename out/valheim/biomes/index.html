    Key points

    • Six biome guides: Black Forest, Swamp, Mountains, Plains, Mistlands and Ashlands.
    • Each page covers getting a foothold, which resources to take first, and when to start preparing the boss.
    • If the logistics are already done and you only want the fight, use the boss section instead.

    Each biome page covers how to get a foothold after your first entry, which resources to look for first, and when to start preparing for the boss. If the logistics are already done and you only want the boss fight, go to the boss section instead.

    Pick a guide by problem

    • Valheim Bronze Age: Copper, Tin, Gear

      From entering the Black Forest to a working Valheim bronze line: a sensible order for mining, Surtling cores, fuel, tools and your first metal gear.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Swamp Guide: Crypts and First Iron

      With the Swamp Key in hand, work through preparation, landing, finding crypts, mining iron and hauling it home without dying on your first trip in.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Mountains: Cold, Silver and Eggs

      From your first safe climb to silver veins and dragon eggs: routes, supplies, frost resistance, air threats and how to get a heavy load back down.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Plains: Fulings, Barley and Flax

      Enter the Plains in order: scout the edges, fight in small groups, loot camps, then build the dedicated farm that feeds your food and gear upgrades.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Mistlands: Mist Gear, Cores and Eitr

      The order to work through the Mistlands: clear the mist, protect your route home, be careful around Dvergr, and prioritise infested mines for black cores.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Ashlands: Landing and Outposts

      Finish the post-Queen sailing prerequisites, then take landing, shield generators, fortresses and hauling in stages instead of betting it all on one beach.

      v1.0.12

    Scope

    These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
