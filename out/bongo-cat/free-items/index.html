    Bongo Cat Free Items: Hats, Skins and Codes Checked

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Free item sources named in the developer's 63 Steam announcements (February 3, 2025 to October 1, 2026) and the Steam store description, read on October 9, 2026 (dates on this page are UTC); in-game menus and the developer's Discord were not checked

    Key points

    • Bongo Cat is free to play on Steam, and the announcements describe chests, an exchange (10-for-1 in the February 2025 demo update), the free Paw Pass lane, multiplayer sets and tap milestones as ways to get items without payment.
    • As of October 9, 2026 we found no redeem-code system in the developer's 63 Steam announcements or the store description; that is not proof that none exists.
    • Four collaboration giveaway posts from 2025 and early 2026 print no end date, so their status is not confirmed; a post dated June 27, 2026 says the developer "had to disable the incentivized rewards", naming the Nightwater collab and the wishlist ones.
    • Paid things in the official text include supporter items, DLC packs, the premium Paw Pass lane and two Deluxe calendars (Winter 2025 and Lunar New Year 2026).

    Bongo Cat is free on Steam, and the developer's announcements name ways to earn hats and skins without paying: chest drops, the exchange, the free Paw Pass lane, multiplayer sets and tap milestones. As of October 9, 2026 we found no redeem-code system in 63 Steam announcements or the store description, so any code list is not confirmed.

    Are there Bongo Cat codes?

    We did not find any. On October 9, 2026 we searched the full text of the 63 developer announcements in the game's Steam news feed and the store description for the words a code system would need. Nothing in those texts describes a place to type a code or a code that grants an item.

    Search termMatches in the 63 announcementsWhat the matches refer to
    code / codes6, in 5 postsMultiplayer lobby codes (5) and the developer's own program code (1)
    redeem2, in 1 postRedeeming a Paw Pass Ticket and Paw Pass rewards
    promo1"promotional discounts" on DLC packs
    coupon, voucher, giveaway0No matches
    police0No matches

    The same terms return 0 matches in the store description. That is a statement about what we read, not proof that no code feature exists: we did not check the in-game menus ourselves, and we did not read the developer's Discord. The lobby codes in those posts belong to multiplayer lobbies, and nothing in the posts says a lobby code grants an item.

    So if a site lists "Bongo Cat codes" for a named hat, treat the list as not confirmed. This page will add a codes table only when a code appears in an official post.

    Why were incentivized rewards disabled?

    Because of Steam's rules, according to the developer. The post Wild Wonders DLC Release & Summer Sale, posted on June 27, 2026, says: "We had to disable the incentivized rewards (f.e. collab with Nightwater or the wishlist ones) and moved them to owning the full game or removed them entirely, because it was against Steam Guidelines to incentivize this, sorry for that." June 27 is the posting date; the post does not say when the rewards were switched off, and it does not use the word demo.

    Nightwater shows the change in one example. On June 16, 2026 the Summer Event post offered "the Nightwater Backpack for wishlisting the game" and "the Nightwater Cat for playing the demo for 5min". On September 18, 2026 the post Nightwater Release & Collab says "Getting the game will also grant you these four Bongo Cat items".

    The post does not say which older rewards were moved to ownership and which were removed, so the status of each 2025 collaboration item is not confirmed.

    How do you avoid missing a free item?

    The announcements repeat three habits.

    1. Open the game at least once a month, which is the stated condition for the free pass ticket.
    2. Keep the game updated. Giveaway posts carry the line "Don't forget to update your game!", and the Typing Fest item was claimed "by updating your game, following the giftbox and claiming your free Mech!".
    3. Claim inside the window. The first Bongo Cat x Indie Demos post ends its offer with "After that you can't get them anymore."

    Calendars were more forgiving. The Winter Event post of December 1, 2025 says the free Advent calendar "will be available until December 31st, which means you can also open doors from the past". If a demo reward did not arrive, the Indie Demos post suggested closing the other game "(so Steam syncs your playtime)" and waiting a few minutes.

    Can a friend give you hats and skins for free?

    Yes, in two ways the announcements name. The Animal Shelter - Charity Event post of November 10, 2025 says "we will re-enable ALL items for trading with friends, so gift your duplicats to your friends!". The trading rule has changed more than once, and the history is on the exchange and trading page.

    A friend can also pay the taps for your chest. The June 27, 2026 post says players can open a friend's chests and adds "(it costs you 1000 clicks, for your friend it's free)". The Paw Pass launch post says the pass ticket can be gifted "by using Steam's trading feature".

    What is not free in Bongo Cat?

    The official text puts a price on several things, and this list is not exhaustive: single supporter items in the Steam Item Store, the DLC packs, the premium Paw Pass lane, the Deluxe Calendar that the Winter Event post offered for purchase next to the free Advent calendar, and the "Lunar New Year Deluxe" that the Year of the Horse Event post of February 16, 2026 offered for purchase "(until March 16th)". The April 13, 2026 post adds items tied to buying another game: "Everyone who buys Tap Tap Loot will also receive 4 exclusive items!". When we loaded the Item Store on October 9, 2026, the US page showed a listing named Paw Pass Token at $4.99, and every other listing shown on that page was $2.50; the developer's announcement calls the pass item a Paw Pass Ticket, and its own store link opens the listing titled Paw Pass Token. The packs and their contents are listed in hats and skins.
