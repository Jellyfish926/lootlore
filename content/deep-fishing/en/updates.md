# Deep Fishing Updates: Changes Since 28 September 2026

Deep Fishing's Roblox records show 14 store products created between 29 September and 10 October 2026, including Season Pass at 399 Robux and Oni Soulbinder Skin at 1,649. No pass or badge record was created then, the game record's "updated" timestamp read 9 October, and the "EGGS & PETS are COMING!" listing gives a window of 11 to 15 October.

This update log is built from dated official records, not patch notes: the game record, 103 developer products, nine game passes, 13 badges and eight event listings, all read on 10 October 2026. Every time on this page is UTC. A record date says when a record was created or edited. It does not say when a feature reached players.

## What are Season Pass, Skip 1, Skip 10 and Reset Season?

They are four developer product records created within five minutes of each other on 29 September 2026. The names and prices are official. How a season works is not in any record we read.

| Product | Robux | Created (UTC) | Edited (UTC) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Season Pass | 399 | 29 Sep 2026, 21:26 | Not edited |
| Skip 1 | 79 | 29 Sep 2026, 21:27 | 4 Oct 2026, 16:14 |
| Skip 10 | 449 | 29 Sep 2026, 21:27 | 4 Oct 2026, 16:15 |
| Reset Season | 19 | 29 Sep 2026, 21:31 | Not edited |

The description field of all four was empty on 10 October 2026. In the records we read that day, the word "season" appears in two product names, Season Pass and Reset Season, and in no game pass, badge, event listing or line of the game description. So the following are not confirmed: how long a season lasts, what the pass unlocks, what the rewards are, what Skip 1 or Skip 10 skips, and what Reset Season resets.

One piece of arithmetic is safe. Skip 10 costs 449 Robux and Skip 1 costs 79, so the larger product costs about 5.7 times the smaller one (449 ÷ 79 ≈ 5.68). Whether Skip 10 gives ten of whatever Skip 1 gives is a reading of the names, not something the records state.

Season Pass is a developer product; it is not one of the nine game passes. Our advice, not a rule of the game: read what the game itself says about the pass before paying, because the store record we read gives a name and a price and has an empty description.

## Did any game pass or badge change?

The counts match: the records held nine passes and 13 badges on 10 October 2026, the same numbers this site noted on 29 September 2026 (nine and 13). We cannot tell from these responses whether any record was deleted. The edit dates did move. Eight passes carry an edit timestamp of 29 September 2026 at 20:23, and Double Coins carries 7 October 2026 at 12:44.

On 10 October the Double Coins description read "Earn 2x more coins when selling fish! 🪙". This site's own data from 29 September 2026 had the shorter "Earn 2x more coins! 🪙". The pass record does not keep earlier text, so we cannot tie that wording change to the 7 October edit with certainty. The price was 399 Robux in both readings.

In the badge records we read on 10 October 2026, no badge has a creation or edit date later than 5 August 2026. Award counts differ between our two readings: the Welcome! badge showed 2,104,103 awards in this site's reading of 29 September 2026 and 3,051,522 in the response we saved on 10 October 2026 at 05:07 UTC; the [badge list](/deep-fishing/badges/) explains what those counts can and cannot tell you about rarity.

## Why is a record date not a release date?

Because each record logs its own history. Three cases from this page show the gap.

- Oni Soulbinder Skin was created at 23:53 on 9 October 2026, about five hours after the game record's "updated" timestamp of 18:38 the same day (23:53 minus 18:38 is 5 hours 15 minutes). The product record exists and is marked as for sale; whether the skin could be bought in play on 10 October is not confirmed.
- Skip 1 and Skip 10 were edited at 16:14 and 16:15 on 4 October 2026, within 15 minutes of the listed start of "NEW WORLD IS COMING" at 16:00. The records do not connect the two.
- The "EGGS & PETS are COMING!" listing was created on 4 October 2026, a week before its window.

The game record carries two timestamps, "created" (18 July 2026) and "updated" (9 October 2026 at 18:38), and the second does not say what changed.

## How did we read the records, and what is missing?

Everything here was read on 10 October 2026 (UTC) from five Roblox endpoints: games, developer products, game passes, badges and virtual events. The pass and badge lists each came back complete in one response. The product list came back in two: 100 records, then 3 more behind the first response's page cursor.

The events endpoint answered in two ways. Called without a cursor it returned only the one listing whose window had not closed; called with the cursor shown in our sources it returned all eight. We used the eight-listing response. If Roblox stops returning closed listings, the two closed events above can no longer be checked there.

Still not confirmed after this read:

- how seasons work and what Season Pass rewards;
- what Leviathan Bottle, Tail Dragon Egg, Shop Daily Claimable Ad and Oni Soulbinder Skin do;
- what any of the edits changed, apart from the Double Coins wording noted above;
- whether the contents named in event listings shipped.

None of these records held a redemption code; the [codes page](/deep-fishing/codes/) sets out that check. This page reflects the read of 10 October 2026, made before the start time on the "EGGS & PETS are COMING!" listing; a product created after that read is not on it.
