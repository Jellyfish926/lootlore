# Untitled Wheelie Game Cop Chase Rules: Stars, Roadblocks

Untitled Wheelie Game cop chase rules here come from one developer announcement, the AI COPS 👮 Roblox event listing. It lists a 3 star system, roadblocks at 3 stars, cops that give up after losing sight of you "for over 60 seconds", and money for escaping. We read it on 10 October 2026 and have not tested these in game.

This page prints the announcement's twelve numbered lines exactly as the developer wrote them, spelling included, and adds one plain-words note to each. The plain-words notes are our reading. Fines and the two purchases that deal with them have their own page, the [cops and fines guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/cops-fines/). Times here come from the listing's API fields and are written in UTC.

## Where do the cop chase rules come from?

They come from the description field of an event listing titled "AI COPS 👮", owned by Untitled Wheelie Group. The listing was created on 21 September 2026 at 04:17. Its start field is 25 September 2026 at 22:00 and its end field is 26 September 2026 at 22:00. Its edit timestamp falls in the same second as its creation timestamp, so the record shows no later edit.

The text opens with "AI Cops are being added!" and then gives two headed lists, "COP BEHAVIOR:" with nine lines and "WHAT TO EXPECT:" with three. Most lines use "will". The listing was made 4 days 17 hours before its start time (our subtraction), so the wording describes a plan. Treat every rule below as what the developer's announcement says. The other six announcements are on the [patch notes page](/untitled-wheelie-game/patch-notes/).

## What are the nine COP BEHAVIOR rules?

The left column is the developer's text, copied in order.

| No. | Line as written | In plain words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cops will park beside highways, in parking lots, or in random places | Cops park beside highways, in parking lots or in what the line calls random places |
| 2 | Cops will have a much higher chance of chasing illegal bikes | Cops are much more likely to chase bikes the line calls illegal; it does not say which bikes those are |
| 3 | There will be a 3 star system similar to GTA's 5 star system | A star system with 3 stars, which the line compares to a 5 star system in GTA |
| 4 | At 3 stars, cops will begin to set up roadblocks | Cops begin setting up roadblocks at 3 stars |
| 5 | You will be fined if you are caught for various crimes, but if you escape, then you will get the money that the fines would have costed you! | Caught means a fine; escaped means you are paid the amount the fines would have been |
| 6 | If a cop gets juked out, they will spin out. Slowing them down | A cop that is dodged spins and loses speed |
| 7 | If cops lose sight of you, they will patrol the area near the last time they saw you. | Out of sight, cops search around the place they last saw you |
| 8 | If they lose sight of you for over 60 seconds, they give up. | After more than 60 seconds without sight of you, cops give up |
| 9 | Cops don't like wheelies, on any bike too. | Cops dislike wheelies, whatever the bike; the line does not say what they do about one |

## What do the three WHAT TO EXPECT lines say?

| No. | Line as written | In plain words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Single player servers, seperate from the actual game. | Single-player servers, apart from what the line calls the actual game; the line does not say how they are opened |
| 2 | Cops are not easy to get away from. Use tactics to spin them out or hide from them. | The line says cops are not easy to get away from and names two tactics: spinning them out and hiding from them |
| 3 | Getting chased will be a gamble, you either lose money or make money. | The line calls a chase a gamble: you lose money or make money |

"seperate" is the developer's spelling.

## How does the 3 star system work?

The announcement gives two facts about it. Line 3 says there will be "a 3 star system similar to GTA's 5 star system", and line 4 says "At 3 stars, cops will begin to set up roadblocks". The 5 in line 3 belongs to the game it is compared with; the count for Untitled Wheelie Game is 3.

| Number in the announcement | Line | What it counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | COP BEHAVIOR 3 and 4 | Stars in the system, and the star level at which roadblocks begin |
| 5 | COP BEHAVIOR 3 | Stars in the system it is compared with |
| 60 | COP BEHAVIOR 8 | Seconds out of sight before cops give up |

Those are the three figures in the twelve lines, apart from the line numbers. What earns a star, what cops do at 1 or 2 stars, and how a star level drops are not confirmed. Line 5 mentions "various crimes" without naming one, and line 9 says cops "don't like wheelies". Whether a wheelie alone adds a star is our question, not something the text answers.

## Do you get money for escaping the cops?

The announcement says yes. Line 5 reads: "if you escape, then you will get the money that the fines would have costed you!" The third WHAT TO EXPECT line repeats the idea as a gamble: "you either lose money or make money."

No amount appears. In the seven event announcements we read on 10 October 2026, no line states a fine or a payout as a number, so how much a fine costs and how much an escape pays are both not confirmed. Read literally, line 5 makes the two amounts equal, because the escape money is described as what the fines "would have costed you".

Two store records carry "FINES" in their names: the NEVER PAY FINES game pass, 149 Robux on 10 October 2026, and the AVOID FINES developer product, 13 Robux. The pass description reads "Just never have to pay fines for when you get caught". It does not mention escape money, and the announcement does not mention the pass. Whether a pass owner still collects the escape money is not confirmed. For in-game income outside chases, see the [money guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/money/).

## How long until the cops give up?

Lines 7 and 8 set out two stages. First, "If cops lose sight of you, they will patrol the area near the last time they saw you." Second, "If they lose sight of you for over 60 seconds, they give up." So the announcement describes a search phase and then a cut-off at more than 60 seconds.

Line 6 describes one thing that slows a cop: "If a cop gets juked out, they will spin out. Slowing them down". The second WHAT TO EXPECT line points at the same two ideas: "Use tactics to spin them out or hide from them."

Our reading, not a published tip list: the text names two things that work against a chase, making a cop miss and staying out of view. It does not say what counts as sight, whether buildings or tunnels block it, or whether the 60 seconds restart when a cop sees you again. Those are not confirmed.

## Which bikes are the cops more likely to chase?

Line 2 says cops "will have a much higher chance of chasing illegal bikes". The word "illegal" appears once in all the Roblox records we read on 10 October 2026: the game description, seven event announcements, 12 game passes, 23 developer products and the group description. That one use is line 2 itself, so which bikes count as illegal is not confirmed.

Line 9 is about wheelies, not bike types: "Cops don't like wheelies, on any bike too." The line does not say what cops do about a wheelie, so whether a wheelie on its own starts a chase is not confirmed. Our [bikes and parts guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/bikes/) lists the bike names the store records hold; none of those records marks a bike as legal or illegal.

## Do the AI cops run in single player servers?

The first WHAT TO EXPECT line reads "Single player servers, seperate from the actual game." The line is a fragment: it does not say in so many words that AI cops appear only there. Our reading, not confirmed, is that the AI cops in this announcement belong to single player servers and that "the actual game" means the regular servers. How a player opens such a server is not stated.

The game record we read on 10 October 2026 lists a maximum of 10 players per server, and the game description puts "🚔 RUN FROM COPS" first under "WHAT YOU CAN DO". Neither record says whether cops appear in the 10-player servers. The [how-to-play guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/how-to-play/) covers what the description lists.

## What does the announcement leave out?

After this read, these points are not confirmed:

- the size of a fine and the size of an escape payout;
- what raises or lowers the star level;
- which bikes are "illegal bikes";
- how to enter a single player server, and whether progress or money carries over to what the announcement calls "the actual game";
- whether the rules were changed after the listing was written.

We have not tested these in game. This page reports one announcement, created more than four days before its start time, and the listing does not record what shipped.
