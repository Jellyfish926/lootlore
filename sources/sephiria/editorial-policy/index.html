Editorial Policy of the Sephiria Wiki, Stated Fully

Last updated 2026-09-01

Every guide on this wiki is written under five standing rules. This page states them, so readers can hold pages to them.

Sourcing: announcements first, community second, nothing third

TEAM HORAY's Steam announcements are the only tier treated as settled fact — patch notes are quoted with their version number and date. Community measurements and data audits form the second tier: used, credited to the measurer, and labelled as community work. There is no third tier. A number that circulates with no traceable origin — however many sites repeat it — stays off these pages, and several well-known "Sephiria facts" fail exactly that test.

Conflicts get printed, not adjudicated

When two credible sources disagree, both versions appear with their origins. The standing example: the developer describes "about 300 artifacts" while the most thorough community audit catalogues 236 — our artifacts page carries both numbers and says why neither is treated as wrong. Picking the tidier figure would read better and inform worse.

Beta numbers wear a label

Sephiria spent fifteen months in Early Access, and many values were last publicly adjusted in the 0.9.x–0.10.x era. Those appear here with that provenance stated — "last publicly adjusted in 0.10.14, not reconfirmed for 1.0" — rather than silently promoted to current fact. When a 1.0-era patch touches such a value, the label comes off and the citation updates.

Version stamps are commitments

The stamp at the top of a guide — the patch it was last reviewed against — is a promise that someone re-read the page against that build's public record. Patches like 1.0.26 and 1.0.30, which changed weapon rules and artifact behaviour, trigger passes through every affected page; the patch-notes section additionally records which of our guides each update invalidated, so the correction trail is public.

Advertising pays for hosting and buys nothing else

The site runs third-party display ads. The network picks the creatives; no advertiser previews content, sponsors a page, or learns what we are about to publish. If any page here ever carries paid placement or affiliate links, this policy will say so before the page does — as of the date above, none do.

Corrections and contact

Errors get fixed with the updated date moved, and materially misleading ones get an inline note rather than a silent rewrite. The contact page is the route; a report citing a patch-note line or carrying a screenshot goes to the front of the queue. The wiki is maintained by one person, which is a limitation and also the reason its rules can actually be enforced on every page.

An independent fan archive for Sephiria. Every figure here traces back to a TEAM HORAY patch note or the Steam store page, and where those two disagree both are printed. Nothing is filled in from memory.
