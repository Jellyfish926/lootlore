# Southern Mudding Limiteds: All 22 Robux Vehicle Prices

Southern Mudding sells 22 single vehicles for Robux, priced from 200 to 325, and 20 of them carry "Limited" in the name. On 2 October 2026 Roblox's product data marked every one as for sale. That flag belongs to the product record; it does not tell us whether the in-game shop still shows an older Limited.

This page lists all 22 with the names exactly as the developer registered them, then covers the other paid extras in the same store: gift versions, tornado spawns and one event unlock. Everything comes from the [Roblox developer products API](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/8719555347/developerproducts?limit=100).

## Is an older Limited still on sale?

We cannot confirm it from public data. Every one of Southern Mudding's 61 developer products, including Limiteds from early 2026, has its for-sale flag set to true. The flag belongs to the Roblox product record. Whether the in-game shop still shows an older Limited is controlled by the game itself and is not visible in public data, so the flag is a necessary condition, not proof.

What we will not do is guess when a Limited "comes back". The developer publishes no rotation schedule on Roblox. If a vehicle you want is missing from the in-game shop, the only official signals are the weekly notes in the game description, the weekly event titles and the developer's own channels, which the [community page](/southern-mudding/community/) covers.

## Why do several products share almost the same name?

Because the Southern Mudding developer reuses generic names. Four products are UTVs: (Limited) UTV 2, (Limited) UTV, [Limited!] UTV and [Limited] UTV. Six are 6x6 pickup trucks: 6x6 Pickup Truck, 6x6 Pickup Truck 2, (Limited) 6x6 Pickup Truck 3, (Limited) 6x6 Pickup Truck, [Limited] Pickup Truck 6x6 and [Limited] Off-Road 6x6 Pickup Truck.

Each is a separate product with its own ID, price and creation date, which suggests separate vehicles. The data cannot prove it: without pictures or descriptions, two products could in principle unlock the same model. Match the name and price on the in-game button with the table above before you pay.

The bracket style changed over time. Round brackets, "(Limited)", appear on seven products created between January and July 2026. Square brackets, "[Limited]", appear on eleven, starting on 19 June 2026 and on every vehicle product since 9 July. One product uses "[Limited!]" and one "(Limited Special Offer)". Two early 6x6 pickup trucks have no tag at all.

Compare those with the vehicle packs: the [game pass guide](/southern-mudding/gamepasses/) shows that every pack works out at 145 Robux per vehicle or less. ## Can you buy a vehicle for a friend?

Yes. Each of the 22 has a matching gift product at the same price, created the same day. The names follow the vehicle: [Limited] Gift Storm Chaser Vehicle (229), (Limited) Gift UTV 2 (200), Gift 6x6 Pickup Truck (250), (GIFT Special Offer) Gooseneck Camper + Truck (325), and so on. How you choose the recipient is handled in game and is not described in the data.

## How much does a tornado cost in Southern Mudding?

The store has three products that spawn a tornado for a set time. All three were created on 1 April 2026, two days before an event listing titled "Storm Chasing Update!" started:

| Spawn Tornado 5 Minutes | 200 | 5 | 40 |

The per-minute column is our arithmetic; the longest option is only slightly cheaper per minute. What the tornado does to vehicles, how far it reaches and whether tornadoes also appear without a purchase are not shown in public data. A related product, [Limited] Storm Chaser Vehicle (229 Robux), was created on 31 July 2026, the day the "📡Tornado Chaser Upd!🌪️" event listing started. It is named for chasing storms, but nothing official says it behaves differently in one.

## What is Delivery Event Unlock Now?

A 200-Robux developer product created on 12 February 2026. The product itself has no description, but the developer's event listings give context: "Trucking Event!", subtitled "SM's First Event!", ran from 13 to 20 February 2026 with the description "Deliver cargo to unlock a new truck." The product was created the day before that event started, so it is likely a paid shortcut to the same unlock. That link is our inference from the dates; which truck it was and whether the product still does anything need an in-game check. We list it so the count of 61 products adds up: 22 vehicles, 22 vehicle gifts, 13 pass gifts (12 named as gifts and one probable), three tornado spawns and this one.
