# Bongo Cat Events and Paw Pass: Full Timeline

Bongo Cat runs limited seasonal events that usually last three to four weeks and add 20 exclusive items to the drop pool; when an event ends, those items stop dropping. Since September 1, 2026 there is also the monthly Paw Pass, with free and paid tracks and rewards that never expire. Collab giveaways are listed too.

## How does the Paw Pass work?

The Paw Pass brings new cosmetics every month instead of only at seasonal events. The [launch post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1842212951313382) sets out the rules:

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Changes monthly; the first was Circus, and the October 1, 2026 post says "This month's theme is Halloween" |
| Tracks | Free and premium; the pass has themed skins, hats, emotes and chests up to Legendary, and the paid track has more of them |
| Bongo Coins | 100 per track per pass |
| Spending coins | Not confirmed as open yet: the launch post says coins "can be used later" to buy the premium track for 500 coins or supporter cosmetics |
| Progress | Reaching milestones by playing normally; claiming does not spend taps |
| Deadline | None: "the rewards are claimable forever" |
| Free ticket | Claimed automatically if you open the game at least once a month |
| Offline | You cannot redeem rewards offline, but taps still count |
| Premium | Buy a Paw Pass Ticket in the [Steam Item Store](https://store.steampowered.com/itemstore/3419430/), then redeem it in-game with Get Pass |

The pass opens from the ticket button at the bottom of the inventory. Joining late is not a problem: the developer says you can always get a Paw Pass Ticket to unlock past passes. Events still happen alongside it, and during events both tracks also drop event chests. We have not found an official number of taps per milestone, so this page does not give one; the two lanes, each monthly theme and what the coins can be used for later are broken down in the [Paw Pass and Bongo Coins guide](/bongo-cat/paw-pass/).

## What happens to event items after the event ends?

Each seasonal announcement repeats the same rule: after the end date, "the items will no longer be dropable." They stay in the inventories of players who got them, and the announcements name no other way to get them later, which leaves trading with other players; see [exchange and trading](/bongo-cat/exchange-trading/) for the market rules. Calendars were more forgiving. The advent calendar could be opened until December 31 even for past days, and Deluxe calendar buyers keep receiving rewards after the event.

Event odds can also shift. For the 2025 winter event the developer "moved many items to lower rarity tiers" so players would see more variety, and called it a temporary measure. During the 2025 autumn event, an Uncommon drop was more likely to be an event item, without changing the overall odds on the [hats and skins](/bongo-cat/hats-skins/) page.

## How do you get ready for the next event?

Keep a stock of duplicates. Before the 2025 autumn event the developer warned players not to "trade in ALL your dupli-cats" ([emoji update post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1811772772443374)), and each event post then urges "exchange, exchange, exchange and open your chests" while the event pool is live. Keep the game updated, since several events required the latest version. If you play with friends, the Bongo Royale showed that events can also change [multiplayer lobbies](/bongo-cat/multiplayer/) for a few days.
