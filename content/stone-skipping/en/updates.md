## How often does +1 Stone Skipping update?

Often. Between 10 September and 1 October 2026, within 22 calendar days, new passes or products were created on 12 different days. A creation date proves the item existed by then. It does not prove the item was visible in game that day, and it says nothing about gameplay changes that do not involve the store.

## Is there a pattern to the eggs?

Yes, roughly a weekly one. Rainbow Egg products were created on 10 September and Admin and Pirate Egg products on 12 September. Astronaut followed on 19 September, Chocolatier on 26 September and Dragon on 1 October, so the last four themed eggs sit five to seven days apart.

The timing also tracks the world events. Astronaut Egg products were created the day before the World 3 event started, Chocolatier Egg products the day before the World 4 event, and Dragon Egg products two days before the World 5 event. No listing says that an egg belongs to a world, so the timing is all we can show. Egg prices and bundle savings are worked out in the [eggs and pets guide](/stone-skipping/pets/).

## When did Worlds 2, 3 and 4 arrive?

Two of them can be dated from Roblox's event listings. Besides the upcoming event, the listing has returned two earlier ones:

| Official event title | Listed on (UTC) | Starts (UTC) | Ends (UTC) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World 3 + New Content | 15 September, 14:32 | 20 September, 18:00 | 23 September, 18:00 |
| WORLD 4 + UPDATE | 22 September, 11:53 | 27 September, 16:00 | 1 October, 16:00 |
| ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 5 | 26 September, 09:04 | 3 October, 16:00 | 3 October, 18:00 |

The two earlier events have no description, so each gives a title and a window, not the exact moment a world opened. No event listing names World 2. We found no store product whose name says it skips to a world, so no creation date marks a world; what Golden Skip, Diamond Skip, Golden Zone and Admin Zone do is not stated. One caveat: a default request to the event endpoint usually returns only the upcoming event; a request with a page cursor returned all three every time we tried.
