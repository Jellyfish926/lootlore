# +1 Stone Skipping Eggs and Pets: Robux Prices, Bundles

Pets in +1 Stone Skipping give "powerful boosts", according to the official description, and the store shows they hatch from eggs. It sells seven kinds of egg for Robux: Rainbow at 45, Admin at 175, and Pirate, Astronaut, Chocolatier, Dragon and Witch at 59 each. Every egg also comes in x3 and x8 bundles.

That one line of description is all the developer has published about pets. The rest of this page is store data: names, prices and dates, copied from Roblox on 10 October 2026.

All 21 products were on sale. These are the Robux versions only. Whether the same eggs can also be hatched with currency you earn by playing is not shown in public data and needs an in-game check.

## How much do the x3 and x8 bundles save?

Our arithmetic on the official prices. "Saving" compares the bundle with buying the same number of single eggs.

| Egg | Robux per egg, single | Per egg in x3 | Per egg in x8 | x8 saving |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rainbow Egg | 45.0 | 33.0 | 31.1 | 111 Robux (31%) |
| Admin Egg | 175.0 | 153.0 | 143.6 | 251 Robux (18%) |
| Pirate, Astronaut, Chocolatier, Dragon, Witch | 59.0 | 38.3 | 34.9 | 193 Robux (41%) |

Two things stand out. The themed eggs have the steepest bundle discount, and most of it already arrives at x3: 115 Robux for three is 35% less than three singles. The Admin Egg has the smallest discount. It costs about three times as much as a themed egg as a single and about four times as much in the bundles.

## Why is the Admin Egg so much more expensive?

The store does not say. No official source lists what any egg contains or the odds of each pet. Until the odds are recorded in game, treat any drop table you see elsewhere with caution. The Admin Egg is also the only egg with [GIFT] versions, at the same three prices.

## Which pets and packs are sold directly?

![Store icon: a yellow blocky dog with a jewelled gold crown, a red and white cape and a red sceptre](pet-king-doggy "Official icon of the King Doggy product")

An icon is not a contents list. The Starter Pack icon suggests a pet plus boosts, and the "+1" on the Devil Pack icon looks like the pet pass icon, but neither is confirmed. King Doggy costs more than any single egg bundle except Admin Egg x8.

## How many pets can you hatch and equip?

The base numbers are not published. What the store shows is that both can be raised with game passes. Three passes are named +1 Pet (69 Robux), +3 Pets (195) and +6 Pets (499), which most likely add pet slots. Three more are named Hatch +3 Eggs [STACKS] (25), Hatch +8 Eggs [STACKS] (145) and Hatch +16 Eggs [STACKS] (299), which most likely let you hatch several eggs at once.

Per-unit prices and gift versions for these six are worked out in the [game pass guide](/stone-skipping/gamepasses/).

## Is there a pattern to new eggs?

Five themed eggs were created at gaps of five to seven days: Pirate on 12 September, Astronaut on 19 September, Chocolatier on 26 September, Dragon on 1 October and Witch on 7 October. All five share one price ladder. The Dragon Egg records were created two days before the official World 5 event on 3 October, and the Witch Egg records three days before the start time on the WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE listing; the listings do not connect eggs to worlds. The [updates page](/stone-skipping/updates/) has the full timeline.

## What would we buy?

Our opinion, from prices alone. If you want to try paid eggs in +1 Stone Skipping, a themed x3 at 115 Robux gets most of the bundle discount for a small spend. We would not buy an Admin Egg or King Doggy before the odds and boosts are known. For everything else in the store, see the [full Robux product list](/stone-skipping/shop/).
