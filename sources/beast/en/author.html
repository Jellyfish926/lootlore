Site Information

Author: Jellyfish

Every guide on beastofreincarnation.online is written and edited by Jellyfish, the independent games-content creator who runs this site. This page exists so readers know who stands behind the pages, what the site does and does not claim to be, and how to get something corrected.

What this site is

beastofreincarnation.online is an independent, fan-made guide hub for Beast of Reincarnation — Game Freak's action RPG published by Fictions, released August 4, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. It is not a wiki, not a content farm, and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by GAME FREAK, Fictions, Happinet, Nintendo, Sony, Microsoft or Valve.

The guides are written in English first and adapted into 日本語, Français, Español, Italiano and Deutsch. The localized editions are translations of the English source, not independently generated text.

How the content is checked

  • Source ranking. Publisher materials (Game Freak / Fictions announcements, official store listings, official patch notes) rank highest and are stated as fact. Platform data (Steam global achievement statistics, Metacritic and OpenCritic aggregates) is quoted together with the date it was read, because it moves. Tested community documentation is named inline. Auto-generated wiki content is not used as a source.
  • Dated review notes. Pages carry a visible "last reviewed" line stating the date and, where relevant, the game version the page was checked against. Those dates are also what this site's structured data reports as dateModified.
  • No fabrication. Stats, boss names, quest steps and trophy lists are never invented. Where a claim cannot be traced to a source, the page says it is unconfirmed rather than guessing.
  • Patch-driven updates. Each shipped game update triggers a review of the pages it touches; see the patch notes page for the version history the guides track.

The full rules, including how corrections are logged, live in the editorial policy.

Contact and corrections

Corrections are welcome and are the fastest way to improve a page. Report an error via the contact page or by email to zsn2740784715@gmail.com. Verified corrections are typically applied within 48 hours, and material ones are acknowledged on the page itself.
