    American Plains Mudding (listed on Roblox as "[🌲FOREST🌲] American Plains Mudding") is a vehicle roleplay game by the verified group of the same name. You drive, tow and customize trucks, trailers and off-road machines across mud pits, farm fields and water, and the developer lists a new update for nearly every Saturday.

    About this guide

    The experience was created on 29 July 2021. At 11:13 UTC on 9 October 2026 it showed 714,911,302 visits, 8,238,764 favourites, 377,627 likes against 45,410 dislikes, and 6,750 people playing. This hub covers what the developer has actually published on Roblox: the description, the store, the badges and the event listings.

    What can you do in the game?

    The description gives the list in one sentence: "So grab your friends and take to the mud pits, test your skills with towing, or head to the fields for some farming - from boating and off-roading to creating your own unique builds and setups." It calls the game, which it shortens to APM, "a one-of-a-kind roleplay world built on your suggestions".

    What the public data does not show is how you earn or spend in-game money, what the map's places are called, or how any vehicle performs. We mark those gaps on each page instead of guessing.

    What did the 10/3 update add?

    Four lines, quoted from the description as it stood on 9 October 2026:

    • "New ATV", with three sub-items: "Animated Suspension", "Front and Rear Cargo Box Attachments" and "Windshield Attachment"
    • "New Forest (Map Update Part 1 of 2)"
    • "Sitting In Vehicles Requires Being Whitelisted"
    • "Bug Fixes (Water Flinging Fixed)"

    The game's title prefix matched: "[🌲FOREST🌲]". The update page lines these notes up with the event listing for 3 October.

    When does the game update?

    On Saturdays, going by the developer's own Roblox event listings. All 60 listings on record, from 16 August 2025 to 17 October 2026, start on a Saturday in UTC, and 27 of the 30 with starts since 28 March 2026 begin between 13:45 and 14:15 UTC. As of 9 October 2026, the next one is "APM Map Update 🗺️", listed for Saturday 10 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC. A listing is a schedule, not proof of when the game changed.

    What can you buy with Robux?

    Eight game passes, from 195 to 490 Robux, which together cost 2,470 Robux. Four change spawning: 4 Vehicle Spawner, 4 Trailer Spawner, Any Slot Spawner and Anywhere Trailer Spawner. Three unlock premium content: Premium Vehicles, Premium Trailers and Premium Customization. One, Premium Private Servers, adds commands and doubles spawn slots in private servers.

    The store also holds 11 developer products, all named "[GIFT]". Eight are gift versions of those passes at the same price. Three are gifts of limited holiday vehicles. The game pass guide has every price, the spawn slot guide explains the four-to-eight slot system, and the limited vehicle page covers the five holiday passes that were off sale when we checked.

    How do you get the Hidden Vehicle badges?

    By finding hidden vehicles, according to the badge text: "Congrats, you have found a Hidden Vehicle." There are four separate badges with that name and a fifth called Hidden Trailer. Together the four Hidden Vehicle badges had been awarded 5,765,575 times by 9 October 2026. Roblox's data gives no locations, and we have not confirmed any in game, so the badge guide sticks to counts and dates.

    Why is there no codes page?

    Because no official source we could read lists a code. The game description and the group description carry none, and the group has no shout. The group wall request returned an error, and the game's social links need a signed-in, age-verified account. What the description does offer is a free vehicle: "Like 👍 and Favorite ⭐ the game to obtain a vehicle!". Our community page covers the group, the codes question and what we could not confirm.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who made American Plains Mudding?
    The Roblox group American Plains Mudding, which carries Roblox's verified badge and is owned by the account Bebsharky. The group had 3,699,198 members on 9 October 2026. See the community page.
    When does American Plains Mudding update?
    We found no fixed update day in the Roblox sources we could read, but all 60 of the developer's Roblox event listings start on a Saturday. As of 9 October 2026, the next is listed for Saturday 10 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC. See the update schedule.
    Are there American Plains Mudding codes?
    We found no code in the game description or the group description, and the group has no shout, so we list none. The description does say "Like 👍 and Favorite ⭐ the game to obtain a vehicle!". Details are on the community page.
    How many players fit in a server?
    Fifteen, according to the Roblox listing read on 9 October 2026.
    Is American Plains Mudding free?
    Yes. The listing has no entry price. Robux purchases are optional: eight game passes on sale and 11 gift products, covered in the game pass guide.
