Where our content comes from

Every guide starts from the sources described in our editorial policy: official store pages and developer announcements first, platform data such as Steam achievement statistics second, and our own play sessions in the current build of the game third. Community material — wikis, forum threads, videos — is treated as a lead to check, never republished as fact on its own. Where software tools (including AI-assisted drafting) help write or translate a page, a person still verifies every fact against a source before it publishes and is responsible for the result.

How we verify, and what "unverified" means

A claim is verified when it traces to an official source, to platform data, or to something we confirmed ourselves in-game. When a detail is reported by players but has not cleared that bar — a rumoured drop rate, a community-estimated number nobody official has confirmed — the page says so in plain language rather than presenting it as settled fact; some pages label such figures explicitly as unconfirmed or community-reported. That label is not a hedge to avoid work, it is the honest state of the information: treat anything marked this way as a starting point for your own testing, not a guarantee.

Accuracy is dated, not permanent

We date our reviews and record the game version checked, but games are moving targets. Numbers, mechanics, prices and availability can change with any patch, regional storefront or platform difference, often faster than any guide site can track. Treat every guide as accurate as of its stated review date, not as a standing promise about the game's current state — if the live game disagrees with a page, the game is right and we want to hear about it through the contact page.

Purchases and opinions

Any opinion on whether a game is worth buying, or which edition to choose, is exactly that — an opinion, formed at a stated price and time, not financial or purchasing advice. Check the current price, platform, edition contents and regional availability on the official store before buying anything.

Advertising and affiliate links

This site is funded by display advertising served through an external ad network; that network chooses which ads appear, we choose the words on the page, and the two never meet — no advertiser previews a page before it publishes and no placement is sold against specific content. Where a page contains an affiliate link (a link that could earn the site a commission on a purchase), it is marked as such near the link itself. An affiliate relationship never changes a verdict: a game or product we would not recommend stays not-recommended whether or not a link on the page is affiliated.

How often this changes

This page is reviewed whenever our sourcing practices, advertising setup or affiliate relationships change, and at minimum whenever the editorial policy it references is revised. The "Last reviewed" date at the top reflects the most recent check, not a fixed schedule.
