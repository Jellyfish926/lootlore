2:Tb1b,<p>DragonSword Guide covers <strong>DragonSword: Awakening</strong> — mainly its combat system, because that is the part players consistently get wrong and the part most guides describe inaccurately.</p>
<h2>Why we started with combat</h2>
<p>DragonSword: Awakening runs on a tag-team model: Active Skills apply Status Ailments, Signal Skills detonate them, and Switching Signals hand the chain between Heroes. Play it like a normal action RPG and it still functions — you just do a fraction of the damage, and nothing on screen tells you that is what is happening.</p>
<p>Most of the coverage we found treated it as a standard party game with an unusual skill naming scheme. It is not, and that gap is why this site exists.</p>
<h2>Where our information comes from</h2>
<p>At the foot of every guide sits a <strong>Sources checked</strong> box naming exactly which materials that page leans on. Our evidence ranking, in order: Hound13 Inc.'s published material and the Steam listing first, community documentation second and labelled as such, our own play third and labelled as such.</p>
<p>This matters more than usual for DragonSword: Awakening. It released on 22 July 2026, and community Hero lists <strong>disagree with each other</strong> on ability details. When two sources conflict and neither is the developer, we say they conflict rather than picking the one that reads better.</p>
<h2>What we will not print</h2>
<p>Numbers we cannot trace. That means no full recipe table, no complete per-Hero ability lists, and no tier ranking. Tier lists in particular are the easiest page on this site to write and the hardest to defend — a confident ranking built on unverified kit data is just an opinion wearing a table.</p>
<h2>Who writes it</h2>
<p>Independent players, not Hound13 Inc. No review copies, no early access, no editorial input from the studio. This is not an official channel and is not endorsed by them.</p>
<h2>Sending a correction</h2>
<p>Email <strong>hello@dragonsword.site</strong> or use the <a href="/contact/">contact page</a>. Include the page and ideally where the correct information comes from. Corrections take priority over everything else we do, because a wrong page costs a reader more than a missing one.</p>

Commercial arrangements do not affect what goes in a guide; pages carrying affiliate links disclose it. What advertising stores on your device is set out in the <a href="/privacy-policy/">privacy policy</a>.</p>
