3:T22ba,<p>Sephiria supports <strong>online co-op for up to four players</strong>, with item trading and reviving. It is a genuinely different game with a group — but there are three things worth knowing before you organise a session, and one of them is a limitation TEAM HORAY has publicly acknowledged rather than fixed.</p>
<figure class="fig"><img src="/images/sephiria-hero-cloud-sea-1200.webp" srcset="/images/sephiria-hero-cloud-sea-800.webp 800w, /images/sephiria-hero-cloud-sea-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, 720px" alt="A bunny overlooking the cloud sea from the tower in Sephiria" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Official screenshot — Sephiria, TEAM HORAY</figcaption></figure>
<h2>Getting into a game</h2>
<p>Two routes:</p>
<p><strong>Public lobbies.</strong> Create or browse from the multiplayer UI. A refresh button was added in patch 0.10.5. The lobby list shows each room's game version.</p>
<p><strong>Private lobbies.</strong> Steam friend invite, or a <strong>join code</strong>. You read the code by <strong>interacting with the tablets in the lobby</strong> — this is not signposted well and is the most common "where is the code" question.</p>
<p>Between the two, players who hit connection trouble consistently report that <strong>Steam friend invites are markedly more stable than join codes</strong> — one thread describes first-floor failures as common on codes and largely gone after switching to invites. If your group is on each other's friends lists anyway, use invites and skip the codes entirely.</p>
<p>One planning constraint: there is <strong>no mid-run join</strong>. The party you leave the lobby with is the party for the whole run — a friend who arrives twenty minutes late, or a player who drops, cannot be slotted in without abandoning and restarting. (Reported by third-party guides rather than official documentation, but no player report contradicts it.)</p>
<p>Everyone gathers in the <strong>Assembly Space</strong>, which has a training dummy for checking damage. Chat is on Enter.</p>
<h2>Progress is per-player, not per-host</h2>
<p>This is the good news, and it has been true since patch 0.5.0: <em>"Destiny imprint progress is now individualized."</em> Your stats, your unlocked items, your weapon, your costume and your name all come with you. Sapphire counts persist through multiplayer sessions. Chest of Possibilities rolls and their escalating costs are private per player.</p>
<p>You are not playing on someone else's save. You are bringing your own character into their run.</p>
<h2>Chapter gating, and the bugs it caused</h2>
<p>You are not supposed to join a lobby ahead of your own story progress, and this has leaked repeatedly:</p>
<ul><li><strong>0.11.3</strong> warned that clearing Chapter 5 while still on Chapter 4 could hard-block your progression.</li><li><strong>0.11.4</strong> auto-reset the bogus Chapter 5 clear record on login.</li><li><strong>1.0.24</strong> fixed both mismatched-progress invites and a case where a Chapter 5 player was softlocked by clearing the game inside a Chapter 6 lobby.</li></ul>
<p>The practical rule: <strong>play with people at a similar chapter.</strong> The developer has patched three separate variants of this, which suggests the underlying assumption runs deep in the code.</p>
<p>It also explains the common complaint that public lobbies look empty — rooms only list while the host is still in the lobby, and players may only see lobbies matching their own chapter progress.</p>
<h2>How difficulty scales</h2>
<p>Originally, monster <strong>HP</strong> scaled with player count. That was changed: now the <strong>monster count</strong> increases, and multiplayer monster HP was slightly reduced. That is a meaningfully better design for a game about crowd control — more targets rather than spongier ones.</p>
<p>Hard Mode's <strong>Festival of Blood</strong> got its own multiplayer scaling in 1.0.21, because enemies healing 15% every time they hit <em>someone</em> does not divide well across four players. Healing effectiveness now runs at <strong>66% with 2 players, 50% with 3, 33% with 4</strong>.</p>
<h2>The ping problem</h2>
<p>This is the honest caveat. TEAM HORAY has stated that Sephiria uses <strong>Steam's built-in multiplayer transport, which has a ceiling around 5 Mbps</strong>, and that late-game item volume exceeds it. The symptom is <strong>ping spikes into the 200–2000 ms range</strong> as runs get deep and inventories fill.</p>
<p>Players report it as region-dependent, with European sessions worst, and there are cases of 240 ms between two people on the same local network. There was no complete fix at the time of writing.</p>
<p>If you are planning a long co-op session, this is the thing that will end it — not difficulty.</p>
<h2>Other known issues</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Linux / Proton.</strong> Players have been unable to create or join rooms under Proton 9. Switching to Proton 10 resolved it.</li><li><strong>Duplicate items not merging.</strong> Frequently reported as a co-op bug; it is not. Duplicates only merge and upgrade for players who have taken <strong>Wisdom level 10</strong>, which unlocks [Unique] Stacking. Without it you simply get two separate copies.</li><li><strong>Kicked players cannot rejoin.</strong> Clients now also leave when the host's game ends, and the "Leave Lobby" button is disabled during exploration.</li></ul>
<h2>Two open bugs worth knowing before a long session</h2>
<p><strong>Desync after lag spikes.</strong> A player whose connection hiccups can come back visibly out of position — walking through walls or standing outside the arena from everyone else's point of view — and the drift does not correct on revive or even on moving to the next floor. The report thread proposes host-view resyncs at those moments; nothing shipped yet as of this page's date.</p>
<p><strong>The interaction freeze.</strong> The nastier one: mid-run, one player can suddenly move but not attack, interact, or see teammates' actions, and the state persists until the next area. Reports cluster around use of the anvil and the root upgrade stations. This one has developer eyes on it — TEAM HORAY's MegurinE replied in the thread asking players to send video and logs to the studio's email — so it is acknowledged, just not yet fixed.</p>
<p>Neither bug is common enough to make co-op not worth playing. But if you are attempting deep <a href="/guides/hard-mode/">Hard Mode</a> clears with a group, the freeze bug is an argument for banking progress in shorter sessions rather than one marathon — and co-op is otherwise the <em>easier</em> way to chase the Root's Retreat achievements, since revives buy back the mistakes that end a solo attempt outright.</p>
<h2>What co-op actually changes about a run</h2>
<p>Beyond the obvious: with four players you can <strong>trade items</strong>, which turns the <a href="/guides/combo-effects/">combo system</a> from a solo lottery into a negotiation. If one player is two <a href="/artifacts/artifacts-and-tablets/">artifacts</a> short of a threshold and another is holding a duplicate they cannot use, that trade is worth more than either item's raw stats.</p>
<p>Reviving fallen teammates also changes the risk calculus — a solo mistake ends the run, a co-op mistake costs the group tempo. Groups can afford to take Derangement and Mini Boss nodes that a solo player should skip.</p>
<h2>Finding people</h2>
<p>Public lobby browsing has the visibility problems described above. The community's recommended route is the Discord's looking-for-group channel, which is where most organised groups actually form.</p>
<h2>Checked against 1.0.30</h2>
<p>Three co-op rules changed after this page was first written, all in the 13–20 August patch burst. <strong>Revive HP (1.0.26):</strong> HP you gain above your maximum from another player's revive — the game calls it temporary HP — no longer vanishes the moment you heal, but <em>all</em> of it is now lost whenever your maximum HP changes, so a Green Heart room or an HP artifact pickup will wipe it. <strong>Chapter 6 in multiplayer (1.0.26 / 1.0.29):</strong> rejoining a lobby after the final boss was defeated no longer blocks progression, and the freeze that could follow a game over in a Chapter 6 lobby is fixed in 1.0.29. <strong>Lobby names (1.0.30)</strong> are capped at 64 characters. Superconductor users also now get a notification in multiplayer when the special cannot fire for lack of Storm Clouds (1.0.26).</p>
<h2>Not verified</h2>
<p>We have no confirmed figure for what the item-trading interface will and will not let you hand over, and no developer statement on whether a transport change is planned. The bandwidth ceiling is quoted from TEAM HORAY's own explanation in the Steam discussions, not inferred from testing.</p>5:["category","guides","d"]
