Site Information

About This Site

beastofreincarnation.online is an independent, fan-made guide hub dedicated to Beast of Reincarnation — Game Freak's action RPG published by Fictions, released August 4, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, and covered here from announcement through the live patch cycle.

Who runs this site

This site is curated and edited by Jellyfish, an independent games-content creator, with the goal of building the most useful English, Japanese, French, Spanish, Italian and German resource for Beast of Reincarnation players. We are a small independent project — not a content farm, and not affiliated with any publisher.

Our editorial standards

  • Sources first: pages are built from publisher announcements, official store listings, Game Freak's patch notes, Steam's public achievement statistics, and named editorial guide teams whose data we can cross-check. Auto-generated wiki content is never a source. Each guide lists what it was checked against.
  • Post-launch discipline: since the August 4, 2026 release, the core guides — bosses, trophies, walkthrough, performance, review — have been rewritten around live-game data: real aggregate scores, real global completion rates, and the actual patch history. Nothing on this site still asks you to wait for content.
  • No fabrication: we never invent stats, boss names, quest steps or trophy lists. When we have caught our own errors — a speculative boss order, an unofficial item name — the fixed pages say so openly.
  • Corrections: spot an error? Contact us — verified corrections are typically applied within 48 hours, and material ones are acknowledged on the page. The full rules live in our editorial policy.

Update schedule

The site tracks the game's patch cycle: each update (three shipped in the first month — see the patch notes page) triggers a review of the pages it touches, and moving numbers like Steam ratings and achievement percentages are re-read and re-dated when cited. English is updated first; the five localized editions follow, translated from the English source rather than generated independently.

Languages

All guides are written natively in English and adapted into 日本語, Français, Español, Italiano and Deutsch, using official localized terminology wherever the publisher provides it.
