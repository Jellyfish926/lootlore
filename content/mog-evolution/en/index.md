# +1 Mog Evolution Guide: Appeal, Ascend and Robux Shop

+1 Mog Evolution is a free Roblox clicker made by the group Navoj Mog. Every click earns Appeal, you upgrade your hammer towards Bonesmash for more, you earn Wins to ascend into a stronger body, and you climb the leaderboard to become a "True Adam". This hub covers the loop, the progression steps and every Robux product.

Every number on this site comes from Roblox's own data for the game and shows the date we checked it. On 1 October 2026 the experience had passed 31.7 million visits and 344,000 favourites, with 265,568 likes against 5,291 dislikes, about 98% positive.

## How popular is +1 Mog Evolution?

Very, for a game that is one month old. Roblox counted 7,523 players online when we checked on 1 October 2026, and the Navoj Mog group had 5.46 million members. Its title currently starts with "[W3]", and an official event starts on 3 October. The developer also runs a small test copy, explained on the [community page](/mog-evolution/community/).

## How does progress work?

The description names four steps: clicks give Appeal, the hammer upgrades to Bonesmash, Wins let you ascend, and the leaderboard leads to True Adam. The store fills in the rest. Rebirth normally needs you to reach a "level cap", ascending moves you to "the next body", and paid skips exist for Worlds 2 and 3. The [progression guide](/mog-evolution/progression/) lines these up and marks which thresholds are still unpublished.

## Which boosts does the store sell?

Four kinds of boost, all sold as developer products rather than game passes: an Auto Clicker at 22 clicks per second for 39 Robux, VIP at 10x Appeal for 99 Robux, nine Power multipliers from x1.5 to x256, and nine treadmill belts from x2 to x999. The [Appeal guide](/mog-evolution/appeal/) ranks them by value, and the [limited unlocks page](/mog-evolution/limiteds/) covers the five 399-Robux items such as the Gigachad body and Claviculars Hammer.

## Why is there no codes page?

Because we could not see a single code in an official source. The game description has none, the Navoj Mog group has no description and no pinned shout, and the event listing has none. Other fan sites show zero active codes as well. The [community page](/mog-evolution/community/) explains where official codes would appear.

## How this guide uses its sources

This is an unofficial fan guide. Facts come first from what Navoj Mog has published on Roblox: the game description, its places, its developer products, its event listing and the group page. We do not list codes, body requirements, Appeal-per-click values or Ranked rules until an official source confirms them. Buying suggestions are our advice, not rules of the game.
