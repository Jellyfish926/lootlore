3:Tdc8,<h2>A weapon is a tree you commit to, not an item you find</h2>
<p>The first thing to understand about Sephiria's weapons is that you do not really pick one per run — you pick which of six upgrade trees you want to feed. Two players on the same class can finish a run with completely different damage profiles depending on which branches the Anvil offered and which ones they took.</p>
<p>That reframes the usual question. "Which weapon is strongest" has no useful answer. "Which tree do I have deep enough to survive <a href="/bosses/chapters-and-bosses/">chapter 4</a>" does.</p>
<h2>Five of the six are locked</h2>
<p>You start with Sword &amp; Shield. Great Sword, Dagger, Crossbow, Blade and Staff each require two separate things: buying the node in the <a href="/guides/destiny-inscription/">Destiny Inscription</a> tree, <strong>and</strong> completing a training quest with <strong>Moro</strong> in the village afterwards. Buying the node alone does nothing, which is the single most common point of confusion for <a href="/guides/beginner/">new players</a>.</p>
<p>That means your first stretch of the game is an economy problem rather than a combat one. Sapphires come from dying, scaled to how deep you reached, so early runs that end on floor 2 are still productive.</p>
<h2>What you want, and where to look</h2>

<h2>Upgrades replace, they do not add</h2>
<p>This is the part that surprises people coming from other roguelites. A large share of Sephiria's weapon upgrades do not improve a number — they swap out your normal attack, your dash attack or your special entirely. Whirlwind becomes Reassemble or Wound Explosion. Parry becomes a five-dagger throw. The Blade's special becomes Ice Sliding.</p>
<p>So an upgrade choice is closer to picking a subclass mid-run, and taking two upgrades that pull in different directions leaves you with neither.</p>
<h2>The Anvil economy</h2>
<p>Upgrades come from Anvil nodes, and anvils are gated by stage progression — skip them early and later anvils can stop appearing at all. Every experienced player's advice reduces to the same sentence: rush every anvil you see and get to max tier as fast as you can. Anvil offers reroll with dice, and a rerolled option will not come back.</p>
<p>You can plan without spending a run. The in-game <strong>Journal → Weapons tab</strong> shows every path, and the <strong>Comprehensive Training Grounds</strong> has an anvil next to the guard NPC where you can walk an entire tree for free.</p>
