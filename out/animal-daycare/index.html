    Animal Daycare (Anomaly) is a Roblox survival game from the group Day Dreams Games, created on 14 August 2026. You staff a daycare for animal children: inspect every arrival, turn away impostors, feed and play with the kids, keep them calm, then survive the night. This hub covers shifts, badges and the Robux shop from official data.

    How rare is each badge?

    The five Animal Daycare badges are the best public measure of how rare each milestone is. First Day on the Job, for surviving the first night, had been awarded about 3.5 million times since the badges were created on 16 September. For every 100 of those, Still Standing (two nights) had been awarded 61 times, Night Shift Veteran (five shifts) fewer than 5 times, and Daycare Legend (ten shifts) about once in 300. These are badge totals, not a tracked group of players. The badge guide has the exact figures.

    What can you buy with Robux?

    Animal Daycare has no game passes, so nothing permanent is sold as a pass. The 25 Robux products are mostly items for a shift (Lunchbox, Water Gun, coffee refill, Toy Hammer), revives, Lamb Coins and class level unlocks, plus gifts, shop helpers, Boss and a Starter Pack. Lamb Coins get cheaper per coin as the pack grows: the 2,500 pack works out about 39% cheaper per coin than the 50 pack. The shop guide lists every product.

    What has changed since launch?

    We found no patch notes from Day Dreams Games in Roblox's public data, but every Robux product and badge carries the date it was created. Read together, those dates sketch the game's update history. A creation date is when an item was registered, which is not always the day an update was released.

    Why is there no codes page?

    Because we could not confirm a single code from Day Dreams Games. The game description, the group description and the group shout carry no codes, and the game's social links need a Roblox sign-in to read. We would rather leave the page out than send you to type in codes that may never have existed. If an official code appears, it will get its own page.

    How this guide uses its sources

    This is an unofficial fan guide. Facts come first from what Day Dreams Games has published on Roblox: the game description, badges and Robux products. Gameplay videos show far more — specific impostor tells and night threats — but we hold those back until we can confirm them. Buying suggestions are our advice, not a rule of the game.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who made Animal Daycare (Anomaly)?
    The Roblox group Day Dreams Games, owned by the account nevod4u. The experience was created on 14 August 2026 — see game info.
    How many players can join a server?
    Up to 25 per server, according to the Roblox listing. Private servers were not enabled when we checked on 30 September 2026.
    Are there Animal Daycare codes?
    We could not find any code in the game description or the Roblox group, so we do not list any. Official social links need a Roblox sign-in to read.
    Does Animal Daycare have game passes?
    No. The game-pass list was empty on 30 September 2026. Everything paid is a Robux product — see the shop guide.
    Which badge is the rarest?
    Daycare Legend, for surviving 10 shifts. It had been awarded 11,650 times, about 1 for every 300 First Day on the Job badges — see all badges.
