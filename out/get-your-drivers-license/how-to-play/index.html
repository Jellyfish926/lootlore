    How to Play Get Your Driver's License!: Line to Car

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Features named in the official game description and details given in the game pass and developer product descriptions on 2026-10-10 (UTC); written test questions, the course layout, car names and the full ending list are not published and are not covered

    Get Your Driver's License!

    Quick facts

    Free Roblox game by the group Time Will Pass. Its official description lists: take a number and wait your turn, pass the written test, drive the test course, win 1 of 18 cars, unlock every ending, be the Examiner.

    Key points

    • The official description lists its features in this order: line, written test, driving test, cars, endings. It is a feature list and does not spell out the sequence of a run.
    • Store descriptions add detail: the line is counted in places, the waiting room wait is measured in minutes and seconds, and the wording of Retake Test suggests a failed driving test would otherwise mean the line again.
    • Other players can affect your wait. Two product descriptions say people in the line go poof, without saying what that does, and one mentions being knocked out.
    • The game is free to play. Skips cost 9 to 48 Robux and no description says a purchase is required, but we have not played a full run without paying.

    The official description of Get Your Driver's License! lists what you do: take a number and wait your turn, pass the written test, and drive a test course with cows, ducks and ramps. It also lists winning 1 of 18 cars and unlocking endings. The game is free to play, and the store sells skips for 9 to 48 Robux.

    That summary follows the developer's description on Roblox line by line. The description is a feature list, not a manual, so this guide also reads the store listings, which say more about how each stage works than the description does.

    What does the developer say you do?

    The description opens with "Wait in line and get your Driver's License!" and closes with "Can you get your license?" Six feature lines sit between them.

    Official lineWhat it tells you
    Take a number and wait your turnThere is a queue, and you take a number for it
    Pass the written testThere is a written test, and passing it is the goal
    Drive the test course: cows, ducks, ramps and moreThe driving test is an obstacle course with animals and jumps
    Win 1 of 18 cars, from rusty hatchbacks to secret supercarsThere are 18 cars, and you can win one
    Unlock every ending, from Honor Roll to TowedThere are several endings, and the wording invites you to unlock them all
    Be the Examiner and judge other players' drivingPlayers can take the examiner's seat

    The right-hand column is our plain reading of each line. It adds no numbers, because the description gives only one: 18 cars. The description is a list of features. It does not state the order of a run or say that every run wins a car.

    What do the store listings add?

    Each of the 13 developer products has a one-line official description, and several of them give away how a stage works.

    Part of the gameProduct descriptionWhat it suggests
    The line"Jump 5 places ahead in the line."Your position is counted in places
    The waiting room"Skip 2:30 of the wait in the waiting room."Part of the wait runs on a timer
    Written test"Pass the written test straight away."The written test takes time or can go wrong
    Driving test"Go straight to your driving test."The driving test is where this skip takes you
    After a fail"Failed your driving test? Take it again right away, no line."The driving test can be failed, and the wording implies a fail normally sends you back to the line
    Reward"Ticket #001: your next car is Epic or better."Cars come in tiers, and Epic is not the top one

    The third column is inference from the wording, not a published rule. The line guide has prices for every skip.

    What should your first run look like?

    Our suggestion is to play it straight once. The order below follows the description's feature list. We have not played a full run without paying, so read it as a plan and not as a walkthrough.

    1. Take your number and stay in the line. You learn how long the wait is in your server only by sitting through it once.
    2. Sit the written test yourself. Skip Test costs 29 Robux and passes it for you, but you cannot judge the test before you have seen it.
    3. Drive the course slowly. The description names cows, ducks and ramps "and more". We have not mapped the course, so we cannot tell you where they are.
    4. Look at the car and the ending you get. Those are the two rewards the description names. Write both down if you plan to collect them all.
    5. Only then think about skips. If a second wait feels too long, the cheapest products are 9 Robux each.

    What the test asks, and how the examiner scores a drive, are covered as far as official sources go in the driving test guide.

    Can other players interfere with your run?

    Several store listings are aimed at other players. Two mention the line: Kill ("The person ahead of you in line goes poof.") for 9 Robux and Kill All ("Everyone waiting in line goes poof.") for 160. Neither says what going poof does to a player. Scare All puts "A jumpscare on everyone's screen." for 24. A fourth product, Revenge ⚡, reads "Get back at whoever knocked you out." So players can be knocked out, though no listing says how.

    Three of the five game pass descriptions also name other players: "Put someone in time out.", "Pick people up and throw them." and "Bonk people with the ban hammer." The other two are "A very loud airhorn." and an emoji pack. None of the five mentions the test. Prices are in the game pass list.

    How long does a run take?

    We found no official statement. Two hints exist. Skip Time [SALE] removes 2:30 from the waiting room, which suggests the timed part of the wait is at least that long. And Rolimon's, a third-party statistics site, showed an average playtime of 10.06 minutes when we looked on 9 October 2026. That is a tracker's estimate of a whole visit, not an official figure for one run.

    Is Get Your Driver's License! free, and who can play?

    It is free to play. Roblox's record for the game on 10 October 2026 shows the following.

    SettingValue
    PriceFree
    Players per server25
    Content maturityMinimal ("Suitable for everyone")
    Supported languages18, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean
    GenreSimulation · Idle

    A server holds 25 players. The developer does not say how the line is filled, so we do not turn that number into a line length. Which devices the game supports, and whether private servers are offered, could not be read from public data.

    What does this guide leave out?

    Four things players ask about have no official source that we could read: the written test questions and answers, the layout of the course, the names of the 18 cars, and the endings between Honor Roll and Towed. We do not fill those gaps with guesses. The car guide and the driving test guide list exactly what is published and what still has to be checked in the game.
