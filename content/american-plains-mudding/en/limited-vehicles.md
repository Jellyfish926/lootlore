# American Plains Mudding Limited Vehicles: Holiday Passes

American Plains Mudding has five game passes named for limited holiday vehicles: one for Halloween 2023 and one each for Christmas, Easter, Halloween and Thanksgiving. None was on sale on 9 October 2026. Four of the descriptions say the vehicle is only available near its holiday and that you keep it forever.

This page records what Roblox's store data says about each one. It cannot tell you which vehicle a pass unlocks: the records have a name, a one-line description and dates, and nothing else.

## Which limited vehicles exist?

| Pass | Official description | Created | On sale 9 Oct 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 Halloween Vehicle | First-ever limited vehicle! 2023 Limited Halloween Vehicle. | 19 Oct 2023 | No |
| Limited Christmas Vehicle | Only available near Christmas. Get it while you can! (You will keep this vehicle forever) | 14 Dec 2023 | No |
| Limited Easter Vehicle | Only available near Easter. Get it while you can! (You will keep this vehicle forever) | 25 Mar 2024 | No |
| Limited Halloween Vehicle | Only available near Halloween. Get it while you can! (You will keep this vehicle forever) | 24 Oct 2025 | No |
| Limited Thanksgiving Vehicle | Only available near Thanksgiving. Get it while you can! (You will keep this vehicle forever) | 27 Nov 2025 | No |

An off-sale pass shows no price in the data, so we cannot say what any of the five cost while it was on sale. They sit alongside the eight passes on sale on 9 October 2026, which are in the [game pass guide](/american-plains-mudding/gamepasses/).

## How close to the holiday does each pass appear?

Each record was created shortly before its holiday. Creation is not the same as going on sale, but it is the only date the data gives.

| Pass | Record created | Holiday | Days before |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 Halloween Vehicle | 19 Oct 2023 | 31 Oct 2023 | 12 |
| Limited Christmas Vehicle | 14 Dec 2023 | 25 Dec 2023 | 11 |
| Limited Easter Vehicle | 25 Mar 2024 | 31 Mar 2024 | 6 |
| Limited Halloween Vehicle | 24 Oct 2025 | 31 Oct 2025 | 7 |
| Limited Thanksgiving Vehicle | 27 Nov 2025, 01:30 UTC | 27 Nov 2025 | 0 |

The Thanksgiving record was created at 01:30 UTC, which is the evening of 26 November in US time zones, the night before the holiday. So the records were created from the day before to 12 days ahead of the holiday; when each pass actually went on sale is not published.

## Will the Halloween vehicle return in 2026?

The developer has not said. Here is what the official data shows as of 9 October 2026:

- The Limited Halloween Vehicle pass exists, has no year in its name, and says "Only available near Halloween."
- It was off sale on 9 October 2026, 22 days before Halloween.
- In 2025 an event listing for Saturday 25 October read "New Halloween vehicles. Fall Map. And more!", one day after the pass record was created.
- The 2026 listings we read on 9 October run to Saturday 17 October, and neither upcoming one mentions Halloween. The two Saturdays after that are 24 and 31 October.

There is one piece of evidence that a holiday pass can come back. The Limited Christmas Vehicle pass dates from December 2023, yet its gift product was created on 12 December 2025, two years later. A new gift product for an old pass suggests the pass was offered again that December. That is our inference, not a statement from the developer.

If you want the Halloween vehicle, the practical step is to check the game's passes in the last two weeks of October. The [update schedule](/american-plains-mudding/updates/) shows when each Saturday listing starts.

## What do the gift versions cost?

Three of the five have a developer product named as their gift. These are the only prices on record for any limited vehicle.

| Gift product | Robux | Created | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [GIFT] Limited Halloween Vehicle | 145 | 25 Oct 2025 | (none) |
| [GIFT] Limited Thanksgiving Vehicle | 125 | 27 Nov 2025 | Only available near Thanksgiving. Get it while you can! (You will keep this vehicle forever) |
| [GIFT] Limited Christmas Vehicle | 110 | 12 Dec 2025 | Only available near Christmas. Get it while you can! (You will keep this vehicle forever) |

Two cautions. First, Roblox's data marked all three gift products as for sale on 9 October 2026 even though the passes themselves were not. That flag does not show whether the in-game shop offers them out of season, and we have not checked in game. Second, for the eight regular passes the gift price equals the pass price exactly. If the limited ones follow the same rule, the passes cost 145, 125 and 110 Robux when on sale, but nothing official confirms it.

The 2023 Halloween Vehicle and the Limited Easter Vehicle have no gift product.

## Do you keep a limited vehicle?

The descriptions say so in as many words: "(You will keep this vehicle forever)". That line is on the Christmas, Easter, Halloween and Thanksgiving passes. The 2023 Halloween Vehicle description does not include it.

This fits how passes work in general. [Roblox's Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/passes) describe a pass as "a one-time Robux fee to access special privileges inside your game". "Limited" here describes the sale window, not how long you own the vehicle.

## Why are there two Halloween passes?

Because the first one carries its year. "2023 Halloween Vehicle" is described as the "First-ever limited vehicle!", and its record dates from 19 October 2023. "Limited Halloween Vehicle" was created two years later, on 24 October 2025, with the same wording as the other seasonal passes.

Whether they unlock two different vehicles is not stated. The 2025 listing does say "New Halloween vehicles", in the plural. No Halloween pass record dates from 2024, though that does not prove nothing was sold that year.
