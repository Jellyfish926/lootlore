# Deep Fishing Enchants: Enchant Scroll vs Enchant Stones

Deep Fishing's Roblox store has no item called an Enchant Scroll. It sells the Enchant Stone, from 49 Robux, and the Ultra Enchant Stone, from 199 Robux; the only scroll on sale is the Ultra Mutation Scroll. As of 10 October 2026, none of the official Roblox channels we checked explains how enchanting works or what any enchant does.

That leaves two honest answers to "how do you get one". You can buy stones with Robux at the prices below, which come straight from the developer's own product list. At least one fan site also claims that stones can drop from long casts; none of the channels we checked (the Roblox game page description, developer products, game passes, badges, the group description and the events page) says so, and we mark it as not confirmed.

## Is there an Enchant Scroll in Deep Fishing?

Not under that name in anything the developer has registered on Roblox. On 10 October 2026 the game had 103 store products; eight have "Enchant" in the name and four have "Scroll", and no product has both. The game description, the nine [game passes](/deep-fishing/gamepasses/) and the 13 [badges](/deep-fishing/badges/) do not mention enchanting at all.

| Item | Single price (Robux) | Record created | What the listing confirms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| x1 Enchant Stone | 49 | 6 Sep 2026 | Name and price only |
| x1 Ultra Enchant Stone | 199 | 6 Sep 2026 | Name and price only |
| x1 Ultra Mutation Scroll | 129 | 6 Sep 2026 | Name and price only |

All three records were created within half an hour of each other on the same day, which is probably why the names get blended into "enchant scroll". An item with that exact name could still exist in play without being sold for Robux. We have no official text or capture of our own that shows one, so we do not describe it.

Scrolls belong to mutations, not enchants, as far as the names go. Mutated fish and the items around them are covered in the [rarity and mutations guide](/deep-fishing/rarity/).

## How do you get Enchant Stones?

The confirmed route is the Robux shop. Each grade is sold in four bundle sizes, and the listing gives a name and a price with an empty description field. The per-stone column is our own division, rounded to one decimal.

| Product | Robux | Per stone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| x1 Enchant Stone | 49 | 49.0 |
| x3 Enchant Stones | 129 | 43.0 |
| x10 Enchant Stones | 399 | 39.9 |
| x50 Enchant Stones | 1,499 | 30.0 |
| x1 Ultra Enchant Stone | 199 | 199.0 |
| x3 Ultra Enchant Stones | 549 | 183.0 |
| x10 Ultra Enchant Stones | 1,649 | 164.9 |
| x50 Ultra Enchant Stones | 4,499 | 90.0 |

The x50 bundles carry the steepest discount: about 39% off the single price for regular stones and about 55% off for Ultra stones. All eight prices match the ones we recorded for the Robux shop guide on 29 September 2026, and every bundle was still on sale on 10 October 2026. Roblox prices can move without notice, so confirm the number in the game before you pay.

A free route is claimed but not confirmed. At least one fan site says stones can drop during long casts. None of the official channels we checked says so, and none gives a drop rate or distance, so this page does not treat it as a farming method.

## Where and how do you use an Enchant Stone?

Not confirmed. None of the official channels we checked gives the steps, the location or the item a stone is spent on, and the product listing carries no description.

At least one fan site claims that the game has a dedicated place for enchanting and, as noted above, that stones may drop on long casts. Neither claim appears in any of the official channels we checked, which are listed in the next paragraph. For that reason this page does not adopt those claims, and it does not repeat the place names, prices or counts that site prints.

Here is what we checked on 10 October 2026. The game description names Strength, farther throws and new waters, and says nothing about enchanting. The eight Enchant products in the store each have a name, a price and an empty description. The nine game passes and the 13 badges do not use the word in any name or description. The LazyGames group description does not mention it, and the group had no shout. One event listing touches the subject: "Update 6 🔥" has the tagline "World 3 (Snow World) and Rod Entchantments 24 new Fish and much more!" (spelling as written). That announcement ties enchantments to rods and does not mention stones. None of these official channels says where a stone goes or what it changes.

Until LazyGames publishes the steps, or we capture the enchant screen ourselves, this section stays a list of open questions, not a walkthrough.

## Which enchants exist, and what do they do?

Not confirmed: none of the official channels we checked has published them. As of 10 October 2026 we found no enchant names, no effect sizes and no odds in the game description, the store, the game passes, the badges, the Roblox group description or the event listings. The [Discord server that calls itself official](/deep-fishing/discord/) has announcement channels that need a Discord account, so we could not read them.

Do not treat an enchant table written for another Roblox fishing game as Deep Fishing's. Enchant names, effects and odds from a different game tell you nothing about this one.

What the Ultra grade adds is also not confirmed. The name suggests a stronger or rarer result, and the price is about four times the regular stone, but neither fact tells you the odds. We will add an enchant table the day a list can be checked against the game.

## Is an Ultra Enchant Stone worth four times the price?

We cannot answer that with numbers. A single Ultra stone costs 199 Robux against 49, a ratio of roughly 4 to 1; at the x50 size it is 4,499 against 1,499, almost exactly 3 to 1. So the gap narrows as the bundle grows, but the benefit you get for it is not in any channel we checked.

Our advice, not a rule of the game: if you want to test enchanting, start with the cheapest unit and see the result yourself. One regular stone at 49 Robux is the cheapest way to learn where a stone is spent, what an attempt costs and whether results are random. At least one fan site claims enchanting happens at a dedicated location, which is not confirmed, so check in the game that you can reach the place where a stone is spent before you buy. A 4,499-Robux bundle bought before you have seen any of that is a blind purchase.

## Should you buy stones before rods or passes?

We would not. The official description names Strength and farther casts as the way to reach rarer fish, and the [rod list](/deep-fishing/rods/) has the Robux unlock price for each rod. What a stone does for your casts is not in any channel we checked, so we cannot say what the same Robux buys you.

For comparison, 399 Robux buys ten Enchant Stones or the Double Coins pass, which the developer describes as "Earn 2x more coins when selling fish! 🪙". The [Rods & Upgrades section](/deep-fishing/gear/) sets every purchase type side by side. The rest of the shop, including lucky chests and one-throw boosts, is in the [Robux shop guide](/deep-fishing/shop/).

## What does the EGGS & PETS event listing say about enchants?

The game's Roblox event listing, read on 10 October 2026, has an event titled "EGGS & PETS are COMING!"; the listing gives a start of 16:00 UTC on 11 October 2026 and an end of 16:00 UTC on 15 October 2026. Its tagline reads: "We are adding Pets and Eggs and also Item Crafting and much more!". It does not mention enchants, and an event listing is a plan, not a patch note.

This page reflects the store as read on 10 October 2026, before that start time; an enchant product created or renamed after that read is not on it. If you have a screenshot of the enchant screen or of an item named Enchant Scroll, the [author page](/deep-fishing/author/) explains how to send it.
