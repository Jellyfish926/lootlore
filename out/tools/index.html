    Tools & trackers

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-09-25

    Most guide pages are there to be read. A few are there to be used: trackers that remember what you have already done, side-by-side comparisons, and short quizzes that answer a buying question in a minute. This page collects every one of them across the hubs, so you do not have to remember which game keeps its tracker where.

    Each page below lives inside its game's own hub and works without an account. The trackers keep your ticked-off progress in your own browser, so nothing is uploaded and nothing is lost when you close the tab — but it is per-device, not synced. The comparison pages are static tables you can read straight off.

    Why so few

    A tool only earns a page when it does something a table cannot: remember state, filter a long list, or turn a decision into two or three questions. Everything else is better as a plain data table you can read at a glance, which is why most of the site is exactly that. The complete reading list is on the guides index, and what changed recently is on the updates page.
