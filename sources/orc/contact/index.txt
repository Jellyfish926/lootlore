<h2>What is most useful to send</h2>
<p><strong>Corrections with a source.</strong> If a turret figure here does not match your build, tell us what you see and which version you are on. Balance has already shifted once — Mumpitz Games shipped update 2026.08.02.13.35 four days after launch — so stat sheets go stale.</p>
<p><strong>Achievement details we are missing.</strong> We do not have the in-game display names for the eleven achievements, only Steam's internal identifiers. If you have the exact strings, that fills a gap we have flagged on the achievements page.</p>
<p><strong>Layouts that worked when a published one did not.</strong> The most useful guides are the ones written because somebody else's assumed an upgrade you did not have.</p>
<h2>What we will not do</h2>
<p>We do not accept paid placements, sponsored pages or link insertions, and we do not republish content from other sites.</p>
<p>We also will not add a number we cannot attribute, even a plausible one. If you send a figure, please say where it came from — your own measurement is a perfectly good answer.</p>
