    Valheim Beginner Guides: Start Here

    Language: English中文

    Key points

    • Five guides that cover the first playthrough: progression order, day one, crafting, combat and corpse runs.
    • No idea what to do next on an existing save? Go straight to the progression order page.
    • Every page keeps its source links and check date, and assumes vanilla standard survival.

    Playing for the first time? Read the opening guide, then look at bases, food and combat. Already have a save but no idea what to do next? Go straight to the progression order. Stuck on a recipe or a corpse run? Jump to that page instead of rereading everything.

    Pick a guide by problem

    • Valheim Progression Order by Biome

      The Valheim progression route from Meadows to Deep North, with each stage's goals and the checks that tell you when you are ready for the next biome.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim First Day: Survive Night One

      A clear Valheim opening route for tools, shelter, food and a bed on day one, plus the jobs that are not worth rushing before your first night.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Crafting & Repair Guide

      How Valheim crafting stations, smelting and forging fit together, with a fixed order for checking missing recipes, materials, station level and repairs.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Combat Basics: Block and Dodge

      Learn Valheim combat in a practisable order: why a raised shield still lets damage through, why stamina matters most, and how to pick your main weapon.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Death Recovery: Get Your Gear

      A reliable Valheim corpse run: what to do after respawning, how to approach your tombstone, how to recover and leave, and how to split roles in a group.

      v1.0.12

    Scope

    These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
