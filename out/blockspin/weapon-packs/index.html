    BlockSpin Weapon Packs: Robux Prices for Kits and Ammo

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Developer product and event listing records from Roblox APIs, read on 2026-10-10; all dates UTC. Prices are Robux prices at the read time, not in-game cash prices. The product list is selected by words in the product name, which is our grouping and not an official category. Damage, fire rate, in-game cash prices, pack contents and how long a Limited Time Pack stays on sale are not confirmed

    Key points

    • In the Roblox records read on October 10, 2026 (UTC), 17 BlockSpin products marked for sale have a weapon, ammo or throwable word in the name, from Shotgun Ammo Boost at 180 Robux to RPG Kit at 4,310 Robux.
    • Every price on this page is a Robux price. Damage, fire rate and in-game cash prices are not in the records we read and are marked not confirmed.
    • Limited Time Pack: AK47 costs 140 Robux less than AK47 Kit (3,270 − 3,130) and Limited Time Pack: RPG costs 150 less than RPG Kit (4,310 − 4,160); what each contains is not confirmed.
    • Revenge Pack, Revenge Pack 2 and Revenge Pack 3 cost 190, 290 and 390 Robux, and their records do not say what is inside.

    BlockSpin weapon packs cost between 180 and 4,310 Robux in the Roblox records we read on October 10, 2026 (UTC): 17 products with a weapon, ammo or throwable word in the name. These are Robux prices, not in-game cash prices. Damage, fire rate and cash prices are not in those records and are not confirmed.

    The list comes from the developer's own product records, so the names and Robux prices are official. We picked the 17 by the words in each name; Roblox's records have no weapon category. None of the 17 records has a description, which means the contents of a Kit or a Pack are not confirmed either.

    Which weapon packs can you buy with Robux in BlockSpin?

    Twelve of the 17 names carry a weapon or tool name. They are sorted by price, and each price is in Robux.

    The creation date is the day the record was made. It is not proof of the day a pack reached players, and every one of the 12 records was edited later, on May 13, 2026; an edit date does not say what changed.

    The words Kit and Pack are part of the product names. The records do not list what comes with either one, such as ammo or more than one item, so this page does not describe the contents. The same goes for Brainrot Slap Tool and Sledge Hammer: each is a name and a price, and whether either one works as a weapon in a fight is not confirmed.

    How much do ammo boosts and throwables cost in Robux?

    Five more records. The per-unit column is our own division and applies only if "x5" and "x10" are the number of items delivered, which the records do not state.

    ProductRobuxPer unit (our division)
    Shotgun Ammo Boost180Not applicable

    Two of the ammo records say Boost and one says Upgrade. Whether that is a one-off refill, a larger carrying limit or something else is not confirmed, and the records do not say which guns count as pistols, rifles or shotguns.

    Is a Limited Time Pack cheaper than the Kit with the same name?

    By price, yes, for the two weapons that have both records. The difference is our subtraction.

    Weapon nameKit (Robux)Limited Time Pack (Robux)Difference (our subtraction)
    AK473,2703,1303,270 − 3,130 = 140
    RPG4,3104,1604,310 − 4,160 = 150

    A lower price does not make it the same product for less. The records do not say what a Kit holds that a Limited Time Pack lacks, or the reverse, so which one is the better buy is not confirmed.

    How long "Limited Time" lasts is not confirmed. The product records we read have no end date, and the four Limited Time Pack records were created on January 21 and February 7, 2025. On October 10, 2026, at least 20 months after those dates by our count, the four were marked as for sale. Whether the game shows them in its shop at any given moment is not something the record can tell you.

    Hunting Rifle and Remington appear only as Limited Time Packs in these records; no Kit record carries either name.

    What is in Revenge Pack, Revenge Pack 2 and Revenge Pack 3?

    Not confirmed. The three records have no description. Revenge Pack costs 190 Robux, Revenge Pack 2 costs 290 and Revenge Pack 3 costs 390, so each step adds 100 Robux by our subtraction (290 − 190 and 390 − 290). The first record was created on March 5, 2025 and the other two on March 10, 2025.

    We list them here because they sit next to the weapon packs in the Robux shop guide, which counts these 20 records as one group. The name alone does not say that a Revenge Pack holds a weapon, and this page does not claim it.

    Do the records show weapon damage, fire rate or in-game cash prices?

    No, not in the Roblox records we read on October 10, 2026. We searched the saved text of the game record, the product list, the game pass list, the badge list and the event listings for "damage", "fire rate" and "cash price" and found no match. The 17 product records here give a name, a Robux price, a for-sale flag and two dates, and their description field is empty.

    That leaves a gap, and this page does not fill it from other sites: a stat table copied from elsewhere is not something we could check against an official record. Whether a given weapon can also be bought with in-game cash, and for how much, is not confirmed.

    Which official event listings mention a gun?

    Three of the 14 Roblox event listings we read on October 10, 2026 mention a gun. An event listing is the developer's announcement and gives no price.

    • "NEW GUN????", subtitle "SURPRISE NEW GUN", listed for November 15 to November 22, 2025.
    • "NEW SKORPION GUN", listed for April 4 to April 11, 2026.
    • "Mansion Upgrades: Part 2", listed for May 9 to May 16, 2026, with the description "A brand new gun has arrived with Legendary+, exclusively in the Mansion Weapon Crate".

    None of the 68 product records we read has "Skorpion" or "Legendary" in its name or description. A record named Mansion Upgrade: Personal Weapon Crate does exist, at 2,000 Robux; the Mansion upgrades guide covers it. Which gun the third listing means is not stated in the listing.

    Should you spend Robux on a weapon pack?

    That is your call, and one official rule belongs in it. The game description warns: "You drop everything in your inventory on death, place items into the safe in your house to keep them secure." Whether an item from a Robux pack is exempt from that rule is not confirmed. The beginner guide covers the rule and the house safe.

    Our advice, not a rule of the game: read the purchase prompt in the game before you confirm, since the record gives a name and a price and no contents. Tools that promise free weapons outside the game are a different matter; the cheats and bans page covers what Roblox's rules say about them.

    How did we pick these 17 products?

    The developer product list came back as 68 records in one response, with no further page. Sixty-six were marked for sale; we set aside the two of those whose names begin "OLD PRODUCT", which leaves 64. From those we took each name containing AK47, Glock, RPG, MP5, P226, Remington, Hunting Rifle, Sledge Hammer, Slap Tool, Firework Launcher, Ammo, Molotov or Grenade. That gives 17 records with a combined price of 26,718 Robux by our sum; with the three Revenge Packs it is 20 records and 27,588 Robux (26,718 + 190 + 290 + 390).

    A product named after something else that happens to include a weapon would be missed by this method. If you spot one, the author page explains how to report it.
