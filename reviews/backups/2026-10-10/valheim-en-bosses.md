# Valheim Boss Guides: All Eight Fights

These are ordered by main progression stage. Confirm the summon or entry conditions first, then sort out gear, food and an exit. The guides give first-playthrough thinking, do not rely on terrain exploits, and do not treat one recorded kill as something any setup can repeat.

## Pick a guide by problem


![A blue-skinned giant stepping out of a dark conifer forest as a Viking warrior advances with a weapon](ss04 "A large enemy stepping out of a dark conifer forest: read the arena before you commit")
### [Eikthyr boss guide](/valheim/eikthyr/)

Bring two deer trophies, learn to handle the melee and lightning attacks, and turn the drops into the mining power you need for the Black Forest.

### [The Elder boss guide](/valheim/elder/)

Prepare for The Elder in the Black Forest: where the summon items come from, how to move under ranged and root pressure, and what to set up for the Swamp afterwards.

### [Bonemass boss guide](/valheim/bonemass/)

Summon Bonemass with ten Withered bones, pick the right damage type, handle the poison clouds and adds, and use the Wishbone afterwards to start on the Mountains.

### [Moder boss guide](/valheim/moder/)

Covers summoning Moder and preparing the arena, how to respond differently in the flying and grounded phases, and why the dragon tears are the key drop for the next stage.

### [Yagluth boss guide](/valheim/yagluth/)

Summon Yagluth in the Plains, learn to tell the melee burst, ranged breath and meteor pressure apart, and put the drops to work in the Mistlands.

### [The Queen boss guide](/valheim/queen/)

The prerequisites for entering the Queen's fortress, the pressure from heavy knockback, adds and multi-level terrain, and what to prepare for melee and magic builds.

### [Fader boss guide](/valheim/fader/)

Fader's summon preparation, the arena pressure created by ground spikes, fire and meteors, and the step towards the Deep North after the win.

### [Kall Fimbulbringer boss guide](/valheim/kall/)

A framework for the three-phase fight: in phase one the boss attacks directly; in phase two it hides inside an invulnerable ice block and summons avatars of the previous seven bosses in turn; in phase three it returns with stronger moves and elemental damage. Covers entry prep, stamina management and how to review a wipe.

## Scope

These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
