# Deep Fishing Rarity Tiers, Mutations and Secret Odds

Deep Fishing's own badges name three high rarity tiers — Legendary, Mythical and Secret — plus mutated fish as a separate track. Badge totals show how rare each one really is: roughly 42% of players with the Welcome badge had caught a Legendary, 29% a Mythical and about 9% a Secret when we read the totals on 10 October 2026.

The developer has not published a drop-rate table. The one percentage in the Roblox records we read on 10 October 2026 is a line in the event listing "Mutation Roll + Rarity💫": "0.1% chance to get Rekin Shark". What we do have is better than a guess: Roblox publicly counts how many times every badge has been awarded. Those counts are the most honest rarity chart available for this game.

## Which rarity tiers does the game confirm?

The developer created one "first catch" badge for each notable tier. Their names and descriptions come word for word from the [Roblox badge API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/10526853622/badges?limit=100).

| Badge | Official description | Times awarded (10 Oct 2026) | Per 100 Welcome badges |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome! | You played Deep Fishing! | 3,051,522 | 100 |
| First Mutation | You caught your first Mutated Fish! | 2,594,470 | 85.0 |
| First Legendary | You caught your first Legendary! | 1,276,006 | 41.8 |
| First Mythical | You caught your first Mythical! | 884,418 | 29.0 |
| First Secret | You caught your first Secret! | 271,663 | 8.9 |

The last column is our own arithmetic: each badge's total divided by the Welcome! total. It answers a plain question — out of everyone who has the game's entry badge, how many ever got that far?

The event listing "Mutation Roll + Rarity💫" also names an Exotic rarity ("Added the new Exotic rarity"); it has no badge, so it has no row here. Tiers below Legendary are not named in the Roblox records we read on 10 October 2026, so we do not list them.

## How rare is a Secret fish, really?

Rare, but not impossible. About 9 in every 100 Welcome! badge holders had one on 10 October 2026. Compare that with Legendary: roughly 4.7 players had a Legendary for every one who had a Secret.

Two cautions keep these numbers honest. First, the badges were created on 28 July, about ten days after the game record was created on 18 July, so very early players who never came back are missing from every total. Second, the ratio counts people, not casts — it says nothing about how long each player fished before the first Secret.

The official description does say that new waters hold rarer fish. That is why the [rod ladder](/deep-fishing/rods/) and distance upgrades matter more for Secret hunting than any single boost.

## Why are mutations so much more common?

First Mutation is the second most common badge in the game, ahead of even the 50 Catches milestone. So a mutation is not a rare tier sitting above Secret; it is something most players see early.

The badge wording, "your first Mutated Fish", treats mutation as a state a fish can have. The official store backs that up: the **More Mutations** game pass has the description "Find more mutated Fishes! 🧬", and separate products are named Mutation Reroll and Ultra Mutation Scroll.

## Does Auto Sell ever hurt rarity hunting?

It can. The **Auto Sell** pass sells your fish automatically, which is great for volume. But if you are chasing the First Mutation or First Secret badge — or want to reroll a mutation — check what you caught before it is gone. Our suggestion is to keep a close eye on your hauls while hunting a specific badge, and lean on Auto Sell once you are back to farming coins. The full pass list is on the [game pass page](/deep-fishing/gamepasses/).

## Which boosts lean towards rare fish?

The store sells three luck passes (Lucky, Super Lucky, Ultra Lucky), four Server Luck products from x2 to x16, and four One Throw products. None of their records publishes a number, and the product records have no description. Our advice: use luck when you are already reaching new water, and focus on distance first. The [Robux shop guide](/deep-fishing/shop/) lists every one with its price.
