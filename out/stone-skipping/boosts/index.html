    Key points

    • Twelve Skill Multiplier tiers cost from 5 to 3,999 Robux on 10 October 2026; buying all twelve would cost 12,980 Robux.
    • Three Wins multipliers tagged [PERMANENT] are sold: 2x for 59, 5x for 229 and 10x for 575 Robux.

    • The official description names free ways to grow too: training, levelling up, pets and Rebirth.

    +1 Stone Skipping sells its Skill and Wins boosts as developer products, not game passes. There are twelve Skill Multiplier tiers from 5 to 3,999 Robux, three Wins multipliers tagged [PERMANENT] from 59 to 575 Robux, twelve Skill Packs, six Wins Packs and three boost potions. None of them has an official description.

    So this +1 Stone Skipping page is a price guide, not an effects guide. We can tell you exactly what each product costs and when it appeared. We cannot tell you what multiplier a tier gives or how long a potion lasts, and we will not guess a number.

    Going by the name, a "pack" most likely hands over a one-off amount of Skill or Wins, but the amounts are not published. The [TIER] tag on Skill Packs may mean the pack offered depends on how far you have progressed; that is our guess from the name. Wins Pack 5 exists twice: 459 Robux is about 20% below 575, which matches the [20% OFF] tag. Which of the two the game shows you is something to check on the purchase prompt.

    Are the Wins multipliers really permanent?

    Their names say so: 2x Wins [PERMANENT] costs 59 Robux, 5x Wins [PERMANENT] costs 229 and 10x Wins [PERMANENT] costs 575. Roblox's documentation describes a developer product as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once", while a pass is "a one-time Robux fee". The tag is the developer's wording; the listing does not say how the game stores the purchase.

    The prices line up with the Wins Packs. 2x Wins costs the same as Wins Pack 2, 5x Wins the same as Wins Pack 4, and 10x Wins the same as the full-price Wins Pack 5. Whether the three multipliers stack or replace each other is not stated.

    What are Skill Boost, Wins Boost and Boost Pack?

    Three products with potion icons: Skill Boost (29 Robux, a blue star-shaped bottle), Wins Boost (29 Robux, a yellow one) and Boost Pack (95 Robux, a bottle wrapped in blue flame). They carry no [PERMANENT] tag, so they are likely timed boosts, but no duration or strength is published. Each has a [GIFT] version at the same price, created on 30 September 2026.

    How can you grow without spending Robux?

    The official description lists the free routes: every bounce gives +1 Skill, training and levelling up make you throw farther, farther zones pay more Wins, pets give boosts and Rebirth makes you stronger. The store sells paid items around most of them. Training Zones, Auto Wins and Auto Rebirth are game passes, covered in the game pass guide, and eggs are priced in the eggs and pets guide. How to play walks through the free loop.

    What does the listing leave out?

    Each of these is not shown in public data and needs an in-game check:

    • the multiplier value of each Skill Multiplier tier, and whether tiers must be bought in order;
    • the amount of Skill or Wins in each pack;
    • the duration and strength of the three potions;
    • whether the three Wins multipliers stack;
    • how paid multipliers combine with pets and Rebirth.

    Where would we spend first?

    Our opinion, from prices alone. Skill Multiplier [TIER 1] at 5 Robux is the cheapest product in the whole +1 Stone Skipping store and the least risky way to see what a tier does. 2x Wins [PERMANENT] costs the same as a one-off Wins Pack 2, so if both work as named the multiplier should be worth more over time. We would hold off on tiers 8 to 12 until their values are known. Every other product is priced in the full Robux product list.
