Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

This policy explains what information is collected when you use Guide Atlas and the guide hubs it hosts, and what that information is used for. The short version: we run a reference site, not a data business — we collect as little as the site's operation allows.

What we collect

You can read every page on this site without creating an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail just to read a guide. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability; these are not used to identify individual readers.

Analytics

We may use aggregate analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand which pages are read and which searches bring visitors here. This data is viewed only in aggregate — counts, not people — and is used to decide which guides to update or write next.

Advertising and cookies

Guide Atlas is supported by third-party advertising, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Other advertising or affiliate partners we work with may also set cookies when you interact with their content; any such partner is bound by its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site remains fully readable with cookies disabled. Readers in the EEA/UK and California have additional rights over personal data under GDPR and CCPA respectively — since we hold no reader accounts, requests usually concern log or cookie data held by our providers, and we will help route them.
