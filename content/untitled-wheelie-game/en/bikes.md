# Untitled Wheelie Game Bikes, Parts and Backfire Guide

The store records name the Tuttiro Bike, the Eblox Dragster and two bike groups called "Ebikes" and "Emotos"; the developer's event announcements, read on 10 October 2026, add Normal Moped, Junkyard Moped and Jetson Ebike. All three bike passes are off sale today. Backfire is a part-like item sold with Robux, in three levels. The full bike list and prices are not in the records we read on 10 October 2026.

The official description promises you can "Buy bikes, upgrades, and parts" and "Paint and customize your bike". This page collects the bike-related names in the store records, checked on 30 September 2026; names from the event announcements, read on 10 October 2026, are on the [patch notes page](/untitled-wheelie-game/patch-notes/).

## Which bikes has the developer named?

| Name | How it was sold | Official description | On sale now? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EBike Pack ⚡ | Game pass | Gives you all Ebikes in the game. (Not Emotos) | No |
| Tuttiro Bike | Game pass | None | No |
| Eblox Dragster | Game pass | None | No |

The EBike Pack line tells us something useful: the game has at least two bike groups, "Ebikes" and "Emotos", and the pack covered only the first. The Tuttiro Bike and Eblox Dragster are named but not described, so we cannot say what kind of bike either is.

None of these passes had a price listed on 30 September 2026, because Roblox's store data shows no price for a pass that is off sale.

## What is the best bike in Untitled Wheelie Game?

We cannot tell you, and neither can anyone quoting official numbers. The developer's Mopeds + Rain announcement lists "40MPH stock, 80MPH modded" for Normal Moped and "35MPH stock, 160MPH modded" for Junkyard Moped; we found no handling or price data, and no speed for any other bike, in the records we read on 10 October 2026. Rankings on other sites are opinions. Our advice: compare bikes in the in-game shop, and pick the one whose wheelie you can hold longest — the developer stresses balance, throttle and brake, as the [how-to-play guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/how-to-play/) explains.

## What does Backfire do?

Backfire is sold as a part with three steps, all added on 4 September 2026:

| Product | Robux |
| --- | --- |
| INSTALL BACKFIRE | 25 |
| LEVEL 2 BACKFIRE | 30 |
| LEVEL 3 BACKFIRE | 40 |

Buying all three costs 95 Robux in total (our sum). The products carry no description. On a real motorbike, "backfire" means the pops and flames from the exhaust, so it is probably a cosmetic or sound effect rather than a performance part — but that is our guess from the name, not something the developer has confirmed.

## Can you make your bike faster?

The developer once sold a pass called Extra Bike Speed ("Adds speed to your bike"). It is off sale now. The description also lists "upgrades" among the things you can buy, so speed upgrades may exist for in-game money, but their names and costs are not published. If you are saving up, the [money guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/money/) covers the fastest official ways to earn.

## How do you paint and customize a bike?

"Paint and customize your bike" is one of the official activities. A Bike Customization pass ("Customize the color of your bike.") existed from 5 June 2026 but is off sale today. We could not confirm whether painting is now free for everyone or needs something else, so check the customization options in the game.

The developer also sells More Helmets 🪖 for 99 Robux, the one rider-gear pass still on sale. Its description is empty.
