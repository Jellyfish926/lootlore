# One Tap Updates: 6 Official Event Listings and Dates

One Tap has six official update listings on Roblox, all posted by the developer group Stringless Banjo: Valentines, Update 2, Update, ☀️Summer Update!, Revert and Game Revert. The first is listed for 14 February 2026 and the newest for 15 August 2026. The records we read on 10 October 2026 hold nothing newer.

A listing is the developer's own announcement, with a title, a text and a start and end time. It is the most detailed official record we have of what changed, but it is still an announcement: the times below are when each listing was scheduled, which is not proof of the minute a change reached players.

## Which One Tap updates has the developer listed?

All times are UTC, as Roblox records them.

| Listing | Subtitle | Listed start | Listed end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Valentines | Valentines | 14 February 2026, 21:14 | 15 February 2026, 17:00 |
| Update 2 | One Tap Second Update | 13 March 2026, 12:10 | 21 March 2026, 16:00 |
| Update | More Stuff lol! | 24 April 2026, 16:00 | 8 May 2026, 17:00 |
| ☀️Summer Update! | Summer Update | 15 June 2026, 14:00 | 30 June 2026, 05:00 |
| Revert | Revert | 9 August 2026, 13:20 | 9 August 2026, 15:15 |
| Game Revert | Revert | 15 August 2026, 05:20 | 15 August 2026, 07:15 |

The end time is when a listing closes on Roblox. It is not a release date, so we date each update by its listed start.

## What did Update 2 add?

Update 2 has the longest text of the six. Under the word "Content:" it gives 12 lines, quoted here exactly:

- "3 New Maps (Removed temporarily in new servers to fix some issues)"
- "New Battlepass season"
- "New Cosmic and Glitched weapon cases"
- "New Robux Energy Sword and Proto Cases"
- "New Karambit Case Additions"
- "New Level Reward Sniper and Knife"
- "New Limited Weapons"
- "Leaderboard Tag and Kill Effect reward for top 100 (Given after reset)"
- "Reporting players"
- "Improved anti-cheat"
- "Crosshair size customization"
- "New Leaderboards (Delayed, fixing it don't worry!)"

Two of the 12 carry a caveat in the developer's own words: the maps were pulled from new servers, and the new leaderboards were delayed. Roblox shows the listing was last edited on 16 March 2026, three days after its listed start. The record does not say what the edit changed. The case names in the Update 2 listing also appear in product names (Cosmic, Glitched, Energy Sword, Proto, Karambit); the records do not show when each product reached the in-game shop. The [cases page](/one-tap/cases/) has their prices.

## What was in the April One Tap update?

The listing titled Update, subtitle "More Stuff lol!", opens with "Final List:" and gives six lines:

- "28 total new weapons! These weapon models are also much higher quality than previous updates."
- "2 New Weapon cases"
- "New BP Season"
- "4 New Level Reward Skins"
- "Leaderboard prize additions"
- "New Limited Weapons"

It closes with "Small update, saving the big stuff for the summer update!!" The listing does not name the 28 weapons or the two cases. We read BP as battle pass because the March listing says Battlepass; the April listing does not spell it out. The [battle pass page](/one-tap/battle-pass/) has the Robux prices of the products named Premium Battlepass and Skip.

## What do we know about the One Tap Summer Update?

Very little from the listing itself. Its whole text is one sentence: "Join One Tap now to take part in the Summer Event alongside other new content!" It names no weapons, cases or rewards.

The store fills in some names, with a limit. Five products called Sun Points were created on 10 June 2026, five days before the listed start, and nine products for Vintage Case, Battle Case and Guardian Case were created on 15 June, the day of the listed start. The listing mentions none of them, so the link between those records and the Summer Event is our reading of the dates and no more.

## Why was One Tap reverted twice in August?

Two short listings say so. The first, titled Revert and listed for 9 August 2026, reads: "Game has been reverted to a previous version for testing."

The second, titled Game Revert and listed for 15 August 2026, gives a reason: "One Tap has been experiencing some backend issues after publishing the lunar update for the game. We have not been able to pin point the exact issue yet but we have decided to revert to a previous version. The new Kill effects and tags are no longer in the game for now but weapons have been re added."

So, in the developer's words, a lunar update was published, caused backend problems and was rolled back, with its kill effects and tags removed "for now" and its weapons added back. No listing is titled for a lunar update, and none since says whether the kill effects and tags have returned. Three products named Moon Case were created on 3 August 2026; no listing mentions them.

## When was One Tap last updated?

There is no single official answer, so here are the dated facts we have, all read on 10 October 2026:

- The newest event listing is Game Revert, 15 August 2026.
- The newest product records are the three named Moon Case, created on 3 August 2026.
- Four product records were edited on 30 September 2026: 1 Karambit Case, 1 Energy Sword Case, Nightmare 1 and Dragon Bundle. The records do not say what changed.
- All four game pass records were last edited on 10 August 2026.

Roblox's game record also has a field called "updated". It read 8 October 2026 when we checked, while a second Roblox endpoint for the same game showed 19 August 2026. The two values disagree with each other, so this page does not use the field as an update date.

## Where can you watch for the next One Tap update?

The six listings are the only official announcements we could read, so the event listings on the game's Roblox page are the place we check. The records do not show that every update gets a listing: the two Revert listings were created about two minutes before their listed start, and nothing is listed after 15 August 2026. Four of the six listing records were created more than two weeks before their listed start; the Summer listing record dates from 5 April 2026, ten weeks before 15 June. So some listing records existed well ahead of their start date; the records do not show when each one became visible. The group's shout was empty on 10 October 2026, and we could not read the game's social links without an account; the [game info page](/one-tap/game-info/) explains why.
