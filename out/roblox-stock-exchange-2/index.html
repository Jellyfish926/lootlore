    Roblox Stock Exchange 2 is a free trading simulator on Roblox, made by the group Summit Productions Development and created on 13 July 2026. You start with a small account and grow it by trading simulated stocks, ETFs, futures and IPOs, with algorithmic bots, levels and rebirths on top. These guides cover the code, passes, badges, shop and updates.

    About this guide

    The developer's own disclaimer sets the tone for everything here: "All markets and currencies are simulated. This game does not involve real-money trading or provide financial advice." We explain how the game's systems are described in official sources. We do not tell anyone how to invest.

    Is there a code?

    Yes, one is printed in the official description, and we have not tested it in the game. Near its end the description has three lines: "THANKS FOR 10K LIKES", "CODE: TOOLS" and "NEXT CODE RELEASES AT 15,000 LIKES". The game had 12,004 likes when we checked, 2,996 short of that 15,000 mark. What TOOLS gives is not stated in the description. The codes page tracks it and explains why the longer lists on other sites are not copied here.

    What has the developer announced beyond the four markets?

    The official description names four markets: stocks, ETFs, futures and IPOs. It also says you can use leverage and short the market. Those are the only things to trade that the description itself lists.

    Separately, official Roblox event listings have announced more features. A listing is an announcement, so this table records what was announced and when each event was listed to start. It does not show that a feature is live.

    What does Robux buy?

    Roblox Stock Exchange 2 sells two kinds of thing. The 13 game passes are one-time purchases, priced from 39 to 999 Robux, and twelve of them have a specific description, such as "24 hours offline instead of 8", "Up to 50x leverage at any level" and "0.02% fees". The 31 developer products cost 9 to 999 Robux and include time skips, AI Tokens and four algo bot purchases. Start with the Passes & Robux Shop section, or go straight to the algo bot guide.

    How far do most players get?

    The badge totals give a rough answer. Out of every 100 Welcome badges awarded, about 90 First Trade badges and 69 First Profit badges have been awarded. Shorting is much rarer at 27, and only about 2 in 100 have built an algo bot or rebirthed. The badge list has all 11 with exact counts.

    Which update events has the developer listed?

    Twelve, with listed start dates from 17 August to 10 October 2026. The Custom Offices event is listed to start on Saturday 10 October 2026 at 20:00 UTC, and its listing reads: "Your own 3d office you can walk inside of and upgrade". That start time in seven time zones is on the updates page.

    Who runs the game?

    Summit Productions Development is a Roblox group with 276,709 members. Its description is empty and it has no pinned shout, so the game description, the pass, badge and product texts, and the event listings are the developer text we can quote. The community page covers the group, its other games and the Discord server that calls itself official.

    How this guide uses its sources

    This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Roblox or Summit Productions Development. Fees without a pass, leverage limits by level, rebirth costs and bot returns are left out because the developer has not published them.     Frequently asked questions

    Who makes Roblox Stock Exchange 2?
    The Roblox group Summit Productions Development, which had 276,709 members on 8 October 2026. Details are on the community page.
    Is there a code?
    Yes. The official game description prints one code, TOOLS, and says the next code releases at 15,000 likes. We have not tested whether it redeems in the game. See the codes page.
    Is this real stock trading?
    No. The description says all markets and currencies are simulated and that the game does not involve real-money trading or provide financial advice. The how-to-play guide covers what you actually do.
    How many players fit in one server?
    Up to 35, according to the Roblox game record we read on 8 October 2026.
    What is the cheapest game pass?
    Custom Timeframes, at 39 Robux on 8 October 2026. All 13 passes are compared in the game pass guide.
