# American Plains Mudding Guides: Vehicles, Slots, Commands

New to American Plains Mudding? Start with the how-to-play guide, which sets out what the official description says you can do and suggests an order for a first session. Then read the update schedule, because a new update is listed for almost every Saturday. The other seven guides answer narrower questions about vehicles, spawning, commands, passes, badges and the group.

All nine guides stick to what the developer has registered on Roblox: the game description, eight badges, 14 game pass records, 11 developer products and 60 event listings. Each one says plainly when something is our advice and when a detail still needs an in-game check.

## What do these guides not cover yet?

We have no pages for a map, for hidden vehicle locations, for money-making, for controls or for a full commands list. Those things are visible inside the game but not in its Roblox listing or store data, and the only other sources are videos and fan sites we have not verified. Codes have not appeared in any official place we can read. We hold those pages back until we can check them in game, and we do not fill them with guesses.

## Scope

American Plains Mudding is a live Roblox game with a new update listed for almost every Saturday; as of 9 October 2026 the next listing starts on 10 October 2026. Every guide shows its check date. Badge totals, visits, member counts and prices are snapshots from about 11:13 UTC on 9 October 2026.
