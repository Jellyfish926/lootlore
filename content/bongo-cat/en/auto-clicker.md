# Bongo Cat Auto Clicker: Rules and Official Automation

We found no official Bongo Cat rule that allows or forbids an auto clicker: the 63 Steam announcements read on October 9, 2026 lack the words clicker, macro, cheat and ban. The one tap automation they name is The Typer Was Replaced. On our reading of two 2025 patch notes, faster clicking may not add regular chests; not confirmed.

## Is an auto clicker allowed in Bongo Cat?

Not confirmed either way. We found no rule on automated input in the official Steam announcement feed, and the description on the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3419430/Bongo_Cat/) says "Every time you press a key, Bongo cat will punch your taskbar. Type, click, play, work to collect more points." and gives no rule on automated input. On October 9, 2026 we searched the title and full text of the 63 announcements for the words players use for this topic:

| Search term | Announcements containing it | Which ones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| auto clicker, clicker | 0 | None |
| macro | 0 | None |
| cheat | 0 | None |
| ban, banned (whole word) | 0 | None |
| exploit, script, hack | 0 | None |
| bot, bots (whole word) | 1 | March 11, 2025 |
| afk | 2 | April 16 and April 20, 2026 |
| typer | 2 | April 13 and April 16, 2026 |
| tap() | 1 | April 16, 2026 |

So three things are not confirmed: whether the game can tell automated input from typing, whether any account has been restricted over it, and whether a click-rate limit exists. This page does not cover third-party auto clickers or macro tools, and we do not recommend one. The Farmer Was Replaced, described below, is a different case: a separate game on Steam with an official tie-in to Bongo Cat. The developer's forum replies on clickers and automatic chest opening are quoted on [is Bongo Cat safe](/bongo-cat/is-it-safe/).

## What is The Typer Was Replaced, the official automation?

It is an official collab between Bongo Cat, Tap Tap Loot and The Farmer Was Replaced, a separate programming game sold on Steam. In the April 16, 2026 post the developer says they "tested around a bit with something called "IPC" short for "inter process communication". That allows two programs to "talk" to each other." The result: "you can automate both Bongo Cat and Tap Tap Loot. While your drone farms wheat, your adventurer fights monsters and your bongo cat taps along." The post names one function, `tap()`, called in a loop inside The Farmer Was Replaced.

The other studio confirms it from its side. The Farmer Was Replaced's [own announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2060160/view/1829528821315211) of April 13, 2026 says "we built an integration to automate their games". Bongo Cat's April 16 post words the tie-in differently: its section on the collab speaks of testing IPC and of a "small collab".

Bongo Cat is free, but the collab needs The Farmer Was Replaced, which its [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2060160/The_Farmer_Was_Replaced/) listed at US$9.99 (US region) on October 9, 2026. Neither post says how many taps one `tap()` call adds, whether there is a speed cap, or whether automated taps count the same as typed ones for chests, achievements and the Paw Pass. All three are not confirmed.

The February 5, 2026 notes mention a second link between programs: "an IPC connection to Tap Tap Loot, giving you buffs if you equip certain items". That line is the one mention of IPC in those notes, and it does not say what the connection carries, so whether it involves taps is not confirmed.

## Do more clicks get you more chests?

Possibly not past a point, but that is our reading and not an official statement. The pieces come from different posts. The 30-minute figure is from the Steam Next Fest Demo Update of February 23, 2025 ("Increase the drop timer to 30min"), a patch to the demo before the full release of March 5, 2025. After that update no announcement calls any length of time the drop timer; the [Steam Error Fix](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1799088287821846) post of May 8, 2025 says "You might need to keep Bongo Cat running for 30min until it will work normally again", without saying that this is the chest timer. The 1,000 figure is from four posts: the March 18, 2025 notes ("only shows chest popup if you have 1000 clicks"), the [May 8, 2025 notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1799088287826807) (an option to disable the "-1000" number when you open a chest), and the June 27 and July 12, 2026 posts on opening a friend's chest. The [April 20, 2025 update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1797185861746045) added a popup showing the taps subtracted when you buy a chest. No post says the timer and the 1,000 clicks are two gates on the same chest, so that pairing is not confirmed. The basics are on [how to play](/bongo-cat/how-to-play/).

| What clicks pay for | The gate | What extra clicks change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular chest | Popup at 1,000 clicks (March 18, 2025 notes); 30-minute drop timer (February 23, 2025 demo update) | If both apply together, nothing once you have 1,000 before the timer ends; not confirmed |
| A friend's chest | "it costs you 1000 clicks, for your friend it's free" ([June 27, 2026](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1836506165556399)) | Not confirmed; not stated in the announcements we read |

By our arithmetic, three premises give a ceiling of 48 regular chests in 24 hours (24 × 2), using 48,000 clicks (48 × 1,000): that the demo update's 30-minute timer applies to the full release, that it allows one regular chest per timer, and that each chest takes 1,000 clicks. That is a derived figure, not an official one. After the February 23, 2025 demo update no announcement calls any length of time the drop timer (the May 8, 2025 Steam Error Fix post mentions 30min without calling it the chest timer), and none says one chest per timer. If the premises hold, clicks beyond that pace add nothing to regular chests.

No source we read ties rarity to clicking. The store description lists the drop pool chances as Common 90%, Uncommon 9.5%, Rare 0.49%, Epic 0.01% and Legendary 1 in 500000, marked "Subject to change", and none of the 63 announcements links those odds to how many taps you have. The expected number of chests per tier is worked out on the [legendary odds page](/bongo-cat/legendary-odds/).

## What did the developer do about bot farming?

The response the announcements document targeted trading, not input. On March 11, 2025 the developer wrote: "I noticed bot accounts farming thousands of items and by moving the items to one single account and then selling a ton of them in bulk." Trading between two users was removed that day, with the Community Market left open, and the post added "if i can handle the bots then i will try to bring this back". On November 10, 2025 a post re-enabled "all items for trading with friends"; it does not say that trading between any two users came back. The rule-by-rule history is on [exchange and trading](/bongo-cat/exchange-trading/).

What the feed does not contain matters as much. None of the 63 announcements announces an account ban or a wipe of farmed items: ban (as a whole word), wipe and rollback have 0 matches in their titles and text. The word revert appears in two posts. One is a patch note of March 18, 2025, "Revert controller changes". The other is the one offer to revert something for players, from May 9, 2025, when a bug traded in non-duplicate items and the developer offered to "revert your last exchange" for affected players ([fix notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1799088287868198)). Whether accounts were actioned without an announcement is not confirmed.

## What happened to the 5 million click reward?

It was lowered. The February 5, 2026 post says: "we added a special skin and hat for everyone who reaches 1 million clicks (this was edited back down from 5M, sorry for all the troubles)". That parenthesis is the post's one mention of 5M, and it gives no reason. One month later, the [anniversary post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1826362059921578) of March 5, 2026 said the tap-amount achievements added that day give cosmetics too.

So raw tap totals do unlock things. The thresholds and unlock rates are on the [achievements page](/bongo-cat/achievements/). Resetting the visible counter does not touch them: the March 28, 2025 notes describe an "Option to reset your current taps (doesn't reset your achievement)".

## Does Bongo Cat flag you as AFK?

In lobbies, yes. The Afk Warning from April 16, 2026 lets players "see if people are inactive in your lobby (after 2 minutes of inactivity)". The announcement does not say whether being inactive changes drops, pass progress or lobby membership, so any effect beyond the visible flag is not confirmed. Lobby features are covered in the [multiplayer guide](/bongo-cat/multiplayer/).

Steam's own idle state was blamed once. In March 2025 the developer traced the red cross error to this: "the moment Steam detects you as "away" it will mess with the item drops". The March 18, 2025 notes say the update "Should fix the red cross". Neither of those two March 2025 posts says whether automated input keeps Steam from marking you away.

## Should you use one?

Our view, not a rule of the game: we see little to gain. If the 30-minute drop timer from the February 23, 2025 demo update still applies, regular chests are capped by it, and after that update no announcement calls any length of time the drop timer (the May 8, 2025 Steam Error Fix post mentions 30min without calling it the chest timer); none of the 63 announcements ties the store's tier odds, which are marked "Subject to change", to tap counts; and the developer has shipped an official tie-in that automates taps. The open risks are the three not confirmed points above, plus other games' anti-cheat, which is discussed on [is Bongo Cat safe](/bongo-cat/is-it-safe/). Typing as usual is the input the official posts describe.
