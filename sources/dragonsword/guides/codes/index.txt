3:T1f04,<p><em>Game version checked: 1.0.12 (10 September 2026 patch), the newest Update Notes on Steam when we re-checked this page on 9 October 2026. Hound13 has announced Version 1.1 for 15 October 2026 — if it is live when you read this, check its patch notes for anything code-shaped.</em></p>
<p><strong>There are no active DragonSword: Awakening codes, and there is no in-game redeem-code system to check.</strong> This isn't a "check back later" situation the way it usually is for a mobile gacha game — the reason is structural, and it's the same reason the game's marketing keeps insisting it isn't a gacha title.</p>
<h2>Why there's nothing to redeem</h2>
<p>DragonSword: Awakening began development as <em>Project D</em>, a free-to-play gacha mobile game. Our own <a href="/guides/faq/">FAQ page</a> covers that history in more detail, but the short version is: Hound13 scrapped the gacha model entirely before launch and shipped this as a $29.99 premium buy-once PC title instead. Redeem-code systems exist in free-to-play games specifically to distribute gacha currency, pulls, or premium items outside the cash shop — remove the gacha economy, and there's usually nothing left for a code to grant.</p>
<p>A bylined Destructoid codes-tracking article (Ana Mitic, published 3 August 2026; we re-read it on 9 October 2026 and it had not been updated since) confirms this from the outlet's own research rather than assumption: "There are no active codes. There are no expired codes," and directly: "the Devs decided to completely erase the gacha system in DragonSword: Awakening. We're not sure whether the game will have codes." As of Destructoid's most recent update, the game also has no official Wiki or Trello board — the developer's Discord server is the main hub for announcements instead.</p>
<h2>Where the reward system that replaces codes actually lives</h2>
<p>If you're searching for codes because you want free heroes or currency, the honest redirect is to the game's actual unlock and reward systems, all of which are earned through play rather than a code:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Heroes:</strong> Hound13's own figures are 19 playable heroes at launch (its 28 July 2026 Steam FAQ) and "6 story heroes" (said in passing in its 14 August 2026 Q&amp;A recap, without listing them). The per-hero records behind our <a href="/heroes/unlock-planner/">unlock planner</a> only pin down five story heroes by name and leave one hero's unlock path undocumented, so which six is not confirmed — see the <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">Heroes roster page</a>. The mechanism is not in doubt: story heroes join free, the rest are bought in the Exchange with Fate's Invitation tickets, "no gacha, no paid characters, no drop chance," and the game hands out exactly enough invitations to complete the roster over time.</li><li><strong>Cosmetics:</strong> per our <a href="/guides/faq/">FAQ</a>, DLC is roughly twenty cosmetic items — costume sets and rideable Familiars — none of which affect power, and Hound13 has given away at least one free limited-time Familiar (the Abyssal Direwolf) as a promotion rather than a code.</li><li><strong>Upgrade materials:</strong> Karma duplicates, Sacred Light fragments, and Skill Scrolls all come from daily and weekly content rather than a redeemable code — see our <a href="/guides/world-boss-and-raid-guide/">World Boss and Raid Guide</a> and <a href="/guides/awakening-system/">Awakening System guide</a> for where those specifically come from.</li></ul>
<h2>What we re-checked on 9 October 2026</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Steam announcements.</strong> We pulled every announcement on the game's Steam news feed — 83 items, 78 of them posted by Hound13 as Community Announcements, from 10 April to 8 October 2026 — and searched them for redeem, coupon, promo code and gift code. Nothing. The word "code" appears in Hound13's posts only for co-op <strong>invite codes</strong> (how you join a friend's Battle or Raid room), a <code>-dx11</code> launch option, a save-folder path, and a developer answer about porting server code into the client. None of those is a reward code.</li><li><strong>Steam store page.</strong> The store description does not mention codes or a redemption feature.</li><li><strong>Destructoid's tracker.</strong> Still dated 3 August 2026, still "There are no active codes. There are no expired codes."</li><li><strong>Hound13's Discord.</strong> Not checked — we have no access to it, so anything posted only there is not covered by this page.</li></ul>
<h2>Has any code ever existed for this game?</h2>
<p>Not according to the sources we checked. Destructoid's tracking article — the kind of article that exists specifically to catch and log codes the moment a developer posts one — reports zero active and zero expired codes as of its most recent update. That is a meaningfully different signal than "we haven't found one yet": a dedicated codes-tracker with no expired-codes section at all is fairly strong evidence that Hound13 has not issued one — and the Steam announcement feed, which we read in full, agrees. The one channel neither check covers first-hand is the official Discord.</p>
<h2>Could that change?</h2>
<p>Possibly, but nothing in the material we reviewed points to it. Hound13 has shipped DLC and free promotional items (the Abyssal Direwolf Familiar) through direct in-game distribution rather than a code system, which suggests that if the studio wants to run a promotion, its established pattern is to hand it out automatically rather than ask players to type a string into a menu. Our <a href="/guides/patch-notes-and-roadmap/">Patch Notes and Roadmap page</a> tracks Hound13's announced update plans in more detail — check there for anything code-shaped before assuming this page is out of date, since a codes system could in theory be added in a future update the way Localization and the Exchange Shop were added in patch 1.0.7.</p>
<h2>If you landed here from a "codes" video or list site</h2>
<p>A number of low-effort list articles and videos for other games recycle a generic "codes" template regardless of whether the game actually has any — that's a broader pattern across gacha-adjacent guide content, not something specific to DragonSword: Awakening. If you've seen a video or article claiming to have an active DragonSword: Awakening code, treat it with real skepticism: cross-check it against <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/4570720" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Hound13's official Discord server</a> (linked from the game's Steam page) before typing anything into an in-game redemption field, since a fake or expired code claim is a common way for low-quality sites to farm clicks. For ongoing player discussion of anything code-shaped that might surface, the <a href="https://steamcommunity.com/app/4570720/discussions/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">DragonSword: Awakening Steam Community hub</a> is a faster-moving source than any single guide site, this one included.</p>
<h2>What we could not confirm</h2>
<p>We did not check Hound13's official Discord server ourselves, so "no codes" on this page means no codes on Steam (announcements and store page) and none logged by Destructoid — not confirmed for Discord. We could not verify Hound13's internal roadmap for whether a promotional code system is planned, since neither the Destructoid source nor our own <a href="/guides/patch-notes-and-roadmap/">Patch Notes and Roadmap page</a> lists one.
