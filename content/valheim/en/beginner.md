# Valheim Beginner Guides: Start Here

Playing for the first time? Read the opening guide, then look at bases, food and combat. Already have a save but no idea what to do next? Go straight to the progression order. Stuck on a recipe or a corpse run? Jump to that page instead of rereading everything.

## Pick a guide by problem


![Two characters inside a wooden workshop, between a workbench and a drying rack hung with hides](ss13 "Workbench and drying rack: most beginner problems are solved at home, not in the field")
### [Valheim Progression Order by Biome](/valheim/progression/)

Lays out Valheim's route biome by biome, with the goal of each stage and the conditions for moving on, so you can tell what to upgrade now and where to go next.

### [Valheim First Day: Survive Night One](/valheim/first-day/)

A clear opening route that gets tools, shelter, food and a bed done, and says which jobs are not worth rushing on day one.

### [Valheim Crafting & Repair Guide](/valheim/crafting/)

Explains how the Workbench, smelting and forging fit together, and gives a fixed order for checking missing recipes, missing materials, station level and repairs.

### [Valheim Combat Basics: Block and Dodge](/valheim/combat/)

A practice order for defence and attack rhythm: why you still take damage with a shield up, why you should never empty your stamina, and how to pick a main weapon.

### [Valheim Death Recovery: Get Your Gear](/valheim/death-recovery/)

From respawn prep to approaching the tombstone, recovering and leaving, so you stop dying twice in a row, with a clear split of roles for group rescues.

## Scope

These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
