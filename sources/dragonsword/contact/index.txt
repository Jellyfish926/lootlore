<h2>If you are reporting an error</h2>
<p>Say which page, and what it should say instead. If you can add a source — a patch note, a developer post, a repeatable in-game check — the fix goes up much faster, because the slow part is verification, not editing.</p>
<p>This is the message type we prioritise. Given how much unverified Hero and recipe data circulates for DragonSword: Awakening, reader corrections are a meaningful part of how this site stays accurate.</p>
<h2>If two sources disagree</h2>
<p>Send both. Conflicting community information about ability kits and ailment types is common for this game, and we would rather publish that a detail is disputed, with both claims shown, than silently pick the more popular one. Contradictions are useful to us, not an inconvenience.</p>
<h2>Other things worth sending</h2>
<p><strong>Guide requests.</strong> Something you looked for and could not find explained properly. These shape the queue more than our own guesses do.</p>
<p><strong>Broken pages.</strong> Dead links, images that do not load, mobile layout problems — include your device and browser.</p>
<p><strong>Reuse.</strong> Short quotes with attribution and a link are fine. Ask first for anything longer.</p>
<h2>Outside what we can help with</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Game support.</strong> Crashes, purchases, account issues, refunds — that is Hound13 Inc., through the <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/4570720/" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Steam store page</a>. We have no access and cannot escalate anything.</li><li><strong>Cheats, exploits, piracy.</strong> We do not cover them.</li><li><strong>Anything unreleased.</strong> No early builds, no embargoed material, no insider information.</li></ul>
<h2>Reply times</h2>
<p>A few days is typical; factual corrections are faster. If a page is actively misleading people, put that in the subject line.</p>
