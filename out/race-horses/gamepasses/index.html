    Key points

    • Race Horses sells five game passes, priced 199 to 459 Robux; all five together cost 1,635 Robux.
    • Every pass has an official description, from "Get 2x cash from all races!" to "Soar the skies at high speeds!".
    • Each pass can also be bought as a gift for a friend; the gift version of X2 RACE CASH is listed at 249 Robux, 80 less than the pass itself.
    • For earning, X2 RACE CASH is our first pick; for hatching a lot, INSTANT Hatch!.

    Race Horses has five game passes, all on sale: X2 FRUIT CAPACITY (199 Robux), X3 STORAGE (249), X2 RACE CASH (329), INSTANT Hatch! (399) and Rainbow Magic Carpet! (459). Buying all five costs 1,635 Robux. Every pass has an official description, and each has a matching gift product so you can buy it for a friend.

    Names, prices and descriptions are copied from the Roblox game pass API, checked on 1 October 2026. None were discounted.

    What does each pass do?

    In Race Horses, as in any Roblox game, a game pass is a one-time purchase that stays on your Roblox account (standard Roblox behaviour). Developer products such as Double Race Earnings work differently: the developer decides what each purchase grants, and for Double Race Earnings it does not say how long the boost lasts.

    Which pass should you buy first?

    Our suggestions, based only on what each pass says it does:

    • You want more cash: X2 RACE CASH (329). It doubles cash "from all races", and with a race every minute that adds up fastest. The racing guide explains how race cash works.
    • You hatch a lot of eggs: INSTANT Hatch! (399). It replaces every hatch-timer skip; at Mythic skip prices (79 Robux) it pays for itself after six skips. See eggs and rarities.
    • Your storage keeps filling up: X3 STORAGE (249). Triple the slots, useful if you are collecting for the Index.
    • You care for several horses: X2 FRUIT CAPACITY (199). Twice the apples per trip; the care and stable guide covers apples.
    • You want to get around in style: Rainbow Magic Carpet! (459). It is the only pass that is about movement rather than earning; its description promises flight "at high speeds", nothing more.

    Can you gift a pass to a friend?

    Yes. Since 22 September 2026 (24 September for storage) each pass has a gift version sold as a developer product:

    GIFT X2 RACE CASH249Gift X2 RACE CASH to your friends!

    Four gift prices match the pass prices. GIFT X2 RACE CASH does not: it is listed at 249 Robux, while the pass costs 329. The pass record was last updated on 24 September, two days after the gift product was created, so the pass price may have changed and the gift product not followed, or the other way round. We cannot tell which price is current from the data alone; check the in-game price before buying.

    Is X2 RACE CASH better than Double Race Earnings?

    They are sold differently. X2 RACE CASH is a 329-Robux pass described as "Get 2x cash from all races!". Double Race Earnings is a 59-Robux developer product described as "X2 your race earnings!!". Double Race Earnings may be a temporary boost, but the developer does not state its duration. We also could not confirm whether the two stack. If you play every day, the pass is the safer value.

    How do pass prices compare with eggs?

    Putting the store side by side helps decide where Robux go furthest. Our arithmetic, using official prices:

    PurchaseRobuxCompared with
    All five Race Horses passes1,635Less than 2 Mythic Eggs (999 each)
    X2 RACE CASH329About 4 Rare Eggs (79 each)
    INSTANT Hatch!399Just over 5 Mythic hatch skips (79 each)
    Exotic Egg2,799More than all five passes together
    Starter Pack25Cheaper than a single Uncommon Egg (39)

    Passes are permanent; eggs and skips are used up. If you expect to keep playing, a pass that boosts every race or every hatch usually beats a single expensive egg. That is our view, not an official recommendation.
