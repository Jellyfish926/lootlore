# +1 Mog Evolution Codes, Group, Discord and Test Server

There are no +1 Mog Evolution codes we can confirm. On 1 October 2026 the game description, the Navoj Mog group page and the official event listing contained no code, and Roblox hides the game's social links from logged-out visitors. So this page lists zero codes, explains where real ones would appear, and covers the official group.

If a code site shows you a list, ask where it came from. A code that only exists on fan sites is not one we will print.

Roblox also moved the group up to community tier 3 on 11 September 2026. The group has only three roles, and the Admin role holds one member, so there is no public list of moderators or testers to contact.

## Is there an official Discord or Trello?

We could not confirm either. Roblox shows an experience's social links only to logged-in users, and the game page we could read contains no Discord, Trello, YouTube or TikTok link. The group description, where many developers paste invites, is empty.

Until a link appears on the game page or the group, treat any "official" invite with care. Never type your Roblox password anywhere except roblox.com.

## What is "+1 Mog evolution [TESTING]"?

A second experience owned by Navoj Mog, created on 10 September 2026. On 1 October it had 1,287 visits, nobody online, no description, and room for 50 players per server, compared with 12 in the main game. Its name says it is a test copy; whether players are meant to join it is not stated. Release dates of the main game's content are on the [updates page](/mog-evolution/updates/).

## Should you use a script or auto-farm hack?

No. Google suggests "mog evolution script" when you type the game's name, but scripts break Roblox's Terms of Use and can get your account deleted, according to Roblox's own [help article on cheating and exploiting](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203312450-Cheating-and-Exploiting). They are also a common way to spread account-stealing malware. If you want to click less, the developer sells an official Auto Clicker that "Automatically earns Appeal at 22 clicks per second"; it and the other boosts are compared in the [Appeal guide](/mog-evolution/appeal/).
