# Get Your Driver's License! Beginner Guides: Start Here

New to Get Your Driver's License!? Read the how-to-play guide first. It goes through the features in the order the developer lists them: the line, the written test, the driving test, the cars and the endings. Then read the line guide, since the wait is the first thing the description lists, and the driving test guide.

These four guides stay close to what the group Time Will Pass has published on Roblox: an eight-line description, 5 game pass descriptions and 13 product descriptions. Where the developer has said nothing, the guides say so.

## Pick a guide by question

### [How to Play: Line to Car](/get-your-drivers-license/how-to-play/)

What the six feature lines in the description mean, what the store listings add about each one, and a plan for a first run without purchases.

### [The Line: Every Skip Product Priced](/get-your-drivers-license/waiting-line/)

How the wait is described, the five skip products from 9 to 48 Robux with a value table, and what Kill and Kill All say they do to the line.

### [Driving Test and Endings](/get-your-drivers-license/driving-test/)

Cows, ducks and ramps on the course, the two endings the developer names, what a fail costs, and the price of a seat as the Examiner.

### [Developer, Group and Discord](/get-your-drivers-license/community/)

The group behind the game in numbers, its four other experiences, and why no official Discord server can be confirmed.

## What should a new player do first?

Our suggestion is to try one whole run without buying anything. We have not done that ourselves yet. The description's first words are "Wait in line", and the wait is the part every skip is priced against. Until you have sat through it once you cannot tell whether 9 Robux for five places is a bargain.

| Step | Why | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Take a number and wait | It is the first thing the description tells you to do | How to play |
| Sit the written test | Skip Test costs 29 Robux; see the test before you pay to avoid it | How to play |
| Drive the course once | Cows, ducks and ramps are named, but no route is published | Driving test |
| Note your car and ending | The description names both as things to win or unlock | Driving test |
| Compare skips | Only after you know how long your wait is | The line |

The table is our suggested order, not a rule of the game.

## When should you think about Robux?

After that first run, if at all. Get Your Driver's License! is free to play and no description says a purchase is required, but we have not played a full run without paying. When you do want to compare purchases, the [Cars & Robux Shop section](/get-your-drivers-license/robux/) lists all 5 passes and 13 products with official prices.

## What do these guides leave out?

Anything the developer has not published and we have not yet checked in the game. That covers the written test questions, the route through the course, the names of the 18 cars and the endings other than Honor Roll and Towed. There is also no codes page, because no code appears in the official sources we could read.

## Scope

The game was created on 29 September 2026 and its store has already changed more than once. Each guide shows the date it was checked, and counters such as visits and members are snapshots from 9 October 2026. This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Roblox or Time Will Pass.
