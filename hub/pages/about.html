{{BRAND}} is where our game guides live together. Instead of scattering coverage across the web, we give each game a complete, self-contained hub — boss lists, builds, monster data, achievements, endings, patch history — and collect those hubs under one roof so you can find them again.

Why a hub, not a mega-site

Most large guide networks spread themselves across hundreds of games and let the long tail rot. We work the other way around: a game only appears on {{BRAND}} when its hub can genuinely answer the questions players search for, from “is it worth buying” down to the rarest achievement. Five hubs are live today — Beast of Reincarnation, Shift At Midnight, DragonSword: Awakening, Sephiria and Sir, We Have an Orc Problem — with more in research.

How the site is run

{{BRAND}} is a small independent project, not a content farm with a rotating freelancer pool. The same editor is responsible for a game's hub from launch coverage through its patch cycle, which is why pages carry review dates and the game version they were last checked against. Software tools help with drafting and data collection; every fact that reaches a page is verified against official sources or the running game by a person. Our full sourcing rules are in the editorial policy.

Independence

We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any developer or publisher covered on this site. Screenshots and artwork are used for identification and commentary and remain the property of their studios, credited where they appear. If we ever run affiliate links or advertising, that relationship never changes a verdict — a game we would not recommend at full price stays not-recommended.
