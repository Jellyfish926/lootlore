    Key points

    • American Plains Mudding has eight badges; four are separate badges all named Hidden Vehicle and a fifth is Hidden Trailer.
    • The four Hidden Vehicle badges had been awarded 5,765,575 times in total by 9 October 2026; the oldest one accounts for 2,450,309 of those.
    • Roblox's data gives no locations for hidden vehicles, and we have not confirmed any in game, so this page lists none.
    • Tornado Survivor (2,684,652) is the most-awarded badge after the welcome badge; Met a Staff Member was not awarded once in the day before we checked.

    American Plains Mudding has eight badges, and five of them reward finding things: four separate badges named Hidden Vehicle and one named Hidden Trailer. The other three are a welcome badge, Tornado Survivor and Met a Staff Member. Roblox's badge data gives counts and dates for all eight, but no locations.

    That last point matters, because "hidden vehicle location" is one of the phrases Google suggests for this game. We have not been able to confirm any location from an official source or in game, so this page does not list one. What it can give you is everything the developer has registered.

    Why are there four Hidden Vehicle badges?

    Because the developer created four, each a separate record with its own ID and counter. The names and texts are identical, so the only way to tell them apart in the data is by creation date.

    "First" to "Fourth" are our labels, in order of creation. The simplest reading is one badge per hidden vehicle, added over time. The data does not confirm that each badge maps to a different vehicle. The badge ID is the one sure way to tell which of the four you have earned.

    Two details are worth knowing. The fourth badge is less than a year old and has already passed the third in total awards, and it is still being earned faster: 2,352 in the previous day against 1,437. And the fourth badge was created on 23 October 2025, two days before an event listing that read "New Halloween vehicles. Fall Map. And more!". The Hidden Trailer badge followed on 10 December 2025, three days before a listing that mentioned a "Christmas map". The update history has both listings.

    Where are the hidden vehicles?

    We do not know, and no official source says. The badge text confirms only that hidden vehicles exist and that finding one earns a badge. It does not say where they are, what they look like, or whether you keep the vehicle.

    There is also a reason to be careful with old answers. The first and second badge records were both edited on 23 October 2025, within half a minute of the fourth being created. The third badge and the Hidden Trailer badge were both edited on 11 July 2026 at 13:15 UTC, half an hour before that Saturday's listed update. Roblox does not record what an edit changed, so this may be nothing more than a new icon. But a guide made in 2024 describes a map that has since been listed with a fall version, a Christmas version and, in the 10/3 notes, a new forest.

    If you go looking, our only advice is general: explore away from the roads, revisit new areas after a map update, and match any badge you earn against the IDs above.

    These ratios are our arithmetic, and they are not odds. The welcome badge only dates from 11 November 2023, more than two years after the game was created, and the other badges were added at different times. The all-time column therefore understates newer badges. The previous-day column covers the same 24 hours for every badge, but a welcome badge is presumably earned on a first visit, while the others can go to players who have been around for years.

    Even so, the order is clear. No hidden badge is common. On the day before we checked, the four Hidden Vehicle badges were earned 8,869 times in total, while the welcome badge was earned 61,198 times.

    How do you get Tornado Survivor?

    The text says you must have "Experienced a tornado in APM and lived to tell the tale!". It was created on 19 July 2025 and has been awarded 2,684,652 times since, more than any single Hidden Vehicle badge. That pace suggests tornadoes turn up in ordinary play, though the description of the game never mentions weather.

    One official text does: the Premium Private Servers pass lists the command ":weather storm 1", which "Spawns Tornadoes". Whether a tornado spawned by command counts for the badge is not stated. The private server page covers that command.

    Can you still get Met a Staff Member?

    The badge is enabled, but nobody earned it in the day before our check, against 374,295 awards in total. Its text reads "Congrats! You have met a staff member of APM!". The text suggests it is awarded when you meet a staff member; the data does not say how.

    The group lists a handful of ranked roles above Member, including four Moderators, one Head Moderator, one Developer and one Head Developer; the full role table is on the community page. The badge text does not say which roles count as staff.

    What should you check in game?

    • Which vehicle each Hidden Vehicle badge belongs to, and where it is
    • Whether a found vehicle is added to your spawn list and uses one of your spawn slots
    • Whether the 3 October 2026 forest moved or added anything
    • Whether a command-spawned tornado awards Tornado Survivor

    If you can confirm any of these with a screenshot, the how-to-play guide and this page are where it will go.
