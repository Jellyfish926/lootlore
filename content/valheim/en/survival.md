# Valheim Survival & Building Guides

Pick the guide that matches your current bottleneck. Always running out of food? Start with food and farming. Always short on resistance supplies? Read the mead guide. Losing too much time on the road? Read portals and sailing. Base layout and the Rested buff are worth checking together.

## How does food work in Valheim?

Per the community-run Valheim Wiki, you start with 25 health and 50 base stamina, and food raises both for a limited time. Up to three different foods can be active at once, each buff shrinks steadily until the food is digested, and starvation is not possible. The wiki's numbers for five Meadows foods are below; the [food guide](/valheim/food/) explains how to fill the three slots by activity.

| Meadows food (per the wiki) | Health | Stamina | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raspberries | 7 | 20 | 10 minutes |
| Mushroom | 15 | 15 | 15 minutes |
| Grilled neck tail | 25 | 8 | 20 minutes |
| Cooked boar meat | 30 | 10 | 20 minutes |
| Cooked deer meat | 35 | 12 | 20 minutes |

In the wiki's table, berries lean towards stamina and cooked meat towards health, so a mixed plate covers both from day one. You cannot eat a second helping of the same food until the first is partly digested, which the wiki says shows as a flashing icon. Once foraging stops keeping up, a [farm](/valheim/farming/) keeps the slots filled.

## How do you get the Rested buff?

Per the community wiki, you get Resting by sitting by a fire with no enemies near, or by standing near a fire while sheltered. The wiki defines Shelter as a roof plus 80% cover and says Resting will not start while you are Wet. After 20 uninterrupted seconds, it adds, Resting turns into Rested.

| Rested detail (per the wiki) | Value |
|---|---|
| Health regen | +50% |
| Stamina regen | +100% |
| Eitr regen | +100% |
| Skill XP gain | +50% |
| Duration | 7 minutes plus comfort level; 8 minutes at comfort 1 |
| Normal maximum | Comfort 22, or 29 minutes |

Per the wiki, comfort sets how long Rested lasts, and the XP bonus does not change with it. The [Rested guide](/valheim/rested/) covers why the buff fails to appear, and the [first base guide](/valheim/first-base/) covers fires, roofs and smoke.

## What do Wet, Cold and Freezing do?

Both community wikis list all three as cutting health and stamina regeneration, and the table uses the figures the two agree on.

| Effect | When it applies (per the wiki) | Health regen | Stamina regen | What counters it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wet | Rain or being in water; lasts 120 seconds | -25% | -15% | Shelter, or standing next to a fire |
| Cold | At night, or sheltered while Freezing | -50% | -25% | Fire, or frost resistance |
| Freezing | The Mountain, the Moder fight, the Drake event | -100% | -60% | Frost resistance, fire; Shelter reduces it to Cold |

Freezing also deals 1 damage per second, and the wiki says frost resistance only counters it while you are not Wet. Both wikis also list Wet as making you weak to Frost and Lightning, and as applied constantly in the [Swamp](/valheim/swamp/) unless you are under a roof. For the [Mountains](/valheim/mountains/), the wiki lists Frost resistance mead and the Wolf fur cape among the counters; the [mead guide](/valheim/mead/) covers brewing.

## What do you lose when you die?

Per the wiki, you leave a tombstone holding all your equipment, and on the default death penalty you lose 5% of your total levels in each skill. You respawn at the bed you last slept in, or at the Sacrificial Stones if you have none.

| After a death (per the wiki) | Lasts | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Tombstone | Does not expire | Holds your equipment at the spot you died |
| No skill drain | 600 seconds (10 minutes) | Prevents further skill loss from new deaths |
| Corpse run | 50 seconds | Run and jump stamina usage -75%, max carry weight +150, resistant to Blunt, Slash and Pierce |

According to the wiki's Corpse run page, the effect only starts once the grave is completely emptied. The [death recovery guide](/valheim/death-recovery/) turns these numbers into a rescue plan.

## What should you do in your first days?

Follow the order in the [first-day guide](/valheim/first-day/): gather, make shelter work before you make it big, aim for steady food, and do one last check before dark. A fire under a roof is the first survival upgrade: by the wiki's rules above, it gives Rested and dries you off. A bed comes next, since the wiki ties your respawn point to the bed you last slept in.

## What do these guides assume?

These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
