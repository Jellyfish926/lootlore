3:T182b,<p><em>Game version checked: 1.0.12 (10 September 2026 patch). Built from Hound13's Steam announcements and the community wiki's faction pages, named inline.</em></p>
<p>Aria is the only hero on this roster whose <strong>basic attack</strong> was rebuilt after launch, and the only one whose skill descriptions were then wrong about it for a week. If you last played her in August, her core string is not the string you remember.</p>

<h2>Two ailments, both scarce-adjacent</h2>
<p>The clearest official statement of her role is from the <strong>Tag Combo Preview</strong> post of 1 May 2026, where Hound13 walks a three-hero relay: Castella opens with Break and Stun, Lute follows with Stun and Knockdown, and then "<strong>Aria, who loves firepower, stacks Fire and Airborne status effects with her bombs, then hands the offensive opportunity back to Castella.</strong>"</p>
<p>That single sentence is the source for both of her ailments. <strong>Airborne</strong> puts her in what our <a href="/heroes/ailment-coverage/">ailment coverage page</a> calls the most self-sufficient chain in the game, alongside Astria and Eileen — three heroes who all launch, so a Signal armed by Airborne is never waiting on a specific teammate. <strong>Fire</strong> is the opposite: thin, and adjacent to the Burn access that makes <a href="/heroes/kalien-guide/">Kalien</a> an element key rather than a preference.</p>
<p><strong>Not confirmed:</strong> whether Aria's <em>Fire</em> and Kalien's <em>Burn</em> arm the same Signal. Hound13 labels them differently and has never published a mapping. Our <a href="/heroes/ailment-coverage/">ailment coverage page</a> flags the same gap. If they are distinct, she is the roster's only Fire applier.</p>
<h2>The 1.0.11 rework, in Hound13's words</h2>
<p>Patch <strong>1.0.11</strong> (3 September 2026) gives her three lines in its Balance section — more than any hero except Tarte:</p>
<blockquote>- Basic attack changed from <strong>5-hit to 3-hit</strong> - Final basic attack now <strong>ends more quickly</strong> - When switching to another hero while using <strong>[Air Bomb]</strong>, the bomb is now <strong>thrown immediately</strong></blockquote>
<p>Read those together and the intent is legible. A five-hit basic chain on a hero whose value is stacking two ailments and handing off is a commitment; cutting it to three hits with a faster recovery shortens the window where you are locked out of switching. The [Air Bomb] change closes the other half of the same problem: previously, tagging out mid-cast presumably ate the bomb. Now the bomb goes out and you leave. For a tag-team game built on Switching Signals — the <a href="/guides/status-ailments-and-signals/">combat guide</a> explains why that matters — this is a quality-of-life change disguised as a balance note.</p>
<p>She was one of six heroes in that pass. Hound13's 28 August patch preview names them: "Hero balance (Tarte, Lute, Aria, Roxy, Astria, Kalsion)."</p>
<h2>The description that lagged the patch</h2>
<p>Patch <strong>1.0.12</strong> (10 September 2026), Localization section:</p>
<blockquote>Fixed an issue where the description of the number of attacks that fire <strong>[Bomb Arrows]</strong> in Aria's basic attack skill description was not being updated.</blockquote>
<p>So for the week between 3 and 10 September, the game told you a hit count it was no longer doing. Two things worth taking from that. First, <strong>[Bomb Arrows]</strong> is the name of her basic attack skill — the only basic-attack skill name Hound13 has printed for any hero, which finally explains "bombs" on a character the roster page lists under area firepower. Second, this is a live argument for the warning in the player-measured <em>Damage 101</em> guide we quote on our <a href="/heroes/lute-guide/">Lute page</a>: displayed skill numbers in this game are not automatically current.</p>
<h2>The camera note, and why it is a real change</h2>
<p>Patch <strong>1.0.10</strong> (20 August 2026) removed camera rotation sensitivity changes on a list of specific hero skills. Aria's entry is <strong>[Air Bomb]</strong> — the same skill 1.0.11 then touched. She shares that list with Alex, Astria, Castella, Dana, Johnny, Ornette and Tarte.</p>
<p>If you played her before 20 August and felt the camera go heavy mid-bomb, that was deliberate and it is gone.</p>
<h2>Where she sits on a team</h2>
<p>Her faction is <strong>Johnny's Mercenaries</strong> — Lute, Johnny, Castella and Aria, per the community wiki — which is also the exact cast of Hound13's own tag-combo demonstration. That is the closest thing to an officially endorsed team in this game, and it is worth noting that it was built to show ailment breadth, not damage: Break, Stun, Knockdown, Fire and Airborne across three bodies.</p>
<p>Her unlock is an Exchange purchase at the flat price every Exchange hero costs — one Fate's Invitation, per our <a href="/heroes/unlock-planner/">unlock planner</a>. Since price carries no information here, the question is only whether you need a second launcher or a Fire source.</p>
<h2>What this page cannot tell you</h2>
<p>Her Karma trait, her Awakening effects, her Signal Skill names, her Active Skill list beyond [Air Bomb] and [Bomb Arrows], and her post-1.0.11 hit numbers. Nobody has published measured skill percentages for her the way one player did for Lute and Dana.
