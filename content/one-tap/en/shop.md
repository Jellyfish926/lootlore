# One Tap Robux Shop: 97 Products in 13 Groups

One Tap's Robux shop held 97 developer products in the Roblox records we read on 10 October 2026, priced from 5 to 10,000 Robux. Forty-nine have Case in the name. The other 48 include Gems and Sun Points packs, Premium Battlepass and Skip products, Xp Boost products, bundles, limited-named items and four donations. None has a description.

That last point shapes this whole page. Every price below is the Robux price at the time of that read, and every name is copied exactly. The groups are ours: we sorted the products by the words in their names, because Roblox's records carry no category field. Where a name does not explain itself, the product's function is marked not confirmed.

The thirteen counts add up to 97, and the thirteen sums add up to 43,177 Robux. That figure is arithmetic and no bundle at that price is in the records. ## What are the donation products and the product named 500?

Four One Tap records are donations, and each costs the amount in its name: 10 Robux Donation, 100 Robux Donation, 1000 Robux Donation and 10000 Robux Donation. All four were created in the same second on 6 March 2026. No record promises anything in return, so treat them as a tip to the developer.

One record is named just 500 and costs 5 Robux. It was created on 28 January 2026, about two minutes before the 5-Robux product named Case, and both were edited within seconds of each other early the next day. What 500 refers to is not confirmed. It could be an amount of in-game currency, and we will not print that as fact without a source.

## How did we read the One Tap shop records, and what is not confirmed?

The One Tap developer product list came back in one response of 97 records with no further page to fetch. All 97 were marked for sale, none showed a discount and none had text in its description field. All 97 also share one icon, a plain grey cube, so there is no product art to show here.

The most common price is 199 Robux, on 12 records. Fifty of the 97 records show identical creation and edit times. The other 47 have been edited at least once, and an edit date does not say what was changed.

Still not confirmed after this read:

- what any bundle, limited-named item or currency pack contains;
- which records the in-game shop offers right now;
- whether prices inside the game match the record on the day you buy.

Our advice, not a rule of the game: read the purchase prompt before you confirm, because every one of these 97 records gives you a name and a price and nothing else. If a price here no longer matches the game, the [author page](/one-tap/author/) explains how to report it.
