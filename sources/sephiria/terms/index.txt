<h2>Link freely, quote with credit, do not republish</h2>
<p>Every guide here may be linked from anywhere, including deep links to a specific section. Quoting a passage or a table excerpt is welcome with a credit and a link back to the page. What is not permitted: republishing whole pages, mirroring the site, or bulk-extracting its content for republication elsewhere, automated or otherwise. Where a page cites community measurements, the original measurer's credit must survive any quotation.</p>
<h2>The game is TEAM HORAY's</h2>
<p><em>Sephiria</em>, Bunnyville, its bunnies, bosses, artifact names, screenshots and everything else from inside the game belong to TEAM HORAY. This wiki is an independent fan project using that material to identify and discuss the game — the store screenshots on our pages carry attribution in their captions, and takedown requests from the rights holder would be honoured without argument.</p>
<h2>A patch can outdate any page at any time</h2>
<p>This wiki tracks a game that shipped six version numbers in one eight-day stretch. We stamp pages with the version and date they were checked against, but between our reviews the live game can and does move. Content is therefore provided as-is: run choices, purchases and multiplayer sessions built on our pages are your decisions, and an out-of-date page is a correction request, not a liability claim. When a page and the current build disagree, believe the build and <a href="/contact/">tell us</a>.</p>
<h2>Advertising and outbound links</h2>
<p>The site shows third-party display advertising and links out — to Steam, to TEAM HORAY's announcements, to community guides. Advertisers do not see or shape content (the <a href="/editorial-policy/">editorial policy</a> explains the wall), and linked sites answer for themselves. Cookie and data specifics live in the <a href="/privacy-policy/">privacy policy</a>.</p>
