    Animal Daycare Shop: Lamb Coins, Classes and Robux Items

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-09-30 · Checked on version 2026-09-30 update

    Scope: Product names, prices, descriptions and creation dates from the Roblox developer products API on 2026-09-30; class names, class effects and the contents of Boss and Starter Pack are not published

    Key points

    • The game has no game passes; all 25 paid items are Robux products, priced from 19 to 1,499 Robux.
    • Lamb Coins come in packs of 50, 175, 550 and 2,500; the 2,500 pack costs about 0.60 Robux per coin against 0.98 for the smallest.
    • Classes come in 1-Star, 2-Star and 3-Star tiers, and unlocking Levels 2 and 3 costs 498, 648 or 948 Robux in total.
    • Coin packs and Boss can be bought as gifts for another player.

    Animal Daycare (Anomaly) sells no game passes. Everything paid is one of 25 Robux products: items and refills for a shift, two revives, four Lamb Coin packs, level unlocks for 1-Star to 3-Star classes, gift versions, a product called Boss and a limited Starter Pack. Prices run from 19 to 1,499 Robux, and none was discounted on 30 September 2026.

    All names, prices and descriptions come from the Roblox developer products API. Names are copied as the store shows them, including the spelling "Restore Coffie".

    Which items help during a shift?

    Six products act inside a shift. They are developer products, which Roblox lets you buy again and again, unlike a game pass.

    ProductRobuxOfficial description
    Restore Coffie19Refill the coffee and make it available again.
    Get Toy Hammer19Toy Hammer taken or still recharging? Grab another one instantly — no waiting required!
    Lunchbox29Contains 4 snacks. Each use restores +15 Calm. Perfect for stressful shifts!
    Water Gun (20 Bullets)99Contains 15 shots. Use it to scare away ghosts and protect the daycare! 💦
    Revive Yourself99Revive yourself to continue playing with your friends!
    Revive a Friend99Revive your friend to continue the game together!

    Note the Water Gun: its name says 20 bullets, its description says 15 shots. We report both because the store does; test it in-game before counting on either number. What each item does beyond these lines is covered, as far as official data goes, in the how-to-play guide.

    Which Lamb Coins pack is the best value?

    Lamb Coins are a currency sold for Robux. The store describes them as coins "that can be spent on anything" and suggests them for buying "a new class". Bigger packs are cheaper per coin:

    PackRobuxCoins per RobuxRobux per coin
    50 Lamb Coins491.020.98
    175 Lamb Coins1491.170.85
    550 Lamb Coins3991.380.73
    2500 Lamb Coins1,4991.670.60

    The last two columns are our arithmetic. The 2,500 pack is about 39% cheaper per coin than the 50 pack — but only worth it if you already know what you want to spend coins on. Class prices in Lamb Coins are not listed anywhere official, so we cannot tell you how many coins a class costs.

    How much does it cost to level up a class?

    Classes come in three star tiers, and each has a Level 2 and a Level 3 you can unlock with Robux. The store says these unlock a level "of the selected class", which implies you choose a class first.

    Class tierLevel 2Level 3Both levels
    1-Star Class149349498
    2-Star Class199449648
    3-Star Class299649948

    One quirk: the 2-Star and 3-Star Level 2 products are described as unlocking "the third level", while the 1-Star Level 2 product says "the second level". We list them by their names. The 1-Star unlocks were added on 11 September 2026; the 2-Star and 3-Star ones followed on 23 September.

    What the classes are called, how many exist and what each level changes are not published. We will not guess at them.

    What are Boss and the Starter Pack?

    Both are sold without a clear description of what you get.

    ProductRobuxOfficial descriptionAdded
    Boss349Do you want to be so cool like him? I do.11 September
    Starter Pack (Limited)59Great deal for new players. Limited BTW! :D23 September

    The wording ("him", and "this guy" on the gift version) suggests Boss is a character, but whether it is a class, a skin or something else is not stated. Starter Pack contents and how long "Limited" lasts are also not stated.

    Can you buy items for a friend?

    Yes, for coins and Boss. The gift versions cost the same as the originals:

    Gift productRobux
    Gift 50 Lamb Coins49
    Gift 175 Lamb Coins149
    Gift 550 Lamb Coins399
    Gift 2500 Lamb Coins1,499
    Gift Boss349

    The coin gifts were added on 16 September 2026 and Gift Boss on 18 September. How a gift is delivered in-game is not described.

    What are Reroll Shop and Buy Item from Shop?

    Two products refer to an in-game store. Reroll Shop (29 Robux) says: "Didn't find the item you were looking for? Update items in the store!" — so the store's stock changes and can be refreshed. Buy Item from Shop (49 Robux) says: "Don't have enough cash for the item you need? Just buy it!" That suggests the store normally takes an in-game currency the description calls cash. Whether cash and Lamb Coins are the same thing is not stated.

    What should you buy first?

    Our suggestion, not a rule of the game: nothing, until you have played a few shifts. The badge figures show about 61 second-night badges for every 100 first-night badges (totals, not a tracked group). If a shift keeps failing on one problem, the cheapest items target it — a coffee refill or a spare Toy Hammer at 19 Robux, a Lunchbox at 29. Leave coin packs and class levels until you know which class you want. There are no game passes to weigh against these; the game info page covers who runs the store.
