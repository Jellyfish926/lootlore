# Valheim Deep North Guides

Read the overall route first, then the Intricate Key page when you hit that wall, then prepare for Kall. The three guides cover progression order, the key item and the final fight, so you stop bouncing between moulds and finished castings.

## Pick a guide by problem

### [Deep North progression guide](/valheim/deep-north/)

Sorts out the resource and event order of the 1.0 Deep North, linking Ember Charge, Petrified Tissue, Bloodgold, moulds, keys and the invasion into one route you can follow.

### [Intricate Key crafting guide](/valheim/intricate-key/)

Craft the Deep North's single-use key step by step: the difference between the mould and the casting, the workstation requirements, and why you need several for the dungeons that follow.

### [Kall Fimbulbringer boss guide](/valheim/kall/)

A framework for the three-phase fight: in phase one the boss attacks directly; in phase two it hides inside an invulnerable ice block and summons avatars of the previous seven bosses in turn; in phase three it returns with stronger moves and elemental damage. Covers entry prep, stamina management and how to review a wipe.

## Scope

These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
