# All 11 Roblox Stock Exchange 2 Badges and How to Get Them

Roblox Stock Exchange 2 has 11 Roblox badges. Nine mark trading milestones, from your first trade to a net worth above $10,000,000, one welcomes you to the game, and one was given for sharing a server with a game owner. On 8 October 2026, Welcome had been awarded 2,288,320 times and First Rebirth 42,936 times.

Everything on this page comes from the [Roblox badge API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/10495391267/badges?limit=100), read at 03:47 UTC that day. Unlock texts are copied exactly as the developer wrote them.

Ten of the eleven were created on 13 and 14 July 2026, the game's first two days. Roblox describes a badge as "a special award you can gift players when they meet a goal within your game", and here the goals are spelled out in one line each.

## Which badges do you get in your first session?

Welcome to Roblox Stock Exchange 2 arrives for playing. First Trade follows for nearly everyone: 90.1% of Welcome holders have it. First Profit needs a trade that ends in the green, and 68.6% have managed that.

The gap between those two is worth a second look. Roughly one in four players who placed a trade has never had the profit badge. If your first trade is a loss, you are in large company. The [how-to-play guide](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/how-to-play/) suggests an order for the first few steps.

## Why are shorting and futures badges rarer?

First Short and First Futures Trade need you to try shorting or futures; the low totals suggest many players have not. Over the game's whole life the shares are low. In the single day before our check they were much higher:

| Badge | Share of all Welcome awards | Awarded in the past day | Past-day awards per 100 Welcome awards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First Trade | 90.1% | 46,499 | 97.9 |
| First Profit | 68.6% | 46,436 | 97.7 |
| First Short | 26.9% | 30,247 | 63.7 |
| First Futures Trade | 9.4% | 19,052 | 40.1 |
| Hundred Trades | 13.1% | 8,331 | 17.5 |

The last column divides each badge's past-day awards by the Welcome badge's past-day awards (47,508). On that day, First Short and First Futures Trade were awarded 63.7 and 40.1 times per 100 Welcome awards. The data does not show whether these went to new or returning players, and we do not know why the rate was so much higher than the lifetime shares. An interface overhaul was announced for 3 October 2026; the badge data cannot show whether that is related.

## How hard are the net-worth badges?

Millionaire asks for a total net worth over $1,000,000 and had 100,099 awards, or 4.4% of Welcome holders. $10,000,000 Net Worth had 39,765. Put together, about 40 in 100 players who reach the first million go on to ten.

Hundred Trades ("You completed your first 100 trades!") is the volume badge, held by 13.1%. The text counts completed trades; it does not mention profit. None of the 13 passes in the [game pass guide](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/gamepasses/) is described as speeding up a badge.

## What do First Algo Bot and First Rebirth need?

| Badge | Official text | Total awarded | Awarded in the past day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First Algo Bot | You created your first algorithmic trading bot! | 50,910 | 1,404 |
| First Rebirth | You rebirthed for the first time! | 42,936 | 394 |

Both need systems the developer has not documented. The level or net worth that unlocks a bot is not published, and neither are the cost or effects of a rebirth. The store does sell a product called Deploy Algo Bot for 149 Robux; the [algo bot guide](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/algo-bots/) lists it with the three other bot products and explains what their names do and do not prove.

One detail from the totals: First Rebirth (42,936) has been awarded more often than $10,000,000 Net Worth (39,765). So at least some players rebirthed without ever holding the ten-million badge.

## Can you still get Met the game owners!?

Its official text reads: "Wow, 150% profit. You were in a server with one of the game owners". The badge was created on 15 August 2026, a month after the others. It had been awarded 1,305 times in total and 0 times in the day before our check.

The text says what happened, not how to repeat it. You would need to be in a server at the same time as an owner, and nothing public says when that happens. The badge is still enabled on Roblox, so it has not been retired. Official events are where the developer announces things, and they are listed on the [updates page](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/updates/).

## Why do the totals keep changing?

Because they are live counters. Between two reads eight minutes apart on 8 October, the Welcome total rose by 266. Use the shares to compare badges with each other. They are not odds, and a count of badge awards is a different measure from the 5,093,566 visits Roblox recorded.
