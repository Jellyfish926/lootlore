The headline numbers: 24.0% of Steam players have beaten the final boss (“The End of Reincarnation”), but only 2.7% have the 100% capstone (“Duty Fulfilled”) and 3.0% have started NG+ (“Reincarnated”) — full completion demands New Game Plus and the collection grind, not just the credits.

How completion tracking works on each platform

Beast of Reincarnation launches simultaneously on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC — and on PC it ships through two storefronts, Steam and the Microsoft Store. That detail matters more than usual for trophy hunters, because the game is a confirmed Xbox Play Anywhere title: an Xbox purchase (or a Game Pass Ultimate install, since the game arrives on Game Pass day one) shares both save data and achievement progress between Xbox console and the Microsoft Store PC version. The Steam version keeps its own separate achievement track, and the PS5 version lives entirely inside Sony's trophy ecosystem.

How to read the list

The story spine. The boss-seal achievements step down cleanly — Rangifer's End 53.4%, Lacerta's End 43.7%, Taurus's End 34.6%, Corvus's End 28.2%, then the late-game cluster (Ursa, Shidou & Nue, Erymanth, Kunai & Kirin) all landing between 24–25% before The End of Reincarnation at 24.0%. Translation: the early chapters filter out almost half of all players, and nearly everyone who clears Chapter 7 finishes the game. The boss guide maps each of these fights.

The companionship track. Man's Best Friend (63.7%) through Closest Companion (38.2%), Bestie (34.1%) and The Perfect Pal (6.6%) track Koo's Rapport system — the 6.6% endpoint says maxing the bond is a long-haul commitment, so pet the dog early and often (see the Koo guide).

The completionist tier. Everything below 6% — the Blade Collector → Blade Enthusiast → Blademaster weapon chain, Know-It-All, Munitions Master, the Giant Tree growth stages, Floral Profusion — is collection and upgrade content that runs past the credits. With Reincarnated (NG+) at 3.0% and Duty Fulfilled at 2.7%, plan on New Game Plus for the capstone.

The oddities worth flagging. Manual Maniac (12.5%) and Wandering Duelist (9.8%) sit well below their neighbors — optional-content unlocks that are easy to walk past. If you are playing for 100%, look these up before your late chapters rather than after.

Setting up a clean completion run

  • One playthrough will not finish it. With the NG+ achievement at 3.0% and the capstone at 2.7%, the list is built to span two runs. Do not sweat missing collection thresholds on run one.
  • Choose your storefront first. Play Anywhere shares saves and achievements between Xbox console and the Microsoft Store PC version; Steam and PS5 are separate tracks. Split playtime across devices only inside the Xbox/Microsoft Store pairing.
  • Work the Rapport and farming systems as you go. The sub-6% achievements (The Perfect Pal, the Giant Tree stages, Floral Profusion) are slow-burn systems — feeding them from Chapter 1 costs nothing; starting them post-credits is a grind.
  • The parry achievements come naturally. Entangled (56.9%) and its relatives track systems the game forces you to learn anyway — no detour needed.

FAQ

Does Beast of Reincarnation have a platinum trophy?

The PS5 list mirrors the 46-achievement structure with a platinum on top, as with every full-priced PS5 release. The Steam list shown above is the one with public global statistics.

Do Game Pass players earn achievements?

Yes. Beast of Reincarnation is on Game Pass Ultimate from day one, and Game Pass copies earn achievements and Gamerscore identically to purchased copies.

Do achievements transfer between Xbox and PC?

Between Xbox console and the Microsoft Store PC version, yes — Play Anywhere shares saves and achievements automatically. The Steam version is a separate track, and PS5 trophies never mix with either.

Will difficulty settings affect trophies?

No unlock on the list names a difficulty setting, and community reporting agrees the list can be completed on any mode — consistent with the director's stated intent that difficulty be a comfort choice, not a completion gate. See the difficulty page.

Is there a demo to warm up on before a completion run?

No — four weeks after launch there is still no demo on any platform. First Fire at a 92.6% unlock rate says nearly everyone gets through the opening, so just start.
