# How to Spot Impostors in Animal Daycare (Anomaly)

Check every arrival before you let it in. The developer's description of Animal Daycare says to inspect each visitor, search for suspicious clues and use the cameras, because "some visitors may not be real children". A community video adds three tells: devil wings, an animal missing from the camera, and a silent child who walks straight in.

Only the first two sentences above are official. Everything described as "in the video" comes from one community video and has not been confirmed in-game by us.

## What does the developer say about impostors?

| Official source | Exact wording |
| --- | --- |
| Game description | "Inspect every new arrival, search for suspicious clues, and decide who can enter." |
| Game description | "But be careful — some visitors may not be real children." |
| Game description | "Inspect visitors using cameras" · "Find hidden impostors" |
| Imposter Hunter badge | "Expose 25 imposters" |

The official data spells it both ways: "impostors" in the description, "imposters" on the badge. The [how-to-play guide](/animal-daycare/how-to-play/) covers the rest of the shift.

## Which warning signs does the community video show?

These come from the auto-generated captions of [Zac Worthy's beginner guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B1IY_XEyedU), uploaded on 20 September 2026. We have not checked them ourselves.

| Warning sign shown | What the video does |
| --- | --- |
| The arrival has devil wings | Refuses entry and sets off the alarm straight away |
| The animal cannot be seen on the security camera | Named as another anomaly to watch out for |
| A child says nothing at the desk and simply walks inside | Uses the Toy Hammer quickly; the narrator warns it may attack the other children if you are too late |

In the same video, children arrive at the desk and ask to stay the night before you decide.

## How rare is Imposter Hunter?

It had been awarded 20,744 times by 30 September 2026, against 3,499,052 for surviving the first night — about 6 for every 1,000 first-night survivors. The [badge guide](/animal-daycare/badges/) compares it with the survival badges.
