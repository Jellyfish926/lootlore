# American Plains Mudding Game Passes: All 8 with Prices

American Plains Mudding has eight game passes on sale, priced from 195 to 490 Robux; all eight together cost 2,470 Robux. Four change spawning (slot counts, slot categories or where trailers can be spawned), three unlock premium vehicles, trailers and customization, and one upgrades private servers. Each has a gift version at the same price.

Names, prices and descriptions below are copied from the [Roblox game pass data](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/2783797267/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100), checked on 9 October 2026. None carried a discount. The same data holds six more records that were not for sale; they are listed further down.

## What does each American Plains Mudding pass do?

We have sorted the eight into three groups. The grouping is ours; the descriptions are the developer's.

**Spawning**

| Pass | Robux | Official description | Added |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 Vehicle Spawner | 490 | Unlock the ability to spawn 4 vehicles! | 6 Jan 2024 |
| Anywhere Trailer Spawner | 390 | Allows you to spawn trailers from anywhere! | 16 May 2022 |
| 4 Trailer Spawner | 290 | Unlock the ability to spawn 4 trailers! | 17 May 2022 |
| Any Slot Spawner | 290 | Removes trailer and vehicle slot categories. So anything can be spawned in any of the 4-8 slots owned. E.g., all slots could be filled with vehicles, with a cap of 8 if 4 trailer and 4 vehicle spawner gamepasses are owned. (Formerly Premium Rigs) | 4 Aug 2022 |

**Premium content**

| Pass | Robux | Official description | Added |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premium Customization | 390 | Get exclusive access to premium customization features (five are listed; see below) | 10 Feb 2025 |
| Premium Vehicles | 230 | Get access to exclusive vehicles! (Items within this gamepass are subject to change over time) | 14 Aug 2021 |
| Premium Trailers | 195 | Get access to exclusive trailers! (Items within this gamepass are subject to change over time) | 22 Jun 2023 |

**Private servers**

| Pass | Robux | Official description | Added |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Premium Private Servers | 195 | Improve The Private Server Experience! (doubled spawn slots and three commands are listed) | 16 Jun 2023 |

The four spawning passes add up to 1,460 Robux, the three premium passes to 815, and the private server pass is 195. [Roblox's Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/passes) describe a pass as "a one-time Robux fee to access special privileges inside your game".

Neither Premium Vehicles nor Premium Trailers names what it contains, and both warn that the contents can change. We cannot list the vehicles inside them; the [vehicle overview](/american-plains-mudding/vehicles/) collects the types the developer has named elsewhere.

## What does Premium Customization include?

It is the one pass whose description is a feature list. After "Get exclusive access to premium customization features such as:" it gives five items:

- "Ability To Customize Anywhere"
- "Ability To Customize Attachments"
- "Enhanced Window Customization"
- "Enhanced Exhaust Customization"
- "Exclusive Attachments"

The description then makes a promise about price: "This price will continue to rise as we add more content to the game pass. We have many things in store that will be added to this." It was 390 Robux when we checked. If you already know you want it, the developer is telling you that waiting will cost more.

What the list does not tell you is what free customization covers. The 10/3 notes mention attachments for the new ATV, "Front and Rear Cargo Box Attachments" and a "Windshield Attachment", without saying whether they need this pass.

## Are the gift versions cheaper?

No. Every pass on sale has a developer product named after it with "[GIFT]" in front, and all eight cost exactly what the pass costs.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [GIFT] 4 Vehicle Spawner | 490 | 490 | 14 Aug 2024 |
| [GIFT] Anywhere Trailer Spawner | 390 | 390 | 14 Aug 2024 |
| [GIFT] Premium Customization | 390 | 390 | 15 Aug 2025 |
| [GIFT] 4 Trailer Spawner | 290 | 290 | 14 Aug 2024 |
| [GIFT] Any Slot Spawner | 290 | 290 | 14 Aug 2024 |
| [GIFT] Premium Vehicles | 230 | 230 | 14 Aug 2024 |
| [GIFT] Premium Trailers | 195 | 195 | 14 Aug 2024 |
| [GIFT] Premium Private Servers | 195 | 195 | 15 Aug 2025 |

None of the eight has a description, so the data does not explain how you pick the player who receives the gift. An [event listing](/american-plains-mudding/updates/) from 16 August 2025 mentions a "gifting gamepass" among that week's additions, one day after the gift products for Premium Customization and Premium Private Servers were created.

[Roblox's docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products) define a developer product as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once". That is probably why the gifts are products and not passes: you may want to buy the same gift again for another player.

## Which American Plains Mudding pass should you buy first?

Our suggestions, based only on what each pass says it does:

- **You keep running out of vehicle slots:** 4 Vehicle Spawner (490). It is the dearest pass, so be sure slots are what hold you back.
- **You tow a lot:** 4 Trailer Spawner (290) unlocks spawning four trailers. Anywhere Trailer Spawner (390) lets you spawn trailers "from anywhere". The [spawn slot guide](/american-plains-mudding/spawning/) compares all four.
- **You want one category only:** Any Slot Spawner (290) lets every slot hold a vehicle, or every slot a trailer.
- **You build show trucks:** Premium Customization (390), bought sooner not later, given the stated price rises.
- **You host private sessions:** Premium Private Servers (195) doubles slots there and adds commands. See the [private server page](/american-plains-mudding/private-servers/).
- **You want more to drive:** Premium Vehicles (230) and Premium Trailers (195) are the cheapest passes that add vehicles or trailers, but you are buying contents the developer does not list.
