# Author: Jellyfi, Editor of the +1 Stone Skipping Guides

Jellyfi is the editorial byline on {{BRAND}}, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the [+1 Stone Skipping guides](/stone-skipping/) on this site. That means checking every fact against official Roblox data, dating each page, and correcting errors when readers report them. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

## How these guides are researched

The game is on Roblox, so the most reliable facts come from Roblox itself: the official game description, the game's public statistics and content rating, its game pass and developer product lists, its official event listing, its official art and the developer group's own page. Fan wikis and code lists are used to find leads, never as the source for a fact.

![The +1 Stone Skipping icon: a Roblox character in a black jacket throws a grey stone that bounces three times with +1 at each splash, above a purple banner reading WORLD 4](icon "Every guide in this section is published under one editorial byline")

That ranking decides what gets published. For this game the developer has written no descriptions for store items, so the [Robux product list](/stone-skipping/shop/) gives names and prices and marks every effect as unknown or as our reading of a name. Zone names and egg odds appear only on fan sites today; pet boosts and rebirth costs are not published in any official source we can read, so they stay out until they can be checked in the live game or in an official post.

## How codes are handled

Codes are only listed after being seen in an official source for the game. When a code leaves that source it is marked expired and kept on the page with the date it was noticed, rather than deleted. On 2 October 2026 no code could be confirmed, so there is no codes page yet. The [community page](/stone-skipping/community/) shows where we looked.

## How to read the dates on a page

Under the heading of each article, "Last reviewed" is the date the text was last checked against its sources, and "Checked on version" names the game update it was checked against. We found no version numbers for this game, so that label carries the date of the latest update Roblox reports for the experience. Prices, counts and event times, such as those on the [updates page](/stone-skipping/updates/), are snapshots from the check date.

## Reporting an error

If a price, a number or a rule does not match the game you are playing, email us through the [Contact](/contact) page and include the page link. The site-wide writing and correction rules are on the [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy) page.
