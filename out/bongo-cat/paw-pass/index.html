    Bongo Cat Paw Pass and Bongo Coins Explained

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Paw Pass and Bongo Coins rules from the developer's Steam announcements of September 1 and October 1, 2026; individual pass items are not listed

    Key points

    • The Paw Pass is a monthly themed reward track with a free lane and a paid premium lane; rewards have no deadline.
    • You progress by tapping and clicking as usual, and claiming a reward does not spend taps.
    • Each lane pays 100 Bongo Coins per pass. The developer says they can be used later to buy a premium pass for 500 coins or supporter cosmetics; when spending opens is not confirmed.
    • Paw Coins is a fan wiki's label: no post in the game's Steam news feed uses it, and whether it is the same currency as Bongo Coins is not confirmed.

    The Paw Pass is Bongo Cat's monthly reward track, with free and premium lanes. Each lane pays 100 Bongo Coins, which the developer says "can be used later" to buy the premium pass for 500 coins or supporter cosmetics; spending is not confirmed as live. Paw Coins is a fan wiki's label, not confirmed as the same currency.

    Is the Paw Pass free, and what does premium cost?

    The game itself is free to play on Steam (Steam app details API). The developer's launch post says your free pass ticket is claimed automatically as long as you open the game at least once a month, so opening the game once in a month gets you that month's free lane. What happens to the free lane of a month in which you never opened the game is not confirmed. The paid version exists "to support further development" and "includes more cosmetics, chests and emotes". It needs a Paw Pass Ticket. The announcement calls the item a Paw Pass Ticket; the listing we recorded in the Steam Item Store on September 29, 2026 was named Paw Pass Token, at US$4.99, and whether these are two names for the same item is not confirmed. The current price and regional prices are not confirmed either.

    To go premium, click Get Pass, get the Paw Pass Ticket in the Steam Store, then click Get Pass again to redeem it. We could not reload the store page on October 10, 2026, so check the price shown there before you pay.

    How do you level up the Paw Pass?

    There is no separate XP bar to grind. The launch post says you earn rewards "by just playing the game as you always do and reaching its milestones", and that no taps are consumed when you claim one. That is the opposite of chests, which subtract taps from your counter when you buy one; the tapping basics are in how to play.

    1. Open the inventory and press the ticket button at the bottom.
    2. In the "All Passes" list, click a pass to activate it; taps and clicks then count for that pass.
    3. Claim each reward as its milestone is reached.
    4. When you finish a pass, the next one is selected automatically.

    Steps 2 and 4 come from the October 2026 post, which added these instructions. Neither announcement says how many taps a milestone needs or how many milestones a pass has, so both numbers are not confirmed. Offline play is half supported: you cannot redeem rewards without an internet connection, but "your tap progress will still count towards the pass".

    What rewards has each monthly pass had?

    The official Steam news feed has announced two passes as of October 10, 2026. Dates are the days the announcements were posted.

    PassAnnouncedThemeWhat the announcement lists
    First Paw PassSep 1, 2026CircusThemed skins, hats, emotes and cosmetic/emote chests "with up to legendary rarity"; 100 Bongo Coins per lane
    Paw Pass: Halloween EditionOct 1, 2026Halloween"all sorts of skins, hats, emotes" and chests with guaranteed rarity up to legendary; three UI themes

    Irox Games, the developer of Bongo Cat, has not published an item-by-item reward list for either pass, so this page does not name individual skins or hats. A fan wiki has started one for Circus, but it cannot be checked against an official list. UI themes from a pass show up in the BongoDex under the Paw Pass Collection as well as the UI themes collection. For what Legendary means in drop terms, see the odds table in hats and skins.

    Does a Paw Pass expire if you miss the month?

    No. The launch post calls it "not like any other pass, as the rewards are claimable forever and do not have a deadline". The theme changes monthly, but an older pass stays in the game after its month is over.

    To pick up an earlier one, select it in the "All Passes" list and click "Get Pass" or "Unlock". Late starters are covered too: the developer says "you can always get a Paw Pass Ticket to unlock past passes". What is not confirmed is the free lane of a month in which you never opened the game at all, because the announcements only describe the automatic claim.

    Seasonal events still run next to the pass. During them, both lanes also drop event chests, which matters because event items stop dropping when the event ends; the dates are on the events timeline.

    What are Bongo Coins, and are Paw Coins the same thing?

    Bongo Coins are the only currency named in the developer's Steam announcements. Paw Coins is not a name the developer has used there: it comes from a fan wiki's reward list. The developer has not said whether the two names mean the same coins, so that is not confirmed.

    NameWhere it appearsWhat can be confirmed
    Bongo CoinsThe developer's Steam announcement of September 1, 2026Dropped by both pass lanes, 100 coins per lane
    Paw CoinsA fan wiki's Circus reward listA caption on three coin entries; 0 matches in the game's Steam news feed; link to Bongo Coins not confirmed
    Paw Pass TicketThe developer's Steam announcement of September 1, 2026Steam Store item you redeem in-game to unlock a premium lane

    On the official side, the launch post says the pass "drops Bongo Coins in both lanes, free and premium, 100 coins per lane". It adds that these coins "can be used later to buy the premium version of the pass for 500 coins or cosmetics from the supporter items, so that you can earn it for free as well". Neither post states whether spending is already switched on or what a supporter item costs in coins. Both points are not confirmed.

    On the wiki side, the fan wiki page linked above lists three coin entries on its regular pass for Circus, captioned "25 Paw coins", "25 paw coins" and "Paw coins" with no amount, and its premium section is empty (revision of September 5, 2026). That is the wiki's own wording and count. It cannot be matched to the official 100 Bongo Coins per lane, and the page never says Bongo Coins, so we do not treat it as confirmed.

    On October 10, 2026 we searched the full text of all 67 items in the game's Steam news feed, 63 of which are developer announcements: "Paw Coins" has 0 matches, and Bongo Coins is the only currency that appears. We have not seen the in-game label ourselves.

    By our arithmetic, if the pass is the only source of coins, which is not confirmed, a free-only player would have 500 coins after finishing five free lanes, and a premium owner would earn 200 per pass.

    Should you buy the premium lane or save coins?

    Nothing in a pass expires, so there is no reason to buy on the first day of a month. You can finish free lanes first and decide once you see what a theme offers; old passes can still be unlocked later. Saving coins towards a 500-coin premium unlock is the route the developer described, but when coin spending opens is not confirmed, so do not plan a specific pass around it.

    Tickets can also change hands. The launch post says you can "gift the pass ticket to friends by using Steam's trading feature"; the wider rules for swapping items are in exchange and trading. Whether tickets or pass rewards can be sold on the Community Market is not stated in either announcement.
