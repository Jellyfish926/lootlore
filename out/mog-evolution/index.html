    Key points

    • +1 Mog Evolution is a free Roblox clicker from the group Navoj Mog: click for Appeal, upgrade your hammer, earn Wins to ascend and climb the leaderboard to True Adam.
    • The game has no badges and no game passes; everything for sale is one of 43 developer products, from 1 to 999 Robux.

    +1 Mog Evolution is a free Roblox clicker made by the group Navoj Mog. Every click earns Appeal, you upgrade your hammer towards Bonesmash for more, you earn Wins to ascend into a stronger body, and you climb the leaderboard to become a "True Adam". This hub covers the loop, the progression steps and every Robux product.

    How popular is +1 Mog Evolution?

    Very, for a game that is one month old. Roblox counted 7,523 players online when we checked on 1 October 2026, and the Navoj Mog group had 5.46 million members. Its title currently starts with "[W3]", and an official event starts on 3 October. The developer also runs a small test copy, explained on the community page.

    How does progress work?

    The description names four steps: clicks give Appeal, the hammer upgrades to Bonesmash, Wins let you ascend, and the leaderboard leads to True Adam. The store fills in the rest. Rebirth normally needs you to reach a "level cap", ascending moves you to "the next body", and paid skips exist for Worlds 2 and 3. The progression guide lines these up and marks which thresholds are still unpublished.

    Which boosts does the store sell?

    Four kinds of boost, all sold as developer products rather than game passes: an Auto Clicker at 22 clicks per second for 39 Robux, VIP at 10x Appeal for 99 Robux, nine Power multipliers from x1.5 to x256, and nine treadmill belts from x2 to x999. The Appeal guide ranks them by value, and the limited unlocks page covers the five 399-Robux items such as the Gigachad body and Claviculars Hammer.

    What's happening this week?

    Roblox lists an official event for the game, Admin Abuse & Update 4, running from 3 October 2026 at 23:00 UTC to 7 October 2026 at 23:00 UTC. The listing gives only that title. The updates page has the times in other zones and the full timeline since launch, rebuilt from the date each store item appeared.

    Why is there no codes page?

    Because we could not see a single code in an official source. The game description has none, the Navoj Mog group has no description and no pinned shout, and the event listing has none. Other fan sites show zero active codes as well. The community page explains where official codes would appear.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who made +1 Mog Evolution?
    A Roblox group called Navoj Mog. The experience was created on 30 August 2026 — the community page covers the group and the test version.
    How do you get Appeal faster?
    Click more often, upgrade your hammer, and stack the boosts the store sells: an Auto Clicker, VIP, Power multipliers and treadmill belts. The Appeal guide compares them by price.
    What does Ascend do?
    The store says Skip Ascend lets you 'Ascend to the next body instantly, no requirements', so ascending moves you to a new body. The progression guide puts Rebirth, Ascend and Worlds in order.
    Are there +1 Mog Evolution codes?
    Not that we could find in any official source on 1 October 2026, so we do not list any. The community page explains where codes would appear.
    When is the next update?
    Roblox lists an official event, Admin Abuse & Update 4, from 3 October 23:00 UTC to 7 October 23:00 UTC. Details are on the updates page.
