# BlockSpin Guide: Codes, Rules and Getting Started

BlockSpin is a free Roblox open-world action game made by the Cinnamon Go! group and set in a Florida county. You work jobs to earn cash, open random reward cases, level up your character and fight other players. Everything in your inventory drops when you die, so the house safe matters from day one.

The game's own description sums up the loop in one line: "Grind your way through jobs to unbox random reward cases, and level up your character to become the top dog in the city." The studio's website shortens it further: enter the city, grind jobs, fight for dominance.

## What kind of game is BlockSpin?

Roblox lists it under Action, sub-genre Open World Action. The description promises "intense battles, unforgettable finishers, and a world that reacts to your every move", and the goal it gives you is to become "the most powerful gangster around". Its Roblox content rating is Moderate, with light realistic blood and repeated moderate violence.

## What do official BlockSpin sources confirm?

A short list. The official sources we reached contain a short game description, four promotional thumbnails and one code. From those we can confirm that jobs, reward cases, character levels, player fights, a house with a safe, a Quick-11 convenience store, a fast-food restaurant and fishing all exist in the game.

Job names, map locations, weapon lists, vehicle case odds and trading values are not published anywhere official that we could reach. Community sites list them, but they disagree with each other and many are months out of date. Pages on jobs, locations, weapons and vehicles stay unpublished here until each fact is checked in-game.

## How we handle BlockSpin codes

Other code sites list dozens of BlockSpin codes, and two of the biggest disagree on whether the same update codes still work. We only publish codes we saw in an official source. Right now that is W7C28D, printed in the game description itself. If the developers add or remove a code there, the [codes page](/blockspin/codes/) changes the same day we notice, and old codes stay listed as expired.
