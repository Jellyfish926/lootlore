    Jellyfi is the editorial byline on LootWiki, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the Get Your Driver's License! guides on this site. Game facts in them come from Roblox's official records for the game, quoted word for word with the date they were checked. Every guide article published under the byline is listed at the end of this page.

    How are these guides researched?

    The game lives on Roblox, so the most reliable facts are the ones its developer registers there: the game description, the game passes, the store products and the group that owns the game. Those are read directly from Roblox's public data and quoted exactly, emoji and brackets included.

    Official art is treated as a second tier. The icon and the promotional image are the developer's own, but they are drawings made to sell the game, so anything taken from them is labelled as coming from the art.

    Third-party statistics sites are used for one or two labelled figures, such as average playtime. Videos, comment threads and other fan sites are leads only. If one of them is the only source for a fact, that fact stays out.

    What is deliberately missing?

    This game is young, and a lot of what players want is not in any official source: the names of the 18 cars, the full list of endings, the written test questions and the route through the course. These guides do not fill those gaps from memory or from other sites. Most guides include a section on what is not published, and items move out of it only after they have been checked in the game.

    How are codes handled?

    A code is listed only after it has been seen in an official source, and the page says whether it has been tested in the game. For this game no code has appeared in the description or the developer group, so no codes page exists. The how-to-play guide is the place to start instead.

    How do you read the dates on a page?

    Each guide shows a "Last reviewed" date beside the byline. That is the day the text was last compared against its sources. Roblox counters such as visits, likes and group members are snapshots from that day and keep moving.

    How do you report an error?

    The store has changed since the game was created on 29 September 2026: two listings were added after the first batch and one listing was edited on 5 October. If a price, a name or a description no longer matches what you see, use the Contact page and include the page link and, if you can, a screenshot. Screenshots of the car collection, the ending screen and the written test are especially welcome, because those are gaps the official data does not fill. The site-wide writing and correction rules are on the Editorial Policy page.

    Where should you start?

    New players: how to play. Stuck in the wait: the line guide. Planning purchases: the Robux shop guide.
