    All guides, by category

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-09-25

    Every game on LootWiki gets a complete hub, and every hub covers the same ground: how to start, what's trying to kill you, how to build around it, and what's left after the credits. This page cuts across the hubs by category, so if you know what kind of help you need, you can jump straight to it.

    Getting started

    The first hours decide whether a game clicks. For Beast of Reincarnation, the beginner guide covers Emma's core loop and the mistakes that cost early progress, and the full walkthrough takes it chapter by chapter. For Shift At Midnight, the beginner guide explains the diner's routines before the first doppelganger walks in. DragonSword: Awakening starts at its beginner guide and the Status Ailment combat primer; Sephiria at the beginner guide and chapter walkthrough; Sir, We Have an Orc Problem at the turret roles overview; Valheim at the beginner guide and first day.

    Bosses & monsters

    The pages readers use mid-fight. The Nushi boss list gives every Malefact's chapter, level and elemental weakness, cross-checked against Steam completion rates. The monster compendium covers every threat from the Marionette to the Dentist — identification tells first, counterplay second, because two of them can't be fought at all. DragonSword's chapters and bosses and Sephiria's boss chapters (through the final boss and endings) round out the fight coverage, and Valheim's boss order walks each Forsaken in sequence.

    Builds & combat

    For Beast of Reincarnation: builds and skill trees across ranged, stealth and aggressive playstyles, the full weapon list, and the Koo command guide for the dog-side of the one-person-one-dog system. For Shift At Midnight, the survival guide covers weapons and when running beats fighting. Sephiria's build game lives in build routes, all 6 weapon classes and Artifacts & Tablets; DragonSword's in the hero tier list and gear, runes and Karma; Orc Problem's in real turret numbers and the upgrade grind; Valheim's in combat and crafting.

    Achievements & trophies

    Achievement pages track completion data, not just lists: all 46 Beast of Reincarnation achievements with global unlock rates as a built-in difficulty curve, and all 10 Shift At Midnight achievements with rarity percentages. DragonSword's achievement list, Sephiria's achievements and Orc Problem's achievements mapped to levels follow the same data-first format.

    Endings

    No vague spoiler-dodging — the conditions, spelled out: every Beast of Reincarnation ending and all three Shift At Midnight endings and exactly what locks you into each.

    Buying advice

    Before you spend anything: our review verdicts, Beast of Reincarnation's edition comparison and Game Pass answer, and Shift At Midnight's is-it-worth-it breakdown with price and demo details.

    How the hubs are organised

    Each hub is a self-contained site: its own navigation, its own category pages, its own search-friendly structure. We size coverage to the game, not to a template, so hubs differ a lot in size and in languages (multi-language hubs keep the switcher in their own header). As of 2026-09-25: Beast of Reincarnation — 47 guides (EN · 日本語 · FR · DE · IT · ES); Shift At Midnight — 45 guides (EN); DragonSword: Awakening — 44 guides (EN); Sephiria — 63 guides (EN); Sir, We Have an Orc Problem — 36 guides (EN); Valheim — 39 guides (EN · 中文).

    Two habits make these pages more useful than a wiki dump. First, data pages carry their sources: achievement rates come from Steam's global statistics, boss data is cross-checked against launch coverage, and each page names what it checked. Second, every page carries a review date and the game version it was verified against, so you know whether the advice predates the patch you're playing on.

    Looking for a game we haven't covered? Tell us — reader requests decide which hub joins LootWiki next.
