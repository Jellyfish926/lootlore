    How to Play +1 Mog Evolution: Clicks, Hammer and Wins

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-01 · Checked on version 2026-09-30 update

    Scope: Core loop from the official game description; supporting detail from developer product descriptions on 2026-10-01; per-click values and upgrade costs in in-game currency are not published

    +1 Mog Evolution

    Quick facts

    Roblox clicker: every click gives Appeal, the hammer upgrades to Bonesmash, Wins let you ascend to the next body, and the leaderboard leads to True Adam.

    Key points

    • The official description gives the whole loop in four lines: click for Appeal, upgrade your hammer to Bonesmash, earn Wins to ascend, climb the leaderboard to True Adam.
    • Store descriptions add the rest: Rebirth has a level cap, ascending moves you to the next body, and treadmill belts multiply Appeal while you stand on them.
    • Servers hold 12 players and private servers are switched off.
    • How much Appeal one click gives, and what Bonesmash costs, are not published.

    Click to earn Appeal, spend it to upgrade your hammer, earn Wins to ascend into a better body, and climb the leaderboard. That is the whole loop of +1 Mog Evolution in the developer's own description. The rest of this guide explains each step, what the store descriptions add, and which details are still unpublished.

    The description is short, so we read it next to the developer's 43 store items. Many of them describe a mechanic in one sentence, which tells us more about how the game works than the description does.

    What does the official description say?

    Here are the four gameplay lines, word for word, with what each one means for you.

    Official lineWhat it tells you
    "👆 Every Click gives you MORE Appeal"Appeal is the main resource, and clicking is how you earn it
    "🔨 Upgrade your hammer to Bonesmash and get even more Appeal"Your hammer has upgrade levels; Bonesmash is one of them and raises Appeal
    "🏆 Earn Wins to ascend and mog more people"Wins are a second resource, tied to ascending
    "🌍 Climb the leaderboard to become a True Adam"There is a leaderboard, and "True Adam" is the title at the top

    The description then asks you to like and favourite the game "to support future updates". It contains no codes and no links.

    What is Appeal, and how do you get more?

    Appeal is what every click gives you. The store sells Appeal boosts, and their wording shows how Appeal grows beyond clicking:

    • Auto Clicker (39 Robux): "Automatically earns Appeal at 22 clicks per second."
    • VIP (99 Robux): "earn 10x Appeal, plus a VIP name tag."
    • Treadmill belts: the x9, x99 and x999 belts "Earn 9x / 99x / 999x Appeal while standing on it".
    • Power multipliers: nine products from x1.5 to x256, each "Permanently earn … Power".

    One oddity: the Appeal packs are named "+100K Appeal" and so on, but three of their descriptions say "Power" instead. We read Power as another word for Appeal, but the developer has not said so. The Appeal guide compares every boost by price.

    How does the hammer work?

    The description says to "Upgrade your hammer to Bonesmash". It does not say how many levels there are, what Bonesmash costs, or what comes after it. The store adds three hammer-related items:

    Store itemRobuxOfficial description
    Buy Hammer Upgrade [1]29None given
    Buy Hammer Upgrade [2]79None given
    Claviculars Hammer399"Gives x1024 Appeal while equipped."

    So at least some hammer upgrades can be bought with Robux, and at least one hammer is a separate unlock you equip. Whether Bonesmash is one of the two numbered upgrades is not stated. The Claviculars Hammer sits on the limited unlocks page.

    What are Wins, Ascend and Rebirth?

    Wins are the second resource, and the description ties them to ascending. Two store items show how ascending and rebirth work, even though the normal requirements are unpublished:

    • Skip Ascend (29 Robux): "Ascend to the next body instantly, no requirements." Ascending changes your body, and normally has requirements.
    • Skip Rebirth (39 Robux): "Rebirth instantly without reaching the level cap." Rebirth normally needs you to reach a level cap.

    Our reading is that bodies are the "evolution" in the name, but the developer has not said so. Official art shows labels such as SUB 3, Chad and True Adam, and two bodies are sold by name: LTN in the Starter Pack and Gigachad. The progression guide puts Rebirth, Ascend, Stages and Worlds in order.

    What else is in the game?

    The store and the game's places point to more systems than the description lists:

    SystemEvidenceStatus
    Stages 1–3 with teleports"Unlock the Stage 1 teleport" (also 2 and 3)Confirmed by name; what a stage is, is not described
    World 2 and World 3"Skip to World 2" and "Skip to World 3" products; [W3] in the titleConfirmed by name
    Win Pad"2x Win Pad" product, no descriptionName only
    RankedA place called "+1 Mog Evolution [RANKED]" inside the experienceName only; rules unknown

    How many players are in a server?

    Up to 12 per +1 Mog Evolution server, according to Roblox. Private servers are switched off, so you always join a public server. The game is rated Maturity: Minimal, suitable for everyone, and it is free to play.

    Is it free, and is it suitable for younger players?

    Yes to both, by Roblox's own labels. The game costs nothing to join, and Roblox rates it Maturity: Minimal, "Suitable for everyone". Robux purchases are optional and start at 1 Robux; the full list, up to 999 Robux, is on the Robux products page.

    What we could not confirm

    • How much Appeal one click gives at the start, and how hammer upgrades are paid for in play.
    • How Wins are earned (the Win Pad is a likely place, but that is our guess from its name).
    • The level cap, the number of bodies, and what the leaderboard ranks.
    • How the multipliers combine with each other.

    If you can see any of these in the game, the author page explains how to send a correction.
