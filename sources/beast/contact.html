What to include in a correction report

The fastest fixes come from reports that name the page URL, quote the sentence in question, and point at the evidence — a patch note, a store listing, a screenshot from your own game. “The Lacerta section says X but 1.0.9's notes say Y” gets verified and corrected same-day; “your site is wrong” takes longer. Either way we read everything.

Response times

Correction reports are usually handled within 48 hours. Business and partnership mail gets a reply within a week when it is a fit; we do not sell sponsored placements or guest posts, so those pitches go unanswered — see the editorial policy.
