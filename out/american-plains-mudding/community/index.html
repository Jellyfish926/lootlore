    American Plains Mudding Group, Developer and Codes

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-09 · Checked on version 2026-10-03 update

    Scope: Group facts from the Roblox groups API and game facts from the games API, all on 2026-10-09. The game's and group's social links returned 401 without a Roblox login and the group wall returned 404, so none could be read, and no official Discord server could be confirmed.

    Key points

    • American Plains Mudding is made by the verified Roblox group of the same name, owned by the account Bebsharky, with 3,699,198 members on 9 October 2026.
    • No code appears in the game description or the group description, and the group has no shout, so we list no codes.

    American Plains Mudding is made by the verified Roblox group of the same name, owned by the account Bebsharky, with 3,699,198 members on 9 October 2026. No official source we could read lists a code. Roblox would not show us the game's social links, so we could not confirm an official Discord server.

    This page records what each official place said on the day we checked, and what we could not see.

    Who makes the game?

    The creator field on the game's Roblox record points to one group.

    The group predates its game by more than two years and has published nothing else publicly. Its description is a single welcoming line, and it offers no reward for joining.

    When did American Plains Mudding come out?

    Roblox gives two creation dates for the same experience, and we cannot tell you which one marks the first public release.

    SourceCreated
    Roblox games record29 July 2021
    The group's public games list23 July 2021

    Both are official and they are six days apart. The earliest store records back up the summer of 2021: a Donation pass was created on 3 August 2021 and Premium Vehicles on 14 August, as the game pass guide shows. We use 29 July 2021 across this guide because it is the date on the game's own record.

    Are there American Plains Mudding codes?

    None that we can find in an official place. We checked the three places a developer would normally print one:

    • The game description: no code. It has the update notes, a paragraph about the game and the free vehicle line.
    • The group description: one welcome sentence, no code.
    • The group shout: empty.

    What the description does offer is this: "Like 👍 and Favorite ⭐ the game to obtain a vehicle!". That is a reward for two clicks on the game's own page, not a code, and the how-to-play guide covers it.

    Google suggests "codes" as a search for this game, and sites will try to meet that demand. We list a code only after seeing it in an official source. If one appears, it will get its own page with the date and the source.

    Is there an official Discord server?

    We could not confirm one. Roblox's documentation says social media links "are only visible to users who have verified their age as at least 16 years old", and our anonymous request for the game's links returned a 401 error. Neither the game page nor the group page we could read names a Discord server, so we do not name or link one.

    If you are signed in with an age-verified account, open the game's Roblox page and look for a Discord link there. Never type your Roblox password anywhere except roblox.com, and treat direct messages offering free vehicles or Robux as scams.

    How big is the community?

    These are Roblox's own counters, read at 11:13 UTC on 9 October 2026.

    The positive share is our calculation from the two vote counts. Favourites outnumber group members by more than two to one. The description's offer of a vehicle for a favourite may be part of the reason; the data cannot show that.

    Where do official announcements appear?

    In two places we can read. The game description carries the newest update notes, headed "10/3 Update" when we checked. Official Roblox events carry the schedule: 60 listings since August 2025, all starting on Saturdays. Both are on the updates page.

    Two places we could not read: the group wall, where our request came back with a 404 error, and the game's social links, which returned a 401 error. Nothing on this site is taken from Discord messages.

    Is there an official American Plains Mudding wiki?

    We found no official wiki. The game description and the group page mention no wiki. Two independent fan sites exist, americanplainsmudding.wiki and plainsmudding.wiki; both describe themselves as unofficial. This guide is unofficial too. Every number here names where it came from.
