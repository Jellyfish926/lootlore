# Animal Daycare Roblox Guide: Badges, Shop and Shifts

Animal Daycare (Anomaly) is a Roblox survival game from the group Day Dreams Games, created on 14 August 2026. You staff a daycare for animal children: inspect every arrival, turn away impostors, feed and play with the kids, keep them calm, then survive the night. This hub covers shifts, badges and the Robux shop from official data.

By 30 September 2026 the game had passed 13.2 million visits and 209,907 favourites, with 6,207 likes against 1,549 dislikes. Every number on this site comes from Roblox's own data for the game and carries the date we checked it.

## How rare is each badge?

The five Animal Daycare badges are the best public measure of how rare each milestone is. First Day on the Job, for surviving the first night, had been awarded about 3.5 million times since the badges were created on 16 September. For every 100 of those, Still Standing (two nights) had been awarded 61 times, Night Shift Veteran (five shifts) fewer than 5 times, and Daycare Legend (ten shifts) about once in 300. These are badge totals, not a tracked group of players. The [badge guide](/animal-daycare/badges/) has the exact figures.

## What can you buy with Robux?

Animal Daycare has no game passes, so nothing permanent is sold as a pass. The 25 Robux products are mostly items for a shift (Lunchbox, Water Gun, coffee refill, Toy Hammer), revives, Lamb Coins and class level unlocks, plus gifts, shop helpers, Boss and a Starter Pack. Lamb Coins get cheaper per coin as the pack grows: the 2,500 pack works out about 39% cheaper per coin than the 50 pack. The [shop guide](/animal-daycare/shop/) lists every product.

## What has changed since launch?

We found no patch notes from Day Dreams Games in Roblox's public data, but every Robux product and badge carries the date it was created. Read together, those dates sketch the game's update history. A creation date is when an item was registered, which is not always the day an update was released.

| Date (2026) | What appeared |
| --- | --- |
| 14 August | Experience created |
| 2 September | Lunchbox, Water Gun and coffee refill products |
| 4 September | Get Toy Hammer |
| 9 September | Revive Yourself and Revive a Friend |
| 10 – 11 September | Reroll Shop, Lamb Coins, 1-Star class level unlocks, Buy Item from Shop, Boss |
| 16 September | All five badges; Lamb Coin gifts |
| 18 September | Gift Boss |
| 23 September | 2-Star and 3-Star class level unlocks; Starter Pack (Limited) |

The experience listing itself showed an update on 30 September.

## Why is there no codes page?

Because we could not confirm a single code from Day Dreams Games. The game description, the group description and the group shout carry no codes, and the game's social links need a Roblox sign-in to read. We would rather leave the page out than send you to type in codes that may never have existed. If an official code appears, it will get its own page.

## How this guide uses its sources

This is an unofficial fan guide. Facts come first from what Day Dreams Games has published on Roblox: the game description, badges and Robux products. Gameplay videos show far more — specific impostor tells and night threats — but we hold those back until we can confirm them. Buying suggestions are our advice, not a rule of the game.
