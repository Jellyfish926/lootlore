# Phasmophobia Guide: Updates, Events and Achievements

Phasmophobia is Kinetic Games' four-player online co-op ghost-hunting horror game, in Steam Early Access since September 18, 2020 and priced at $19.99 on the US store. This hub follows what the developer has actually published: the 2026 patch notes, the roadmap to 1.0, seasonal events, platforms, difficulty rules and all 54 Steam achievements.

The game changed a lot in 2026. Two houses were reworked, characters became customisable, the developer apologised for one update and spent the summer on fixes, and the 1.0 release slipped a year. Each guide here quotes the official post it relies on and gives the date, so you can tell a current rule from an old one.

## What is coming next?

In the short term, the [Crimson Eye event](/phasmophobia/crimson-eye/) is announced for October 8 to November 1, with a Double XP week from October 15 to 22. After that the developer's notes point to November for the move to Unity 6.

Further out, the June 2026 roadmap lists a second Player Character update, a 42 Edgefield Road rework, Winter's Jest and one more content update before the year ends. Version 1.0 and the Nintendo Switch 2 release are planned for the second half of 2027. The [roadmap page](/phasmophobia/roadmap/) sets the January and June plans side by side, and the [events page](/phasmophobia/events/) explains goals, trophies and Twitch Drops.
