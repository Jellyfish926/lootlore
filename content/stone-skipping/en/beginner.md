# +1 Stone Skipping Beginner Guides: Where to Start

New to +1 Stone Skipping? Throw first: the official description says every bounce gives +1 Skill, training and levelling up make you throw farther, and farther zones pay more Wins. Keep your Robux until you have played a session. Then read [how to play](/stone-skipping/how-to-play/), which goes through the six gameplay lines one by one.

The official description of the game is eight short lines (an opening line, six gameplay lines and a closing line asking for a like and favourite), and the store items have no descriptions at all. These guides stay inside that evidence. Where the developer has said nothing, the page says "not shown in public data" instead of filling the gap with a guess. Store names and prices on this page were read from Roblox at 18:12 UTC on 9 October 2026.

## What should you do in your first session?

Our suggestion, using only what the developer has published. Throw, and watch Skill rise by one per bounce. Train when the game offers it, because levels are what make you throw farther. Try to reach a zone you have not reached before, since farther zones pay more Wins.

When you can hatch a pet, do it and read the boost it shows. Rebirth when the game allows it, because the description ties Rebirth to growing stronger. Leave Robux alone until you know which part of the loop slows you down; the [Robux shop guides](/stone-skipping/robux/) are there for that moment.

## How do you track progress without badges?

When we read Roblox's badge list on 9 October 2026 (UTC), +1 Stone Skipping had no Roblox badges, so there was no badge list to tick off. The markers that official material points to are your level, your longest throw and the world you have reached. Official art sets LEVEL 1 beside LEVEL 999, and the description ends its gameplay lines with "beat your longest throw".

Worlds are dated only by official event titles: World 3 + New Content from 20 September 2026, WORLD 4 + UPDATE from 27 September and ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 5 on 3 October. How a world unlocks is not confirmed in any official source. The [updates page](/stone-skipping/updates/) keeps the dated timeline up to the World 5 event.

Going by creation dates (UTC), 78 of the 89 developer products listed on 9 October 2026 existed by 2 October, and 11 were created after it.
