# Get Your Driver's License! Robux Shop: All 13 Products

The Get Your Driver's License! store lists 13 developer products, priced from 9 to 160 Robux. Five skip parts of the wait, four are pranks on other players, two buy time as the examiner and two are about cars. Each has a one-line official description, quoted in full below.

Names, prices and descriptions come from the [Roblox developer product API](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/10768565603/developerproducts?limit=100), read on 10 October 2026. All 13 were on sale and none showed a discount. The grouping is ours; the store does not label groups.

The totals are our arithmetic. Nobody needs one of everything, and several products overlap. Skip To End, for example, makes Skip Test pointless in the same run if it does what its name suggests.

## Which skip should you look at first?

Skip Line [SALE] and Skip Time [SALE], because they cost 9 Robux each and you can see exactly what they give: 5 places, or 2 minutes 30 seconds. Skip To End at 48 Robux is the one-purchase route to the driving test. Retake Test at 19 only applies after a failed drive.

The two [SALE] tags are part of the product names. Roblox's price record shows no discount on either. The [line guide](/get-your-drivers-license/waiting-line/) works through each case with a value table.

## What do the prank products do?

They act on other people, and two of them on the line itself.

- **Kill** makes "the person ahead of you in line" go poof. One person, 9 Robux.
- **Kill All** does the same to "everyone waiting in line" for 160 Robux. It is the most expensive product in the store.
- **Scare All** sends "a jumpscare on everyone's screen". The store icon is a white ghost under the word BOO!
- **Revenge ⚡** lets you "get back at whoever knocked you out". It is the only official text we read that mentions being knocked out.

None of the four descriptions says what happens next: what going poof does to a player, how long a scare lasts, or what form revenge takes. The game's maturity label on Roblox is Minimal, "Suitable for everyone".

## How much does examiner time cost?

Be The Examiner 📋 buys "5 minutes as the driving examiner" for 29 Robux, which is 5.8 Robux a minute. +1 Minute adds "one more minute as the examiner" for 9. Ten minutes bought as one of the first and five of the second would cost 74 Robux.

The official description of Get Your Driver's License! lists "Be the Examiner and judge other players' driving" as a feature. It does not say whether the role is ever free. The [driving test guide](/get-your-drivers-license/driving-test/) has the full cost table and the open questions.

![Store icon: a stopwatch with a red button on top and a green badge reading +1](prod-plus-minute "Official store icon of the +1 Minute product, 9 Robux")

## Which products are about cars?

Two. Golden Supercar 🏆 costs 39 Robux and is described as "The best car in the game, yours to keep." VIP Ticket 🎟️ costs 149 Robux and makes "your next car" Epic or better. That second description is the only place we found the word Epic in official text, and the only direct sign we found that cars come in tiers. The [car guide](/get-your-drivers-license/cars/) compares the two.

## Which records were created after the first batch?

Roblox records when every product was created and last edited, and the times tell a short story.

| Date (2026, UTC) | Product |
| --- | --- |
| 30 September, 06:18 | The first 11 products, registered within 40 seconds |
| 30 September, 14:45 | Golden Supercar 🏆 |
| 5 October, 20:24 | Golden Supercar 🏆 listing edited (the record does not say what changed) |
| 8 October, 12:07 | Retake Test |

The game itself was created on 29 September 2026. So in its first ten days the store gained one car product and one retake product, and one listing was edited. The other 12 products show the same created and edited time. These times show when a listing was registered or changed, not when a feature reached players.

## Can you buy a product more than once?

By Roblox's definition, yes. A developer product is "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once". That is the difference from the five game passes, which are one-time purchases. It also means prices add up quickly: three Skip Line [SALE] purchases and a Kill come to 36 Robux, and nothing in the listings says a skip carries over to your next run.

Golden Supercar 🏆 is the odd one in the list. Its description says "yours to keep", which is how a pass normally behaves, yet it is registered as a developer product. We have not tested what a second purchase does.

## What is not in the shop?

No product sells a specific ending, and none sells in-game cash. Nothing in the 13 listings mentions a currency at all. The five game passes are a separate list with their own prices, from 9 to 1,200 Robux, and they are covered in the [game pass guide](/get-your-drivers-license/gamepasses/).
