    Race Horses (listed on Roblox as "[INDEX📖] Race Horses") is an idle RNG racing game by the group Gorilla Grafters. You buy eggs, hatch horses with random coats, eye sizes and traits, and enter them in a race that starts every minute. Better horses earn more, any horse can win, and your stable keeps earning while you are offline.

    What is the Index?

    The Index is the newest headline feature: the game's title now starts with "[INDEX📖]" and the description opens with "Index OUT NOW! Collect horses for rewards!" That is everything the developer has published about it. We could not confirm what the rewards are or how many entries the Index holds.

    What we can confirm is how much there is to collect. The description says coats, eye size and traits are all random and that "ultra-rare 1-in-[X] traits and shiny variants" exist, and the badges prove seven rarities. Our horses and Index guide sets out each of those, and flags what is still unknown.

    How rare are the best hatches?

    The hatch badges give a public measure. For every 100 Horsin' Around badges (first common horse), about 54 Racing Stripes (uncommon), 14 Runaway Fashion (epic) and 5 Stallion Supreme (legendary) badges have been awarded. Mythical Beast sits at about 1.2 in 100 and Fruity Foal, for a first Exotic horse, at about 0.2. These compare badge totals, not one group of players, and the later badges are younger.

    What can you buy with Robux?

    Five game passes, from 199 to 459 Robux: X2 FRUIT CAPACITY, X3 STORAGE, X2 RACE CASH, INSTANT Hatch! and Rainbow Magic Carpet!. Buying all five comes to 1,635 Robux. The 32 developer products are mostly eggs (9 to 2,799 Robux), hatch-timer skips and gift versions, plus a 25-Robux Starter Pack. The game pass guide has the full list and our view on which to buy first.

    Why is there no codes page?

    Because no official source we could read lists a code. The game description, the Gorilla Grafters group description ("grafting") and the group shout (empty) carry none, and the group wall and social links need a Roblox sign-in. Third-party sites do publish code lists, but we could not trace them to the developer. If an official code appears, it will get its own page.

    Is this the same game as Horse Race?

    No. Searches for this game often bring up Horse Race, a different Roblox game with its own pets, rebirths and codes. Everything on these pages is about Race Horses by Gorilla Grafters, universe 10387635049.

    What has changed recently?

    The store and badge creation dates show steady updates: hurdle badges created on 7 and 8 September, the Re-roll Horse! product and X2 FRUIT CAPACITY on 10 September, INSTANT Hatch! on 17 September, gifts and the Exotic Egg on 22 September, and X3 STORAGE on 24 September. The update history lines up every date since launch.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who made Race Horses?
    The Roblox group Gorilla Grafters, owned by the verified account N4D_X. The experience was created on 24 June 2026 and Race Horses is the group's only public game.
    What is the Index in Race Horses?
    The game description says only: "Index OUT NOW! Collect horses for rewards!" The rewards are not listed anywhere official. See horses, traits and the Index.
    How many rarities are there?
    Seven: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, mythic and Exotic. Each has its own hatch badge and its own egg in the Robux store — see eggs and rarities.
    Are there Race Horses codes?
    We found no code in the game description, the group description or the group shout, so we do not list any. Third-party sites report codes, but we could not confirm them from an official source.
    How many players fit in a server?
    Eight, according to the Roblox listing. Private servers were not enabled when we checked on 1 October 2026.
