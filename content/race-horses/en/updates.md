# Race Horses Update History: Every New Egg, Pass and Badge

Race Horses was created on Roblox on 24 June 2026 and has registered new badges, passes or products in most weeks since. The biggest recent additions were the Exotic Egg and gifting, both created on 22 September, and X3 STORAGE on 24 September. The listing was last updated on 30 September 2026, and the game now leads with its Index.

## Where do these dates come from?

Gorilla Grafters does not post patch notes on Roblox. What Roblox does record is the date every badge, game pass and developer product was created. Put in order, those dates sketch the update history. A creation date is when an item was registered, which is not always the day players first saw it.

The first five tiers had badges from early July, before any Robux egg was sold for them. Mythic came in late August and Exotic in late September, roughly a month apart. If that rhythm holds, another tier is possible, but nothing has been announced.

## When did the Index arrive?

We could not confirm it. The game is now named "[INDEX📖] Race Horses" and the description opens "Index OUT NOW!", but Roblox does not record when a name or description changed. The [horses and Index guide](/race-horses/horses/) covers what the Index is known to do.

## What does the timeline say about the game?

Our reading of the dates:

- **July was about hatching.** The five original rarities and the first egg products.
- **August was about racing.** Race badges, the cash pass, the Mythic tier and the Starter Pack.
- **September was about convenience and collecting.** Hurdles, re-rolls, instant hatching, gifting, more storage and the Exotic tier.

None of this is a roadmap; the developer has not announced what comes next.

## How often is the game updated?

On 1 October 2026 the listing's "updated" time read 30 September 2026, 09:40 UTC. From 7 to 29 September, something new was registered in the store or badge list every week; before that there were quiet spells, such as 27 August to 7 September. That is the best public sign that the game is actively maintained. To check the newest additions yourself, look at the badge list on the Roblox game page.

## Has any item left the store?

Not as far as Roblox's data shows. All five game passes and all 32 developer products were on sale on 1 October 2026, and none carried a discount. Every one of the 24 badges was enabled. The store data lists no off-sale items for Race Horses, so everything in this timeline could still be bought or earned on that date. An item deleted outright would not appear in the data at all, so we cannot rule that out. Roblox data does not show renames, so an item renamed since launch would appear here under its current name.
