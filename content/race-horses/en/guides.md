# Race Horses Guides: Eggs, Badges, Races, Passes and Index

New to Race Horses? Start with the how-to-play guide, which walks through the developer's own seven-step list and gives a first-hour plan. Then read the eggs guide before you spend anything: it prices all seven tiers, from Common to Exotic. The other six guides answer narrower questions about badges, races, care, passes, the Index and updates.

All eight guides stick to what Gorilla Grafters has published on Roblox — the game description, 24 badges, five game passes and 32 developer products — and say plainly when something is our advice rather than an official rule.

## Pick a guide by question

### [How to Play: Your First Hour](/race-horses/how-to-play/)

The official loop line by line: eggs, a race every minute, random winners, care for XP and stable upgrades. Includes a six-step first-hour plan, server size, devices and the Horse Race mix-up.

### [Eggs and Rarities: All Seven Tiers](/race-horses/eggs/)

Every egg with its Robux price, hatch-timer skip and gift price, plus how rare each tier's hatch badge is. Covers the Exotic tier, which at least one fan site still leaves out.

### [Horses, Traits, Shiny Variants and the Index](/race-horses/horses/)

What the developer actually says about the Index, what is random about each horse, how Re-roll Horse! works, and which horses to keep.

### [Races: Hurdles, Photo Finishes and Prize Cash](/race-horses/races/)

Race rules read from eleven race badges: timing, margins, hurdles, why last place can out-earn first, and the two ways to double race cash.

### [Horse Care, Apples and Stable Upgrades](/race-horses/care-stable/)

Apples and trees, Golden Apples, levels, the 9-slot storage, stable upgrades and offline earnings.

### [All 5 Game Passes with Prices](/race-horses/gamepasses/)

Every pass with its official description, the matching gift products, and a price mismatch worth knowing about before you buy X2 RACE CASH.

### [All 24 Badges and How Rare They Are](/race-horses/badges/)

Exact requirements and award totals for every badge, the five hardest, and a suggested order.

### [Update History](/race-horses/updates/)

A dated timeline from launch on 24 June 2026 to the Exotic Egg and X3 STORAGE in late September.

## What these guides do not cover yet

We have no pages for codes, a trait list, hatch odds or stable level costs. Codes have not appeared in any official place we can read. Trait names and odds are shown inside the game but not in its Roblox listing or store data, and stable costs appear in neither. We hold those pages back rather than publish guesses.

## Scope

Race Horses is a live Roblox game that changes often; the listing showed an update on 30 September 2026. Every guide shows its check date. Badge totals, visits and prices are snapshots from 1 October 2026.
