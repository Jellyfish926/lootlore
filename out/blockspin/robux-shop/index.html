    BlockSpin Robux Shop: 64 Products and Their Prices

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Developer product, game pass, badge and game records from Roblox APIs, read on 2026-10-10; all dates UTC. Of 68 developer product records, 66 were marked for sale; this page counts 64 and leaves out the two for-sale records whose names begin OLD PRODUCT. Prices are the Robux prices at the read time. Groups are ours, sorted by product name, not official categories. Record dates show when a record was created or edited, not when a feature reached players; what a product does is not confirmed unless its record carries a description

    Key points

    • The Roblox developer product records read on October 10, 2026 (UTC) hold 66 BlockSpin products marked for sale; this page counts 64 of them, leaving out two whose names begin OLD PRODUCT, priced from 25 Robux (Tiny Money: $300) to 16,500 Robux (Supreme: $1,000,000).
    • Seven cash packs work out at 12 to about 60.6 in-game dollars per Robux by our own division of the dollar figure in each name by its price.
    • The same day's game pass and badge records were empty: zero game passes and zero badges, so every Robux purchase we found is a developer product.
    • Four of the 64 records carry a description, all four for Mansion upgrades with Car in the name; for the other 60 this page prints name, price and record dates and marks the function not confirmed.

    BlockSpin's Robux shop held 66 products marked for sale in the Roblox developer product records we read on October 10, 2026 (UTC). This page counts 64 of them, priced from 25 to 16,500 Robux, and leaves out two whose names begin "OLD PRODUCT". The same day's records held zero game passes and zero badges.

    The 64 names cover cash packs, Mansion upgrades, weapon packs, vehicles, safe slots and Stock Skips. Every price below is the Robux price at the time of that read. The groups are ours: we sorted the products by the words in their names, and Roblox's records carry no category field for them. Four of the 64 records have a description. For the other 60 this page gives the name, the price and the record dates, and marks what the product does as not confirmed.

That figure is arithmetic and no bundle at that price is in the records. Whether a product can be bought more than once is not confirmed, so it is not a cap on spending either.

    How much in-game cash does each cash pack give per Robux?

    Between 12 and about 60.6 dollars per Robux, if each pack delivers the dollar figure printed in its name. The last column is our own division: dollar figure in the name ÷ Robux price, rounded to one decimal.

    The three cheapest packs share one rate, and the rate climbs from Great upward. By the same division, Supreme gives about five times as many dollars per Robux as Tiny Money (60.6 ÷ 12.0 ≈ 5.05). None of the seven records has a description, so the amount credited in play is a reading of the name and is not confirmed by a second official text.

    For scale, the game description offers a free code worth $500 to players who are starting out; the codes page tracks it. By the Tiny Money rate, $500 is the dollar figure of about 42 Robux (500 ÷ 12 ≈ 41.7).

    Which Mansion products are in the Robux shop?

    Nine records have a name starting with "Mansion": Mansion (Forever Access) at 2,999 Robux and eight named "Mansion Upgrade:", from +1 Extra Car Space at 150 to Humvee at 8,000. Four of the eight carry an official description; all four have "Car" in the product name. The Mansion upgrades guide has the price, creation date and description text for each of the nine.

    What weapon, ammo and throwable products are sold for Robux?

    Seventeen product names include a weapon, ammo or throwable word, from Shotgun Ammo Boost at 180 Robux to RPG Kit at 4,310. Three more are named Revenge Pack, Revenge Pack 2 and Revenge Pack 3 (190, 290 and 390 Robux); their names do not say what is inside. The price table for these 20 records, and the price gap between each Limited Time Pack and the Kit of the same name, are in the weapon packs guide. These are Robux prices. Damage, fire rate and in-game cash prices are not in the records we read.

    Which vehicle-style products cost Robux?

    Five records have a name that reads like a vehicle. One of the five, Rezvani, is called a car by another official record we read; the other four names are in no event listing we read that day.

    • Mazede: 350 Robux, record created July 3, 2025
    • Christmas Low Rider Pack: 500 Robux, record created December 5, 2025
    • Dirt Bike: 910 Robux, record created July 3, 2025
    • Stang: 2,999 Robux, record created July 3, 2025
    • Rezvani: 8,000 Robux, record created July 28, 2025

    The game's Roblox event listings include one titled "NEW CAR DROPPING!" with the subtitle "Introducing the Rezvani", listed for August 2 to August 3, 2025. That listing is an announcement and gives no price. What Mazede and Stang are, and what Christmas Low Rider Pack contains, is not confirmed. A sixth vehicle-style name, Mansion Upgrade: Humvee, sits in the Mansion group above.

    Does BlockSpin have game passes or badges?

    Not in the records we read on October 10, 2026: the game pass list for BlockSpin came back with zero entries, and so did the badge list. Everything on this page is a developer product. A pass or badge added after that date would not be here. Developer, rating and server facts are on the game info page.

    How did we read the shop records, and what is not confirmed?

    The developer product list came back in one response of 68 records with no further page to fetch. Sixty-six of the 68 were marked for sale, and this page counts 64 of those. The two for-sale records we left out are named "OLD PRODUCT DO NOT BUY" (850 Robux) and "OLD PRODUCT" (250 Robux). Their names mark them as old and one says not to buy, so we do not list them as shop items; that is our choice, and the for-sale flag on both was on when we read them. The other two of the 68 were not marked for sale and had no price, and they are left out as well.

    Five of the 68 records have text in the description field, and four of those five are among the 64. Fifty-six of the 68 share an edit date of May 13, 2026. An edit date does not say what was changed, and a creation date is the day a record was made, which is not proof of when a product reached players.

    Still not confirmed after this read:

    • what any product without a description does, including Protect Item, Passive Mode, Stock Skip, Revenge Pack and Quick Revive;
    • whether a product can be bought more than once;
    • whether prices differ inside the game from the record on the day you buy.

    Our advice, not a rule of the game: read the purchase prompt in the game before you confirm, because 60 of these 64 records give you a name and a price and no description. If a price here no longer matches the game, the author page explains how to report it.
