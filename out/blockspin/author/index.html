    Jellyfi is the editorial byline on LootWiki, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the BlockSpin guides on this site. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

    How these guides are researched

    BlockSpin is a Roblox game, so the most reliable facts come from Roblox itself: the official game description, the game's public statistics and content rating, and the developer group's own pages. After that comes the studio's website and posts its developers make on the Roblox Developer Forum. Community wikis, videos and code sites are used to find leads and cross-check, never as the only source for a fact.

    That ranking decides what gets published. If a detail — a job name, a price, a drop chance, a code — only appears on community sites, it stays out of the published guides until it can be checked in the live game or in an official post.

    How codes are handled

    Codes are only listed after being seen in an official BlockSpin source. When a code leaves that source it is marked expired and kept on the page with the date it was noticed, rather than deleted.

    How to read the dates on a page

    Under the heading of each article, "Published" is the date the page went live on this site and "Last checked" is the date the text was checked against its sources. Roblox games update without version numbers, so these dates are the best guide to how fresh a page is.

    Reporting an error

    If a code, a number or a rule does not match the game you are playing, email us through the Contact page and include the page link. The site-wide writing and correction rules are on the Editorial Policy page.
