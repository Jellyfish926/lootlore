3:T1c1a,<p>The <strong>lightning boomerang</strong> spellbook is the one magic build in Sephiria with a documented high-tier clear behind it: a Traditional Chinese Steam guide, updated mid-August 2026, reports final-boss DPS "over 10K without trying, 30K–50K+ with good pickups" on Hard Mode 60. The catch, in the author's own words, is that the power curve is not smooth — the build feels weak until a handful of specific artifacts land, then jumps. This page is that handful, in the order the author takes them.</p>
<p>Like every route on this site it is one player's attributed run, not a ranking — the <a href="/builds/build-routes/">builds overview</a> explains why we do not publish a tier list.</p>

<h2>The idea in one sentence</h2>
<p>Cast the <strong>Lightning Boomerang</strong> spellbook continuously, keep its cooldown near zero with <strong>Academy</strong> items, keep MP full with the <strong>Lake</strong> water jug, and stack <strong>compasses</strong> in a row directly under the boomerang for damage. Everything else is support.</p>
<h2>Weapon and costume</h2>
<p>The guide recommends three weapons and rules out a fourth:</p>
<div class="tw"><table><thead><tr><th>Weapon</th><th>When</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Magic-crit Blade (the katana line with magic critical damage)</td><td>Highest ceiling — take it if you are chasing numbers</td></tr><tr><td>Lightning Staff</td><td>Safer; better defensive tools if your dodging is weak</td></tr><tr><td>Lightning Sword &amp; Shield</td><td>Same reasoning as the staff — <a href="/weapons/all-weapons/">Sword &amp; Shield</a> blocks cost MP, so this pairs naturally with a high-MP build</td></tr><tr><td>Magic great sword</td><td>The author refuses it: "no defensive ability at all"</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>Costume is a trade-off the author leaves to you. Ghost or Red Cloak keep MP comfortable early but have no lifesteal, which hurts on high tiers; bringing a lifesteal stone through the Wish Fountain instead makes the early floors MP-starved. Frog and Skeleton are also listed as workable.</p>
<h2>The core artifacts — take on sight</h2>
<p>These are the pieces the build does not function without. The names below are our translation of the guide's Traditional Chinese; where the English item name is confirmed elsewhere we use it, and where it is not we say so.</p>
<ul><li><strong>Lightning Boomerang</strong> spellbook — the main damage source.</li><li><strong>Water Jug</strong> (Lake tag) — "without this your damage stops growing and the boomerang cooldown never gets good." It activates at <strong>200 MP</strong>, which you reach through the Lake combo bonus plus <strong>Broken Branch</strong> (+60 max MP) and <strong>Sapphire</strong> (+33% max MP). The Lake tag also carries the <strong>Cup</strong>, the only MP-steal artifact the author knows of — take it if you are not on the Ghost costume.</li><li><strong>Hourglass</strong> and <strong>Pocket Watch</strong> (Academy tag) — the two cooldown reducers. "Take on sight; the build is not fun until both are in."</li><li><strong>Meditation Book</strong> (Academy) — without it "the late game is very hard to play": casts stall, you get burst down, and you cannot chain casts.</li><li><strong>Compass</strong> — as many as you can find, placed in a contiguous row under the boomerang. Each is +35% at the author's count; six in a line is +210%. The <a href="/guides/inventory-grid/">tablet and grid rules</a> for "contiguous with nothing in between" apply here exactly.</li></ul>
<p>Secondary damage, all optional: the <strong>Lightning Magic Bolt</strong> (your only real damage until the boomerang appears, if you did not start Ghost), <strong>Thunder Armour</strong> and <strong>Judgement of Thunder</strong> — take them when MP allows.</p>
<h2>Pickup priority</h2>
<p>The author's order, which we reproduce rather than reorder:</p>
<p><strong>Core artifacts &gt; Magitech ≥ Lake (to 200 MP) ≥ Precision &gt; Academy (non-core) ≥ Elemental and other damage.</strong></p>
<ul><li><strong>Magitech</strong>: spellbooks and lightning damage. Some elemental damage early "makes the run more comfortable."</li><li><strong>Lake</strong>: only the water jug and the MP-steal cup really matter; the rest of the tag is low value but you take it for the combo count and the MP ceiling.</li><li><strong>Academy</strong>: Meditation Book, Hourglass, Pocket Watch, then anything with combo count or cooldown. The author is specific about why the Crabapple is low priority: its spellbook speed-up is +14 against the Pocket Watch's +80 and the Coin's +30.</li><li><strong>Precision</strong>: crit damage and legendaries first.</li><li><strong>Elemental</strong>: the Elemental Book first as a secondary base-damage source, the rest for combo count.</li><li><strong>Alchemy</strong>: only the Potato, for ignoring enemy defence; the Traveller's Blessing is skippable.</li></ul>
<p>For survivability the author prefers evasion over the Shield Bag's defence route, and notes the <strong>Battle Bracelet + Elastic Band</strong> combination lets a single dash become near-permanent evasion if you keep landing dodges.</p>
<h2>What the DPS numbers mean</h2>
<p>The 10K / 30K / 50K figures are the author's final-boss readings on Hard Mode 60, from a recorded run, not from the training dummy. They are not reproducible by us and we have not tried; we print them because they are the only published magnitude for a spellbook build and because the author is candid that they depend on luck — "usually you can still clear with a few pieces missing, it just isn't as satisfying."</p>
<h2>Patch notes that touch this build</h2>
<p>Nothing in the 1.0.19–1.0.30 line rebalances a lightning spellbook. Two adjacent changes are worth knowing: <strong>Green Sawblade</strong> (1.0.26) now applies its buff correctly when 30 or more MP is spent in one cast, which matters for a build that burns MP in bursts; and the <strong>1.0.26 preset expansion</strong> (7 → 15 slots, with a lock) means you can keep this loadout saved next to a melee one. If you run the staff variant, 1.0.30 changed <strong>Flourish Staff</strong> so holding attack during a special resumes attacking immediately afterwards.</p>
