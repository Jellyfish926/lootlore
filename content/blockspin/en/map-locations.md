# BlockSpin Map Locations: Stores, Jobs and Key Places

BlockSpin's city is set in a Florida county, according to the game's official description. The official promotional art confirms two places you will visit early: a Quick-11 convenience store and a fast-food restaurant. Other locations below come from community sources and are waiting for an in-game check.

## Which locations are confirmed so far?

| Location | What it is for | Confirmed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your house | Holds the safe where the game tells you to store items | Official description |
| Quick-11 | Convenience store; community guides list a Shelf Stocker job here | Official thumbnail (store); community (job) |
| Burger Place | Fast-food restaurant; community guides list a Janitor job here | Official thumbnail (restaurant); community (name, job) |
| The Butcher's Cut | Community guides list a Cook job here | Community only |
| Car Dealership | Sells vehicle cases, per an old community wiki page | Community only (2025) |
