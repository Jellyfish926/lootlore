Sephiria Weapons: All 6 Classes

Six classes, more than fifty upgrades each. Which is strongest, which is kindest to a new player, and how the trees branch.

Sephiria Weapons: All 6 Classes, Unlocks and Upgrades

All six Sephiria weapon classes, their special attacks, how each unlocks via Moro's training quests, and how the Anvil upgrade trees branch.

A weapon is a tree you commit to, not an item you find

The first thing to understand about Sephiria's weapons is that you do not really pick one per run — you pick which of six upgrade trees you want to feed. Two players on the same class can finish a run with completely different damage profiles depending on which branches the Anvil offered and which ones they took.

That reframes the usual question. "Which weapon is strongest" has no useful answer. "Which tree do I have deep enough to survive chapter 4" does.

Five of the six are locked

You start with Sword & Shield. Great Sword, Dagger, Crossbow, Blade and Staff each require two separate things: buying the node in the Destiny Inscription tree, and completing a training quest with Moro in the village afterwards. Buying the node alone does nothing, which is the single most common point of confusion for new players.

That means your first stretch of the game is an economy problem rather than a combat one. Sapphires come from dying, scaled to how deep you reached, so early runs that end on floor 2 are still productive.

Upgrades replace, they do not add

This is the part that surprises people coming from other roguelites. A large share of Sephiria's weapon upgrades do not improve a number — they swap out your normal attack, your dash attack or your special entirely. Whirlwind becomes Reassemble or Wound Explosion. Parry becomes a five-dagger throw. The Blade's special becomes Ice Sliding.

So an upgrade choice is closer to picking a subclass mid-run, and taking two upgrades that pull in different directions leaves you with neither.

The Anvil economy

Upgrades come from Anvil nodes, and anvils are gated by stage progression — skip them early and later anvils can stop appearing at all. Every experienced player's advice reduces to the same sentence: rush every anvil you see and get to max tier as fast as you can. Anvil offers reroll with dice, and a rerolled option will not come back.

You can plan without spending a run. The in-game Journal → Weapons tab shows every path, and the Comprehensive Training Grounds has an anvil next to the guard NPC where you can walk an entire tree for free.

Why the inventory page matters here

Weapon upgrades are half a build. What the Artifacts and Tablets in your grid do to those upgrades is the other half — a fast weapon that stacks an effect is a different weapon depending on what sits beside it in the bag.
