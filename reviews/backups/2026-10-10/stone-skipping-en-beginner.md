# +1 Stone Skipping Beginner Guides: Where to Start

New to +1 Stone Skipping? Read how to play first: it explains Skill per bounce, levels, zones, Wins, pets and Rebirth using the developer's own words. Then check the updates page for the World 5 event on 3 October 2026, and the community page for the Meow Labs group and the state of codes.

The official description of the game is six short lines, and the store items have no descriptions at all. These guides stay inside that evidence. Where the developer has said nothing, the page says "not shown in public data" instead of filling the gap with a guess.

## What should you do in your first session?

Our suggestion, using only what the developer has published. Throw, and watch Skill rise by one per bounce. Train when the game offers it, because levels are what make you throw farther. Try to reach a zone you have not reached before, since farther zones pay more Wins. When you can hatch a pet, do it and read the boost it shows. Leave Robux alone until you know which part of the loop slows you down; the [Robux shop guides](/stone-skipping/robux/) are there for that moment.

## What do these guides not cover yet?

There is no zone list, no pet tier list, no stone list and no rebirth calculator. The numbers those pages need are not published in any official source we can read, and we have not yet recorded them in game. The how to play guide ends with a table of what is still unknown, so you can see the gaps rather than trip over them.
