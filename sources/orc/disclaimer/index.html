Disclaimer: an Unofficial Orc Problem Reference

Last updated 2026-09-01

One page, four plain statements about what this site is and is not.

Fan-run, not official

The Orc Problem Field Manual has no relationship with Mumpitz Games. Nobody here works for them, none of the content is reviewed by them, and no money flows in either direction. Sir, We Have an Orc Problem and all associated names and imagery are their property, referenced here so the pages can talk about the game at all. The place to reach the actual developers is their Steam page and Discord, not our contact form.

Every figure has an expiry risk

This site's rule is that a number appears only when it can be traced to Steam's own API, a named player guide, or Mumpitz Games' store text — always with the date it was read. The game, meanwhile, keeps updating: two balance-affecting patches shipped within three weeks of launch. A turret figure measured on 31 July can drift from the build you are playing today. Where a page's date is older than the newest update on the update history page, trust the game over the page.

Nothing here is buying advice

Pages that mention the game's price, the Supporter Pack, or Steam Deck compatibility describe what the store listed on the retrieval date. Prices move, sales happen, ratings get revised. Check the live Steam listing before spending money; if a purchase disappoints, Steam's refund policy — not this site — is the remedy.

Outbound links and advertising

Guides link to Steam community pages, third-party articles and the developer's announcements because citing sources beats asserting things. A link means "this is where the claim comes from," never "we vouch for everything on that domain." The display advertising on this site comes from an ad network, arrives unvetted by us, and pays for hosting; an advertised product appearing next to a guide is proximity, not endorsement. Details on ad cookies live in the privacy policy.

Spotted a claim here that the current build contradicts? That is exactly what the contact page is for — corrections with a screenshot or a source get applied fastest.

A small reference for a small game. Every number here is traced back to either Steam's own API or a named player guide, and where the community has not measured something we say so instead of estimating it.
