3:T244e,<p>TEAM HORAY says Sephiria has <strong>about 70 tablets</strong>. The most careful community dataset catalogues <strong>62</strong> with full mechanics, and its maintainer believes at least one is still missing. Neither number is wrong; they are counting different things.</p>
<p>What matters more than the count is that tablets fall into <strong>four mechanically distinct families</strong>, and knowing which family a tablet belongs to tells you where it can go before you read a single number.</p>
<figure class="fig"><img src="/images/sephiria-inventory-transference-panel-1200.webp" srcset="/images/sephiria-inventory-transference-panel-800.webp 800w, /images/sephiria-inventory-transference-panel-1200.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, 720px" alt="Sephiria Transference panel with options to increase or reduce artifact level and rotate" width="1200" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Transference: increase level, reduce level, rotate. Official screenshot — Sephiria, TEAM HORAY</figcaption></figure>
<h2>Family 1 — discrete offsets</h2>
<p>The largest group. The tablet buffs specific cells at fixed relative positions.</p>
<p>Footprint sizes across the dataset: 1 cell (12 tablets), 2 cells (15), 3 (8), 4 (10), 5 (4), 6 (2), and 8 for <strong>Thorns</strong>, the widest. Ten tablets have no discrete cells at all because they belong to another family.</p>
<p>Two hard limits worth memorising: <strong>maximum reach is 3 cells</strong> (Manhattan distance), and <strong>the largest single-cell buff is +5</strong>, held by <strong>Conviction</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Twelve tablets carry negative cells</strong> — Exploitation, Unity, Gaze, Nurture, Competition, Advent, Wave, Trick, Disconnection, Last Stand, Flag and Defensive Move. A careless layout with these actively lowers your artifact levels. They are not traps; they usually pay for their debuff with a larger buff elsewhere in the same pattern. But you have to place them deliberately.</p>
<h2>Family 2 — line buffs</h2>
<p>Twelve tablets buff an <strong>entire row, column or diagonal</strong>. Some reference themselves; others anchor to a fixed grid line.</p>
<p><strong>Miracle, Transference, Cohesion, Justice, Shade, Vigilance, Foundation, Brilliance, Simultaneity, Rebellion, Courage, Requital.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Requital is the strongest single line effect: +3 to its entire row.</strong> On a 6-wide grid that is +18 of distributed value from one cell. Foundation and Simultaneity are the +1 workhorses, and Foundation matters disproportionately because the <strong>Endure talent at level 5 hands you one at the start of every run</strong>.</p>
<p>Line tablets are what you <a href="/builds/build-routes/">build</a> a layout <em>around</em>. Place one first, then stack discrete tablets onto the cells you want lifted highest.</p>
<h2>Family 3 — board parity</h2>
<p>Exactly one tablet: <strong>Curse</strong>, obtained from the Skeleton costume.</p>
<p>It applies a chessboard pattern across the <strong>entire grid</strong> — odd cells +1, even cells −1. A cell at offset (dx, dy) counts as odd when dx + dy is odd.</p>
<p>Three consequences that make it unlike anything else:</p>
<ul><li>Its own position does not limit its reach. It always covers the whole board.</li><li>The parity of the cell it sits on decides <strong>which colour</strong> gets the buff — move it one cell in any direction and the entire pattern inverts.</li><li><strong>Rotation does nothing.</strong></li></ul>
<p>Two parity tablets on same-parity cells stack; on opposite-parity cells they cancel exactly.</p>
<h2>Family 4 — edge-anchored</h2>
<p>Four tablets are <strong>inactive unless placed on a specific grid edge</strong>:</p>
<div class="tw"><table><thead><tr><th>Tablet</th><th>Required edge</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Good Will</td><td>bottom</td></tr><tr><td>Shade</td><td>top</td></tr><tr><td>Flag</td><td>left</td></tr><tr><td>Justice</td><td>left or right</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>Off their edge they contribute nothing while still consuming a cell. Community tooling flags them explicitly as <em>"INACTIVE (needs edge: …)"</em> because this is the single most common layout mistake.</p>
<p><strong>Place these first when planning, or do not pick them up.</strong></p>
<h2>Rotation</h2>
<p>Tablets rotate in 90° steps with <strong>R</strong>, or since patch 1.0.19 with a <strong>middle mouse click</strong>.</p>
<p>But <strong>18 of the 62 cannot be rotated at all</strong>: Good Will, Justice, Destiny, Shade, Desire, Vigilance, Cheers, Foundation, Distribution, Three-Headed, Dry, Simultaneity, Disconnection, Thorns, Entrance, Exit, Hospitality, Flag.</p>
<p>A further <strong>14 are rotatable but symmetric</strong>, so only one or two rotations are actually distinct — spinning them is wasted effort. Self-referential row and column buffs swap axis at 90° and 270°; diagonal line buffs flip direction.</p>
<h2>The full name list</h2>
<p>In dataset order: Hope, Miracle, Transference, Exploitation, Cohesion, Good Will, Justice, Unity, Destiny, Chivalry, Wit, Gaze, Pulsation, Nurture, Shade, Binary Star, Link, Competition, Desire, Advent, Vigilance, Wave, Trick, Dedication, Cheers, Harvest, Advance, Foundation, Distribution, Three-Headed, Past, Future, Stack, Brilliance, Handshake, Approximation, Power, Compression, Dry, Simultaneity, Disconnection, Daydream, Thorns, Rebellion, Conviction, Preparation, Entrance, Exit, Oaths, Peace, Courage, Hospitality, Honor, Junction, Rally, Progress, Last Stand, Flag, Defensive Move, Requital, Curse, Wedge.</p>
<p>One naming note: a 2025 patch describes a tablet called <strong>"Spike"</strong> whose effect matches the current <strong>Dry</strong>. No tablet named Spike exists in the current game. If you find it referenced somewhere, that page predates the rename.</p>
<h2>Where tablets come from</h2>
<p>Tablet nodes, shops, the <strong>Mystic Pot</strong> in its pre-1.0 form (which converted <a href="/artifacts/artifacts-and-tablets/">artifacts</a> into tablets), and the Endure talent's level-5 Foundation. One tablet is exclusive to the <strong>Dimensional Rift</strong> and two more to the <strong>Wandering Witch</strong>.</p>
<p>Critically, tablets are <strong>gated content</strong>: the free <em>Tablet</em> node in Destiny Inscription must be purchased before tablet nodes or tablet objects appear in runs at all. It is the first thing to buy.</p>
<h2>Merging with the Tablet Combiner</h2>
<p>Added in patch 0.10.14, the Tablet Combiner appears in every Safe Zone after Wasteland, next to the merchant. It costs <strong>200 Leaf</strong> and works once per combiner.</p>
<p>The merge rules, as modelled by the community solver:</p>
<ul><li>Both sources' <strong>discrete effects combine</strong>, baked at whatever rotations you chose at merge time.</li><li>Both sources' <strong>line buffs combine</strong>.</li><li>The result is <strong>non-rotatable if either parent was</strong>.</li><li>Parity effects carry over; if both parents had one, the odd and even values sum.</li><li><strong>Edge requirements:</strong> neither parent → none. One parent → inherited. Identical → inherited. One set contained in the other → the narrower wins.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Disjoint or partially overlapping edge requirements are blocked</strong>, on the explicit grounds that the in-game behaviour is unconfirmed. That is a sensible position to copy rather than experiment with, because a merge is irreversible and the combiner is one-use.</p>
<p>The obvious high-value merge is a line tablet into a strong discrete tablet — Requital plus Conviction gives you +3 across a row <em>and</em> +5 on one cell, from a single occupied slot.</p>
<h2>What changed in 1.0.19</h2>
<p>The only tablet balance in the 1.0 window:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Nurture</strong> rarity Advanced → Common</li><li><strong>Trick</strong> rarity Advanced → Common</li><li><strong>Competition</strong> downward bonus +2 → +3</li><li><strong>Wave</strong> upper-right diagonal +2 → +3</li><li><strong>Preparation</strong> lower-right diagonal +1 → +2</li><li><strong>Daydream</strong> reworked from "+1 on each of the 4 diagonals plus +1 on the 4 cells beyond" to <strong>"+2 on each of the 4 diagonals"</strong> — fewer cells, higher values</li></ul>
<p>Patches 1.0.21 through 1.0.24 contain <strong>zero</strong> tablet balance changes. One tablet-adjacent fix landed in 1.0.23: the Alchemy combo failed to trigger when a tablet was obtained directly into the Side Bag.</p>
<h2>Where placement is explained</h2>
<p>This page is the taxonomy. How buff values become artifact levels, how to engineer a high-value cell, and how Tablet Engraving frees up slots are covered on the <a href="/guides/inventory-grid/">inventory grid</a> page.</p>
<h2>Provenance</h2>
<p>Mechanical detail here comes from an <strong>open-source community dataset</strong> whose values match the 1.0.19 patch notes exactly on every item they both cover — Competition +3, Wave +3, Preparation +2 and Daydream's four diagonals all line up. That agreement is why we are willing to cite it for things TEAM HORAY has never published.
