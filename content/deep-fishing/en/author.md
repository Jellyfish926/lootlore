# Author: Jellyfi, Deep Fishing Guide Editor

Jellyfi is the editorial byline on {{BRAND}}, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the [Deep Fishing guides](/deep-fishing/) on this site. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

## How these guides are researched

Deep Fishing is a Roblox game, so the most reliable facts are the ones the developer registers on Roblox itself: the game description, the badge list, the game passes and the store products. Those are read directly from Roblox's public data and quoted word for word, with the date we checked them.

![View from inside a giant pink fish mouth, looking out at a Roblox angler on a dock](art02 "Every guide in this section is published under one editorial byline")

Fan wikis, code sites and YouTube videos are used only as leads. If one of them is the only source for a fact — a code, a coin price, a fish name, a water's unlock cost — that fact is left out until something official confirms it. That is why some popular topics, such as a full fish list, do not have a page yet.

Buying orders and "what to do first" advice are editorial suggestions, not rules of the game, and each page says so where it matters.

## How to read the dates on a page

"Published" is when the page first went live. "Last checked" is when the text was compared against its sources. Roblox numbers such as badge totals, visits and prices are snapshots from that day; badge totals in particular grow every day.

## Found a mistake?

The game updates often. If a price, name or menu no longer matches the game, use the Contact page and include the page link and a screenshot from the game. Screenshots of the starter rod, the enchant screen or the codes box are especially welcome, because those are gaps we could not fill from official data.
