# Author: Jellyfi, Animal Daycare Guide Editor

Jellyfi is the editorial byline on {{BRAND}}, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the [Animal Daycare guides](/animal-daycare/) on this site. The byline covers research, fact checks against official Roblox data and updates when the game changes. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

## How these guides are researched

Animal Daycare (Anomaly) is a Roblox game, so the most reliable facts are the ones the developer registers on Roblox itself: the game description, the badge list and the Robux products. Those are read directly from Roblox's public data and quoted word for word, spelling quirks included, with the date we checked them.

![A lamb child holding up a drawing of a shadowy creature with red eyes](th2 "Every guide in this section is published under one editorial byline")

Fan wikis, code sites and YouTube videos are used only as leads. If one of them is the only source for a fact — a code, an impostor tell, a night threat, a class name — that fact is left out of published pages until something official or our own check confirms it. That is why popular topics such as codes and impostor clues do not have a page yet.

Buying orders and "what to do first" advice are editorial suggestions, not rules of the game, and each page says so where it matters.

## How to read the dates on a page

"Published" is when the page was first published. "Last checked" is when the text was compared against its sources. Roblox numbers such as badge totals, visits and prices are snapshots from that day; badge totals in particular grow every day.

## Found a mistake?

The game updates often. If a price, name or menu no longer matches the game, use the [Contact](/contact) page and include the page link and a screenshot from the game. Screenshots of the class menu, the in-game shop or an official codes box are especially welcome, because those are gaps we could not fill from official data.
