    +1 Mog Evolution Rebirth, Ascend, Wins and Worlds Guide

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-01 · Checked on version 2026-09-30 update

    Scope: Progression systems as named in the official description and developer product descriptions on 2026-10-01; numeric thresholds (level cap, Wins per ascend, world requirements) are not published

    Key points

    • Rebirth normally needs you to hit a level cap; Ascend moves you to the next body and needs Wins.
    • The store names two bodies, LTN and Gigachad; official art shows the labels SUB 3, MTN, Chad, Chopped, Mogger and True Adam, but not their order.
    • Three Stages have teleports, and there are skips to World 2 and World 3 at 99 Robux each; how the worlds unlock otherwise is not published.
    • The actual thresholds are not published, so we do not guess them.

    Progress in +1 Mog Evolution runs on two resets plus Stages and Worlds. Rebirth needs you to reach a level cap. Ascend uses Wins to move you to the next body. Stages and Worlds are named in the store but not described. The developer publishes no numbers for any of these, but the store wording shows how they connect.

    Below, each system gets one short section built from the official text, then a table of the Robux skips, so you can decide whether skipping is worth it.

    How do the progression systems fit together?

    This is our reading of the official wording, from the first click to the top of the leaderboard. Each step gives its source in brackets.

    1. Click for Appeal. Every click gives Appeal (game description).
    2. Upgrade your hammer. Bonesmash is the named upgrade (game description).
    3. Rebirth at the level cap. Rebirth normally happens once you reach "the level cap" (Skip Rebirth description).
    4. Earn Wins and ascend. "Earn Wins to ascend" (game description); ascending takes you "to the next body" (Skip Ascend description).
    5. Reach Stages and Worlds. Stages 1–3 have teleports, and there are skips to World 2 and World 3 (product names only; what they contain is not described).
    6. Climb the leaderboard. The goal is to "become a True Adam" (game description).

    The order of steps 3 to 5 is not stated anywhere. Rebirth might come before Ascend or run alongside it, and we cannot tell from the wording.

    What does Rebirth do?

    The only official sentence is in the Skip Rebirth product (39 Robux): "Rebirth instantly without reaching the level cap." So you have a level, it has a cap, and reaching the cap lets you rebirth. What rebirth resets and what it gives are not described.

    One more clue: the x9, x99 and x999 treadmill belts say "no rebirths needed". That suggests some treadmill belts are normally unlocked through rebirths. The Appeal guide covers the belts.

    What does Ascend do, and what are bodies?

    Ascending moves you "to the next body", according to the Skip Ascend product (29 Robux, "no requirements"). The game description says you "Earn Wins to ascend". How many Wins each body needs is not published.

    How do Wins work?

    Wins are the resource for ascending. Three store items touch them:

    • X2 Wins (49 Robux): "Permanently earn double Wins."
    • 2x Win Pad (49 Robux): no description. Its name suggests a pad where you collect Wins, but that is our guess.
    • Starter Pack (9 Robux): "the LTN body, +2,000 Appeal and +50 Wins. Can only be bought once."

    What are Stages and Worlds?

    Three products unlock teleports to Stage 1, 2 and 3, for 2, 5 and 9 Robux. Two more skip to World 2 and World 3, for 99 Robux each, with no description. The experience is titled "[W3] +1 Mog Evolution", which suggests World 3 is the newest. When each skip appeared is on the updates page.

    Our advice: the skips are one-off shortcuts, while X2 Wins is permanent. If Wins are what holds you back, a permanent doubler usually beats paying 29 Robux per body. The full store is on the Robux products page.

    Is there a Ranked mode?

    There is a place inside the experience named "+1 Mog Evolution [RANKED]", listed by Roblox. Its description is empty, and we found no official rules, entry requirements or rewards for it.

    What still needs checking in the game?

    • The level cap, and what Rebirth resets and rewards.
    • How many Wins each Ascend costs, and the full body order.
    • What a Stage is, and how World 2 and World 3 unlock without paying.
    • What the leaderboard ranks, and how Ranked works.
