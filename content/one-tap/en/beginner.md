# One Tap Beginner Guides: Rules, Rewards and Updates

New to One Tap? Read the how-to-play guide first. It covers the three weapon rules the developer has published: one-shot snipers, two-shot secondaries and melee. Then read the rewards guide, since levels, daily and quests are the progress the description names, and keep the update log for when something in the game changes.

These four guides stay close to what the group Stringless Banjo has published on Roblox: an 18-line description, six event listings, four pass icons and the game's public record. Where the developer has said nothing, the guides say so.

## Pick a guide by question

### [How to Play: Weapon Rules, Unlocks and Bans](/one-tap/how-to-play/)

What "FFA arena, no teams" means in practice, the official line for each weapon class, what you can unlock and equip, and the notice about permanent bans.

### [Rewards: Levels, Daily, Quests and XP Boosts](/one-tap/rewards/)

The three reward sources in the description, the level and leaderboard prizes named in update listings, and what the Xp Boost products and Refresh Quest cost.

### [Updates: 6 Official Event Listings and Dates](/one-tap/updates/)

Every listing from Valentines in February 2026 to Game Revert in August, quoted in full, with the store records created around each one.

### [Game Info: Developer, Platforms and Servers](/one-tap/game-info/)

The group behind the game, the platforms the description lists, the 8-player server size, the content rating and why no official Discord server can be confirmed.

## What should a new One Tap player do first?

Our suggestion is to play a few One Tap matches before you read a single price. We have not played for these guides yet, so the order below is built on the official description and nothing else.

| Step | Why | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learn the sniper | The description leads with "One-shot snipers" | How to play |
| Aim for the head with a secondary | "Two-Shot Secondaries (One to the head)" | How to play |
| Find levels, daily and quests in the menus | They are the three reward sources the description names | Rewards |
| Read the ban notice | Farming with alts is named next to cheating | How to play |
| Check the update log | Maps and leaderboards have been pulled and delayed before | Updates |

The table is our suggested order, not a rule of the game.

## When should you think about Robux?

After those first matches, if at all. One Tap is free to play, and no line in the description says a purchase is needed. The store is large, though, with 4 passes and 97 products, and none of the products has a description. When you do want to compare purchases, the [Cases & Robux Shop section](/one-tap/robux/) has every recorded price.

## What do these One Tap guides leave out?

Anything the developer has not published and we have not yet checked in the game. That covers controls, map names, how a match is scored, weapon stats, the quest list and what each level pays. There is also no codes page, because no code appears in the official sources we could read.

## Scope

The game record was created on 4 December 2025 and the developer has posted six event listings since, the newest for 15 August 2026. Each guide shows the date it was checked, and counters such as visits and members are snapshots from 10 October 2026. This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Roblox or Stringless Banjo.
