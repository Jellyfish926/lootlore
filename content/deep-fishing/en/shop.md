# Deep Fishing Robux Shop: Boosts, Chests, Enchant Stones

Besides rods and passes, Deep Fishing's Robux shop sells enchant stones, four kinds of lucky chest, server-wide luck, a Revive All Fish option, eight paid stat upgrades and cheap one-throw boosts. Enchant Stones start at 49 Robux and Ultra Enchant Stones at 199; both product records were created on 6 September 2026.

This page lists the developer products registered for the game on Roblox — 103 of them when we read the list on 10 October 2026 — minus the rod unlocks, Exclusive rods and skins (see the [rod list](/deep-fishing/rods/)), pass gifts (see [game passes](/deep-fishing/gamepasses/)) and mutation rerolls and scrolls (see the [rarity guide](/deep-fishing/rarity/)). Product listings give a name and a price, not a description, so we only state an effect when the name itself states it.

## What are Enchant Stones in Deep Fishing?

Enchant Stones are a consumable sold in two grades. Their product records were created on 6 September 2026, and their names confirm that some form of enchanting exists in the game.

| Product | Robux |
| --- | --- |
| x1 Enchant Stone | 49 |
| x3 Enchant Stones | 129 |
| x10 Enchant Stones | 399 |
| x50 Enchant Stones | 1,499 |
| x1 Ultra Enchant Stone | 199 |
| x3 Ultra Enchant Stones | 549 |
| x10 Ultra Enchant Stones | 1,649 |
| x50 Ultra Enchant Stones | 4,499 |

What an enchant does and what the Ultra grade adds are not in the official records we read on 10 October 2026. One event listing, "Update 6 🔥", has the tagline "World 3 (Snow World) and Rod Entchantments 24 new Fish and much more!" (spelling as written), an announcement that ties enchantments to rods. Fan guides also mention a paid island, which we have not verified, so we do not describe enchant effects here; the [enchants page](/deep-fishing/enchants/) sets out what is confirmed and why the store has no Enchant Scroll. If you have a screenshot of the enchant screen, the [author page](/deep-fishing/author/) says how to send it.

## Which lucky chests can you buy?

Four chest types exist; their product records were created in two waves.

| Chest | Single | Bundle | Record created |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Element Lucky Chest | 99 | x3 for 249, x10 for 799 | 25 Aug 2026 |
| Pirate Lucky Chest | 79 | x5 for 349 | 26 Sep 2026 |
| Charm Lucky Chest | 99 | x5 for 399 | 26 Sep 2026 |
| Hero Lucky Chest | 149 | x5 for 649 | 26 Sep 2026 |

Chest contents and drop odds are not published. The Pirate, Charm and Hero chest records were created on the same day as the two Exclusive rod records, so they may belong to the same update, but the records do not say so.

## What is Revive All Fish?

"Revive All Fish" is a 39-Robux product whose record was created on 29 August 2026 and edited on 18 September. The name suggests that fish in the game can be revived; what that means in play, and when you would need it, is not explained in the listing. Players searching for a "revive heart" may be looking for the same system, but we have not confirmed a link, so treat it as an open question.

A separate product, "Skip Big Mouth Timer" (79 Robux, record created 3 September), suggests that something called Big Mouth runs on a timer. The event listing "Mutation Roll + Rarity💫" carries four lines about it: "Added Big Mouth Fish", "Feed it every 12 Hours", "Feeding it grants Permanent Luck boost" and "Complete the Luck boost to get Big Mouth Fish". What the product itself skips is not stated in its record.

## Which boosts last for one cast only?

The "One Throw" products, whose records were created on 18 September, include the two cheapest items in the shop at 8 Robux each.

| Boost | Robux |
| --- | --- |
| More Fish (One Throw) | 8 |
| Freeze Fish (One Throw) | 8 |
| Mutation Luck (One Throw) | 19 |
| Collect All (One Throw) | 24 |

Use them on a cast you already expect to go far. A one-throw boost on a short cast is a small price, but still wasted. Mutation-focused players should also read the [rarity and mutations guide](/deep-fishing/rarity/) for rerolls and Ultra Mutation Scrolls.

## What do the release dates tell you?

The creation dates double as a rough update log: gifts in early August, Element chests and rod skins on 25 August, Revive All Fish on 29 August, bulk mutation rerolls on 1 September, enchant stones and Ultra Mutation Scrolls on 6 September, one-throw boosts on 18 September, a chest-and-rod wave on 26 September, season products on 29 September, Leviathan Bottles on 30 September, Oni Soulbinder Skin on 9 October, and Tail Dragon Eggs on 10 October (UTC dates). A product's creation date is when it was registered, not always the day its update went live.
