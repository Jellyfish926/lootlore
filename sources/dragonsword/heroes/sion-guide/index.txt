<p>Sion is documented almost entirely through bug fixes. Hound13 has never written a trailer post, a kit description or a balance note for him. What exists is four patch lines, and they happen to be unusually informative — because each one tells you what the <strong>Relic</strong> is by describing a way it used to break.</p>

<h2>The Relic is a physical object on the map</h2>
<p>Our <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">roster page</a> gives him one line: "Places a <strong>Relic</strong> on the field." Patch <strong>1.0.10</strong> (20 August 2026) tells you what kind of thing that is, by grouping it:</p>
<blockquote>Fixed an issue where the aim target of projectiles was misdirected when certain objects were placed - Othello's <strong>[Ice Spear]</strong> object - Cerese's <strong>[Ice Pillar]</strong> - <strong>[Relic] placed by Sion</strong> - Charlotte's <strong>[Clone]</strong></blockquote>
<p>That is the most useful sentence anyone has written about Sion. The Relic sits in the same category as an ice spear, an ice pillar and a clone — a <strong>persistent world object with collision</strong>, solid enough that the game's own auto-aim was targeting it instead of enemies. It is not a buff aura or a status effect; it is a thing you put down and that other things can bump into.</p>
<p>Which means two practical consequences worth knowing before the fix and after it. Before 1.0.10, dropping a Relic in a ranged team's firing line was actively harmful. After it, placement is free of that penalty — but the Relic is still a body in the arena, and this game's <a href="/guides/status-ailments-and-signals/">status ailment and Signal system</a> is built on hitting the target you meant to hit.</p>
<h2>The dodge key was eating it</h2>
<p>Patch <strong>1.0.8</strong> (7 August 2026) is four fixes with one shared cause — dashing by <em>holding</em> the dodge key:</p>
<blockquote>- Fixed an issue where the character was switched to non-combat state when using dash by holding the dodge key - Fixed an issue where <strong>Sion's Relic placed on the field was retrieved</strong> when using dash by holding the dodge key - Fixed an issue where Theresia's <strong>[Inquisition]</strong> state was ended during combat when using dash by holding the dodge key - Fixed an issue where Kalsion's <strong>[Royal Flame]</strong> state was ended during combat when using dash by holding the dodge key</blockquote>
<p>Our <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">roster page</a> already records this cluster. Read from Sion's side, the detail that matters is the verb: the Relic was <strong>retrieved</strong>, not destroyed. So placement is reversible by design — there is a pick-up, and a held dodge key was triggering it by accident. If you played him in the game's first two weeks and could never keep a Relic down, that was this.</p>
<p>This also puts him in a specific group: three heroes whose <em>persistent state</em> was being silently cancelled by a movement input. The other two are <a href="/heroes/theresia-guide/">Theresia</a> and <a href="/heroes/kalsion-build/">Kalsion</a>. All three are worth re-testing if you formed an opinion pre-1.0.8.</p>
<h2>The dodge key got a lot more interesting in 1.0.11</h2>
<p>The same patch that touched Sion added the game's first dedicated defensive mechanic. Hound13's specification, verbatim:</p>
<blockquote>Added a new feature that triggers <strong>Just Dodge</strong> when perfectly evading an enemy attack with Dodge. When Just Dodge triggers, the following effects are applied: - <strong>1 second of invincibility</strong> - <strong>3 seconds of guaranteed critical hits</strong> - <strong>10% recovery of Active Skill Rage gauge</strong> - <strong>Half of the Dodge Stamina cost is recovered</strong></blockquote>
<p>That is a global change, not a Sion change, but it lands hardest on a hero whose relationship with the dodge button has been fraught since launch. Three seconds of guaranteed crits is a large window, and the Rage refund feeds Active Skills directly.</p>
<h2>And he can move in combat now</h2>
<p>Also in <strong>1.0.11</strong>:</p>
<blockquote>Fixed so that <strong>Sion can now perform parkour while in combat state</strong>.</blockquote>
<p>Hound13 does not say why this was Sion-specific, and no other hero is named. <strong>Not confirmed:</strong> whether other heroes always could and Sion was the exception, or whether this is a new capability. What is clear is that vertical traversal mid-fight is now available to him and was not before — in an open-world game where the <a href="/guides/bosses/">bosses page</a> records arena geometry mattering, that is a real positioning tool.</p>
<p>Patch <strong>1.0.12</strong> (10 September) does not mention him at all.</p>
<h2>Where he sits</h2>
<p>His faction is the <strong>Organa Order</strong> — Theresia, Ornette, Sion and Tarte, per the community wiki. Our <code>config/heroes.json</code> classifies him as a <strong>story</strong> unlock, meaning he joins as the campaign progresses and never costs a Fate's Invitation. The <a href="/heroes/unlock-planner/">unlock planner</a> therefore never has to price him, which removes the only decision most heroes present.</p>
<p>On ailments he is a blank. Neither the <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">roster page</a> nor the <a href="/heroes/ailment-coverage/">ailment coverage page</a> records one for him, and nothing in the patch notes through 1.0.12 names one. That is a genuine problem for team building in a game where Signals arm off ailments — until somebody documents what the Relic does, you cannot plan a chain around him.</p>
<h2>What this page cannot tell you</h2>
<p>What the Relic <em>does</em> once placed, his weapon, his ailment, his Karma trait, his Awakening effects, his skill names beyond [Relic], and whether the Relic can be destroyed by enemies. Hound13 has published none of it, and no player-written Steam guide covers him. Everything above is inference from four maintenance lines, and it is labelled as such.</p>5:["category","heroes","d"]
