3:T217c,<p>Hard Mode — internally <em>Root's Retreat</em> — is not a difficulty slider. It is a points budget you spend on individual cruelties, and the total you have spent is your <strong>tier</strong>. Clearing a tier is what pays out.</p>

<h2>Unlocking it</h2>
<p><strong>Clear Chapter 2</strong>, then talk to the Tree and choose the Hard Mode dialogue keyword. It moved to dialogue in patch 0.7.55, having previously been a straight Destiny Inscription purchase; if the dialogue stops appearing after you clear Chapter 4, a later patch added an unlock path through the Destiny Inscription menu instead. You enable it at the dungeon entrance.</p>
<p><strong>Tiers run 1 to 60.</strong> The cap was 30 before 1.0.</p>
<h2>The rule that catches everyone</h2>
<p><strong>You can only claim the next reward tier per clear.</strong> Clearing tier 30 five times walks you up to tier 15 — it does not jump you to 30. Overshooting the requirement gives you nothing extra.</p>
<p>So the efficient path is to clear tier 1, then 2, then 3, in order. Setting a punishing tier early feels productive and is not.</p>
<h2>The modifiers</h2>
<p>Seventeen are documented, split across the game's three groups. Their maximum tier values sum to exactly 60, which is a decent check that the set is complete.</p>

<h2>Which modifiers are cheap and which actually hurt</h2>
<p><strong>Cheap tiers for the reward:</strong> <em>Evil Energy</em> (1 tier), <em>Tension</em> (1 tier), <em>Severed Exchange</em> (2 tiers) and <em>Rebellion of Machines</em> (2 tiers) are the least disruptive per tier point if you are climbing for the Talent Points. Severed Exchange only costs money; Rebellion of Machines only punishes carelessness around traps.</p>
<p><strong>Disproportionately painful:</strong> <em>Dizziness</em> removes choices when you pick up <a href="/artifacts/artifacts-and-tablets/">artifacts</a> and <a href="/tablets/all-tablets/">tablets</a>, which attacks the build system directly rather than your HP bar. <em>Deterioration</em> at maximum cuts level-up healing by 60%, and level-ups are a primary heal source. <em>Tension</em> is one tier but changes boss fights categorically — no potions means no recovery mid-fight.</p>
<p><strong>Festival of Blood</strong> deserves a note. Enemies heal 15% of their HP when they damage you (<a href="/bosses/chapters-and-bosses/">bosses</a> 6%), which turns any build that trades hits into a stalemate. In multiplayer, patch <strong>1.0.21</strong> scaled the healing down with party size — <strong>66% effectiveness at 2 players, 50% at 3, 33% at 4</strong> — because four players getting hit was healing bosses faster than the group could damage them.</p>
<h2>The reward schedule</h2>
<div class="tw"><table><thead><tr><th>Tier</th><th>Reward</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1</td><td><em>Root's Retreat</em> achievement + Default background plate</td></tr><tr><td>3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 30</td><td><strong>+1 Talent Point each</strong></td></tr><tr><td>15</td><td><em>Root's Retreat 15</em> achievement + Bronze plate</td></tr><tr><td>30</td><td><em>Root's Retreat 30</em> achievement + Pale plate</td></tr><tr><td>35</td><td>+2 Talent Points</td></tr><tr><td>40</td><td>+3 Talent Points</td></tr><tr><td>50</td><td>+5 Talent Points + Ivory plate</td></tr><tr><td>60</td><td>Nothing beyond a distinct character border</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>That is <strong>20 Talent Points</strong> in total from Hard Mode, which is what takes you from the 25 available in Destiny Inscription to a full 45.</p>
<p>Note there is <strong>no achievement above tier 30.</strong> Steam's global stats show 44.0% of players have cleared tier 1, 30.1% tier 15, and 19.4% tier 30.</p>
<h2>What changed in 1.0</h2>
<p>The cap went from 30 to 60 and six new elements arrived: <strong>Festival of Blood, False Vanguard, Deterioration, Tension, Heavy Heart and Proliferation.</strong> Existing modifiers were deepened — Protective Armor gained a third level, Ferocious Claws and Tenacious Body went from 5 levels to 7 with the final level costing 2 points.</p>
<p>One modifier was <strong>removed</strong>: <em>Lingering Regret</em> (No Duplicate Artifact Combos) was deleted in 0.900.6 as incompatible with the reworked combo system. If you find it listed somewhere, that page has not been updated since April 2026.</p>
<h2>A climbing route</h2>
<p>Because rewards come one tier per clear, the sensible approach is to add the cheapest available modifier each time rather than build a "theme."</p>
<p>Start with the 1-tier and 2-tier entries — Evil Energy, Tension, Severed Exchange, Rebellion of Machines, False Vanguard, Heavy Heart — which gets you to tier 11 with relatively little pain. Then work up Ferocious Claws and Tenacious Body level by level, since each level is roughly one tier and enemy stats are the modifier type your build can most directly answer. Save Dizziness, Deterioration and Combat Stance for last.</p>
<h2>Checked against 1.0.30</h2>
<p>Two Hard Mode elements were misbehaving in some boss fights and were fixed in <strong>1.0.26</strong>: <em>Tension</em> was not being applied at all in certain boss rooms, and <em>Festival of Blood</em> was triggering on targets that had already been defeated. If a tier felt inconsistent between bosses before mid-August, that was the bug, not your build. <strong>1.0.28</strong> also fixed the Hard Mode difficulty level not changing properly on a gamepad. The 1.0.21 multiplayer healing scale-down for Blood Festival (66% / 50% / 33% at 2 / 3 / 4 players) is unchanged.</p>
<h2>Not verified</h2>
<p>TEAM HORAY's 1.0 announcement says <strong>18</strong> Hard Mode elements. Only 17 are documented, and those 17 sum to exactly the 60-tier cap. The eighteenth is unaccounted for — it may be a counting difference on the developer's side, or an element nobody has catalogued. We are not going to pad the list to reach 18.</p>
