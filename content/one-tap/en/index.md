# One Tap Guide: Cases, Passes, Rewards and Updates

One Tap is a free Roblox sniper shooter by the group Stringless Banjo, listed as [FPS] One Tap and created on 4 December 2025. It is a free-for-all with no teams: snipers are one-shot, secondaries are two-shot or one to the head, and servers hold 8 players. These guides cover the rules, the rewards and the Robux prices.

The official description opens with "Boom Headshot!" and calls the game "a fast-paced FPS sniper FFA arena, no teams, just pure chaos!" These guides are built from that description, the passes and products the developer has registered on Roblox, six official event listings and the group's public record. We have not checked anything in a live match yet, and each page says where that matters.

## What does Robux buy in One Tap?

Two kinds of thing. The 4 game passes are one-time purchases from 79 to 499 Robux: 2x Money, 2x Case Luck, 2x Level XP and Double Voting Value. The 97 developer products run from 5 to 10,000 Robux. By name they cover cases, Gems and Sun Points packs, a product named Premium Battlepass, six products named Skip, Xp Boost products, bundles, limited-named items and four donations.

None of the 97 product records has a description. So the guides in the [Cases & Robux Shop section](/one-tap/robux/) print each name and price exactly as recorded and say plainly when a function is not confirmed. Start with the [game pass list](/one-tap/gamepasses/) or the [battle pass page](/one-tap/battle-pass/).

## Why is there no codes page?

Because we could not find a single code in an official source. On 10 October 2026 we read the game description, the group description, the group shout and all six event listings. The description has 18 lines of text and none of them is a code. The Stringless Banjo group description is one sentence, and its shout is empty. Social links on the game and the group could not be read without an account. If a code turns up in an official place, a codes page for One Tap will follow.

## Who makes One Tap?

Stringless Banjo is a Roblox group created on 27 November 2025, a week before this game's record. Its description reads "Just me and my friend making fun roblox games lol". The [game info page](/one-tap/game-info/) covers the group, its owner account, platforms, server size and why we cannot confirm a Discord server.

## How this guide uses its sources

This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Roblox or Stringless Banjo. One Tap facts come from Roblox's own records for the game: its description, game passes, store products, event listings and group. Suggestions such as a buying order are our advice, not a rule of the game. Weapon stats, case contents and odds, map names and quest lists are left out because the developer has not published them and we have not yet checked them in the game.
