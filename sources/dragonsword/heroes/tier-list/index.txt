<p>Before you read any DragonSword: Awakening tier list — including the two on this page — know this: <strong>the two most-cited English rankings disagree with each other, badly.</strong> One site puts Reina in B tier; the other puts her in S. There is no in-game compatibility chart, no official ranking, and the game has been out since 22 July, which is not long enough for a settled meta.</p>

<p>So instead of publishing a third confident ranking, this page puts the two existing ones side by side and tells you where they agree — because the agreements are the only part you should treat as reliable.</p>

<p>One housekeeping note that trips up every reader comparing sources: <strong>English media cannot agree on spellings.</strong> Kalsion/Calcion, Cerese/Seris and Sion/Zion are the same three heroes — GameRant and games.gg use the first spelling of each pair, Pro Game Guides uses the second. Any tier list that appears to rank "both" is ranking one hero twice.</p>
<h2>Where they actually agree</h2>
<p>Only a few placements survive both lists, and those are the ones worth building around:</p>
<p><strong>Kalsion is S on both.</strong> The reasoning converges too: a melee fire hero whose burn-plus-launch chains do sustained combo damage with real area control. If you want one aggressive pick that no source disputes, this is it.</p>
<p><strong>Ornette is S on both.</strong> Shock application plus the Holy Circle critical-damage buff makes her the roster's clearest "makes everyone else better" pick — a support whose value does not depend on which damage dealer you run.</p>
<p><strong>Alex grades B or lower on both.</strong> Both lists read him as a defensive kit the game does not currently reward — general utility is low, and nothing in the ailment economy needs what he provides.</p>
<p><strong>Eileen is bottom of the one list that rates her</strong> and unrated on the other. Between that and her Airborne overlap with Astria and Reina, there is no argument for prioritising her with your early tickets.</p>
<h2>Why the disagreements happen</h2>
<p>The Reina gap (B versus S) is the informative one. Pro Game Guides grades her as a narrow airborne specialist; games.gg grades the airborne loop itself as one of the strongest patterns in the game and her as its best operator. Both descriptions of her kit are the same — the disagreement is about <strong>how much the aerial playstyle is worth</strong>, which depends on how you like to fight.</p>
<p>Theresia splits the same way: highest damage ceiling in the game on one list, A-tier on the other because that ceiling sits behind the hardest execution in the roster. If you will not practise her, her S placement is not for you.</p>
<p>The honest conclusion: past the four consensus calls above, <strong>tier placement is measuring the reviewer's playstyle as much as the hero.</strong></p>
<h2>Building a team instead of collecting S ranks</h2>
<p>Both sources and the Steam community converge on the same construction rule, which matters more than any individual grade: <strong>one hero who applies an ailment, one who cashes it in, one who holds the team up.</strong> Players in the Steam discussion thread describe it exactly that way — pair the applier with the payoff, never three appliers of things nobody answers.</p>
<p>Worked examples from games.gg: Theresia or Lute as the main damage pick, Castella for knockdown control, Dana or Roxy in the third slot. Their aggressive alternative stacks damage-over-time: Kalsion, Ornette and Charlotte layering burn, shock and poison. Pro Game Guides' beginner advice is simpler — take Kalsion or Cerese first for damage with built-in control, or Sion/Dana if you would rather play the balanced-support route.</p>
<p>One roster fact shapes all of this: <strong>Lute is the only hero with an on-demand heal</strong>, and this game has no natural HP regeneration. A tier list can afford to put him mid-table; your actual party mostly cannot.</p>
<h2>Spending Fate's Invitations against this table</h2>
<p>Eleven heroes are ticket purchases — you pick them directly in the Exchange, no randomness. Story <a href="/guides/chapters-and-bosses/">chapters</a> and Hero Quests keep supplying tickets, so nothing is permanently missable; the only cost of a "wrong" pick is time. Against the table above, the low-regret early buys are the two consensus S picks (Kalsion arrives via story, so in practice: Ornette if you need support, Dana or Charlotte if you need damage flexibility). Full unlock mechanics are on the <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">all heroes page</a>.</p>
<h2>What this page refuses to rank</h2>
<p>Sion, Astria, Eileen and Othello carry a "not rated" from games.gg because that site marked their evaluations incomplete, and we are not going to fill the gap with our own guesses. Four more heroes — Ryza, Jerome, Veronica and Logan — are confirmed free <a href="/guides/patch-notes-and-roadmap/">roadmap</a> additions for later in 2026 and cannot be ranked before they exist.
