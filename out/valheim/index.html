    Valheim Walkthrough: Start to Deep North

    Author Jellyfi · Published 2026-09-17 · Last checked 2026-09-17 · Checked on version 1.0.12

    Language: English中文

    Key points

    • Work out which stage you are in, then solve the one problem in front of you — you do not have to read the whole site first.
    • New save? Start with the first day, the biome progression order, combat basics and death recovery.
    • Stuck rather than under-geared? Check food, the Rested buff and mead before you craft another armour piece.
    • The main route runs Meadows → Black Forest → Swamp → Mountains → Plains → Mistlands → Ashlands → Deep North.

    About this guide

    This site breaks Valheim's main progression route into guides you can use straight away: work out which stage you are in, then solve the one problem in front of you. You do not have to read a whole manual first, and you do not have to grind materials down a gear checklist.

    Playing for the first time? Start here

    • Valheim First Day: Survive Night One: get tools, shelter, food and a spawn point done.
    • Valheim Progression Order by Biome: work out what to do now and what the next biome needs.
    • Valheim Combat Basics: Block and Dodge: learn to defend, hit in short bursts and keep stamina to escape.
    • Valheim Death Recovery: Get Your Gear: turn a corpse run into a prepared rescue.

    Continue your main route

    • Black Forest: Valheim Bronze Age: Copper, Tin, Gear → The Elder boss guide
    • Swamp: Swamp progression guide → Bonemass boss guide
    • Mountains: Mountains progression guide → Moder boss guide
    • Plains: Plains progression guide → Yagluth boss guide
    • Mistlands: Mistlands progression guide → The Queen boss guide
    • Ashlands: Ashlands progression guide → Fader boss guide
    • Deep North: Deep North progression guide → Intricate Key crafting guide → Kall Fimbulbringer boss guide

    Not sure what is stopping you? Check these first

    When nothing seems to die fast enough, the answer is not always one more armour piece. Check food, the Rested buff and your mead first.

    If you have the resources but cannot use them, read crafting and repair. If nothing ever makes it home, check portals and your sailing routes.

    Planning a long run with friends? Agree on co-op and server rules first. Coming back from an older version? Read the 1.0 save and world choice guide and the mods troubleshooting guide.

    How this site uses its sources

    This is unofficial guide content. Game mechanics follow official information first, and specific play advice is compiled from traceable community sources. A "recommendation" is one workable plan, not the only or strongest way to play. Sources stay in the text, and vanilla standard survival is the default. Unverified drop rates, exact damage numbers and speedrun times are never presented here as settled facts.
