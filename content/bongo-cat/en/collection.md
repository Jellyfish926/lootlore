# Bongo Cat Hats, Skins and Achievements Guides

This section is for collectors. It explains where items come from and how rare each tier is, how to turn duplicates into better items or sell them, which limited events have already come and gone, and what every Steam achievement asks for. The four guides link to each other, so start with whichever goal you have.

## Pick a guide by goal

![A cat in a red samurai helmet with a paw-print banner above the tap counter](ss03 "Rare looks come from many sources, not just chests")
### [Bongo Cat Hats and Skins: Rarity and Sources](/bongo-cat/hats-skins/)

The official drop odds from Common to Legendary, the non-droppable Unique tier, a table of every item source from chests to collaborations, and the supporter packs sold as DLC.

### [Bongo Cat Exchange, Trading and Market Rules](/bongo-cat/exchange-trading/)

How ten duplicates become one higher-rarity item, the rules that catch people out, when trading with friends was switched off and on again, and what can be sold on the Community Market.

### [Bongo Cat Events and Paw Pass: Full Timeline](/bongo-cat/events/)

The seasonal and charity events with their dates, the advent and Lunar New Year calendars, collab giveaways, and the rules of the monthly Paw Pass, including what happens if you join late.

### [All 28 Bongo Cat Achievements and Unlock Rates](/bongo-cat/achievements/)

The complete achievement table with requirements copied from Steam and global unlock rates, grouped by taps, items, emojis, lobbies and exchanges, with a suggested order.

## How the four pages fit together

Items come from chests at fixed odds, the exchange moves them up a tier, events and the Paw Pass add limited ones, and several achievements reward the same activities. A player chasing a specific Legendary will usually need all four pages: the odds to set expectations, the exchange to climb, the event list to know whether it can still drop, and the market rules if it cannot.

## What these pages will not tell you

There is no redeem-code system in the game, so there is no codes list here, and there are no Steam Points Shop items for this app at the time of checking. We also do not publish market prices: they are set by players and move too fast to be useful in a guide. What you will find instead is the rules behind the prices, so you can judge whether an item is worth exchanging for, waiting for, or buying.

## Scope

Figures come from the Steam store page, Steam's public achievement stats and the developer's announcements, each linked on the page that uses it. We do not list individual hats with a rarity, because the official item definitions are not public without an API key and third-party lists cannot be checked against them. New starters should read [how to play](/bongo-cat/how-to-play/) first.
