3:T23fb,<p>Talents are free to respec at any time outside the dungeon — at the mirror, or with <strong>P</strong>. That single fact should change how you approach them: there is no wrong permanent choice, only a wrong choice for the run you are about to start.</p>
<p>You get <strong>25 points</strong> from Destiny Inscription and <strong>20 more</strong> from Hard Mode tier rewards, for <strong>45 total</strong>. That is enough for two level-20 capstones with change, or three trees at 10 plus one at 15.</p>
<p>To try a spread against those 45 points before you commit them, the <a href="/guides/talent-planner/">talent planner</a> adds up the totals across all seven trees and flags every breakpoint you land on.</p>
<p>Assignment unlocks with the <em>Home Repairs</em> quest. Willpower additionally requires <em>Terrain Recovery</em>, and Ingenuity has its own Destiny Inscription quest.</p>
<p>If you have bought Home Repair on the Destiny Inscription tree and still cannot see talents, the missing step is the errand itself: talk to <strong>Ken</strong> in Bunnyville, gather <strong>two Wood</strong> from the forest southwest of town, place them at the damaged section of your house, and use the <strong>Hammer</strong> he hands you. Walking into the repaired house opens the talent screen. It sounds skippable; it is not — and the associated <em>DIY</em> achievement sits at <strong>91.7%</strong> unlocked, so if talents feel hidden, you are in the small minority that simply has not run the errand yet.</p>

<p>† <strong>Willpower's fountain numbers conflict in the official record.</strong> The <a href="https://steamcommunity.com/games/2436940/announcements/detail/1819386365106847" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">0.10.0 notes</a> state that <em>"the maximum capacity obtainable through the talent 'Willpower' has been reduced from 6 to 5"</em> — yet the same post lists +1 at level 5 and +4 at level 20, which is +5 on a base of 4. The numbers in this table are the per-level lines as officially published; the stated maximum contradicts them, and nothing in the 1.0 patches has re-confirmed either figure. Fuller discussion on the <a href="/artifacts/artifacts-and-tablets/">artifacts page</a>.</p>
<h2>The three that decide runs</h2>
<p><strong>Endure 5</strong> is the highest-value five points in the game for a new player. A <strong>Foundation</strong> tablet at the start of every run is a free +1 across an entire row of your bag — before you have found a single tablet. Every artifact you place in that row starts at level 1 instead of 0.</p>
<p><strong>Ingenuity 20</strong> is the highest ceiling. +7% universal damage per <em>active combo type</em> means six combos at their minimum thresholds is +42% on everything. It was buffed from 4% in patch 0.10.0, which tells you TEAM HORAY intended it to be a build-defining capstone rather than a rounding error. It is also the one that rewards the <a href="/guides/combo-effects/">combo</a> breadth strategy specifically.</p>
<p><strong>Ingenuity 10</strong> is quietly excellent on its own: one extra option offered on every weapon, item and Miracle choice. In a game where anvil rerolls cost dice, a permanent extra option is compounding value.</p>
<h2>The two traps</h2>
<p><strong>Survival 20 costs you 12 inventory slots.</strong> Read that row again. The capstone unlocks Tablet Imprinting — the ability to consume a tablet and permanently engrave its effect into the bag, freeing the cell — but the price is twelve slots off a grid that only has thirty. It is a genuine endgame trade for players who have enough engraved buffs that they no longer need the space, and a disaster if taken early. A player who has run it describes engraving as <em>"helpful for getting a first god run going"</em> — note "first god run," not "<a href="/guides/beginner/">first run</a>."</p>
<p><strong>Wisdom 10 is not "+2 combo."</strong> What it unlocks is <em>[Unique] Stacking</em>: duplicate artifacts get offered, and taking one adds a star to the copy you already hold rather than giving you a second copy. There is a combo interaction, but TEAM HORAY stated it was deliberately <strong>not</strong> applied to combo-changing artifacts such as Chalcedony Key and White Paper, and separately fixed a bug where the interface displayed a combo increase that was never applied. The "+2 combo" shorthand circulating on auto-generated wikis predates both of those.</p>
<p>Wisdom 10 also grants <strong>+1 die on boss kill</strong>, which is the part people forget and which is worth taking on its own.</p>
<h2>Suggested spreads</h2>
<p><strong>First clear (25 points):</strong> Endure 5 → Survival 10 → Endure 10 → remaining into Wrath or Swiftness. You are buying survivability and inventory space, which is what actually gets you past chapter 3.</p>
<p><strong>Damage build (45 points):</strong> Ingenuity 20 → Wrath 20 → 5 into Endure for the Foundation tablet. This assumes you can reliably assemble five or six combos, which needs a wide artifact pool from Destiny Inscription.</p>
<p><strong>Engraving build (45 points):</strong> Survival 20 → Ingenuity 5 → Endure 5 → the rest into Willpower. This only makes sense once you know which tablets you want engraved; the 12-slot penalty has to be paid back by engraved buffs.</p>
<p><strong>Reroll build:</strong> Wisdom 20 gives +1 free reroll per reward and +20% EXP. Combined with 6 starting dice from Destiny Inscription, this is the version of the game where you mostly get the offers you want.</p>
<h2>Checked against 1.0.30</h2>
<p>No talent value moved between 1.0.25 and 1.0.30. The Survival 20 capstone is safer to use than it was: <strong>1.0.30</strong> fixed Tablet Imprinting consuming the tablet without applying its effect when the activation conditions were not met. Preset handling also changed in <strong>1.0.26</strong> — slots went from 7 to 15 with a lock function, and <em>Reset Slot</em> became <em>Delete Slot</em>, which cannot be undone — worth knowing if you keep one preset per talent spread.</p>
<h2>What is not settled</h2>
<p>The talent that grants Tablet Imprinting has been called <strong>Survive</strong>, <strong>Endurance</strong> and <strong>Survival</strong> in different translation passes of the patch notes. We use Survival because that is what current community data uses, but check the in-game name before quoting it.</p>
<p>Per-point values in the table come from community data maintained against 1.0.25 rather than from a developer document — TEAM HORAY has never published a talent table. Two values are confirmed directly in patch notes: Ingenuity 20's scaling (4% → 7%) and the Wisdom 10 rework.</p>
<p>Two further discrepancies we are tracking rather than resolving. One third-party wiki lists an eighth tree, <strong>Greed</strong> (+3% Leaf drops per point), with its milestones marked unconfirmed even there — no second source corroborates it, so it stays out of our table. And an early-access-era guide referred to a talent called <strong>Patience</strong> that matches no current tree name; it is most likely an old name for Endure, but nobody has documented the rename, so treat any advice mentioning it as pre-1.0.</p>5:["category","guides","d"]
