# Bongo Cat Guide: Hats, Chests and Achievements

Bongo Cat is a free desktop pet on Steam from Irox Games. A small cat sits on your taskbar and taps every time you type or click, and those taps pay for chests full of cosmetic hats and skins. This hub explains how drops, exchanges, errors and achievements actually work, with the developer's own posts as the source.

The game launched on March 5, 2025, and the developer's one-year anniversary post says 5.8 million players had downloaded it by then ([anniversary announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1826362059921578)). It runs in the background while you work, study or play something else, so most questions are about what it does quietly: how fast chests come, what the odds are, and whether it is safe to leave running.

## How rare is each item tier?

These are the drop pool chances printed on the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3419430/Bongo_Cat/), which the developer marks as subject to change:

| Rarity | Chance per chest drop |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 90% |
| Uncommon | 9.5% |
| Rare | 0.49% |
| Epic | 0.01% |
| Legendary | 1 in 500000 |

Most players will never see an Epic from a chest. The route up is trading ten duplicates of one tier for one item of the next tier, covered in [exchange and trading](/bongo-cat/exchange-trading/). Limited event items and the monthly Paw Pass are the other big sources, listed in [events and the Paw Pass](/bongo-cat/events/).
