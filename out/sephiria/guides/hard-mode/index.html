    Sephiria Hard Mode: All Modifiers and 60-Tier Rewards

    By Jellyfi · Published 2026-08-10 · Last reviewed 2026-09-04 · Game version checked 1.0.30

    Hard Mode (Root's Retreat)

    Quick facts

    A points-budget difficulty system, internally named Root's Retreat. Tiers run 1 to 60 (cap was 30 before patch 1.0). Only the next reward tier is claimable per clear -- overshooting gives nothing extra.

    Tell us which guide you need, report a wrong number or suggest a tool. It lands straight in the editor's inbox.

    Request a guide

    Hard Mode — internally Root's Retreat — is not a difficulty slider. It is a points budget you spend on individual cruelties, and the total you have spent is your tier. Clearing a tier is what pays out.

    Unlocking it

    Clear Chapter 2, then talk to the Tree and choose the Hard Mode dialogue keyword. It moved to dialogue in patch 0.7.55, having previously been a straight Destiny Inscription purchase; if the dialogue stops appearing after you clear Chapter 4, a later patch added an unlock path through the Destiny Inscription menu instead. You enable it at the dungeon entrance.

    Tiers run 1 to 60. The cap was 30 before 1.0.

    The rule that catches everyone

    You can only claim the next reward tier per clear. Clearing tier 30 five times walks you up to tier 15 — it does not jump you to 30. Overshooting the requirement gives you nothing extra.

    So the efficient path is to clear tier 1, then 2, then 3, in order. Setting a punishing tier early feels productive and is not.

    The modifiers

    Seventeen are documented, split across the game's three groups. Their maximum tier values sum to exactly 60, which is a decent check that the set is complete.

    Which modifiers are cheap and which actually hurt

    Cheap tiers for the reward: Evil Energy (1 tier), Tension (1 tier), Severed Exchange (2 tiers) and Rebellion of Machines (2 tiers) are the least disruptive per tier point if you are climbing for the Talent Points. Severed Exchange only costs money; Rebellion of Machines only punishes carelessness around traps.

    Disproportionately painful: Dizziness removes choices when you pick up artifacts and tablets, which attacks the build system directly rather than your HP bar. Deterioration at maximum cuts level-up healing by 60%, and level-ups are a primary heal source. Tension is one tier but changes boss fights categorically — no potions means no recovery mid-fight.

    Festival of Blood deserves a note. Enemies heal 15% of their HP when they damage you (bosses 6%), which turns any build that trades hits into a stalemate. In multiplayer, patch 1.0.21 scaled the healing down with party size — 66% effectiveness at 2 players, 50% at 3, 33% at 4 — because four players getting hit was healing bosses faster than the group could damage them.

    That is 20 Talent Points in total from Hard Mode, which is what takes you from the 25 available in Destiny Inscription to a full 45.

    Note there is no achievement above tier 30. Steam's global stats show 44.0% of players have cleared tier 1, 30.1% tier 15, and 19.4% tier 30.

    What changed in 1.0

    The cap went from 30 to 60 and six new elements arrived: Festival of Blood, False Vanguard, Deterioration, Tension, Heavy Heart and Proliferation. Existing modifiers were deepened — Protective Armor gained a third level, Ferocious Claws and Tenacious Body went from 5 levels to 7 with the final level costing 2 points.

    One modifier was removed: Lingering Regret (No Duplicate Artifact Combos) was deleted in 0.900.6 as incompatible with the reworked combo system. If you find it listed somewhere, that page has not been updated since April 2026.

    A climbing route

    Because rewards come one tier per clear, the sensible approach is to add the cheapest available modifier each time rather than build a "theme."

    Start with the 1-tier and 2-tier entries — Evil Energy, Tension, Severed Exchange, Rebellion of Machines, False Vanguard, Heavy Heart — which gets you to tier 11 with relatively little pain. Then work up Ferocious Claws and Tenacious Body level by level, since each level is roughly one tier and enemy stats are the modifier type your build can most directly answer. Save Dizziness, Deterioration and Combat Stance for last.

    Checked against 1.0.30

    Two Hard Mode elements were misbehaving in some boss fights and were fixed in 1.0.26: Tension was not being applied at all in certain boss rooms, and Festival of Blood was triggering on targets that had already been defeated. If a tier felt inconsistent between bosses before mid-August, that was the bug, not your build. 1.0.28 also fixed the Hard Mode difficulty level not changing properly on a gamepad. The 1.0.21 multiplayer healing scale-down for Blood Festival (66% / 50% / 33% at 2 / 3 / 4 players) is unchanged.

    Not verified

    TEAM HORAY's 1.0 announcement says 18 Hard Mode elements. Only 17 are documented, and those 17 sum to exactly the 60-tier cap. The eighteenth is unaccounted for — it may be a counting difference on the developer's side, or an element nobody has catalogued. We are not going to pad the list to reach 18.

    Difficulty ratings you may see attached to individual modifiers ("Threat: Easy / Extreme") are one player's subjective assessment, not game data.
