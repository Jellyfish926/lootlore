3:T1b95,<p>Crossbow is the only one of Sephiria's six weapons built around a magazine, and that single fact explains almost everything else about how it plays: a finite ammo count, a dedicated reload key, and a special attack that most of its upgrade tree exists to reshape. Players report ranged builds having a better win rate generally, which tracks in a game with 60-plus enemy types and a lot of contact damage — but the class only works once you understand what actually happens when the magazine runs dry.</p>

<h2>What the base weapon does</h2>
<p>TEAM HORAY described the design intent plainly when Crossbow was announced: increase fire rate and ammo capacity to spray arrows, or lay mines to hit several enemies at once. That's the whole kit in one sentence — a magazine-fed normal attack, a dash attack that places a mine, and an MP-funded special bolt that reloads or pierces depending on the branch you take. Unlocking it costs the <strong>Ranged Weapon Training</strong> node in the <a href="/guides/destiny-inscription/">Destiny Inscription</a> tree, the same structure that gates all five non-starting weapons.</p>
<p>Crossbow shipped in patch 0.7.35 with 16 enhancement tree variants — 6 Tier 2 and 10 Tier 3 — which is the clearest public statement TEAM HORAY has made about the shape of any weapon's upgrade tree. Every upgrade in the class carries the <strong>Colossal Crossbow:</strong> prefix, and per the <a href="/weapons/all-weapons/">all weapons</a> page, the tree includes Gunpowder Synthesis, Scattershot Shooter, Frost Veil, Reload Device, Fire Mine, and Rapid Freeze Crystal among its named branches.</p>
<h2>The reload problem, and how the tree solves it</h2>
<p>Every Crossbow build has to answer one question: what happens when you're empty? The tree offers at least two different answers, and they produce genuinely different playstyles rather than variations on one build.</p>
<p><strong>Reload Device</strong> turns the special attack into a high-speed reload instead of a damage tool, trading burst for uptime — a route that rewards pairing with on-hit or attack-speed support elsewhere in the bag, since the special is no longer your damage cooldown. <strong>Rapid Freeze Crystal</strong>, on the Frost Veil branch, removes magazine consumption entirely while its timed cold-firing state is active, which sidesteps the reload question altogether rather than answering it. A third branch drops the magazine question and goes wide instead: <strong>Scattershot Shooter</strong> converts the normal shot into a close-range explosion, which functions closer to a shotgun than a rifle and asks you to reposition between magazines rather than fight the reload timer directly.</p>
<p>These are meaningfully different engines, not minor variations of one ranked build — a build leaning on Reload Device plays nothing like one built around Frost Veil's ammo-free window, and picking between them earlier rather than later avoids wasting anvil rerolls on upgrades that don't support your route.</p>
<h2>What patch history tells you about the current build</h2>
<p>The most recent change to the class is also the most concrete: <strong>1.0.30</strong> gave the Frozen Bow a flat capability buff — its arrows now pass through walls, which removes what was previously its clearest weakness in wall-heavy rooms. That's a meaningful upgrade to an already popular pick, and it's recent enough that guides written before 20 August 2026 won't mention it.</p>
<p>Two smaller items are worth knowing if you're troubleshooting something that feels broken rather than choosing a route: <strong>1.0.23</strong> fixed the Minigun's MP cost applying incorrectly in decimal units below 1, and a Superconductor multiplayer notification issue (relevant if a Storm Cloud-consuming special can't fire) was addressed the same patch window on the Great Sword side, documented on the <a href="/patch-notes/1-0-25-to-1-0-30/">1.0.25 to 1.0.30 patch page</a>. Neither is a build decision, but both explain behavior that otherwise looks like a bug in your setup rather than a known one.</p>
<h2>Where Crossbow sits among the other five weapons</h2>
<p>Unlike <a href="/weapons/sword-and-shield-guide/">Sword &amp; Shield</a>, whose entire identity runs through a single held guard, Crossbow's kit is built from independent, swappable pieces — magazine management, mine placement, and special-attack identity can each be upgraded on separate branches without one determining the others. That modularity is also why "best Crossbow build" doesn't resolve into a single answer the way some classes' does: Reload Device, Frost Veil, and Scattershot Shooter are three different answers to the same magazine problem, and which one fits depends on what the rest of your run's <a href="/artifacts/combo-tags-and-key-artifacts/">artifact tags</a> are already supporting.</p>
<p>If ranged damage output specifically is what you're chasing, cross-reference the <a href="/guides/damage-types/">damage types</a> page for how Frost Veil's cold application interacts with Frostbite as a status rather than a flat number — the mechanic underneath "cold support" branches is documented there, not on this page.</p>
<h2>What we're not printing</h2>
<p>We have no confirmed per-branch DPS figures for Crossbow — TEAM HORAY has published magazine-size and upgrade-count statements, but no numeric damage coefficients for Reload Device, Frost Veil, or Scattershot Shooter specifically, so exact numbers here are <strong>not confirmed</strong>. Magazine size itself is also version-sensitive: check the current in-game tooltip before committing a Hard Mode run to a specific reload plan, since coefficients can shift between patches without always making the patch notes headline.</p>
