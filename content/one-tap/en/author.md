# Author: Jellyfi, Editor of the One Tap Guides

Jellyfi is the editorial byline on {{BRAND}}, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the [One Tap guides](/one-tap/) on this site. Game facts in them come from Roblox's official records for the game, quoted word for word with the date they were checked. Every guide article published under the byline is listed at the end of this page.

## How are these guides researched?

The game lives on Roblox, so the most reliable facts are the ones its developer registers there: the game description, the game passes, the store products, the event listings and the group that owns the game. Those are read directly from Roblox's public data and quoted exactly, brackets and capital letters included.

![Official game icon: a grinning yellow block-headed character in a blue shirt aims a blocky green and black sniper rifle, while a red-haired character on a rooftop behind aims a green rifle back](icon "Every guide in this section is published under one editorial byline")

Official art is treated as a second tier. The four game pass icons carry a line of text each, and for those passes the icon is the only official wording we could read. Anything taken from an image is labelled as read from the art.

Other fan sites, videos and comment threads are leads only. If one of them is the only source for a fact, that fact stays out.

## What is deliberately missing from the One Tap guides?

A lot of what players want is not in any official source: weapon stats, what each case contains and at what odds, map names, the quest list and what each level pays. These guides do not fill those gaps from memory or from other sites. Most guides include a section on what is not stated, and items move out of it only after they have been checked in the game.

## How are One Tap codes handled?

A code is listed only after it has been seen in an official source, and the page says whether it has been tested in the game. For One Tap no code has appeared in the description, the developer group or the event listings, so no codes page exists. The [how-to-play guide](/one-tap/how-to-play/) is the place to start instead.

## How do you read the dates on a page?

Each guide shows a "Last reviewed" date beside the byline. That is the day the text was last compared against its sources. Roblox counters such as visits, likes and group members are snapshots from that day and keep moving.

## How do you report an error?

The store has grown since the first batch of records on 29 December 2025, and four product records were edited as recently as 30 September 2026. If a price or a name no longer matches what you see, use the [Contact](/contact) page and include the page link and, if you can, a screenshot. Screenshots of case contents, the battle pass screen and the quest list are especially welcome, because those are gaps the official data does not fill. The site-wide writing and correction rules are on the [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy) page.

## Where should you start?

New players: [how to play](/one-tap/how-to-play/). Comparing case prices: the [cases guide](/one-tap/cases/). Planning purchases: the [Robux shop guide](/one-tap/shop/).
