# BlockSpin Beginner Guide: Jobs, Cases and Death Drops

In BlockSpin you work jobs to earn money, open random reward cases for gear, level up your character and compete with other players for control of the city. The one rule to learn before anything else: you drop everything in your inventory when you die, so keep what you value in the safe in your house.

That rule comes straight from the warning in BlockSpin's official description, and it shapes almost every decision a new player makes. The rest of this guide sticks to what the developers themselves state or show, and flags where you will need to find out in-game.

## What is the core loop, in the developers' words?

The description lays it out in one sentence: "Spin the block. Grind your way through jobs to unbox random reward cases, and level up your character to become the top dog in the city." Broken into steps:

| Step | What the description says | What it means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work | "Grind your way through jobs" | Jobs are the official starting point |
| Unbox | "unbox random reward cases" | Rewards are random, so two players on the same path can end up with different gear |
| Level | "level up your character" | Progress is tied to your character, not only to your items |
| Compete | "become the most powerful gangster around" with "intense battles, unforgettable finishers" | Other players are the challenge |

The studio's website adds its own short version: enter the city, grind jobs, fight for dominance.

## Why does dying cost you so much in BlockSpin?

Because the game does not let you keep your inventory. The official warning reads: "You drop everything in your inventory on death, place items into the safe in your house to keep them secure." Anything you carry is at risk every time you leave home.

A few habits follow from that rule:

- **Carry only what the next trip needs.** If you are heading to a job, you probably do not need your best items on you.
- **Bank your progress at home.** A run of good case openings is only safe once it is in the safe.
- **Know what a fight can cost.** Losing means your inventory drops; the description does not say who can pick up what you drop.

Roblox rates BlockSpin as Moderate for repeated violence, and the promotional art shows armed robbery as part of the setting.

## Who else is on your BlockSpin server?

Up to 28 players share a server, according to Roblox's game data on September 29, 2026. That is small enough that you will keep running into the same people, and anyone nearby may target you.

Roblox also reports that private (VIP) servers cannot be created for this game, so there is no quiet server to grind alone in.

## What can you do in BlockSpin besides jobs?

The official BlockSpin thumbnails show more than one way to spend your time. One shows a character fishing from a lawn chair at a lakeside, captioned "Fishing". Another shows a robbery at a Quick-11 convenience store. A third shows a high-end car, and cars are clearly part of the game's image. The description itself mentions a world that "reacts to your every move".

What we cannot tell you yet is the exact list of jobs, what each one pays or where each building sits on the map. Community sites publish lists, but they disagree and many are out of date, so we will add those details only once they are checked in-game.

## What should you do in your first BlockSpin session?

1. **Redeem the starter code.** W7C28D is in the official description and gives new players $500 — details on the [codes page](/blockspin/codes/).
2. **Find your house and its safe** before you have anything worth losing.
3. **Start a job** and learn the area around it while your inventory is still close to empty.
4. **Open reward cases as you earn them**, then move anything you want to keep into the safe.
5. **Stay away from exploit tools and "free money" scripts.** Roblox bans for exploits; the [cheats and bans page](/blockspin/cheats-bans/) explains what happens next.
