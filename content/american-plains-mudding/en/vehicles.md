# American Plains Mudding Vehicles and Trailers Overview

American Plains Mudding does not publish a vehicle list, so this page collects every vehicle and trailer type the developer has named in official text: the game description, 60 event listings, the game passes and the badges. It covers ATVs, pickups, an SUV, campers, farm machinery and trailers, with no stats or prices.

That limit is deliberate. Nothing on Roblox's public record says what a vehicle costs, how fast it is or how you unlock it, and we have not checked those in game. What the record does give is a dated trail of what was added.

Most of 2026 is a blank in this table. From 24 January to 12 September 2026, 33 of the 34 listings are titled only "APM Update", so whatever was added in those months is not named in any text we can read. The [update history](/american-plains-mudding/updates/) has every named listing.

## How do you get more vehicles?

Official text names four routes to vehicles, and a fifth, hidden vehicles, whose reward is not stated.

| Route | What the text says | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Like and favourite](/american-plains-mudding/how-to-play/) | "Like 👍 and Favorite ⭐ the game to obtain a vehicle!" | Free |
| Premium Vehicles pass | "Get access to exclusive vehicles!" | 230 Robux |
| Premium Trailers pass | "Get access to exclusive trailers!" | 195 Robux |
| Limited holiday passes | "Only available near" each holiday | Off sale on 9 Oct 2026 |
| Hidden vehicles | "Congrats, you have found a Hidden Vehicle." | Not stated; the badge text does not say you receive the vehicle |

Both premium passes add "(Items within this gamepass are subject to change over time)", so their contents are not fixed and not listed; prices for every pass are in the [game pass guide](/american-plains-mudding/gamepasses/). The holiday passes are covered in the [limited vehicle guide](/american-plains-mudding/limited-vehicles/). For hidden vehicles, the badge text confirms a badge; it does not say the vehicle becomes yours. The [badge guide](/american-plains-mudding/badges/) has the counts for all four.

How ordinary vehicles are unlocked is the biggest gap. The description mentions no in-game currency and no prices. Whether everything outside the passes is free to spawn is something only the game itself can answer.

## Why is there no American Plains Mudding tier list?

Because a tier list needs numbers, and the developer publishes none. Speed, torque, weight, towing capacity and mud performance are not in the description, the passes or the listings. A November 2025 listing does mention "4WD" among its additions, but it does not say which vehicles have it.

Ranking vehicles from videos would mean passing on someone else's impressions as fact. We would sooner leave the page without a ranking until the vehicles can be compared in game, on the same mud, with the same setup.
