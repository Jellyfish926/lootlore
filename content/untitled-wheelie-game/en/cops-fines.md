# Untitled Wheelie Game Cops: Fines, Passes and Tips

If the police catch you in Untitled Wheelie Game, you pay a fine. Two Robux purchases deal with it: the NEVER PAY FINES game pass (149 Robux) and the AVOID FINES developer product (13 Robux), whose effect is not described. No Roblox record we read on 10 October 2026 states a fine amount.

The pass description says you "never have to pay fines for when you get caught". The police are a headline feature. "RUN FROM COPS" is the first item in the game's official list of activities, and the 🚔 emoji is part of the game's name on Roblox.

## NEVER PAY FINES or AVOID FINES: which is better?

Both come from the developer's own store data, checked on 30 September 2026.

| Purchase | Type | Robux | Official description | Added |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NEVER PAY FINES | Game pass | 149 | Just never have to pay fines for when you get caught | 26 September 2026 |
| AVOID FINES | Developer product | 13 | None | 21 September 2026 |

A game pass on Roblox is a one-time purchase that stays with your account. A developer product can be bought again and again, which usually means a single use — but the developer does not say what AVOID FINES covers. If it clears one fine, the pass costs the same as about 11 or 12 of them (149 ÷ 13 ≈ 11.5, our calculation). That is a big "if", so treat the maths as a rough guide.

Our suggestion: if you get caught often, the pass is the simpler choice. If you rarely meet the police, keep your Robux.

## How can you avoid fines without spending Robux?

These are our tips, not published rules:

- **Bank your earnings early.** A fine has to be paid, so a pizza run or two before a risky ride gives you a cushion. The [money guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/money/) covers income.
- **Ride where you can see ahead.** The official art puts the police on the same highway as the traffic you are swerving through.
- **Practise wheelies away from the chase.** A dropped wheelie in front of a police car is the worst time to learn. The [how-to-play guide](/untitled-wheelie-game/how-to-play/) explains what the physics depend on.

## Is this the "AI Cops" wheelie game?

Be careful: two different Roblox games advertise AI cops. Untitled Wheelie Game (🚔) is made by Untitled Wheelie Group. Another experience, "[AI COPS + MORE👮] Wheelie District 🏍️", is made by a different group, Wheelie District Studios, and was created in February 2026. Guides and codes for one do not apply to the other. The [community page](/untitled-wheelie-game/community/) shows how to find the right group.
