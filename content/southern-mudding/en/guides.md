# Southern Mudding Guides: Vehicles, Passes and Updates

New to Southern Mudding? Start with the how-to-play guide, which walks through what the official description says you can do and gives a first-session order built on the four badges. Then read the update schedule, because the game changes every Friday. The other seven guides answer narrower questions about vehicles, spawning, nitrous, passes, Robux vehicles, badges and the group.

All nine guides stick to what the developer has registered on Roblox: the game description, four badges, 13 game passes, 61 developer products and the developer's weekly event listings. Each one says plainly when something is our advice and when a detail still needs an in-game check.

## Pick a Southern Mudding guide by question

### [How to Play: Your First Session](/southern-mudding/how-to-play/)

The official activities line by line, the four badge steps, default spawn limits, server size and a six-step first session that starts with joining the group.

### [Vehicle Types and How to Get Them](/southern-mudding/vehicles/)

The five classes the description names, what the five vehicle packs promise, the 22 single vehicles sorted by type, and why we do not publish a tier list.

### [Spawn Limits, Slots and Trailers](/southern-mudding/spawning/)

The default of two vehicles and two trailers, as implied by the pass descriptions, what Spawn 4 Vehicles, Spawn 4 Trailers, Any Slot Spawning and Portable Trailer Spawner change, and the trailers named in official data.

### [Nitrous and Customization](/southern-mudding/nitrous/)

What the 25 September notes confirm about nitrous for pickup trucks, the two 80-Robux customization passes, and a list of what to check in game.

### [All 13 Game Passes with Prices](/southern-mudding/gamepasses/)

Every pass with its official description, the cost per vehicle of each pack, and the gift versions that are listed below, or above, the pass price.

### [Limited Vehicles and Robux Shop Prices](/southern-mudding/limiteds/)

All 22 vehicles sold as developer products with prices and creation dates, what the for-sale flag does and does not prove, tornado spawn prices and the event unlock.

### [All 4 Badges with Award Counts](/southern-mudding/badges/)

Exact requirements, official totals, how rare Claimed a House is, and what the data says about houses and the Luxury Houses pass.

### [Update Schedule and History](/southern-mudding/updates/)

The Friday timing from the developer's own event listings, time-zone conversions, the 25 September notes and a dated history since September 2025, including all 43 weekly event titles.

### [Codes, Group and Discord](/southern-mudding/community/)

The verified group, its free pickup truck offer, who runs it, why there is no codes list and what we could not read as an anonymous visitor.

## What these guides do not cover yet

We have no pages for a map, for money-making, for individual vehicle stats or for codes. The map's locations, the in-game currency and every vehicle's performance are visible inside the game but not in its Roblox listing or store data. Codes have not appeared in any official place we can read. We hold those pages back until we can check them in game, and we do not fill them with guesses.

## Scope

Southern Mudding is a live Roblox game that changes weekly; the listing showed an update on 25 September 2026 and another was scheduled for 2 October. Every guide shows its check date. Badge totals, visits, member counts and prices are snapshots from about 11:20 UTC on 2 October 2026, read a few hours before that day's update.
