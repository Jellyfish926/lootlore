# BlockSpin Money and Gear Guides: Jobs, Weapons, Cars

Money in BlockSpin starts with jobs, and almost everything you spend it on — weapons, cars, cases — can be lost when you die. These guides cover where to earn, where to go and what to buy, with every detail checked against the live game before publishing.

## Pick a guide by what you need

### [BlockSpin Jobs Guide: Where to Work and Earn Cash](/blockspin/jobs/)

Which jobs are available, where each one is, how jobs feed reward cases and character levels, and how to work without handing your inventory to the next player who walks in.

### [BlockSpin Map Locations: Stores, Jobs and Key Places](/blockspin/map-locations/)

The stores, job sites and other places in BlockSpin's Florida county city, including the locations players search for most.

### [BlockSpin Weapons Guide: How to Get Guns and Melee](/blockspin/weapons/)

Where weapons come from — dumpsters, crates, airdrops and reward cases — and why a strong weapon is also a target.

### [BlockSpin Vehicles Guide: Car Cases and Drop Chances](/blockspin/vehicles/)

What each vehicle case can drop and how likely each car is.

## How these guides are checked

Community sites disagree about BlockSpin's jobs, prices and drop chances, and many of their numbers date from 2025. Each guide in this section is held back until its facts have been confirmed in the current game. Until then, the [getting started guides](/blockspin/getting-started/) cover everything the developers have confirmed officially, and the [BlockSpin hub](/blockspin/) links to all of them.
