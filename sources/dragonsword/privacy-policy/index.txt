<h2>Turning things off, first</h2>
<p>Most people arrive here looking for this, so it goes at the top:</p>
<ul><li>Clear or block cookies in your browser. Nothing on the guide breaks; some counting stops.</li><li>Switch off personalised advertising at <strong>adssettings.google.com</strong>.</li><li>Opt out vendor by vendor at <strong>aboutads.info</strong>.</li><li>Use a content blocker or a private window if you prefer. We do not try to detect either.</li><li>Email hello@dragonsword.site and ask what we hold, or ask us to delete a conversation.</li></ul>
<h2>About the people behind this guide</h2>
<p>dragonsword.site is an independent fan guide. There is no parent company. The contact address above reaches the person who writes the pages.</p>
<h2>Things you send us on purpose</h2>
<p>We do not run accounts, logins, comments, or payments — so we never see a name, a postal address, or card details.</p>
<p>Email is the only channel that leaves anything behind. When you write to us, your message stays in the inbox until the conversation is done.</p>
<h2>Cookies, in plain terms</h2>
<p>We set none ourselves. The ones you might find belong to the services below.</p>
<p>Google Analytics tells us which guides get read and roughly where readers came from. It sees an approximate location, the kind of device, the browser, and the pages opened. We only ever look at the totals — the point is working out which guide to write next.</p>
<h2>How advertising works here</h2>
<p>Advertising is what keeps the guide free to read.</p>
<p>Google acts as a third-party vendor and uses cookies to show advertisements on these pages. Through those cookies, Google and its partners can shape what you see based on your visits here and elsewhere.</p>
<p>Additional advertising networks may set their own cookies or similar technologies to measure how an advert performed. They each operate under their own privacy policies, and we have no control over what they gather.</p>
<h2>Links to stores</h2>
<p>A few pages link to shops that sell the game. Buying through one may earn us a commission, and it never costs you more. Those shops set a cookie so the sale can be attributed. It has no bearing on what we recommend — if something is not worth buying, the page says so.</p>
<h2>Younger readers</h2>
<p>The guide is not written for anyone under 13 and we do not knowingly gather information from that age group. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and we will delete it.</p>
<h2>How long anything is kept</h2>
<p>Google holds its own records on its own servers, in countries that may differ from yours, under terms it publishes. On our side there is no database at all — only the mailbox described above.</p>
