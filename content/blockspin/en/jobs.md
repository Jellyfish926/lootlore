# BlockSpin Jobs Guide: Where to Work and Earn Cash

Jobs are BlockSpin's built-in income. The official description tells you to "grind your way through jobs to unbox random reward cases, and level up your character", so a job is the first thing to do after redeeming the new-player code. Community guides currently report three jobs: Janitor at Burger Place, Shelf Stocker at Quick-11 and Cook at The Butcher's Cut.

## Which jobs can you work?

| Job | Location | Official confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Janitor | Burger Place | Official thumbnail shows a worker mopping a fast-food restaurant floor; job and building names from community guides |
| Shelf Stocker | Quick-11 | Official thumbnail shows the Quick-11 store; job name from community guides |
| Cook | The Butcher's Cut | Community guides only |

One community guide notes that more jobs have been reported in newer videos and are still being rechecked. Treat this table as the confirmed minimum, not the full list.

![A masked character with a pistol stands behind a worker mopping a fast-food restaurant floor](th4 "Official promotional thumbnail: a fast-food job scene")

## How do jobs connect to cases and levels?

The developers describe jobs as the way to earn toward random reward cases and character levels. Community guides add that each job has its own progression, so sticking with one job can make it more efficient over time. We have not verified pay rates, and they change with updates, so none are listed here.

## Is it safe to work a job?

Not entirely. Servers hold up to 28 players, and the game drops your whole inventory when you die. Anyone who sees you at a job knows roughly where you will be for the next few minutes. Leave valuables in your house safe before you start a shift — the [beginner guide](/blockspin/beginner/) explains the death-drop rule.

![Two masked characters rob the Quick-11 convenience store while the clerk raises his hands](th3 "Official promotional thumbnail: Quick-11 is also a robbery target")

## Is there a quest for working jobs?

A community value site describes a sidebar quest list that includes "Work 3 Different Jobs", with the three jobs above recommended as the easiest. We have not confirmed the quest or its reward.

## What about fishing and ATMs?

Fishing appears in official promotional art as its own activity, and community guides cover it separately from jobs. YouTube guides describe ATM money routes that use an in-game tool; we have not verified how that works. Both will get their own sections once checked. The official code for new players is on the [codes page](/blockspin/codes/), and map details are being collected for the [locations guide](/blockspin/map-locations/).
