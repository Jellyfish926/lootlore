This hub explains how drops, exchanges, errors and achievements actually work, with the developer's own posts as the source.

    About this guide

    The game launched on March 5, 2025, and the developer's one-year anniversary post says 5.8 million players had downloaded it by then (anniversary announcement). It runs in the background while you work, study or play something else, so most questions are about what it does quietly: how fast chests come, what the odds are, and whether it is safe to leave running.

    How rare is each item tier?

    These are the drop pool chances printed on the Steam store page, which the developer marks as subject to change:

    RarityChance per chest drop
    Common90%
    Uncommon9.5%
    Rare0.49%
    Epic0.01%
    Legendary1 in 500000

    Most players will never see an Epic from a chest. The route up is trading ten duplicates of one tier for one item of the next tier, covered in exchange and trading. Limited event items and the monthly Paw Pass are the other big sources, listed in events and the Paw Pass.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Bongo Cat free?
    Yes. The Steam store lists it as free to play. Optional supporter items and DLC packs are sold separately, and none of them are needed to get chest drops — see hats and skins.
    Does Bongo Cat have codes?
    No. None of the developer's announcements describe a redeem-code system. Free items come from chests, events, the Paw Pass and collaborations instead — see events and the Paw Pass.
    How rare is a Legendary hat?
    The store page lists the chest pool as Common 90%, Uncommon 9.5%, Rare 0.49%, Epic 0.01% and Legendary 1 in 500000, marked as subject to change. The practical route up is the exchange.
    Is Bongo Cat a keylogger?
    The developer has said the game only counts how many keys you press and does not process them further, and that the code is not obfuscated so others can check. Read the full sourced answer in is Bongo Cat safe.
    How many achievements are there?
    28 on Steam, from Bongo Beat 1 (about 91% of players) to Bongo Beat Diamond at 25 million taps (0.4%). The full table is on the achievements page.
