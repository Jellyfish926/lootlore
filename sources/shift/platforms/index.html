Shift At Midnight platforms

Shift At Midnight is on Windows PC through Steam, and the developer’s launch post also names Xbox and Game Pass. Steam lists no Mac or Linux version, and no official announcement mentions PlayStation, Switch or mobile. Valve’s compatibility report covers Steam Deck, SteamOS and Steam Machine. Requirements and compatibility details are below.

Last verified 10 October 2026 · game version: 1 September 2026 patch

Which devices can run Shift At Midnight?

The developer’s release announcement names three ways to play: Steam, Xbox and Xbox PC Game Pass. The Steam version is Windows only, and Valve’s store data marks macOS and Linux as unsupported. That store data also lists nine supported languages for the game, each one marked as having full audio support. The remaining rows are platforms that no official announcement mentions.

Steam publishes a minimum and a recommended tier. The figures below are copied from the Steam store page, which lists a 64-bit processor and operating system as mandatory. Both tiers also list a broadband internet connection.

Between the two tiers, memory goes from 8 GB to 16 GB, the graphics card from a GeForce GTX 1050 Ti to a GeForce GTX 1660 Ti (6GB), and DirectX from Version 11 to Version 12. Two more details from those lists: both tiers ask for the same 3 GB of storage, and the minimum tier accepts Windows 10 or 11 while the recommended tier names Windows 11 only. If your machine sits between them, the system requirements page explains what each tier means in practice. Crashes and launch failures on supported hardware belong on troubleshooting instead.

Does it work on Steam Deck and with a controller?

Valve’s compatibility report for the game, read on 10 October 2026, returns three results: Steam Deck, SteamOS and Steam Machine. Each carries the same category value, 2, and a fourth field, labelled frame, is empty. The report returns a number and a list of test names, not badge wording. Is Shift At Midnight on PS5, PS4 or Nintendo Switch?

None has been announced. We re-read all 31 official Steam announcements for the game on 10 October 2026, and none of them mentions PlayStation or Nintendo hardware. The release announcement names only Steam, Xbox and Xbox PC Game Pass. We did not check the PlayStation or Nintendo stores. Steam’s store data names Bun Muen as the developer and Kwalee as the publisher.

Can you play it on a phone, Mac or Linux?

No mobile version has been announced. None of the 31 official Steam announcements up to 10 October 2026 mentions iOS, Android or mobile. We did not check the App Store or Google Play, so we make no claim about what is listed there.

Mac and Linux are also out. Steam marks both as unsupported, and the store page publishes no requirements for either. Valve’s compatibility report is a separate record from that platform list, and it returns results for Steam Deck, SteamOS and Steam Machine, covered above.

Is Shift At Midnight on Game Pass?

Yes at launch. The launch announcement on 22 July 2026 reads “OUT NOW on Steam, Xbox & Game Pass”, and the earlier release-date post names “Xbox PC Gamepass” as well. The Xbox store listing showed the game as included with PC Game Pass and Xbox Game Pass Ultimate when it was last verified on 12 August 2026 against that listing.
