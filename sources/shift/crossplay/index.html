Is Shift At Midnight crossplay?

Partly, according to the developer. Bun Muen’s Steam announcement of 10 July 2026 said Xbox and PC Game Pass players will have crossplay, and that Steam players will only be able to play with other Steam players. No official patch note up to 10 October 2026 changes that. We have not tested either side ourselves.

Last verified 10 October 2026 · game version: 1 September 2026 patch

Can Steam and Xbox players play Shift At Midnight together?

Not according to the developer. The 10 July 2026 announcement says Steam players will only be able to play with other Steam players. The same post says Xbox and PC Game Pass players will have crossplay. That is a pre-launch statement, and we have not tested either side. Our system requirements page recorded a cross-platform co-op label on the Xbox store listing, last verified on 12 August 2026 against that listing and not re-checked on 10 October 2026. No official post describes a setting that changes the split.

What did the developer actually say about crossplay?

The rule comes from Bun Muen’s release-date announcement on Steam, posted 10 July 2026. It reads: “Xbox and PC Gamepass players will have crossplay. Steam players will only be able to play with other Steam players.” The launch post on 22 July 2026 then confirmed the game was out on Steam, Xbox and Game Pass.

We re-read every official Steam announcement for the game on 10 October 2026. Four patches have shipped since launch day, on 23 July, 29 July, 20 August and 1 September 2026, and none of their changelogs mentions crossplay. The 10 July 2026 statement is the most recent official text on crossplay that we found.

Is Steam crossplay coming in a later update?

Not that anyone official has said. Older versions of this page stated that full crossplay was promised as a post-release update. We could not find that promise in any official Steam announcement, so we have withdrawn it. The table below separates what is on the record from what is not.

Which store should a mixed group buy on?

The game costs $9.99 on Steam, with no discount running on 10 October 2026, and the developer’s launch post put it on Game Pass on 22 July 2026. The Xbox store listing showed a $9.99 price, included with PC Game Pass and Xbox Game Pass Ultimate, when it was last verified on 12 August 2026 against that listing (see system requirements). It was not re-checked on 10 October 2026.

Can Remote Play Together or Family Sharing get around it?

Nothing official says so. Remote Play Together is not among the categories the Steam store returns for the game on 10 October 2026. The full list reads: Single-player, Multi-player, Co-op, Online Co-op, Steam Achievements, Adjustable Text Size, Custom Volume Controls, Stereo Sound, Steam Cloud and Family Sharing.

Family Sharing is on that list. No official announcement says that either feature connects a Steam copy to an Xbox or PC Game Pass lobby, and we have not tested it, so we make no claim beyond the list itself.

Does the store you choose change anything else?

Both sides launched on the same day, but the surrounding services differ. On Steam you get ten Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud saves (added in the 20 August 2026 patch) and Family Sharing. The Xbox store listing carried Xbox cloud saves, Xbox Play Anywhere, an Optimized for Xbox Series X|S badge and a cloud-playable label tied to Game Pass Ultimate when it was last verified on 12 August 2026 against that listing; none of that was re-checked on 10 October 2026. Our achievements page quotes Steam’s global unlock rates, because those are the numbers Valve publishes.

Every changelog we cite was posted to the Steam page, and we could not confirm the date each fix reached Xbox.

What if you are all on Steam and still cannot join?

The developer has covered lobby failures in a separate post. On launch day, 22 July 2026, an announcement titled “CAN’T JOIN A LOBBY?” published a beta-branch workaround, with one instruction attached: “Everyone you play with must also follow these instructions”. The same post says the team was “working towards creating a proper fix in the main version of the game”. Our troubleshooting page covers lobby problems.
