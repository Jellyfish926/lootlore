# Race Horses Badges: All 24 and How Rare Each One Is

Race Horses has 24 badges. Seven reward your first hatch of each rarity, eleven come from races, and six cover feeding, levelling, hatching, collecting and retiring horses. The rarest is So Long, Partner, for retiring a horse with 10 or more wins: on 1 October 2026 it had been awarded 2,168 times, against 3.1 million for a first common horse.

Every Race Horses badge name and requirement below is copied from the [Roblox badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/10387635049/badges?limit=100), punctuation included. Award totals are a snapshot from 1 October 2026 and rise every day.

## What are the five hardest badges?

By award totals, these five are the rarest badges in Race Horses. Our reading of each is below; none of it is an official tip.

1. **So Long, Partner** (2,168). You need one horse with at least 10 wins, and then you have to retire it. The badge implies a retired horse leaves your racing stable; we have not confirmed whether that can be undone, so many players may simply keep their winners.
2. **Not Just Horsing Around** (3,822). Fifty wins across your stable. With one race a minute and random winners, this is a long grind.
3. **Fruity Foal** (6,102). Only eight days old when we checked, and it needs an Exotic horse, the top tier. The Exotic Egg costs 2,799 Robux in the store.
4. **Horsepower** (7,458). Ten wins with the same horse. It pairs naturally with So Long, Partner: earn this first, then decide whether to retire.
5. **Hatcher** (7,784). Fifty eggs. Surprisingly rare given how many players hatch a first horse; it was added on 22 August, so early players who left never had the chance.

## Why is Super Sprinter more common than Winning Stride?

Super Sprinter, for finishing in under 35 seconds, had been awarded 1,748,435 times, about 124,000 more than Winning Stride. Both arrived on 12 August, so time is not the reason. The likely explanation is that a fast finish does not need first place, while only one horse wins each race. This is our inference from the numbers, not something the developer has said.

## What should you aim for after your first hatch?

Our suggestion for a sensible Race Horses badge order, based on how common each badge is:

- **Day one:** Horsin' Around, Winning Stride, Super Sprinter and Final Stretch, four of the five most-awarded badges.
- **First week:** Apple A Day (100 apples to one horse) and Racer's Riches ($100k in one race).
- **Long term:** Man's Best Friend (level 30), then Horsepower and Full House.

Which rarity to chase is a separate question. The [horses and Index guide](/race-horses/horses/) explains what makes one horse different from another.
