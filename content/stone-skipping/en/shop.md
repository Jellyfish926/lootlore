# All 90 +1 Stone Skipping Robux Products and Prices

+1 Stone Skipping lists 90 developer products, and 88 of them were on sale on 10 October 2026. Prices run from 5 Robux for Skill Multiplier [TIER 1] to 3,999 Robux for Skill Multiplier [TIER 12]. Below is the complete list in seven groups, with names copied exactly from Roblox. No product has an official description.

Prices change whenever the developer edits the store, so check the in-game prompt before you buy. The 11 game passes are a separate list; they are in the [game pass guide](/stone-skipping/gamepasses/).

## What do the totals add up to?

Our sums from the official prices, to show the scale of the +1 Stone Skipping store:

- every on-sale product once (88 products): 29,330 Robux;
- the same without [GIFT] products (74 products): 25,510 Robux;
- all 14 [GIFT] products: 3,820 Robux;
- all twelve Skill Multiplier tiers: 12,980 Robux;
- all 11 game passes, which are a separate list: 2,353 Robux.

Nobody needs every product, and Roblox lets a developer product be bought more than once. The sums only show where the store's weight sits: the twelve Skill Multiplier tiers alone are about half of the non-gift total (12,980 of 25,510).

## How did we build this list?

From Roblox's public developer-products listing for +1 Stone Skipping, read on 10 October 2026. It returned all 90 items on one page with no further pages. Names are copied exactly, including brackets and capital letters. Creation dates are UTC. The game has no badges, and its game passes come from a different listing.
