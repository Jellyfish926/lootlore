# Valheim Walkthrough: Start to Deep North

From your first small hut to the final challenge in the Deep North.

This site breaks Valheim's main progression route into guides you can use straight away: work out which stage you are in, then solve the one problem in front of you. You do not have to read a whole manual first, and you do not have to grind materials down a gear checklist.

## Playing for the first time? Start here

- [Valheim First Day: Survive Night One](/valheim/first-day/): get tools, shelter, food and a spawn point done.
- [Valheim Progression Order by Biome](/valheim/progression/): work out what to do now and what the next biome needs.
- [Valheim Combat Basics: Block and Dodge](/valheim/combat/): learn to defend, hit in short bursts and keep stamina to escape.
- [Valheim Death Recovery: Get Your Gear](/valheim/death-recovery/): turn a corpse run into a prepared rescue.

## Not sure what is stopping you? Check these first

When nothing seems to die fast enough, the answer is not always one more armour piece. Check [food](/valheim/food/), the [Rested buff](/valheim/rested/) and your [mead](/valheim/mead/) first.

If you have the resources but cannot use them, read [crafting and repair](/valheim/crafting/). If nothing ever makes it home, check [portals](/valheim/portals/) and your [sailing routes](/valheim/ships/).

Planning a long run with friends? Agree on [co-op and server rules](/valheim/co-op/) first. Coming back from an older version? Read the [1.0 save and world choice guide](/valheim/save-1-0/) and the [mods troubleshooting guide](/valheim/mods/).

## How this site uses its sources

This is unofficial guide content. Game mechanics follow official information first, and specific play advice is compiled from traceable community sources. A "recommendation" is one workable plan, not the only or strongest way to play. Sources stay in the text, and vanilla standard survival is the default. Unverified drop rates, exact damage numbers and speedrun times are never presented here as settled facts.
