    Deep Fishing Robux Shop: Boosts, Chests, Enchant Stones

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10 · Checked on version 2026-10-09 update

    Scope: Product names, prices and creation dates from the Roblox developer product API on 2026-10-10 (UTC); what each product does is not in its record and is marked not confirmed

    Key points

    • Enchant Stones (from 49 Robux) and Ultra Enchant Stones (from 199) have store records created on 6 September 2026.
    • There are four lucky chest types: Element, Pirate, Charm and Hero, from 79 to 149 Robux each.
    • Revive All Fish costs 39 Robux, and Skip Big Mouth Timer costs 79 — both product records had an empty description on 10 October 2026.
    • Four products with (One Throw) in the name cost from 8 to 24 Robux; that each applies to a single cast is our reading of the name.

    Besides rods and passes, Deep Fishing's Robux shop sells Enchant Stones, four kinds of Lucky Chest, Server Luck products, Revive All Fish, eight Stat Upgrade products and four One Throw products. Enchant Stones start at 49 Robux and Ultra Enchant Stones at 199; both product records were created on 6 September 2026.

    This page lists the developer products registered for the game on Roblox — 103 of them when we read the list on 10 October 2026 — minus the rod unlocks, the two [Exclusive] rods and the two [Skin] rod products (see the rod list), pass gifts (see game passes) and mutation rerolls and scrolls (see the rarity guide). Product listings give a name and a price, not a description, so this page prints names and prices and marks what a product does as not confirmed. Oni Soulbinder Skin has no "Rod" in its name, so it is listed in this page's last table; the rod list repeats it with the same caveat.

    What are Enchant Stones in Deep Fishing?

    Enchant Stones are sold as developer products in two grades, and their records were created on 6 September 2026. The names show that these products exist; how enchanting works in play is not confirmed.

    ProductRobux

    What an enchant does and what the Ultra grade adds are not in the official records we read on 10 October 2026. One event listing, "Update 6 🔥", has the tagline "World 3 (Snow World) and Rod Entchantments 24 new Fish and much more!" (spelling as written), an announcement that ties enchantments to rods. Fan guides also mention a paid island, which we have not verified, so we do not describe enchant effects here; the enchants page sets out what is confirmed and why the store has no Enchant Scroll. If you have a screenshot of the enchant screen, the author page says how to send it.

    What is Revive All Fish?

    "Revive All Fish" is a 39-Robux product whose record was created on 29 August 2026 and edited on 18 September. Its description field is empty, so what it revives, and when you would need it, is not confirmed. Players searching for a "revive heart" may be looking for the same system, but we have not confirmed a link, so treat it as an open question.

    A separate product, "Skip Big Mouth Timer" (79 Robux, record created 3 September), also has an empty description. The event listing "Mutation Roll + Rarity💫" carries four lines about Big Mouth: "Added Big Mouth Fish", "Feed it every 12 Hours", "Feeding it grants Permanent Luck boost" and "Complete the Luck boost to get Big Mouth Fish". What the product itself skips is not stated in its record.

    Which products are named One Throw?

    The "One Throw" products, whose records were created on 18 September, include the two cheapest items in the shop at 8 Robux each.

    ProductRobux
    More Fish (One Throw)8
    Freeze Fish (One Throw)8
    Mutation Luck (One Throw)19
    Collect All (One Throw)24

    Their description fields are empty. Reading "(One Throw)" as "applies to a single cast" is our inference from the name, not a published rule; on that reading, our advice is to save one for a cast you expect to go far. Mutation-focused players should also read the rarity and mutations guide for rerolls and Ultra Mutation Scrolls.

    The records for Stat Upgrades [7] and [8] were created on 5 September, extending the original six. Which stat each one raises and how many coins each pack holds are not listed. The season, bottle, egg, ad and Oni Soulbinder Skin records were created between 29 September and 10 October 2026 (UTC) with empty descriptions, so what each one does is not confirmed; the updates page lists their dates. What the three Skip Gift products do is not confirmed: each record has a name, a price and an empty description. Separately, the game's description says joining the LazyGames group gives "rewards and updates" — see community links.

    What do the record creation dates tell you?

    The creation dates double as a rough update log: the Skip Gift products on 6 August, Element Lucky Chests and the two [Skin] rod products on 25 August, Revive All Fish on 29 August, four mutation reroll bundles on 1 September, Enchant Stones and Ultra Mutation Scrolls on 6 September, the One Throw products on 18 September, three more Lucky Chest types and the two [Exclusive] rods on 26 September, the four season products on 29 September, the x1, x3 and x10 Leviathan Bottle records and Shop Daily Claimable Ad on 30 September, Oni Soulbinder Skin on 9 October, and the four Tail Dragon Egg records and x50 Leviathan Bottles on 10 October (UTC dates). A product's creation date is when it was registered, not always the day its update went live.
