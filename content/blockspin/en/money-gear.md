# BlockSpin Money and Gear Guides: Robux Shop and Mansion

These guides cover the part of BlockSpin's money and gear that Roblox's own records document: what is sold for Robux and what each product costs. Each price carries the date it was read, and anything a record does not state, such as what an unlabelled product does, is marked not confirmed.

## Pick a guide by what you need

### [BlockSpin Robux Shop: 64 Products and Their Prices](/blockspin/robux-shop/)

The shop in one place. The records read on October 10, 2026 (UTC) hold 66 products marked for sale; the page counts 64 of them, leaving out two whose names begin "OLD PRODUCT", and sorts them into nine groups. The groups are ours, sorted by the words in each product name, and are not an official category. The cash packs are worked out as in-game dollars per Robux. It also covers safe slots, Stock Skips, gang products, and the zero game passes and zero badges in the same day's records.

### [BlockSpin Weapon Packs: Robux Prices for Kits and Ammo](/blockspin/weapon-packs/)

The 17 products with a weapon, ammo or throwable word in the name, from 180 to 4,310 Robux, plus the three Revenge Packs. These are Robux prices and not in-game cash prices; damage and fire rate are not in the records and the page says so.

### [BlockSpin Mansion Upgrades: 9 Robux Products and Prices](/blockspin/mansion-upgrades/)

The nine Mansion products from 150 to 8,000 Robux, the four official descriptions copied as written (all four are on car-related upgrades, and two of them mention the underground garage or car storage), and the record and event dates around the Mansion Underground update.

## How these guides are checked

Each page is built from Roblox's public records for BlockSpin: the developer product list, the game pass and badge lists, the event listings and the game record. Product names and prices are copied as written, sums and per-Robux rates are labelled as our own arithmetic, and a record date is treated as the day a record was made, which is not proof of a release date.

A list of jobs and what each pays, map locations, vehicle case odds and in-game cash prices are not in those records, so this section has no guide on them. For the official code, the death-drop rule and the developer facts, see the [getting started guides](/blockspin/getting-started/), or go back to the [BlockSpin hub](/blockspin/).
