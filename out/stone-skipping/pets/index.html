    +1 Stone Skipping Eggs and Pets: Robux Prices, Bundles

    Key points

    • Six kinds of egg are sold for Robux: Rainbow (45), Admin (175) and four themed eggs at 59 each, all with x3 and x8 bundles.
    • The x8 bundle of a themed egg costs 279 Robux, about 35 per egg, which is 41% less than eight singles.
    • King Doggy costs 999 Robux; its icon shows a crowned yellow dog, but the listing does not say what you receive.
    • Hatch odds, pet names and pet boost values are not shown in public data and need an in-game check.

    Pets in +1 Stone Skipping give "powerful boosts", according to the official description, and the store shows they hatch from eggs. It sells six kinds of egg for Robux: Rainbow at 45, Admin at 175, and Pirate, Astronaut, Chocolatier and Dragon at 59 each. Every egg also comes in x3 and x8 bundles.

    That one line of description is all the developer has published about pets. The rest of this page is store data: names, prices and dates, copied from Roblox on 2 October 2026.

    How much do the x3 and x8 bundles save?

    Our arithmetic on the official prices. "Saving" compares the bundle with buying the same number of single eggs.

    EggRobux per egg, singlePer egg in x3Per egg in x8x8 saving
    Rainbow Egg45.033.031.1111 Robux (31%)
    Admin Egg175.0153.0143.6251 Robux (18%)
    Pirate, Astronaut, Chocolatier, Dragon59.038.334.9193 Robux (41%)

    Two things stand out. The themed eggs have the steepest bundle discount, and most of it already arrives at x3: 115 Robux for three is 35% less than three singles. The Admin Egg has the smallest discount. It costs about three times as much as a themed egg as a single and about four times as much in the bundles.

    Why is the Admin Egg so much more expensive?

    The store does not say. No official source lists what any egg contains or the odds of each pet. Until the odds are recorded in game, treat any drop table you see elsewhere with caution. The Admin Egg is also the only egg with [GIFT] versions, at the same three prices.

    Which pets and packs are sold directly?

    Three products look like they hand you a pet without hatching. We describe the icons because there is no description text. Skill and Wins potions sold on their own are covered in the boosts guide.

    ProductRobuxWhat its icon shows
    Starter Pack39A white cat-like pet with a rainbow horn, two star-shaped potions and a lightning bolt
    Devil Pack299A black horned pet with red eyes, three star-shaped potions and a small pet marked +1
    King Doggy999A yellow dog wearing a crown and a red cape, holding a sceptre

    An icon is not a contents list. The Starter Pack icon suggests a pet plus boosts, and the "+1" on the Devil Pack icon looks like the pet pass icon, but neither is confirmed. King Doggy costs more than any single egg bundle except Admin Egg x8.

    How many pets can you hatch and equip?

    The base numbers are not published. What the store shows is that both can be raised with game passes. Three passes are named +1 Pet (69 Robux), +3 Pets (195) and +6 Pets (499), which most likely add pet slots. Three more are named Hatch +3 Eggs [STACKS] (25), Hatch +8 Eggs [STACKS] (145) and Hatch +16 Eggs [STACKS] (299), which most likely let you hatch several eggs at once.

    Per-unit prices and gift versions for these six are worked out in the game pass guide.

    Is there a pattern to new eggs?

    Four themed eggs were created at gaps of five to seven days: Pirate on 12 September, Astronaut on 19 September, Chocolatier on 26 September and Dragon on 1 October. All four share one price ladder. The Dragon Egg appeared two days before the official World 5 event on 3 October; the listing does not connect the two. The updates page has the full timeline.

    What would we buy?

    Our opinion, from prices alone. If you want to try paid eggs in +1 Stone Skipping, a themed x3 at 115 Robux gets most of the bundle discount for a small spend. We would not buy an Admin Egg or King Doggy before the odds and boosts are known. For everything else in the store, see the full Robux product list.
