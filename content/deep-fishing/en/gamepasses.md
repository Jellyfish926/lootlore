# Deep Fishing Game Passes: All 9 Passes and Prices

Deep Fishing has nine game passes, priced from 99 to 599 Robux: three luck tiers, Double Coins, Double XP, More Mutations, Auto Sell, Fish Magnet and VIP. The developer describes each in a single line, and that is all that is officially published — no percentages. Fish Magnet and Auto Sell are the cheapest passes that change how a session feels.

All names, prices and descriptions below come from the [Roblox game pass API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/10526853622/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100). Eight of the nine were created on launch day, 18 July 2026; Auto Sell followed on 19 July. None carried a discount when we read the list on 10 October 2026.

| VIP | 249 | Exclusive VIP perks and rewards! | Perks not listed |

| Double Coins | 399 | Earn 2x more coins when selling fish! | Doubles coin income from selling |
| Ultra Lucky | 599 | The best luck boost! | Top luck tier |

The last column is our plain-English summary of the official line, not extra information. How the three luck passes stack with each other, and how big each boost is, are not stated.

## Which pass should you buy first?

It depends on what slows you down. Our suggestions, based only on what the passes claim to do:

- **Selling feels like a chore:** Auto Sell (149). It removes a step from every cast.
- **You want more fish per session:** Fish Magnet (99). It pulls fish towards your bait.
- **Upgrades feel slow:** Double Coins (399). More coins per haul means the next [rod](/deep-fishing/rods/) arrives sooner.
- **You are hunting rare catches:** a luck pass or More Mutations — but only once you can reach new water. Our [rarity guide](/deep-fishing/rarity/) explains why distance comes first.

VIP is the hardest to judge: "Exclusive VIP perks and rewards" does not say what the perks are. We would not buy it for power until the perks are documented.

## Are the luck passes worth it?

Luck is the one boost the developer sells in three tiers, so it clearly matters in this game. Lucky costs 99, Super Lucky 249 and Ultra Lucky 599 — each step costs more than twice the one before it. Without published numbers, the cheapest tier is the safest test.

Luck is also sold outside the pass list: Server Luck products from x2 to x16 affect the whole server, and a one-throw Mutation Luck boost costs 19. Those are covered in the [Robux shop guide](/deep-fishing/shop/).

## Do passes carry over or run out?

Roblox game passes are one-time purchases tied to your account, not consumables. That is different from the developer products in the shop, such as one-throw boosts or enchant stones, which are used up. If you want a permanent effect, a pass is the right kind of purchase; if you only want a single lucky throw, the shop is cheaper.
