Disclaimer for the Unofficial Sephiria Wiki

Last updated 2026-09-01

What this site is, what it is not, and where its limits sit — stated once, plainly.

Independent means independent

No one at this wiki works with, for, or on behalf of TEAM HORAY. The studio has not reviewed, approved or sponsored anything here. All Sephiria names, imagery and game content referenced on these pages remain the property of TEAM HORAY; we reference them because writing about a game requires naming its parts. Official channels — the Steam page and the developers' Discord — are where the studio actually speaks.

Our accuracy model has known edges

This wiki's habit of dating claims and naming sources is described in the editorial policy. The edges to know about: numbers last publicly adjusted in the 0.9.x–0.10.x beta era are labelled that way rather than presented as confirmed 1.0 values; where the developer's own figures and community audits disagree — the artifact count is the standing example — we print both rather than deciding; and a patch like 1.0.26 or 1.0.30 can invalidate a page between our review passes. The version stamp at the top of each guide tells you how stale the page could be.

On spending money

References to Sephiria's price or platform availability describe the Steam listing as of the date given next to them. We sell nothing, take no commission on anything, and give no purchase advice beyond what the cited reviews and store data say. Verify against the live store page before buying.

On links and advertising

Citations link outward because claims should be checkable — to Steam announcements, community guides and data audits. An outbound link endorses the specific claim it supports, nothing more. Display ads on this site come from an external network that selects them without our involvement; ad revenue keeps the site running and buys exactly zero influence over what any page says. Ad-related cookies are covered in the privacy policy.

Found a problem?

The contact page reaches the person who maintains this wiki. Corrections that arrive with a screenshot, a patch-note line or a source link are the ones that get applied same-day.
