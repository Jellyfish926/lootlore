# Author: Jellyfi, Race Horses Roblox Guide Editor

Jellyfi is the editorial byline on {{BRAND}}, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the [Race Horses guides](/race-horses/) on this site. The byline covers research, fact checks against official Roblox data and updates when the game changes. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

## How these guides are researched

Race Horses is a Roblox game, so the most reliable facts are the ones the developer registers on Roblox itself: the game description, the 24 badges, the five game passes and the 32 developer products. Those are read directly from Roblox's public data and quoted word for word, capital letters and exclamation marks included, with the date we checked them.

![A first-person hand offers a red apple to an orange blocky horse, with a prompt reading E FEED](th2 "Every guide in this section is published under one editorial byline")

Fan wikis, code sites and YouTube videos are used only as leads. If one of them is the only source for a fact — a code, a trait name, a hatch chance, a stable cost — that fact stays off our published pages until something official or our own check confirms it. That is why there is no codes page and no trait list yet.

Buying orders and "what to do first" advice are editorial suggestions, not rules of the game, and each page says so where it matters. Ratios such as "per 100 first-hatch badges" are our own arithmetic on official totals.

## How to read the dates on a page

"Published" is when the page first went live. "Last checked" is when the text was compared against its sources. Roblox numbers such as badge totals, visits and prices are snapshots from that day; badge totals grow every day.

## Found a mistake?

The game updates often. If a price, name or menu no longer matches the game, use the [Contact](/contact) page and include the page link and a screenshot from the game. Screenshots of the Index, the trait odds screen or an official codes box are especially welcome, because those are gaps we could not fill from official data.
