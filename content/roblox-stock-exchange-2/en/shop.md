# Roblox Stock Exchange 2 Robux Shop: All 31 Products

Roblox Stock Exchange 2 has 31 developer products, priced from 9 to 999 Robux. They cover time skips, algo bot purchases, Trader Spotlight slots, AI Tokens, a Season 1 Trader Pass and thirteen products with pass-style names. Only one carries an official description. For the other thirty, the name and the price are all the developer has published.

Roblox defines a developer product as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once", which separates this list from the 13 permanent passes in the [game pass guide](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/gamepasses/). All 31 were on sale on 8 October 2026.

## What do the time skips do?

Their names suggest it, but none has an official description. Skip to Market Open (9 Robux) implies the market has opening hours. Simulate Day (39) and Simulate Week (99) appear to move the simulation forward by a day or a week. The developer has not said what a simulated day does to open positions, bots or interest.

One official line may put a value on a day. The VIP game pass includes "1 free Sim Day per day", and a single Simulate Day is 39 Robux here.

## What is the Season 1 Trader Pass?

It is the newest product, created on 2 October 2026, and the only one with a description: "Unlocks the premium track of Trader Pass Season 1, The Golden Bell: 22 animated exclusives and 20,000 AI tokens. Cosmetic only, no cash or boosts."

So for 199 Robux you get the premium track of Trader Pass Season 1, "The Golden Bell": 22 animated exclusives and 20,000 AI tokens. The developer says it is cosmetic only, with no cash or boosts. A 20,000 AI Tokens pack on its own costs 49 Robux. How long Season 1 lasts and what the free track holds are not stated in the listing. Seasons were announced with an event starting 12 September, described on the [updates page](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/updates/).

## How much do AI Tokens cost?

Three packs, all created on 9 August 2026:

| Product | Robux | Tokens per Robux (our maths) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 AI Tokens | 29 | about 172 |
| 20,000 AI Tokens | 49 | about 408 |
| 75,000 AI Tokens | 69 | about 1,087 |

The big pack is the better rate by a wide margin: 15 times the tokens of the small pack for a little over twice the price. No official text says what the AI Tokens are used for. The Unlimited AI Usage pass (79 Robux) mentions SummitAI and "no daily or weekly cap", and the Trader Pass includes 20,000 AI tokens. No listing says how the token packs and that pass relate.

## What is Trader Spotlight?

Three products sell it by duration: 15 minutes for 49 Robux, one hour for 149 and one day for 499. None has a description. The name suggests your trader profile is featured to other players for that time, but what a spotlight shows, and to whom, is not stated. Nothing in the listing says it affects your trades.

## Which products share a name with a game pass?

Eleven do, and for seven of them the price matches the pass. Four differ:

| Name | As a game pass | As a developer product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watchlist Pro | 49 | 99 |
| Executive Terminal | 159 | 139 |
| Order Flow | 199 | 249 |
| VIP | 229 | 399 |

Two more products, Offline Earnings (199) and Company Mogul (249), have pass-style names but no pass with the same name. Their names resemble the Overnight Desk and More Player Companies passes, though the listing does not link them. Since the listings do not explain the duplicates, check which price the Roblox purchase prompt shows before you confirm.

## What about the one-off purchases?

Skip Recovery, Double Earnings and Trader Starter Pack cost 19 Robux each, and Instant Acension costs 249. The names point at systems (a recovery wait, an earnings multiplier, a starter bundle, an ascension step), but none of the four has a description. We do not know how long Double Earnings lasts or what the starter pack contains.

Not everything in Roblox Stock Exchange 2 can be bought. The official art for the Challenges update describes "Challenge-only cosmetics" as "Not in the shop. Not on the item market."

## Where are the algo bot products?
