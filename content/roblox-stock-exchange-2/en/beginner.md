# Roblox Stock Exchange 2 Beginner Guides: Start Here

New to Roblox Stock Exchange 2? Read the how-to-play guide first. It explains what you can trade, the order tools the game names and a sensible order for your first session. Then try the one code printed in the official description, look at the badges to see what lies ahead, and check the update calendar for the listed event dates.

These five guides stay close to what Summit Productions Development has published on Roblox: the game description, 11 badges, 13 pass descriptions and 12 event listings. Where the developer has said nothing, the guides say so.

## Pick a guide by question

### [How to Play: Trades to Rebirth](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/how-to-play/)

What the seven feature lines in the description mean, how the official art shows buying and closing a position, and what the pass descriptions say about leverage, fees and offline time.

### [Codes (October 2026): TOOLS](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/codes/)

The single code printed in the official description (not tested in game), the 15,000-like mark the description sets for another code, and why six more codes from other sites are not listed.

### [All 11 Badges and How to Get Them](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/badges/)

Every badge with its exact unlock text and award total, from First Trade to First Rebirth, plus the one badge that depends on meeting a game owner.

### [Updates: Official Event History](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/updates/)

All twelve official events in the developer's words, the listed Custom Offices start (10 October 2026) in seven time zones, and what the event art adds.

### [Discord, Group and Developer](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/community/)

The group behind the game in numbers, its four other experiences, and how to check whether a Discord server is really the developer's.

## What should a new player do first?

Keep the first hour small. Place a plain trade, close it, and read the result before you touch leverage or shorting. The badge totals back this up: nearly everyone gets First Trade, about two thirds get First Profit, and only about a quarter ever short a stock.

| Step | Why | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Try TOOLS | It is printed in the official description (untested) | Codes |
| One long trade, then close it | First Trade and First Profit are the two most common badges after Welcome | How to play |
| Read the order tools | Limit orders, stop losses and take profits are named in the description | How to play |
| Check the calendar | An official event start is listed for every Saturday from 22 August to 10 October 2026 | Updates |

The table is our suggested order, not a rule of the game.

## When should you think about Robux?

Later, if at all. The game is free, and the description's pitch is growing a small account, not buying a big one. When you do want to compare purchases, the [Passes & Robux Shop section](/roblox-stock-exchange-2/robux/) lists all 13 passes and 31 products with official prices.

## What do these guides leave out?

Anything the developer has not published. That includes the fee you pay without a pass, the leverage allowed at each level, what a rebirth costs and returns, and which bot settings work best. Other sites print numbers for some of these. We could not trace them to an official source, so they are not here.

## Scope

Official update events have been listed weekly. Each guide shows the date it was checked, and counters such as badge totals and visits are snapshots from 8 October 2026. All markets and currencies in the game are simulated, and nothing in these guides is financial advice.
