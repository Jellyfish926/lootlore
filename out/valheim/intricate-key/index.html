    Valheim Intricate Key: Mould to Mörkhalla

    Author Jellyfi · Published 2026-09-17 · Last checked 2026-09-17 · Checked on version 1.0.12

    Scope: Vanilla standard survival; world modifiers, mods and later patches may change the rules

    Language: English中文

    Intricate Key

    Quick facts

    Crafted at
    Frost Foundry (Cast made at Black Forge using Bloodgold + Mould: Intricate Key)
    Used for
    Unlocks Gates of Mörkhalla in the Deep North; consumed on use
    Material
    Cast: Intricate Key ×None, Liquid Frost ×5

    Black Forge

    Quick facts

    Requires
    Requires a roof and 70% cover to be usable; source structure Workbench
    Unlocked by
    Workbench
    Material
    Black marble ×10, Yggdrasil wood ×10, Black core ×5

    Frigid Kiln

    Quick facts

    Requires
    Source structure Workbench; transforms Ice into Liquid Frost (1 per 30s, consuming 5 Ice, holds up to 25 Ice)
    Unlocked by
    Workbench
    Material
    Stone ×20, Frostcore ×10, Ice ×5

    Frost Foundry

    Quick facts

    Requires
    Fueled with Liquid Frost; a Cast is placed at the center to create the final item, consuming 5 Liquid Frost over 50 seconds

    Key points

    • The order is: find Mould: Intricate Key, prepare Bloodgold, craft the cast at the Black Forge, finish the item at the Frost Foundry.
    • Each Cast: Intricate Key uses one key mould and five Bloodgold; a cast is not a usable key.
    • The Frost Foundry needs Liquid Frost, which comes from processing ice in the Frigid Kiln.
    • The key is consumed when the door opens, so plan for three: at least three moulds and fifteen Bloodgold.

    The Intricate Key is not something you can use as soon as you have the mould. The full order is: find the key mould, prepare Bloodgold, craft the key cast at the Black Forge, then finish the item at the Frost Foundry. If your inventory still says Mould or Cast, you are not done yet.

    First, find the right mould in the Winding Tunnels

    While exploring the Deep North tunnels, confirm the item is named Mould: Intricate Key and not one of the equipment moulds. Do not judge by icon colour, and store it separately the moment you pick it up. The search and the processing are outlined in this key guide.

    If one dungeon does not produce it, note how far you searched and move on to other entrances — do not assume your save is broken because the item did not drop. Check for missed branches and pickups before you decide to leave.

    What one key costs

    Each Cast: Intricate Key uses one key mould and five Bloodgold, crafted at the Black Forge; after that it still has to be processed at the Frost Foundry. You can cross-check the recipe against the cast item data and the crafting steps.

    Gather the materials first, then stand at the correct workstation and check the recipe. If it does not appear, work through the mould's name, whether the metal has been processed, the station's actual level and which crafting tab you are on — do not immediately start building another machine.

    Why a cast still will not open the door

    The cast is an intermediate item. It goes into the Frost Foundry along with Liquid Frost, which comes from processing ice in the Frigid Kiln. When the process finishes, take out the item named Intricate Key and then head out. Check the input and the fuel separately; putting the cast in does not mean the station has finished its work.

    Process only what you need the first time and confirm the output before you batch anything. If you run out of fuel halfway, go home and restock — do not keep feeding critical moulds into a station whose state you are unsure of.

    Is the key still there after the door opens?

    The Intricate Key is consumed when you open the door, so do not treat it like the Swamp Key. The normal route ahead needs several Mörkhalla runs, so plan the materials for three keys: that means at least three of the target mould and fifteen Bloodgold, before counting anything you spend on gear — see the Deep North progression guide.

    You can make them one at a time and advance round by round; there is no need to carry every key on the first run. That way a failure costs you less to recover, and it is easier to track which dungeon you have opened and which event round you have finished.

    Four things to check before you leave

    The item name is the finished key; the map marker for this run is clear; your return point and healing supplies are ready; and the number of completed rounds matches what is in storage. The key only gets you into the next stage — it does not clear the dungeon or the invasion that follows. Once the door is open, keep confirming your objective section by section instead of pushing straight down.
