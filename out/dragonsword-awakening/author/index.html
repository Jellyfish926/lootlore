    Jellyfi

    Editor · DragonSword Guide

    Runs DragonSword Guide; every figure is traced to a Hound13 patch note, the Steam store page or a named community source before it is printed.

    What this site covers

    DragonSword Guide is an independent reference for DragonSword: Awakening (Hound13 Inc.), built around the systems that actually decide a fight rather than a flat page-per-topic listing. It has no affiliation with Hound13 Inc. — see the disclaimer.

    How pages are checked

    Every page starts from a named primary source — a Hound13 Inc. patch note, the Steam store page and public API, or a community guide with an author and a posting date — and lists which ones it used at the bottom under Sources checked for this page. Numbers travel with the date they were read, because unlock rates and tuning move between patches. Where two sources disagree, both are printed rather than one being picked; where nobody has published a figure, the page says so instead of estimating. The full rules, including how corrections are handled, are in the editorial policy.

    Corrections and contact

    If a page contradicts the live game, that is worth reporting — reports with a screenshot or a source link are the fastest to act on. Contact
