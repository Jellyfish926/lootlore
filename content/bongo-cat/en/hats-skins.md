# Bongo Cat Hats and Skins: Rarity and Sources

Bongo Cat hats and skins drop from chests at five official odds, from Common at 90% down to Legendary at 1 in 500000. A sixth tier, Unique, cannot drop at all. For anything rare, chests are only one route: the exchange, limited events, the Paw Pass, multiplayer, achievements and supporter packs all add items.

## What are the rarity tiers and drop odds?

The chest odds come from the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3419430/Bongo_Cat/), under "Item drop pool chances", with the note "Subject to change". The Unique tier was added in the [September 2026 update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1842212951313382).

| Rarity | Chest drop chance | Roughly | How else to get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 90% | 9 in 10 drops | Chests |
| Uncommon | 9.5% | about 1 in 10 | Exchange 10 Commons |
| Rare | 0.49% | about 1 in 200 | Exchange 10 Uncommons |
| Epic | 0.01% | 1 in 10,000 | Exchange 10 Rares |
| Legendary | 1 in 500000 | — | Exchange 10 Epics; some special items |
| Unique | Not droppable | — | Special events such as advent calendars |

The "Roughly" column is plain arithmetic on the store percentages, not an extra official figure. The practical reading: a Rare is something you will see from chests over weeks of play, while Epic and Legendary mostly come through the [exchange](/bongo-cat/exchange-trading/) or from other players.

## Where do hats and skins come from?

| Source | What you get | Cost | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chests | Random item from the current drop pool | Taps (1,000 per chest per the developer's notes) | [How to play](/bongo-cat/how-to-play/) |
| Exchange | One item of a higher tier for ten of one tier (demo update wording) | Duplicates | [Exchange and trading](/bongo-cat/exchange-trading/) |
| Limited events | 20 exclusive items per seasonal event | Taps, during the event only | [Events](/bongo-cat/events/) |
| Paw Pass | Monthly themed skins, hats, emotes and chests | Free track; paid track optional | [Events](/bongo-cat/events/) |
| Multiplayer | One of three fruit sets; the emote chest (lobby-only from Oct 2025; emotes back in single-player since [Jul 12, 2026](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1837955055360738)) | Playing in a lobby | [Multiplayer](/bongo-cat/multiplayer/) |
| Achievements | New skins and hats for tap-count milestones | Taps | [Achievements](/bongo-cat/achievements/) |
| 1 million clicks | A special skin and hat | Taps | [Typing Fest post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1823825466497567) |
| Collaborations | Items for owning partner games | Varies | Announced per collab |
| Item Store / DLC | Supporter skins and hats | Paid | Below |

The achievement rewards come from the [anniversary update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1826362059921578), which says "Not all achievements will give you new cosmetics, but the new tap amount ones will." The 1 million clicks reward was originally set at 5 million and "edited back down". The permanent pool has also grown: the [May 26, 2025 update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1800357164536345) says it "Adds 40 new skins" and "Adds 40 new hats".

Collaboration rules changed in June 2026: rewards for wishlisting or trying other games were removed or moved to owning the full game, because the developer found they were "against Steam Guidelines". Rewards tied to buying a partner game are a different case: Tap Tap Loot's launch post promised four items (Goo Cat, Jester Hat, Bunny Ears and Bunny Skin) to everyone who buys it.

## Which supporter packs and DLC can you buy?

Single supporter items are sold in the [Item Store](https://store.steampowered.com/itemstore/3419430/); on September 29, 2026 the US store listed single skins and hats such as Bumble Kitten, Meowxolotl, Yin and Hades at $2.50 each, and the item the developer's September 1, 2026 announcement calls a Paw Pass Ticket at $4.99 (that store listing was titled Paw Pass Token). Since April 2026 the packs are also sold as DLC ([announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1829528821315214)):

| DLC | Store release | Sets named on the store page |

| [Mecha Pack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4600310/) | Apr 13, 2026 | S.L.A.S.H., P.U.R.R, B.O.N.K. |
| [Squirrel Pack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4600320/) | Apr 13, 2026 | Three squirrel sets |
| [Wrestler Pack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4600330/) | Apr 13, 2026 | Six Seven Lives, Cutie Punch, Ribbit Rampage |
| [Wild Wonders](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4856340/) | [Jun 27, 2026](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1836506165556399) | Bumble Kitten, Meowxolotl, Sea Purrtle |

None of these are needed for chests. Note that Item Store purchases cannot be resold: in May 2025 the developer "Made the item store items non marketable" ([patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1799088287826807)).

## How do you track what you are still missing?

Open the collection, also called the BongoDex. It shows collection progress, and since January 2026 you can click any cosmetic there to preview it on your cat before you own it ([update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3419430/view/1821922921810120)). The September 2026 update added a search bar to the BongoDex and a filter and sort window in the inventory. Empty event collections are hidden to keep the list short.

This page does not list the rarity of each individual hat. The store page only publishes tier odds, and the full item definitions are not public without an API key, so a per-item table would rest on third-party lists. For the achievements that award items, see the [achievements table](/bongo-cat/achievements/).
