# How to Play Untitled Wheelie Game: Wheelies and Jobs

In Untitled Wheelie Game you ride a bike around a shared map, lift the front wheel and try to hold the wheelie while traffic comes at you. You earn money by delivering pizzas, spend it on bikes, parts, upgrades and paint, and try not to get caught by the police. Up to 10 players share a server.

Everything below comes from the developer's own description on the [Roblox game listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/93844268955707/Untitled-Wheelie-Game), checked on 10 October 2026. Where we add advice, we say so.

## How do you hold a wheelie?

The developer describes the physics in one sentence: they are "designed to feel as realistic as possible, with a realistic balance point and responsive throttle and brake physics." In practice that means three things to learn:

1. **Throttle lifts the front.** More power raises the front wheel.
2. **The balance point is where it stays up.** Past it you loop; short of it the wheel drops.
3. **Brake brings it back down.** A touch of brake stops the bike tipping over backwards.

That is our reading of the official sentence, not a published tutorial. The keys or buttons for throttle and brake are not listed anywhere official, which is why we do not print a controls table. The in-game settings or prompts are the place to check.

## Why does traffic matter?

The game advertises "interactive traffic" and "oncoming traffic", and both promotional images show cars sharing the road with the rider. Our advice: practise long wheelies on an empty stretch before you try to weave between cars.
