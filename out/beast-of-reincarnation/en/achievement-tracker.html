    Beast of Reincarnation Achievement Tracker

    Global unlock percentages read from Steam on September 21, 2026 · refreshed weekly

    Language: English日本語FrançaisEspañolDeutschItaliano

    Completion · Tool

    Tick off all 46 achievements (the PS5 trophy and Xbox lists match one-for-one), watch your percentage, and let the tracker name the unlock that is statistically your easiest next — and the rarest one you still lack. Progress is saved in this browser only.

    0 / 46 unlocked (0%)

    Unlock rates are Steam’s global figures captured 2026-09-21, refreshed weekly. Your ticks are saved only in this browser.

    Reading the numbers

    Every percentage is Steam’s global unlock rate for Beast of Reincarnation: the share of all Steam owners who have earned that achievement over the life of the game. It is a moving figure — story achievements dip every time a sale brings in new players and climb back as they finish — so this page re-reads Steam weekly and prints the capture date under the list. On the 21 September read the most common is Purifier's Mantle at 94.6% and the rarest is Duty Fulfilled (Unlock every achievement.) at 3.2%. The trophies page has the same list with commentary on where completion runs stall, and the PS5 and Xbox mappings.

    38 of 46 are hidden

    Steam publishes no description for 38 of the 46 achievements, which is unusually many — Game Freak has chosen to keep most boss, ending and collection conditions unstated. The tracker shows those as hidden achievement rather than inventing a condition. Where this site has established what a hidden achievement needs from play or patch notes (the Nushi boss achievements follow the seal order; the Rapport chain is documented on the Koo guide), the trophies page says so; where it has not, it says that instead.

    A sensible order

    The tracker’s “easiest next” is purely statistical, but it tracks the campaign closely. The top of the list is the opening hours — first campsite, first attribute enhancement, first shrine — and the first Nushi. The middle band is the chapter-by-chapter boss seals and the Rapport milestones with Koo, which arrive naturally if you follow the walkthrough and pet the dog. The bottom band is the completion work: hidden achievements whose names point at weapon and Bloom Art collection (Blade Enthusiast, Blademaster, Floral Profusion) and at the ending (Reincarnated) — their conditions are unpublished, so treat the names as hints — and finally Duty Fulfilled, the only one Steam describes: unlock every achievement. The endings page explains which ending-related unlocks are missable.

    Version 1.0.9 (24 August) is the newest patch at the time of writing; the achievement list still counts 46 entries, and the patch notes page will say if a later patch changes that.

    What this tool is not

    It does not read your Steam, PlayStation or Xbox profile — you tick boxes by hand, which is why it works without signing in and cannot be wrong about what you own. Your platform’s own trophy page will tick for you; what it will not do is tell you which of the remaining items is statistically the cheapest, and that is what this page adds.
