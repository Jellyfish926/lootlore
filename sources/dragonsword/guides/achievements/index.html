DragonSword Achievements: All 60 and Unlock Rates

By Jellyfi · Last verified 2026-09-01

DragonSword: Awakening has 60 Steam achievements — patch 1.0.9 (13 August 2026) added the sixtieth, Conqueror of Barduk. 43 carry a public description; the other 17 are hidden, so Steam shows a name and nothing else.

Every percentage below was read off Valve's global stats page on 1 September 2026. They drift upward as the population plays. Treat them as a snapshot.

Prefer to tick these off as you go? The achievement tracker uses the same Steam data, refreshed weekly, and saves your progress in your browser.

What the distribution looks like

38 of the 60 sit below 10%. Ten are above 20%, and two now clear 40%.

The most-unlocked is the hidden Lucky! at 45.9%; the most-unlocked with a visible description is Anything in Here? — "Opened a Treasure Chest" — at 37.0%. The rarest described one is We Solve Anything, which wants every Area Quest completed, at 1.2%.

One caution. We do not know what denominator Valve uses, and Hound13 has not said. So "37.0% have opened a chest" is a fact about the stats page; any conclusion about how far a typical player gets is an inference.

Names the achievement list confirms and nothing else does

The stats page is the most useful document about this game that Hound13 did not write as marketing, because it names things.

Seventeen bosses, split between "Subduer of ~" (defeat three times) and "Conqueror of ~" (defeat on a stated difficulty). They have their own bosses page.

Five Hero Quests with owners: Cerese, Kalien, Ornette, Dana and Theresia. The other fourteen of the nineteen heroes have no Hero Quest achievement. That could mean they have no Hero Quest, or that theirs awards nothing — the list cannot distinguish the two, so we claim neither.

Four named side quests: Marmot King Hog, Anne, Bart and Ninian.

The collectible categories the game tracks: Treasure Chests, Treasure Maps, Waypoints, Dungeon Gates, Goddess Statues of Organa, Eona's Legacy, the Slide Puzzle, the Platform Puzzle mini-game, Area Quests, Sudden Missions, Hunt Monsters and Raids.

The band under 2% is all exploration

Six of the seven rarest described achievements have nothing to do with combat: We Solve Anything (1.2%), Hold On, Stand Right There (1.8%), There's a Marmot in That Burrow! (1.9%), In Search of Hidden Treasure (2.0%), Puzzle Solved by Pushing (2.1%) and Gotta Open That Suspicious Chest (2.4%).

That is a clean signal about the shape of the completion problem. The hard part is not the Nightmare bosses — those sit at 2.8% to 7.6%. The hard part is finding things: treasure maps and slide puzzles are unlocked by roughly one player in seventy.

These numbers move, including ours

Unlock rates rise over time, so any percentage carries an implicit date. Watch ours drift across three readings:

  • Lucky! — 43.6% in our early-August check, 44.2% on 12 August, 45.9% on 1 September.
  • Status Ailment Master — 9.5%, then 9.8%, now 10.8%.

An achievement percentage for this game with no date attached is not wrong so much as unusable.

Where players are actually stuck

Three Steam forum threads are people asking about specific achievements: "Status Ailment Master Achievement", "2 Titles / Achievements help" and "Ill Stand in the Sky Achievement with a strong Reina?". The first has zero replies. Nobody has publicly answered it.

Which matches the data: the hidden achievements are where people get stuck, and no source, official or player-made, explains them.

What is not on this page

The conditions for the 17 hidden achievements. Steam does not publish them and Hound13 has not described them in any announcement we have read. Sites listing exact triggers for Status Ailment Master or Gas! Gas! Gas! did not get them from Hound13, because Hound13 has not said. We would rather leave seventeen blanks than guess.

A recommended completion order, or an estimate of how long 100% takes. Both would require knowing what the hidden achievements ask for.

Per-achievement walkthroughs. Where a solution exists in a source we can name — Hagen, for instance — it lives on the page for that subject.
