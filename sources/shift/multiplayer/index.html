Shift At Midnight multiplayer and co-op

Yes. Shift At Midnight has online co-op for up to three players by design, and the host can raise a lobby to six since the 23 July 2026 patch. There is also a full single-player mode. Steam lists Online Co-op and no local co-op category, and the developer says Steam players can only play with other Steam players.

How many players can join one lobby?

Three is the designed size. The Steam store page describes the game as “An online co-op detective horror for up to 3 players”, and the launch post says “Play solo or with up to 2 extra friends”. One day after launch, the 23 July 2026 patch let the host pick a higher cap. The changelog reads: “When creating a lobby, you can now select a max player count of 6 players.”

Bun Muen was blunt about the six-player option in that same patch note. The game “is designed and has always been marketed around a maximum of 3 players”, and a bigger lobby “will likely become too chaotic, and is not recommended for your first playthrough”. The changelog was posted on Steam, and we could not confirm the option on the Xbox versions (checked 10 October 2026).

How do you host a game with friends?

The host creates a lobby from the game and chooses the maximum player count at that point. That much is stated in the 23 July 2026 patch note. No official post documents the invite steps or menu names, so we do not list them here. Our troubleshooting page covers what to check when a friend cannot see or join your lobby.

Two official statements matter before anyone tries to join. On stores, the developer’s 10 July 2026 announcement says Steam players will only be able to play with other Steam players, and that Xbox and PC Game Pass players will have crossplay. We have not tested either, and the crossplay page sets out what is on the record. On branches, the launch-day “network-issues-patch” beta fix came with this instruction: “Everyone you play with must also follow these instructions”.

A Steam-only public server browser was announced on 10 July 2026 for launch or shortly after. No later changelog mentions it shipping, so its status is not confirmed as of 10 October 2026.

How does voice chat work?

The game has built-in proximity chat. The store page puts it plainly: “proximity chat enhances the experience”, and the 22 July 2026 launch post lists “proximity chat” among the things to expect. The 20 August 2026 patch added individual volume sliders for each player. The 23 July 2026 patch also removed the profanity filter.

Can you try co-op before buying?

Yes. A free “Shift At Midnight Multiplayer Demo” has been on Steam since 29 September 2025, and Steam lists it as Online Co-op. It is a separate app from the full game, and Steam lists it as free. Our demo page lists what it includes.

Is solo worth playing?

Solo is an officially supported way to play. Steam lists the game under Single-player as well as Online Co-op, and the launch post says “Play solo or with up to 2 extra friends”. The store page does lean toward groups, saying “Multiplayer is where the game thrives”. No official statement compares how solo and co-op play, so we do not rank them. If you are playing alone for the story, the endings page sets out the three outcomes. For live numbers on how many people are playing, see player count.

How should two or three players split the work?

No official post describes roles for co-op. The split below is our own suggestion, not an official system and not something we can source. It assumes the three players the developer says the game “is designed and has always been marketed around”.

RoleMain jobRule to hold
CounterServes customers and runs the scanner and databaseDoes not leave for noises
FloorWatches behaviour, restocks, spots what the scanner missesCalls out anyone stepping around the counter
ResponseCarries the weapon and handles huntsOwns time-critical objects

On a Marionette night, according to our Jack-in-the-Box page, which cites Game Rant’s music box guide, three complete melodies from the wind-up music box summon the Marionette, and holding E to rewind the box before that third pass stops the encounter. That page also suggests giving one player the box for the whole encounter; in the split above, also our suggestion, that is the Response role.

Where can you find other players?

The official Discord server is the developer’s own suggestion. Bun Muen’s recent patch notes on Steam end by asking players to join it, and each of those notes carries the invite link. We do not copy invite links here; the 1 September 2026 patch note on the Steam store page carries one.

Two official statements apply to any group you find. The developer has said Steam players will only be able to play with other Steam players, and the 23 July 2026 patch note says the host selects the maximum player count when creating a lobby. Our beginner’s guide covers the first shifts.
