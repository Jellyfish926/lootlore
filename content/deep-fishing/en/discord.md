# Deep Fishing Discord, Roblox Group and Wiki Links

The Roblox group behind Deep Fishing had 476,428 members on 10 October 2026 and is owned by the verified account JoJocraftHP. Its name is LazyGames. The game's description tells players to join that group "for rewards and updates". A Discord server called Deep Fishing describes itself as the official one; there is no official wiki.

This page records exactly what each link is, what it said on the day we checked, and which parts we could not confirm.

## Which Roblox group makes the game?

The experience's creator field on Roblox points to one group, and that group describes itself as the game's home.

| Group ID | 34744238 | Roblox groups API |
| Description | "🔥Welcome to Lazy Games. 💤🎮" and, on a second line, "🩵Home of Deep Fishing! 🐟" | Roblox groups API |

The name includes a full stop — "LazyGames." — which is an easy way to tell it apart from look-alike groups. The group's shout field was empty when we read it on 10 October 2026.

## Why join the group?

Because the developer says so. The last line of the official description reads: "⭐ Like the game, favorite it, and join the group for rewards and updates!" What the group reward is exactly is not stated there. Fan sites describe it, but we have not confirmed the details, so check in-game after joining.

The store also has three products named Skip Gift [1], Skip Gift [2] and Skip Gift [3]; what they do is not confirmed, and our [Robux shop guide](/deep-fishing/shop/) lists their prices.

What we could not do: Roblox only shows an experience's social links to signed-in users through its API, so we could not confirm that LazyGames links this exact server from the game page. Treat it as "the server that calls itself official" until you see the link on the Roblox experience page yourself while signed in.

Never enter your Roblox password on a site that claims to hand out codes or items through Discord.

## Where do codes get announced?

Nowhere we could read. The official description and the LazyGames group carry no codes, and the Discord announcement channels need a Discord account. The [codes page](/deep-fishing/codes/) records what each official Roblox record held on 10 October 2026. We do not copy code lists from other sites, because we could not find any code in the official Roblox records we read on 10 October 2026. When we can confirm a code from an official post, it will go on that page.

## Is there an official wiki?

Not an official one. The Fandom wiki at deepfishing.fandom.com contained only two pages, both placeholders, when we checked on 29 September 2026; on 10 October 2026 its API answered HTTP 410 (Gone), so we could not reread it. Two independent fan wikis exist (deepfishing.wiki and deep-fishing.wiki).
