# Phasmophobia Getting Started: New Player Guides

Phasmophobia leaves a lot for you to work out. You get a truck, a set of equipment, a dark location and a Journal, and the rest is up to you and whoever is on voice chat. The four guides in this section cover what the developer has put in writing about the basics, so you start with facts and not with half-remembered advice from 2021.

## Pick a guide by question

What a contract asks of you, why the Training mission is worth doing first, the default keys added in 2026, the five-photo, five-video and three-sound limits, and what you lose when you die.

### [Phasmophobia Platforms, Price and PC Requirements](/phasmophobia/platforms-price/)

The $19.99 US Steam price, minimum and recommended PC specs, which consoles the developer lists, how optional crossplay works, Steam Deck test results and the Switch 2 plan.

The developer's own descriptions of Amateur through Insanity, the Custom Difficulty option groups, the 15x reward cap set in February 2023, the Apocalypse challenge thresholds and why a 0x game earns no progress.

### [All 54 Phasmophobia Achievements and Unlock Rates](/phasmophobia/achievements/)

Every Steam achievement with its exact requirement text and global unlock rate, the six that show no description, and which newer ghost types have no achievement at all.

## Quick answers before you pick a page

- Is it solo or co-op? Both. Steam lists single-player and online co-op, and the store page says up to 4 players. The solo quality-of-life changes are in [how to play](/phasmophobia/how-to-play/).
- Do you need a microphone? The store page advertises full voice recognition, and the patch notes describe a text mode as well. Console setup steps are in the same guide.
- Which difficulty first? The developer labels Amateur "Recommended for new Ghost Hunters!". The trade-offs of each mode are on the [difficulty page](/phasmophobia/difficulty/).
- Can PC and console friends play together? Yes, crossplay is optional and updates ship on all platforms together; see [platforms and price](/phasmophobia/platforms-price/).
- Do 0x custom games count for achievements? No: since August 2026 they "will no longer contribute towards any progress, such as achievements and leaflets". The details are on the [achievements page](/phasmophobia/achievements/).

## What these guides leave out, and why

You will not find an evidence chart for every ghost, hunt thresholds or equipment statistics here yet. The developer's posts give those only in fragments, and the latest new ghost, the Deildegast, was announced by name only. Community wikis fill that gap, and we would rather add those tables after checking them in game than repeat numbers we cannot trace to a source.

What the developer does publish is more than most players realise: unlock levels, media limits, reward rules, default keybinds and nearly every change made in 2026. That is the material these pages are built from.

## Where to go after the basics

Once contracts feel routine, the questions change from "how does this work" to "what changed this month". That is the job of the [Updates & Events](/phasmophobia/updates-events/) section: the 2026 patch timeline, the roadmap to 1.0, the Crimson Eye event and the Twitch Drop schedule.

## Scope

These guides describe the Steam version at v0.19.1.0, with console notes where the developer has given them. Rules quoted from older patch notes are dated, because a 2022 number is not a 2026 promise. This hub is unofficial and not affiliated with Kinetic Games.
