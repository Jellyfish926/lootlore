# Animal Daycare Night Events: Ghosts, Intruders, Black Cat

Night is where Animal Daycare (Anomaly) turns into a survival game. The developer only promises "mysterious nighttime events" and names one threat, ghosts, which the Robux Water Gun is sold to scare away. A community beginner video shows more: intruders, a black cat, a black monster and nightmares, each handled a different way.

Only the ghost threat and the items in the first table are official. Everything else comes from one community video and has not been confirmed in-game by us.

## What does official data say about the night?

| Official source | Exact wording |
| --- | --- |
| Game description | "Prepare the daycare for nighttime." · "Survive mysterious nighttime events" |
| Water Gun (20 Bullets), 99 Robux | "Use it to scare away ghosts and protect the daycare!" |
| Restore Coffie, 19 Robux | "Refill the coffee and make it available again." |
| Lunchbox, 29 Robux | "Each use restores +15 Calm. Perfect for stressful shifts!" |
| Get Toy Hammer, 19 Robux | "Toy Hammer taken or still recharging? Grab another one instantly" |

Prices and the rest of the store are on the [shop page](/animal-daycare/shop/).

## Which threats does the community video show?

These come from the auto-generated captions of [Zac Worthy's beginner guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B1IY_XEyedU), uploaded on 20 September 2026. We have not checked them ourselves.

| Threat shown | Counter shown in the video |
| --- | --- |
| Ghost seen on the cameras | Use the camera flash; the narrator says the Toy Hammer does not work on ghosts |
| Intruder appears | Activate the electric shock; if it gets inside, use the Toy Hammer |
| A child turns into a monster | Use the Toy Hammer |
| Black cat | Cannot be escaped; prevent it by finding and cleaning the black circle spot |
| Black monster | Do not look at it for too long, and do not touch it |
| Nightmare | Find the clock immediately, or you get a jump scare |

The video says only the camera flash scares ghosts away, while the official Water Gun description says the gun scares away ghosts too. We list both until we can test them.

![Promotional art: a tall black creature with a wide grin and long clawed arms in a dark daycare playroom](th3 "Official promotional art: one of the night's creatures")

## How does the video prepare for bedtime?

In the video, the night starts with a routine: get the beds ready, turn on the sleep music and switch off the lights. The narrator advises drinking coffee regularly to replenish what the video calls sanity, calming crying children and fixing anything that breaks. Whether "sanity" is the same as the Calm value restored by the official Lunchbox is not stated anywhere official.
