# BlockSpin Weapons Guide: How to Get Guns and Melee

Weapons in BlockSpin come from several places rather than one shop. A community wiki lists dumpsters, weapon crates and airdrops as sources, and the official description ties random reward cases to the job grind. Whatever you get, it drops with the rest of your inventory when you die, so a strong weapon is also the thing other players want to take.

## Where do weapons come from?

| Source | What community guides say | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dumpsters | Can contain weapons | Community wiki, October 2025 |
| Weapon crates | Random weapon rewards | Community wiki, October 2025 |
| Airdrops | Crate drops with loot; community site describes cues and risks | Community sources |
| Reward cases from jobs | Official description: jobs lead to "random reward cases" | Official (contents not specified) |

## Which weapons are in the game?

Official art shows pistols. YouTube titles mention an M16 described as legendary and very rare, and a melee weapon tier list exists. We have not verified a current weapon list, so none is published here yet.
