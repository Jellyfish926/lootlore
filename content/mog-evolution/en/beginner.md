# +1 Mog Evolution Beginner Guides: Where to Start Playing

New to +1 Mog Evolution? Read how to play first: it explains clicking for Appeal, the Bonesmash hammer, Wins and the True Adam leaderboard. Then read the progression guide for Rebirth, Ascend and Worlds, check the updates page for this week's event, and the community page for codes and the official group.

The developer's own description of the game is four short lines. These guides expand each line with what Navoj Mog has written elsewhere on Roblox, mainly in the descriptions of its 43 store items, and they say clearly where the official information stops.

## What should you do in your first session?

Our suggestion, using only what the developer has published: click steadily and upgrade your hammer whenever the game lets you, since the description promises more Appeal from Bonesmash. Keep an eye on your level, because Rebirth needs you to reach the level cap. Collect Wins, since they are what lets you ascend into the next body. Once you have Robux to spare, the [Appeal guide](/mog-evolution/appeal/) shows which boosts give the most for the price.

## What these guides do not cover yet

There is no body tier list, no Appeal calculator and no Ranked guide, because the numbers they need are not published anywhere official. When the developer or the game's own screens confirm them, we will add them here.
