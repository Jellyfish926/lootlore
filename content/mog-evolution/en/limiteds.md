# +1 Mog Evolution Gigachad, Claviculars Hammer and Smoothie

Five unlocks in +1 Mog Evolution cost exactly 399 Robux each. Three are described: the Gigachad body (x4 Appeal), the Claviculars Hammer (x1024 Appeal) and the Smoothie (x2048 Charisma). Two World-limited items, LOOKSMEOWXER and MANGO MOGGER, have no description at all.

Each described item is a permanent, one-per-player unlock that works "while equipped". Here is what the developer says about each, and how we would choose.

## What does each 399-Robux unlock do?

| Item | Added | Type | Effect, in the developer's words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gigachad [LIMITED] | 10 Sep 2026 | Body | "Gives a x4 body Appeal multiplier while equipped." |
| Claviculars Hammer | 10 Sep 2026 | Hammer | "Gives x1024 Appeal while equipped." |
| Smoothie | 24 Sep 2026 | Not stated | "Gives x2048 Charisma while equipped." |
| LOOKSMEOWXER [W2 LIMITED] | 26 Sep 2026 | Not stated | No description |
| MANGO MOGGER [W3 LIMITED] | 26 Sep 2026 | Not stated | No description |

All five are developer products, not game passes, and all were on sale on 1 October 2026.

## Is the Gigachad body really limited?

Not in the usual sense. Its full description reads: "Permanently unlock the Gigachad body in +1 Mog Evolution. Gives a x4 body Appeal multiplier while equipped. One unlock per player. The 1,500-copy counter is informational; purchases remain available after it reaches zero."

So the counter you may see in the game does not stop sales. You do not need to rush to buy it before the number runs out. The "x4 body Appeal multiplier" wording suggests other bodies have their own multipliers too, but the developer has not published them. The body system is explained in the [progression guide](/mog-evolution/progression/).

## How strong is the Claviculars Hammer?

On paper, very. "Permanently unlock Claviculars Hammer in +1 Mog Evolution. Gives x1024 Appeal while equipped. Unlimited availability; one permanent unlock per player."

x1024 is the largest Appeal multiplier in the store's wording, ahead of the x999 treadmill belt. But the two are not alike: the hammer works while equipped, the belt only while you stand on it. Whether the hammer's x1024 replaces your normal hammer's bonus or adds to it is not stated. Bonesmash, the hammer upgrade named in the game description, is covered in [how to play](/mog-evolution/how-to-play/).

## What is the Smoothie, and what is Charisma?

"Permanently unlock Smoothie in +1 Mog Evolution. Gives x2048 Charisma while equipped. Unlimited availability; one permanent unlock per player."

"Charisma" appears nowhere else in the game's official text. It may be another name for Appeal, as "Power" seems to be in the Appeal packs, but we could not confirm it. Until we can, we treat x2048 Charisma as an unknown stat, not as double the Claviculars Hammer.

## What are LOOKSMEOWXER and MANGO MOGGER?

Both were added to the store on 26 September 2026, and both cost 399 Robux. The tags in their names, "[W2 LIMITED]" and "[W3 LIMITED]", point to World 2 and World 3. Neither has a description, so we do not know whether they are bodies, hammers or something else, or whether they are limited by time or copies. When they appeared is on the [updates page](/mog-evolution/updates/).

## How do they compare with cheaper boosts?

A 399-Robux unlock is a big spend in this store. Here is how the official multipliers line up against the cheaper permanent boosts, sorted by price. The wording differs ("while equipped", "while standing on it", "permanently earn"), so these numbers are not directly comparable.

| Item | Robux | Multiplier, as worded | When it works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VIP | 99 | 10x Appeal | Always (permanent) |
| x256 Power | 199 | 256x Power | Always (permanent) |
| x99 Treadmill | 249 | 99x Appeal | While standing on the belt |
| Gigachad [LIMITED] | 399 | x4 body Appeal | While the body is equipped |
| Claviculars Hammer | 399 | x1024 Appeal | While the hammer is equipped |
| Smoothie | 399 | x2048 Charisma | While equipped |
| x999 Treadmill | 999 | 999x Appeal | While standing on the belt |

If the boosts multiply together, the cheap permanent ones make every 399 unlock stronger. If only the highest counts, the Claviculars Hammer is the biggest confirmed Appeal number in the game.

## Which one should you buy?

Our advice, based only on the official wording:

| If you want… | Pick | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The biggest confirmed Appeal multiplier | Claviculars Hammer | x1024 Appeal, while equipped |
| A body upgrade | Gigachad | x4 body Appeal; no rush, the counter does not end sales |
| To gamble on an unknown stat | Smoothie | x2048, but "Charisma" is unexplained |
| A World-limited collectible | Wait | No description yet; we would not buy blind |

None of the five is mentioned in the game description, which describes the whole loop without them. Before any of these, check the cheaper boosts in the [Appeal guide](/mog-evolution/appeal/). VIP (10x) and the first Power multipliers cost a fraction of 399 Robux. For the full price list, see [all 43 Robux products](/mog-evolution/shop/).
