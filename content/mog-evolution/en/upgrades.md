# +1 Mog Evolution Boosts and Robux Guides: Spend Wisely

Want more Appeal per minute, or wondering if a 399-Robux unlock is worth it? Start with the Appeal guide: it ranks every boost in +1 Mog Evolution by price. Then read the limited unlocks guide before buying Gigachad, Claviculars Hammer or Smoothie, and use the full store list when you want to check one exact price.

These three guides use the developer's own store data from Roblox: names, prices and descriptions exactly as published on 1 October 2026. The game sells no game passes, so every boost is a developer product. Fifteen of them have no description, and we point that out rather than filling the gap.

## Pick a guide by question

### [How to Get Appeal Fast](/mog-evolution/appeal/)

Free methods first: clicking, hammer upgrades and treadmill belts. Then every paid boost: the Auto Clicker at 22 clicks per second, VIP at 10x, nine Power multipliers from x1.5 to x256, nine treadmill belts from x2 to x999, and four Appeal packs, with value per Robux worked out.

### [Gigachad, Claviculars Hammer and Smoothie](/mog-evolution/limiteds/)

The five items that cost 399 Robux each. Gigachad is a x4 body whose copy counter does not stop sales; Claviculars Hammer gives x1024 Appeal; Smoothie gives x2048 "Charisma"; LOOKSMEOWXER and MANGO MOGGER have no description yet.

### [All 43 Robux Products and Prices](/mog-evolution/shop/)

The complete store in four tables, every description copied exactly, from x1.5 Power at 1 Robux to the x999 Treadmill at 999 Robux.

## Where should your Robux go first?

It depends on what is slowing you down. Here is our quick guide:

| Problem | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Clicking is tiring | Auto Clicker, in the [Appeal guide](/mog-evolution/appeal/) |
| Appeal grows too slowly | x1.5 and x2 Power, then VIP |
| Stuck waiting for Wins | X2 Wins, in the [progression guide](/mog-evolution/progression/) |
| Want a top-tier hammer or body | [Limited unlocks](/mog-evolution/limiteds/) |
| Just want the price of one item | [Full store list](/mog-evolution/shop/) |

Our general advice: play a while before you spend. Multipliers are worth more once you know which part of the game you spend most time on, clicking or standing on a treadmill.

## What these guides do not cover yet

How the multipliers combine is not published. We do not know whether VIP, Power, treadmills, bodies and hammers multiply together or whether only the highest one counts, so we never add them up for you. When that is confirmed, the Appeal guide will show combined totals.

## Scope

Prices change when the developer edits the store. Every figure here is a snapshot from 1 October 2026; check the in-game price before you buy.
