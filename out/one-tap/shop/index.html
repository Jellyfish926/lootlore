    One Tap Robux Shop: 97 Products in 13 Groups

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10

    Scope: Developer product records from the Roblox API, read on 2026-10-10; all dates UTC. All 97 records were marked for sale. Prices are the Robux prices at the read time. Groups are ours, sorted by product name, not official categories. No record carries a description, so what a product does or contains is not confirmed. Record dates show when a record was created or edited, not when a product reached players; a record marked for sale is not proof the in-game shop offers it today

    Key points

    • Roblox's developer product records for One Tap held 97 products on 10 October 2026, all marked for sale, priced from 5 Robux to 10,000 Robux.
    • We sort them into 13 groups by name. Products with Case in the name account for 49 of the 97; the rest include currency packs, Premium Battlepass and Skip products, Xp Boost products, bundles, limited-named items and donations.
    • Five Gems packs cost 49 to 2,299 Robux and five Sun Points packs cost 80 to 2,000. No record says how many Gems or Sun Points a pack holds.
    • None of the 97 records has a description, and all 97 share one plain placeholder icon, so names and prices are all the store records give.

    One Tap's Robux shop held 97 developer products in the Roblox records we read on 10 October 2026, priced from 5 to 10,000 Robux. Forty-nine have Case in the name. The other 48 include Gems and Sun Points packs, Premium Battlepass and Skip products, Xp Boost products, bundles, limited-named items and four donations. None has a description.

    That last point shapes this whole page. Every price below is the Robux price at the time of that read, and every name is copied exactly. The groups are ours: we sorted the products by the words in their names, because Roblox's records carry no category field. Where a name does not explain itself, the product's function is marked not confirmed.

    The names do not include a Gem amount, and no record has a description. So the table can show how the prices step up and cannot show Gems per Robux. "Best Value" is the developer's name for the most expensive pack; whether it is the best value depends on a number the records do not hold. All five records were created on 29 December 2025 and have not been edited since. What Gems buy in the game is not stated in any official source we read.

    These five were created on 10 June 2026 and all were edited on 14 June, the day before the developer's ☀️Summer Update! listing was due to start. The listing itself only invites players to "take part in the Summer Event alongside other new content" and does not mention Sun Points. So the tie between the two is our reading of the name and the dates. How many points a pack holds, what they buy and whether they can still be spent are not stated.

    Ten of the 14 cost 199 Robux, three cost 149 and one costs 95. Seven have slot-style names, Limited 1 to 4 and Limited Skin 1 to 3, that do not say which item they sell. Those names do not identify an item, so the records cannot tell you which limited items the shop is selling today.

    Two records share the name Solar Cannon. One is "[LIMITED] Solar Cannon 115" at 95 Robux and the other "[LIMITED OFFER] Solar Cannon" at 149, created about two minutes apart on 29 January 2026. What the 115 stands for is not stated, and neither is the difference between them.

    What are the donation products and the product named 500?

    Four One Tap records are donations, and each costs the amount in its name: 10 Robux Donation, 100 Robux Donation, 1000 Robux Donation and 10000 Robux Donation. All four were created in the same second on 6 March 2026. No record promises anything in return, so treat them as a tip to the developer.

    One record is named just 500 and costs 5 Robux. It was created on 28 January 2026, about two minutes before the 5-Robux product named Case, and both were edited within seconds of each other early the next day. What 500 refers to is not confirmed. It could be an amount of in-game currency, and we will not print that as fact without a source.

    How did we read the One Tap shop records, and what is not confirmed?

    The One Tap developer product list came back in one response of 97 records with no further page to fetch. All 97 were marked for sale, none showed a discount and none had text in its description field. All 97 also share one icon, a plain grey cube, so there is no product art to show here.

    The most common price is 199 Robux, on 12 records. Fifty of the 97 records show identical creation and edit times. The other 47 have been edited at least once, and an edit date does not say what was changed.

    Still not confirmed after this read:

    • what any bundle, limited-named item or currency pack contains;
    • which records the in-game shop offers right now;
    • whether prices inside the game match the record on the day you buy.

    Our advice, not a rule of the game: read the purchase prompt before you confirm, because every one of these 97 records gives you a name and a price and nothing else. If a price here no longer matches the game, the author page explains how to report it.
