# All 43 +1 Mog Evolution Robux Products and Prices

+1 Mog Evolution sells 43 developer products and no game passes. Prices run from 1 Robux (x1.5 Power) to 999 Robux (x999 Treadmill), and every product was on sale on 1 October 2026. Below is the full list, grouped by what each item does, with the developer's own descriptions copied word for word.

Prices change when the developer edits the store, so check the in-game price before you buy. If you only want to know which boost to buy first, the [Appeal guide](/mog-evolution/appeal/) is shorter.

## What are Mogger Pack and Revenge?

We do not know. Both cost 99 Robux and have no description. Mogger Pack was added on 18 September 2026 and edited the next day; Revenge was added on 24 September. Their names alone do not tell us what they contain, and we will not guess.

## Why are there no game passes?

Roblox's game-pass listing for this experience is empty, and so is its badge list. Many Roblox games sell permanent perks such as VIP as game passes. Here the developer sells everything as developer products and writes "Permanent" or "Can only be bought once" into the descriptions instead.

## How did we build this list?

From Roblox's public developer-products listing for the game, read on 1 October 2026. It returned all 43 items on one page with no further pages. Names and descriptions are copied exactly, including brackets and capital letters. The total of 5,910 Robux is our own sum of the 43 prices.
