# Get Your Driver's License! Guide: Line, Test and Cars

Get Your Driver's License! is a free Roblox game by the group Time Will Pass, created on 29 September 2026. Its official description lists what you do: take a number and wait, pass the written test, drive the test course, win 1 of 18 cars and unlock endings. These guides cover each of those and every Robux price.

The official description sums it up in one line: "Wait in line and get your Driver's License!" These guides are built from that description, the store listings the developer has registered on Roblox and the group's public record. We have not played the test course ourselves yet, and each page says where that matters.

## What does Robux buy?

Two kinds of thing. The 5 game passes are one-time purchases from 9 to 1,200 Robux. Three of their descriptions name other players (Time Out, Gravity Gun 🔥 and Ban Hammer), one is a loud airhorn and one is an emoji pack. The 13 developer products cost 9 to 160 Robux. Their descriptions cover skipping the line, passing the written test, examiner time, cars and effects on other players. Start with the [Cars & Robux Shop section](/get-your-drivers-license/robux/), or go straight to the [game pass list](/get-your-drivers-license/gamepasses/).

## Why is there no codes page?

Because we could not find a single code in an official source. On 10 October 2026 we read three official places we could access: the game description, the group description and the group shout. Social links on the game and the group could not be read without an account. The description has eight lines and none of them is a code. The Time Will Pass group has an empty description and no shout. If we see a code in an official place, a codes page for Get Your Driver's License! will follow.

## Who runs the game?

Time Will Pass is a Roblox group created on 27 September 2026, two days before this game. It published five experiences between 27 and 29 September 2026. The [developer page](/get-your-drivers-license/community/) covers the group, its owner account, its other games and why we cannot confirm a Discord server.

## How this guide uses its sources

This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Roblox or Time Will Pass. Game facts come from Roblox's own records for the game: its description, game passes, store products and group. Suggestions such as a buying order are our advice, not a rule of the game. Car names, the full ending list, written test questions and the course layout are left out because the developer has not published them and we have not yet checked them in the game.
