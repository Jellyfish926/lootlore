# BlockSpin Vehicles Guide: Car Cases and Drop Chances

In BlockSpin you unlock cars by opening vehicle cases, according to the community wiki, not by buying a specific model. The wiki lists three cases — Basic, Rare and Elite — with an Omega case above Elite, and says the Elite case costs $16,500 at the Car Dealership. Those figures date from 2025, so check them in-game before relying on them.
