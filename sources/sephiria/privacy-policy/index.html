Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-07

Short version: this archive stores nothing about you itself. What follows lists the third parties that do, what they set, and how to switch them off.

Who maintains this archive

sephiriawiki.site is a one-person fan archive, not a company. Write to hello@sephiriawiki.site and a human reads it.

Information you hand over

There is no account system, no newsletter, no checkout. Nothing asks for a name, an address, or a card number, so nothing of that kind is stored.

The one exception is email. If you write in, that message sits in a mailbox until the thread is finished.

Cookies, and what sets them

No cookie originates from this archive. Every one you may find comes from a service listed below.

Google Analytics counts page views so it is possible to tell which pages earn their place. It records approximate location, device class, browser, and the path taken through the site. It is read as totals, never person by person.

Google AdSense

Running costs are covered by advertising.

Google is a third-party vendor and uses cookies to serve advertisements here. Those cookies let Google and its partners tailor what appears based on visits to this archive and to other sites.

Other third-party advertising partners may also place cookies or comparable technologies to measure performance. Each publishes its own privacy policy, and none of that collection is under this archive's control.

Personalised advertising can be turned off at adssettings.google.com. A broader vendor-by-vendor opt-out lives at aboutads.info.

Store and affiliate links

Some pages point at game retailers through affiliate links. A purchase made through one may pay a commission; the price you pay does not change. Those partners set an attribution cookie. Nothing on this archive is recommended because of a commission.

Turning any of it off

  • Block or clear cookies in the browser. Every page keeps working; some counting simply stops.
  • Use the two opt-out links above for advertising.
  • Run a blocker or a private window. No attempt is made here to detect or defeat either.
  • Ask what is held, or ask for an email thread to be deleted, at hello@sephiriawiki.site.

Age

This archive is not aimed at readers under 13, and no information is knowingly gathered from them. If something of that kind has been sent in by mistake, say so and it will be removed.

Where the data ends up

Google processes its data on infrastructure outside the reader's own country, under its own terms. This archive keeps no analytics database of its own — only the mailbox described above.

Revisions

This page is edited whenever a service is added or dropped. The date at the top is the last edit.
