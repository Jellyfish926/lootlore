    +1 Stone Skipping Updates: Admin Abuse and Event Times

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10 · Checked on version 2026-10-08 update

    Scope: Events from the Roblox virtual-events listing, read at 05:21 UTC on 2026-10-10 (a request with a page cursor returned five listings; a request without one returned the two that had not ended); timeline rebuilt from game pass and developer product creation dates and experience timestamps read the same day; we found no official patch notes. All times UTC

    ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 5

    Quick facts

    Official Roblox event for +1 Stone Skipping hosted by the group Meow Labs, category 'new content'; the listing's description has three lines: Admin Abuse, World 5, New features.

    Description
    - Admin Abuse / - World 5 / - New features
    Notes

    Key points

    • ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 5 was an official Roblox event listing for the game on Saturday 3 October 2026, from 16:00 to 18:00 UTC; that window has ended.
    • The listing's description has three lines: Admin Abuse, World 5 and New features. Its subtitle reads FIRST ADMIN ABUSE!!
    • Earlier listings were World 3 + New Content (from 20 September 2026) and WORLD 4 + UPDATE (from 27 September 2026); on 10 October 2026 the listing also held WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE (10 to 11 October) and Halloween Event (17 October to 1 November). No event listing names World 2.
    • New store items were created on 15 different days within 30 calendar days (10 September to 9 October 2026); we found no patch notes from Meow Labs, so this page dates events and store items, not gameplay changes.

    The official Roblox event ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 5 was listed for Saturday 3 October 2026 from 16:00 to 18:00 UTC, and that window has ended. We found no patch notes from Meow Labs, so the history below is rebuilt from event listings and the creation date of each pass and store product.

    When we read the listing at 05:21 UTC on 10 October 2026, two later events were on it: WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE, from 16:00 UTC on 10 October to 16:00 UTC on 11 October 2026, and Halloween Event, from 16:00 UTC on 17 October to 19:00 UTC on 1 November 2026.

    Every item's price is in the full Robux product list, and the passes are in the game pass guide.

    Three prices differ between this site's reading of 2 October 2026 and the list we read on 10 October 2026: Skill Multiplier [TIER 1] went from 3 to 5 Robux, Golden Skip from 12 to 19 and Diamond Skip from 19 to 29. The [TIER 1] record was last edited on 3 October at 22:55 UTC and the two Skip records on 9 October at 19:24 UTC. The records keep no earlier prices, so which edit changed a price is not confirmed.

    How often does +1 Stone Skipping update?

    Often. Between 10 September and 9 October 2026, within 30 calendar days, new passes or products were created on 15 different days. A creation date proves the item existed by then. It does not prove the item was visible in game that day, and it says nothing about gameplay changes that do not involve the store.

    Is there a pattern to the eggs?

    Yes, roughly a weekly one. Rainbow Egg products were created on 10 September and Admin and Pirate Egg products on 12 September. Astronaut followed on 19 September, Chocolatier on 26 September, Dragon on 1 October and Witch on 7 October, so the last five themed eggs sit five to seven days apart.

    The timing also tracks the world events. Astronaut Egg products were created the day before the World 3 event started, Chocolatier Egg products the day before the World 4 event, Dragon Egg products two days before the World 5 event, and Witch Egg products three days before the start time on the WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE listing. No listing says that an egg belongs to a world, so the timing is all we can show. Egg prices and bundle savings are worked out in the eggs and pets guide.

    When did Worlds 2, 3 and 4 arrive?

    Two of them can be dated from Roblox's event listings, and the listings go on to World 5 and World 6. The endpoint returned five listings when we read it at 05:21 UTC on 10 October 2026:

    Official event titleListed on (UTC)Starts (UTC)Ends (UTC)

    WORLD 4 + UPDATE22 September, 11:5327 September, 16:001 October, 16:00
    ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 526 September, 09:043 October, 16:003 October, 18:00
    WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE3 October, 15:0210 October, 16:0011 October, 16:00
    Halloween Event5 October, 22:2417 October, 16:001 November, 19:00

    The World 3, WORLD 4 and Halloween listings have an empty description, so each gives a title and a window, not the exact moment a world opened. The WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE description has three lines (Admin Abuse, World 6, New Features), and the Halloween Event subtitle is "???". Neither of those two windows had opened at our read, so this page lists no contents for them. No event listing names World 2. We found no store product whose name says it skips to a world, so no creation date marks a world; what Golden Skip, Diamond Skip, Void Skip, Golden Zone and Admin Zone do is not stated. One caveat: on 10 October 2026 a request to the event endpoint without a page cursor returned only the two listings that had not ended; a request with the page cursor in our sources returned all five.

    How do we keep this page current?

    Each review re-reads the Roblox event listing, both store lists and the experience timestamps. The read behind this page was at 05:20 to 05:22 UTC on 10 October 2026, before the start time on the WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE listing, so a store record created after that read is not on this page. What the game is about in the first place is covered in how to play.
