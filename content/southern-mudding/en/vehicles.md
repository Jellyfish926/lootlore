# Southern Mudding Vehicles: Every Type and How to Get It

Southern Mudding's description names five vehicle classes: pickup trucks, semi trucks, trailers, classic cars and American muscle cars, "and more". Beyond whatever is free in game, official data shows four routes to extra vehicles: a group reward, five game-pass packs holding 16 vehicles, a two-trailer pass, and 22 single vehicles sold for Robux.

One caution first. Roblox's public data gives names and prices, not statistics. There is no official speed, torque or mud rating anywhere we can read, so this page tells you what exists and what it costs, not what is fastest.

## How can you get a vehicle in Southern Mudding?

| Route | What it gives | Cost | Stated where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free roster | Not listed in public data | In-game, needs a check | — |
| Join the Roblox group | "a free pickup truck" | Free | Group description |
| Vehicle packs | 16 vehicles across five passes | 290 to 375 Robux per pack | Game pass descriptions |
| Deluxe Trailer Pack | Boat Trailer and Double Jet Ski Trailer | 200 Robux | Game pass description |
| Single Robux vehicles | 22 products, one vehicle each | 200 to 325 Robux | Developer product names |

The group line is the cheapest win: the group description reads "Join the group for a free pickup truck!". Which pickup it is, and how it is delivered, we could not confirm; the [community page](/southern-mudding/community/) explains what the group data does show.

## What is inside the five vehicle packs?

Only counts and categories. The pass descriptions do not name a single vehicle:

| Pass | Robux | What the description promises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic Car Pack | 290 | 4 iconic American classic vehicles |
| Police Vehicle Pack | 290 | 2 police vehicles |
| Deluxe Mudder Pack | 320 | 3 lifted mega mudding trucks |
| Deluxe Car Pack | 345 | 4 iconic American performance vehicles |
| Race Car Pack | 375 | 3 premium race cars |

Deluxe Mudder Pack is the only pack whose description mentions mud. If the name of the game is why you are here, that is the pack the developer points at, though we cannot tell you how its trucks compare with the free ones. Prices per vehicle and gift versions are in the [game pass guide](/southern-mudding/gamepasses/).

## What do the official images show that the store does not?

The seven promotional images on the game page show quad bikes, dirt bikes, a six-wheeled quad, a motorhome, a heavy tanker truck and a large speedboat on a multi-axle trailer. Some may correspond to store names, since a Luxury RV and a Boat Trailer both exist, but promotional art is not a vehicle list and we have not matched any image to an in-game name. No pass or product is named for a quad bike or a dirt bike, but the developer's event listings include "Dirtbike Update" (30 January 2026), "Lawn Mower Update" (20 February 2026), "Boat Update" (27 February 2026) and "ATVs Update!" (13 March 2026), which suggests the roster is wider than the store; the ATVs listing says "Introducing many new ATVs and UTVs".

## Which vehicles arrived on 25 September?

Two vehicles, by the update notes: "New Pickup Truck!" and "New UTV!". The notes give no model names. No developer product was created that week either; the nearest are [Limited] Monster Truck on 18 September and [Limited] Off-Road 6x6 Pickup Truck on 30 September. So the two additions are not in the Robux store data under a new name. They may be bought with in-game money or unlocked another way; that needs an in-game check.

## Which Southern Mudding vehicle is best for mud?

We cannot rank them, and we would rather say so than invent a tier list. A fair ranking needs ground clearance, tyre and drivetrain behaviour in the game's mud, and none of that is published. What the official data supports is narrower:

- **The developer's own "mudding" label** sits on the Deluxe Mudder Pack and on (Limited) Monster Mudding Truck.
- **Pickup trucks come up often.** They are named first in the description, they received nitrous on 25 September, and six of the 22 Robux vehicles are 6x6 pickup trucks.
- **Towing is named too.** The description names towing, hauling and boating, and the [spawning guide](/southern-mudding/spawning/) covers the trailer passes.

If you are deciding where to spend, try the free vehicles first, then judge a paid one from someone else's truck in your server before buying.

## Why does this guide use plain vehicle names?

Because the developer does. Every pass and product in Southern Mudding is registered under a generic name such as Pickup Truck, UTV or Monster Truck, and we copy those names exactly. We do not map them to real-world makes or models.
