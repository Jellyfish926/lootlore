# +1 Stone Skipping Robux Shop Guides: Passes and Prices

Want to know what something costs in +1 Stone Skipping before the purchase prompt appears? Start with the game pass guide for the 11 game passes. Read the eggs and pets guide before buying eggs, the boosts guide for Skill Multiplier tiers and Wins multipliers, and the full list when you need one exact price.

All four guides use the developer's store data as Roblox publishes it: 11 game passes and 78 developer products, read on 2 October 2026. There is one catch that shapes every page. Meow Labs has not written a description for any of the 89 items, so a name and a price are all that is official.

## Which guide do you need?

### [All 11 Game Passes](/stone-skipping/gamepasses/)

Auto Wins, Auto Rebirth, three Training Zones, three pet passes and three Hatch passes, from 25 to 599 Robux. Includes per-unit prices, gift versions, and the two Training Zones that also exist as products at different prices.

### [Eggs and Pets](/stone-skipping/pets/)

Six Robux eggs with x3 and x8 bundles, the saving in each bundle, and the three products whose icons show a pet: Starter Pack, Devil Pack and King Doggy.

### [Skill and Wins Boosts](/stone-skipping/boosts/)

Ten Skill Multiplier tiers with a running total, nine Skill Packs, six Wins Packs, the 2x, 5x and 10x Wins [PERMANENT] products and three boost potions.

### [All 78 Robux Products](/stone-skipping/shop/)

The complete developer product list in seven groups, with creation dates, the 14 [GIFT] products and the two items that are not on sale.

## Where do the prices start?

A quick map of the cheapest entry in each part of the store:

| Part of the store | Cheapest item | Robux |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill | Skill Multiplier [TIER 1] | 3 |
| Utility | Offline Reward x3 | 9 |
| Game passes | Auto Wins, Hatch +3 Eggs [STACKS] | 25 |
| Wins | Wins Pack 1 | 25 |
| Potions | Skill Boost, Wins Boost | 29 |
| Packs | Starter Pack | 39 |
| Eggs | Rainbow Egg | 45 |

Our general advice is to play first. The description names four free ways to grow: training, levels, pets and Rebirth. A paid multiplier is easier to judge once you have seen how fast those move on their own. [How to play](/stone-skipping/how-to-play/) covers the free loop.

## What can these guides not tell you?

Effects. We do not know what multiplier a Skill tier gives, how long a potion lasts, what the odds in an egg are, or how a Training Zone differs from normal training. None of that is shown in public data. The eggs and boosts guides list their open questions, and we will fill them in after an in-game check.

## Scope

Prices change when the developer edits the store. Every figure here is a snapshot from 2 October 2026; check the in-game price before you buy.
