Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, what data is collected when you visit beastofreincarnation.online, which third-party services we use for analytics and advertising, what your choices are, and how to contact us.

Effective date: July 22, 2026 · Site operator contact: zsn2740784715@gmail.com

1. Who we are

beastofreincarnation.online ("this site", "we") is an independent, fan-operated guide website about the video game Beast of Reincarnation. It is curated by an independent games-content creator (see About). We do not require accounts, do not offer purchases, and do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is described below.

2. Data we collect directly

  • Nothing you must provide: the site has no registration, login, comment or newsletter forms.
  • Email: if you choose to email us, we receive your address and message content, used solely to reply. We do not add you to any list or share your address.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) may process standard technical logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) to deliver the site securely.

3. Analytics services

We use analytics to understand which guides help players, in aggregate. These services set cookies or use similar technologies:

ServiceProviderWhat it does
Google Analytics 4Google LLCAggregated statistics: pages visited, country-level location, device type, referral source. IP addresses are not logged or stored by GA4.
Microsoft ClarityMicrosoft CorporationAnonymized usage patterns (scrolling, clicks, session replay with content masking) that help us fix usability problems. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Advertising

This site is funded by display advertising.

  • Google AdSense: we use, or intend to use, Google AdSense to serve ads. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies (including, where you consent, for personalized advertising) based on your visits to this and other websites. You can control ad personalization at Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Third-party vendors may also serve ads; you can opt out of many personalized-advertising cookies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Adsterra: we also use Adsterra, which serves the native display unit near the foot of each guide page. Adsterra and its demand partners may set cookies or use similar identifiers to select, deliver and measure ads. Its handling of that data is covered by its own privacy policy. Ad slots may sit empty while ad fill is pending.
  • Third-party advertising vendors: Google AdSense and Adsterra are the only third-party advertising networks used on this site. Their certified partners may serve ads through them and set their own cookies, governed by their own privacy policies. If we add any further advertising network, it will be named on this page before it goes live.

5. Consent (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

If you visit from a region where consent is required, a consent banner (Google-certified consent management platform) asks for your choice before personalized-advertising cookies are used. You can decline personalized ads and still use the entire site; ads will then be non-personalized. You can change your choice at any time by clearing cookies for this site.

6. Your rights

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold (in practice: email correspondence, as we hold no user accounts). Contact us at the address below.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell personal information. Ad-personalization choices can be exercised via the links in section 4.
  • Do Not Track / opt-outs: analytics can be blocked with standard browser tools and extensions; the site functions fully with them enabled.

7. Children

This site publishes game guides suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention

Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry. Analytics data is retained per the default retention settings of GA4 and Clarity. We hold no other personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we add services that change how data is handled (for example, activating an additional ad network), we will update this page and its effective date before or at the time the change goes live.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: zsn2740784715@gmail.com (see also our Contact page).
