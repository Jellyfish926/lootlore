3:T1ee5,<p>Two different things in Sephiria get called "combining," and conflating them is the single most common way to misread a build guide.</p>
<p><strong>Combos are artifact tags, counted.</strong> Placement is irrelevant. If you hold two #Ember <a href="/artifacts/artifacts-and-tablets/">artifacts</a> anywhere in your bag, the Ember combo is active.</p>
<p><strong>Grid combination is <a href="/tablets/all-tablets/">tablet</a> buffs, summed per cell.</strong> Counting is irrelevant. It is pure geometry.</p>
<p>This page is about the first one. The second lives on the <a href="/guides/inventory-grid/">inventory grid</a> page.</p>

<h2>How combos activate</h2>
<p>A combo <em>"provides additional stats based on the number of artifacts"</em> carrying that tag, and most artifacts in the game now carry one. It fires at a count threshold and then levels up as you add more — combo levels reach at least 10.</p>
<p>Thresholds observed in a real build:</p>
<div class="tw"><table><thead><tr><th>Combo</th><th>Items needed</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Guardian</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>Shadow</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>Magitech</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>Ember</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>Glacier</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>Elemental</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>Lake</td><td>3</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>Most sit at 2. That is a low bar, and it is the key to how the system rewards you.</p>
<h2>The combo list</h2>
<p>Names confirmed in TEAM HORAY's own text: <strong>Planet, Storm Cloud, Mystic (Mystery), Magitech, Lake, Academy, Solar Blade, Shadow, Ember, Glacier, Elemental, Curse, Negotiation, Companion, Sturdy, Precision, Windsong, Protection, Alchemy.</strong> Player guides add <strong>Guardian</strong>.</p>
<p>Four of them have published definitions:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Elemental</strong> — a support combo that enhances your highest elemental attribute.</li><li><strong>Curse</strong> — a support combo built around debuffs.</li><li><strong>Negotiation</strong> — a support combo built around leaf acquisition.</li><li><strong>Lake</strong> — enhances maximum MP and MP-using abilities.</li><li><strong>Academy</strong> — support artifacts for using spellbooks more effectively.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Mystic</strong> deserves its own note because it is the one that touches the grid. In its original form it simply increased Max MP. The October 2025 revamp changed it to <em>"a support combo that doubles the level of a random artifact,"</em> and by 1.0 the community tooling models it as doubling the <strong>net</strong> value of up to <strong>three chosen cells</strong>, negatives included. Those two descriptions — random versus chosen — have never been publicly reconciled, so treat the count of three as the confident part and the selection method as open.</p>
<h2>Why breadth beats depth here</h2>
<p>The <strong>Ingenuity</strong> talent at level 20 grants <strong>+7% Universal Damage Boost per active combo type</strong> (raised from 4%). Per <em>type</em>, not per artifact.</p>
<p>Run the arithmetic. Six combos active at their minimum thresholds is +42% universal damage. Getting six combos to threshold means roughly twelve tagged artifacts, which is a normal mid-run bag. Getting one combo to level 10 means ten-plus artifacts of a single tag, which is a lucky run.</p>
<p>So the advice inverts depending on where you are:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Early, without Ingenuity 20</strong>: go deep. Two or three tags at real depth, because the individual combo bonuses are what you are actually buying.</li><li><strong>Late, with Ingenuity 20</strong>: go wide. Every additional tag at threshold is another 7% on everything.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Fruit Skewer</strong> is the lever for this. It lets you bias which combos' items appear before the run starts — up to six modifiers, eight with the Farmer Squirrel costume. If you are building toward Ingenuity breadth, that is where you set it up.</p>
<h2>Bond Artifacts</h2>
<p>Bond Artifacts are unique items that <strong>connect two combos</strong> at once — a direct answer to the breadth problem. Their drop rule was deliberately loosened: they now enter the drop table when <strong>one</strong> of the two related combos is active, where originally both had to be. Roughly a dozen existed at introduction with five more added later. The Destiny Inscription has a <em>Bond Artifact</em> node that unlocks 23 of them into your pool.</p>
<h2>Wisdom level 10, correctly</h2>
<p>This is the most-misquoted mechanic in the game, so here is the record.</p>
<p>Before patch 0.10.14, Wisdom 10 read: all artifacts are forced to have a unique effect, duplicates combine and raise the artifact's level by 1, and you gain 1 die on boss kill.</p>
<p>After 0.10.14, the same text <strong>plus</strong> <em>"The combined Artifact increases the combo by 2 times."</em></p>
<p>Two important qualifications, both from TEAM HORAY:</p>
<ol><li>In 0.10.16 the developer stated the doubling was <em>"originally planned to be applied to combo-change artifacts as well, but it has been put on hold due to technical issues"</em> — so <strong>Chalcedony Key and White Paper do not get it</strong>, and the UI had been wrongly displaying that they did.</li><li>The current in-game framing is <em>[Unique] Stacking</em>: duplicates are offered and taking one adds a star to the copy you hold.</li></ol>
<p>The shorthand "Wisdom lv10 = +2 combo" that circulates on auto-generated wikis is close enough to be seductive and wrong enough to mislead. It is not a flat +2 to a combo counter.</p>
<h2>Practical rules</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Two of a tag is usually enough to switch it on.</strong> Do not pass on a useful artifact because it "does not match" — check whether its tag is one item away from a threshold.</li><li><strong>Chalcedony Key changes tag based on where you place it</strong> (Sturdy, Ember, Glacier or Magitech). It is a free threshold-filler for whichever combo you are one short on.</li><li><strong>Alchemy has a Side Bag gotcha.</strong> In 1.0.23 TEAM HORAY fixed a case where the Alchemy combo failed to trigger when a tablet was obtained straight into the Side Bag. Items in the Side Bag are inactive; that is a rule, not a bug.</li><li><strong>Combos change how items drop.</strong> Bond Artifacts key off active combos, and Fruit Skewer biases appearance rates. Your current tags shape your future offers.</li></ul>
<h2>Not documented anywhere reliable</h2>
<p>A full artifact → combo tag mapping, and the activation thresholds for most combos, do not exist in any primary source. The seven thresholds above come from a single player's build guide. Combo level scaling — what level 4 grants versus level 10 — is likewise unpublished. If you find a site listing all of it in a neat table, check whether it can tell you where the numbers came from.</p>
<h2>Checked against 1.0.30</h2>
