# How to Play Southern Mudding on Roblox: First Session

To play Southern Mudding, join a server, spawn a vehicle and drive it into the mud. From there the game is an open sandbox: hook up a trailer, haul or tow something, claim a house, and tune your truck in the customization garage. There is no stated win condition; the developer describes off-roading, playing with friends, roleplay and customizing.

That summary comes from the [official Roblox description](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=8719555347) and the game's four badges. The plan further down is our advice, and we say where it rests on something we have not checked in game.

## Which steps do the badges mark?

Southern Mudding has four badges, and they read like a checklist for a first visit. The totals show how far most players get:

| Step | Badge | Official requirement | Per 100 Welcome badges |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Welcome to the game! | You played the game! | 100 |
| 2 | Spawned a Vehicle | Spawn a vehicle to obtain this badge. | 82 |
| 3 | Spawned a Trailer | Spawn a trailer to obtain this badge. | 49 |
| 4 | Claimed a House | Claim a house to obtain this badge. | 9 |

The last column is our arithmetic on Roblox's award totals from 11:19 UTC on 2 October 2026. It compares badge counts, not one tracked group of players. Still, the drop is clear: there is roughly one trailer badge for every two Welcome badges, and fewer than one house badge for every ten. The [badge guide](/southern-mudding/badges/) has the full numbers.

## What should you do in your first session?

Our suggested order. Steps 2 to 5 follow the badges; the reasoning is ours.

1. **Join the Roblox group first.** The group description reads "Join the group for a free pickup truck!". We could not confirm which truck it is or whether you must rejoin the server to receive it, so do this before you load in. See [group, codes and Discord](/southern-mudding/community/).
2. **Spawn a vehicle.** That earns Spawned a Vehicle. The description lists pickup trucks first, and the 25 September update was aimed at them, so a pickup is a reasonable first choice.
3. **Drive off the road.** The map is "purpose built for the off-road". Find mud and learn how your truck behaves before you add a load.
4. **Spawn a trailer and hook it up.** That earns Spawned a Trailer. How trailers attach is not described in public data, so expect to experiment.
5. **Claim a house.** That earns Claimed a House, the rarest of the four. Where the houses are is not in public data either.
6. **Visit the customization garage.** The 25 September update says nitrous for pickup trucks is added there.

## How many vehicles can you have out at once?

Two vehicles and two trailers, going by the pass descriptions: Spawn 4 Vehicles "Increases vehicle spawn limit from 2 to 4!" and Spawn 4 Trailers "Increases trailer spawn limit from 2 to 4!". If those descriptions are current, a new player can run a truck and a second vehicle, each with a trailer, without paying. The [spawning guide](/southern-mudding/spawning/) covers the four passes that change those limits.

## Do you need Robux to enjoy Southern Mudding?

Not as far as the public data shows. Nothing in the description puts the core loop behind a purchase, and none of the four badge requirements mentions one. Passes add vehicle packs, more spawn slots and remote tools; none is described as required. If you want one purchase early, the cheapest are the two 80-Robux customization passes, but read the [game pass guide](/southern-mudding/gamepasses/) first, because several gift versions cost less than the pass itself.
