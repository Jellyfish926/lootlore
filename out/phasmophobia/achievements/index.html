    All 54 Phasmophobia Achievements and Unlock Rates

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-10 · Checked on version 0.19.1.0

    Scope: Names, requirement text and global unlock rates copied from Steam's public achievements page on October 10, 2026 (UTC); the six achievements with no visible text are not explained beyond Steam's own internal IDs, and rates are a snapshot that moves daily

    Key points

    • Phasmophobia has 54 Steam achievements; the most common is Work Experience (59.8% of players) and the rarest are Silver Hunter and Gold Hunter (1.3% each).
    • Twenty-four are Discovered achievements, one per ghost type from Shade (22.5%) down to Deogen (16.8%); newer ghosts such as the Deildegast have none.
    • Six achievements show no requirement text on Steam: Work Experience, They're here, The Bait, Flawless Execution, Escape Artist and Doom Slayed.
    • Since v0.19.0.0, custom games with a 0 multiplier no longer count towards achievements.

    Phasmophobia has 54 Steam achievements. The most common, Work Experience, is held by 59.8% of players; the rarest, Silver Hunter and Gold Hunter, by 1.3% each. Twenty-four are Discovered achievements for identifying a ghost type, and the rest cover contracts, spending, equipment tiers, prestige, weekly challenges and the Apocalypse trophies. Rates were read on October 10, 2026.

    Do custom games and events count towards Phasmophobia achievements?

    In Phasmophobia, custom games with a 0 multiplier do not count. The v0.19.0.0 notes from August 2026 say: "Custom Difficulties with a 0 multiplier will no longer contribute towards any progress, such as achievements and leaflets." The next patch then fixed "an issue that caused several achievements to complete despite the player being on a custom difficulty with a multiplier of 0". (Official-site wording. The Steam post for the same patch words it "prevented several achievements completing and being awarded despite…"; either way the fix is that 0x games should not award them.) The notes single out 0-multiplier games only, which is what you get when you pick the ghost type yourself; they say nothing against custom games that still pay out.

    Phasmophobia's seasonal events have no Steam achievements of their own. None of the 54 names an event; event rewards are in-game ID cards, trophies and cosmetics, covered on the events page.

    Do any achievements unlock in-game rewards?

    One link is documented. The November 2025 Phasmophobia patch notes added the Moneybags ID Card & Badge and say "This'll unlock once the player has earned the 'Break the Bank' achievement." Players who already had the achievement received the card after their first contract on the new version.

    The Player Character Update notes also mention "Unique shirts for prestige and event specific achievements". They do not list which shirts go with which milestone, so we do not either.

    Which Phasmophobia achievements should you go for first?

    A suggested order, based only on the unlock rates above:

    1. The spending and contract ladders come with normal play: Chump Change (41.6%), Rookie (31.1%) and Bare Essentials (25.9%) are among the most common.
    2. Run Training once for No More Training Wheels. It is a single session, and more than 93% of players do not have it.
    3. Work through the Discovered list by playing default difficulties and staying alive after you guess.
    4. Start the weekly challenge early. Challenger Approaching needs one completion; Taking All Challenges needs ten and sits at 1.7%.
    5. Leave the Apocalypse trio for last. At 1.3% to 1.5% they are the rarest achievements in Phasmophobia.
