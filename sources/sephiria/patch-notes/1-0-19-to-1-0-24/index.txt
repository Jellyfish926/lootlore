3:T241e,<p>Sephiria left Early Access on <strong>31 July 2026</strong> and shipped four patches in its first eleven days. This page is the record of that launch window — 1.0.19 through 1.0.24. The patches that followed in mid-August, including the first real weapon-rule changes of the 1.0 era, live on the <a href="/patch-notes/1-0-25-to-1-0-30/">1.0.25 to 1.0.30 page</a>. Before the list, one piece of housekeeping that confuses everyone who goes looking:</p>

<h2>There is no 1.0.20</h2>
<p>The published sequence is <strong>1.0 Update → 1.0.21 Hotfix → 1.0.22 → 1.0.23 → 1.0.24</strong>. No announcement for 1.0.20 exists.</p>
<p>Two further wrinkles:</p>
<ul><li>The announcement titled <strong>"1.0 Update"</strong> carries the <strong>full 1.0.19 patch notes</strong> inside it. That is where all the substantive launch balance lives.</li><li><strong>1.0.21 contains a section titled "Additional Changes Missing from the 1.0.19 Patch Notes"</strong> — real balance changes documented retroactively.</li><li><strong>1.0.25 is referenced inside 1.0.24</strong> as fixing a Training Grounds freeze, but its own notes never appeared in the feed.</li></ul>
<p>If you have been searching for "Sephiria 1.0.20 patch notes" and finding pages that confidently list changes, those pages invented them.</p>
<h2>1.0.19 — the launch balance patch</h2>
<p>Published inside the 1.0 announcement, 31 July.</p>
<p><strong>New content:</strong> 19 Artifacts, 7 Tablets, 1 Combo, 3 Miracles, 12 Potions, 1 Costume, and weapon upgrades — 1 to Great Sword, 4 to Dagger, 4 to Crossbow, 4 to Staff. None to Sword &amp; Shield or Blade.</p>
<p><strong>Chapter 6</strong> arrived with 2 <a href="/bosses/chapters-and-bosses/">bosses</a>, 1 mini-boss, 3 BGM tracks, 3 achievements, and the game's ending and credits.</p>
<p><strong>New systems:</strong></p>
<ul><li><strong>Side Bag</strong> — storage for spare items, unlocked through Destiny Inscription. <strong>Items in it are inactive.</strong></li><li><strong>Mystic Pot reworked</strong> — now converts 1 artifact into a different artifact of the same rarity, or 2 artifacts into one of higher rarity. It previously turned artifacts into tablets.</li><li><strong>Hard Mode expanded</strong> from 30 to <strong>60 tiers</strong>, with six new elements: Festival of Blood, False Vanguard, Deterioration, Tension, Heavy Heart and Proliferation.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Tablet balance:</strong> Nurture and Trick both Advanced → Common; Competition +2 → +3 downward; Wave +2 → +3 on the upper-right diagonal; Preparation +1 → +2 lower-right; <strong>Daydream</strong> reworked to +2 on each of the four diagonals.</p>
<p><strong>Artifact balance:</strong> Frozen Egg now benefits from follower-damage effects; <strong>Eternal Winter</strong>'s physical coefficient cut hard from 72/130/230 to 40/75/125 and rebased on 50% of Cold Damage.</p>
<p><strong>Weapon balance, selected:</strong></p>
<ul><li><strong>Mischievous Prank</strong> reworked from a stat trade into "Special Attack becomes Leaf Explosion; Normal Attack damage is added to Special Attack damage"</li><li><strong>Loving Gaze</strong> — Whirlwind crit chance +50% → +100%, special attack speed 50% faster</li><li><strong>Harsh Trial</strong> — Trance chance 20% → 30%, max stacks 2 → 4</li><li><strong>Lightning Greatsword "S3G"</strong> — Electrocution extra damage 20% → 33%</li><li><strong>Lightning Dagger</strong> — extra damage 20% → 25% of Lightning Damage</li><li><strong>Heidi</strong> — Overheat gain on Sheath 2 → 3, cap 6 → 9</li><li><strong>Adonis</strong> — crit damage "increased by 30%" → "<strong>amplified</strong> by 30%" (amplification sums rather than multiplies — a nerf disguised as a wording change)</li><li><strong>Academy Enchanted Sword</strong> — Parry MP restore 22% → 30%</li></ul>
<p><strong>Renamed stat:</strong> "Special Attack Cost Reduction" became "Special Attack MP Cost Reduction."</p>
<p><strong>UI:</strong> tablets can now be rotated with <strong>middle mouse click</strong>.</p>
<h2>1.0.21 Hotfix — 31 July</h2>
<ul><li>Credits viewable from the title screen after completing the game</li><li><strong>Spinning Staff Demon</strong> — attack warning occasionally stayed on screen</li><li><strong>Guillotine of Bitter Cold</strong> — the UI mislabelled its skill as "Blizzard Hammer"</li><li><strong>Hard Mode "Blood Festival"</strong> — healing effectiveness now scales inversely with party size: <strong>66% at 2 players, 50% at 3, 33% at 4</strong></li><li>Final boss phase 1 radial bullet spread reduced; phase 2 "Pillar Summoner" minion HP −33%</li><li>Hard Mode was being incorrectly marked as cleared after a Game Over</li><li>Ultrawide fix: hovering "Reroll" selected the Auxiliary Bag icon instead</li></ul>
<p><strong>Retroactive 1.0.19 changes documented here:</strong></p>
<ul><li><strong>Sword &amp; Shield Cleave base cost 20 MP → 15</strong></li><li><strong>Guard frames now active from the very beginning of the Cleave animation</strong></li></ul>
<p>Those two are the reason Sword &amp; Shield plays better at 1.0 than most guides written during Early Access suggest.</p>
<h2>1.0.22 — 1 August</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Breath of Ice</strong> (Sword &amp; Shield) was granting +30% dash attack damage instead of the stated +40%</li><li><strong>Droplet of Plitvice</strong> could rarely crash the game when dropped while in use</li></ul>
<h2>1.0.23 — 4 August</h2>
<p><strong>Stages</strong></p>
<ul><li>Merchants that can fight now return to their original position after leaving it</li><li>Suspicious Hat Merchant — adjusted how quickly it starts to flee</li></ul>
<p><strong>Boss: Qliphoth</strong></p>
<ul><li>Projectiles could enter the safe zone in certain situations</li></ul>
<p><strong>Items and <a href="/weapons/all-weapons/">weapons</a></strong></p>
<ul><li><strong>Solis Iubar</strong> — a different special attack triggered at 150 MP or higher</li><li><strong>Minigun</strong> — MP cost now applies correctly in decimal units below 1</li><li><strong>Scabbard of Exorcism</strong> — projectile direction occasionally wrong in multiplayer</li><li><strong>Golden Needle of the North</strong> — could occasionally freeze the game</li><li><strong>Alchemy Combo</strong> — did not trigger when a Tablet was obtained into the Side Bag</li><li>Costume/Talent starting items were kept after being placed in the Side Bag and swapping</li></ul>
<p>The carrot emoji (🥕) in TEAM HORAY's notes marks changes made from community feedback.</p>
<h2>1.0.24 — 7 August</h2>
<ul><li>Appearance variants added to <strong>7 costumes</strong></li><li>Potions now drop separately instead of stacking when dropping several</li><li><strong>Mischievous Prank</strong> — special attack unusable at 40 MP or lower</li><li><strong>Halberd</strong> — attack range not visible in multiplayer</li><li><strong>Absolute Ring</strong> — effect not visible in multiplayer</li><li>The mouse cursor can now leave the game window while navigating UI</li><li>Holding the attack key before a cutscene caused attacks to continue during it</li><li><strong>Collin's</strong> dialogue disabled once he becomes a companion</li><li>Co-op: mismatched-progress invites, and a Chapter 5 player being softlocked by clearing the game in a Chapter 6 lobby</li><li>Steam Profile Rich Presence support</li><li>Unity engine bumped to <strong>6000.3.21f1</strong></li></ul>
<h2>What this means for your build</h2>
<p>Three changes are worth acting on:</p>
<p><strong>Sword &amp; Shield got quietly better.</strong> Cleave at 15 MP with guard frames from frame one is a different ability from the 20 MP version most Early Access guides describe.</p>
<p><strong>Nothing about artifacts or tablets has changed since 1.0.19.</strong> All four subsequent patches contain zero numeric artifact or tablet balance. Any layout advice written after 31 July is still current.</p>
<p><strong>Multiplayer got most of the attention.</strong> Weapon effect errors, Halberd range, Absolute Ring visibility, Scabbard of Exorcism direction, chapter-mismatch softlocks — four consecutive patches have fixed co-op-specific bugs, which suggests it is where the remaining rough edges are.</p>
<h2>The pattern so far</h2>
<p>Two content-free days between 1.0.22 and 1.0.23, three between 1.0.23 and 1.0.24. TEAM HORAY is shipping small, fast, feedback-driven fixes rather than batching them, and marking community-requested changes explicitly. For a studio that spent fifteen months in Early Access before launching, that cadence is consistent with how they ran the beta.</p>
