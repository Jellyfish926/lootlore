    About this guide

    The game changed a lot in 2026. Two houses were reworked, characters became customisable, the developer apologised for one update and spent the summer on fixes, and the 1.0 release slipped a year. Each guide here quotes the official post it relies on and gives the date, so you can tell a current rule from an old one.

    What is coming next?

    In the short term, the Crimson Eye event is announced for October 8 to November 1, with a Double XP week from October 15 to 22. After that the developer's notes point to November for the move to Unity 6.

    Further out, the June 2026 roadmap lists a second Player Character update, a 42 Edgefield Road rework, Winter's Jest and one more content update before the year ends. Version 1.0 and the Nintendo Switch 2 release are planned for the second half of 2027. The roadmap page sets the January and June plans side by side, and the events page explains goals, trophies and Twitch Drops.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does Phasmophobia cost?
    Steam's US store listed it at $19.99 on October 10, 2026, with no discount running. Console store prices were not checked. Specs and platforms are on the platforms and price page.
    When is Phasmophobia 1.0 coming out?
    The developer's June 24, 2026 post moved 1.0 to the second half of 2027, with the Nintendo Switch 2 version at the same time. The Steam store page still says 2026; the newer post wins. Details are on the roadmap page.
    What is the latest Phasmophobia update?
    v0.19.1.0, posted on Steam on October 7, 2026. It prepares the Crimson Eye event and adds a Seasonal Event toggle. The full 2026 timeline is in the patch notes.
    When is Crimson Eye 2026?
    It is announced for October 8 to November 1, 2026 on eight locations, with a Twitch Drop until October 15 and Double XP from October 15 to 22. See the Crimson Eye page.
    Does Phasmophobia have crossplay?
    Yes, optional crossplay between PC, PlayStation 5, PS VR2 and Xbox Series X/S, according to the developer's posts. Updates ship on all platforms together. More on the platforms page.
    How many achievements does Phasmophobia have?
    54 on Steam. The most common is Work Experience at 59.8% of players and the rarest are Silver Hunter and Gold Hunter at 1.3%, read on October 10, 2026. The full table is on the achievements page.
    Does Phasmophobia have codes?
    We found no redeem-code system for in-game rewards: the word redeem appears in none of the 215 official Steam announcements or on the store page. The one discount code the developer posted, in November 2025, was for a physical music box. Cosmetics are unlocked in game, bought with in-game currency, or earned through events and Twitch Drops, explained on the events page.
