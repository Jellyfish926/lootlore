    Valheim Deep North Guides: 1.0 Endgame

    Author Jellyfi · Published 2026-09-17 · Last checked 2026-09-17 · Checked on version 1.0.12

    Language: English中文

    Key points

    • Three guides for the 1.0 Deep North: the progression route, the Intricate Key, and Kall Fimbulbringer.
    • Read the route first, the key page when you hit that wall, then prepare for the final fight.
    • The order exists so you stop bouncing between moulds and finished castings.

    Read the overall route first, then the Intricate Key page when you hit that wall, then prepare for Kall. The three guides cover progression order, the key item and the final fight, so you stop bouncing between moulds and finished castings.

    Pick a guide by problem

    • Valheim Deep North: Eternal Pyre to Endgame

      The 1.0 Deep North order of operations, linking Embers, Petrified Tissue, Bloodgold, moulds, the Intricate Key and the invasions into one workable route.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Intricate Key: Mould to Mörkhalla

      Craft the Deep North's single-use key step by step: mould versus cast, the workstations each stage needs, and why you should plan for more than one key.

      v1.0.12

    • Valheim Kall Fimbulbringer: Three-Phase Plan

      A framework for the three-phase final boss: direct attacks, an invulnerable ice block with seven summoned boss avatars, then a stronger elemental return.

      v1.0.12

    Scope

    These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
