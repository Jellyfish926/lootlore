# Valheim Biome Progression Guides

Each biome page covers how to get a foothold after your first entry, which resources to look for first, and when to start preparing for the boss. If the logistics are already done and you only want the boss fight, go to the boss section instead.

## Pick a guide by problem


![A Viking warrior raising a sword against a sword-wielding skeleton among the dead trees of a swamp](ss17 "Dead trees and standing water: each biome page starts from getting a foothold")
### [Valheim Bronze Age: Copper, Tin, Gear](/valheim/bronze/)

From entering the Black Forest to running a working bronze production line, with a sensible order for mining, Surtling cores, fuel, tools and gear.

### [Swamp progression guide](/valheim/swamp/)

Once you have the swamp key, work through preparation, landing, finding crypts, mining iron and hauling it home, so your first Swamp trip is not a chain of corpse runs.

### [Mountains progression guide](/valheim/mountains/)

From your first safe climb to silver and dragon eggs, breaking down routes, supplies, ranged answers and how to get back down.

### [Plains progression guide](/valheim/plains/)

Enter the Plains in order: scout the edges, fight small groups, take camp resources, then set up dedicated fields for the food and gear upgrades this stage needs.

### [Mistlands progression guide](/valheim/mistlands/)

Breaks down the exploration and production prerequisites of the Mistlands, why you protect your exit, why you approach the Dvergr carefully, and why infested mines come first.

### [Ashlands progression guide](/valheim/ashlands/)

Finish the post-Queen sailing prerequisites first, then handle landing, shields, fortress exploration and hauling resources home in stages, instead of betting a whole expedition on one beach.

## Scope

These guides assume vanilla standard survival. World modifiers, mods and later patches can change how things behave. Every page keeps its source links and check date, and combat plans are first-playthrough advice, not the only solution.
