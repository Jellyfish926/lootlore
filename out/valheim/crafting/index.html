    Valheim Crafting & Repair Guide

    Scope: Vanilla standard survival; world modifiers, mods and later patches can change the rules

    A recipe that will not appear is not always a material shortage. Crafting in Valheim involves material discovery, station type and station level. Break "what I want to make" into three questions: which station makes it, has my character touched the materials it needs, and does that station meet its conditions? Answer those before you go out for more resources.

    You just unlocked a material but see no gear

    After picking up something new, watch for the unlock prompt, then open the matching crafting menu. Some materials feed an intermediate product first, and that product unlocks the gear. Getting ore does not mean the Forge instantly lists every metal item. The full set of crafting types is on the Crafting page.

    In a group, a material a teammate picked up is not a material you have checked against your own recipes. Lay new materials out at base so whoever does the crafting can actually touch them and read the prompts, instead of guessing back and forth in chat.

    Ore to gear takes more than one machine

    The basic chain is raw material, fuel, smelting, forging, then later upgrades. A Smelter needs stone and Surtling cores to build, and a Forge needs copper among other materials — see Smelter and Forge. Fill in whichever step you are missing; you do not need to stockpile an entire age of ore first.

    Keep inputs and outputs in separate storage in the production area. Raw ore and finished bars in the same junk chest is what creates the "I clearly have the material and still cannot make it" feeling. Check fuel and actual output once more before you leave the area.

    When the station level is too low

    Upgrades usually depend on the matching attachment being placed where it counts. After you build one, check whether the station level actually changed; if it did not, look at distance, space and whether it is the right type of attachment. Not everything that looks like furniture is an upgrade piece.

    Also keep crafting a new item and upgrading an existing one apart. The wrong tab or the wrong target item makes the interface behave as if you are short on materials. Confirming by name and quality one item at a time beats hauling every raw material onto your character.

    Make repairing a habit

    Check repairs at the right station as soon as you get home, rather than working out the rules when a weapon breaks in a crypt. Repairing and re-crafting are different things, and repairing does not necessarily cost another set of recipe materials; whether an item can be repaired at all depends on its station and conditions.

    Before an expedition, look over your main weapon, shield, tools and spares one by one. Repairing the axe and forgetting the pickaxe is easy to do on a mining run. If an outpost cannot repair what you are carrying, treat it as a waypoint rather than a full base.

    One general troubleshooting order

    Check the station, then the recipe tab, then whether the materials are actually in an inventory you can use, then level and placement conditions. If it is still wrong, note the item name, the interface prompt and your mod situation, and handle it as a fault. Do not delete a save without a backup to solve what may just be a placement distance.
