# +1 Stone Skipping Game Passes: All 11 with Robux Prices

+1 Stone Skipping has 11 game passes, all on sale on 2 October 2026. The cheapest are Auto Wins and Hatch +3 Eggs [STACKS] at 25 Robux each; the most expensive is Admin Training Zone at 599 Robux. Buying all 11 costs 2,353 Robux. None has an official description, and none was discounted.

Roblox's documentation says passes "let you charge users a one-time Robux fee", so each of these is a one-time purchase.

## What does each pass do?

The developer has not written it down, so this table gives our reading of each name and icon. Treat the last column as likely, not confirmed.

| Group | Passes | Total Robux | Likely meaning, from name and icon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Automation | Auto Wins, Auto Rebirth | 174 | Collects Wins or rebirths for you; the icons are a trophy and two circling arrows |
| Training zones | Koi, Golden, Admin Training Zone | 947 | Access to a special training lane; the Koi icon shows a strip of water with cherry trees, a red gate and a platform; the Admin and Golden icons show a green and a gold lane with a platform |

| Hatch passes | Hatch +3, +8, +16 Eggs [STACKS] | 469 | Hatch more eggs at once; "[STACKS]" suggests the three add together |

How much faster a training zone is, how many pets you can equip without a pass, and whether Auto Wins works while you are away are all not shown in public data. The [how to play guide](/stone-skipping/how-to-play/) covers what the description does confirm.

## Why do two Training Zones show a second price?

The developer product list holds two older entries with the same names as passes. Both were created on 13 September, one day before the passes, and both still have a plain grey placeholder icon. As a pass, Admin Training Zone costs 599 Robux; as a product it is listed at 195. Golden Training Zone is 249 Robux as a pass and 315 as a product.

Two more products from the same day are named Golden Zone (115 Robux) and Admin Zone (289 Robux). We cannot tell from public data whether any of these four is still offered inside the game. If a purchase prompt shows a price that differs from the pass price above, read the prompt carefully before you confirm. All four sit in the [full product list](/stone-skipping/shop/).

## When were the passes added?

In four waves, going by creation dates in UTC. Six passes were created on 14 September: Admin Training Zone, Golden Training Zone, the three Hatch passes and Auto Wins. +3 Pets followed on 15 September and Auto Rebirth on 19 September. Koi Training Zone, +1 Pet and +6 Pets were created on 29 September.

Six passes show an edit on 29 or 30 September: all three Training Zones and all three pet passes. The API does not keep old prices, so we cannot say what changed. The [updates page](/stone-skipping/updates/) puts these dates next to the eggs and the World 5 event.

## Which pass should you buy first?

We cannot rank +1 Stone Skipping passes by effect when no effect is published. What we can say from prices alone:

- The two 25-Robux passes are the cheapest way to see what a pass does in this game.
- In the pet family, +3 Pets has the best rate; +1 Pet is the smallest spend.
- Koi Training Zone costs a sixth of Admin Training Zone. Nothing official says how they differ.
- Paid Skill and Wins multipliers are developer products, not passes; see the [Skill and Wins boosts guide](/stone-skipping/boosts/).

That is our view, not an official recommendation. Check the in-game price before you buy, because prices can change at any time.
