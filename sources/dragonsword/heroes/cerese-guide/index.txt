3:T1a29,<p><em>Game version checked: 1.0.10 (19 August 2026 patch). This page is assembled only from what other pages on this site already document about Cerese — no new claims.</em></p>
<p>Cerese is the hero whose quest unlocks a whole game mode, and the hero this site's own records cannot agree about. Both facts are worth knowing before you spend an invitation on her, and neither is obvious from a roster row.</p>

<h2>She is the gate on Tower of Trials</h2>
<p>Our <a href="/guides/gear-runes-and-karma/">gear, runes and Karma guide</a> records the Tower of Trials as unlocking <strong>in Chapter 3, after talking to Cerese and completing her quest</strong>. That makes her the only hero on the roster whose personal content stands between you and an endgame mode — three towers of thirty floors each, per the same page, and the source of the Tower shards that exchange for the Whirling Thought an Exclusive Karma recipe needs.</p>
<p>Read that as a scheduling fact rather than a power fact. You do not need Cerese <em>built</em> to open the tower; you need her quest cleared. If your Karma progression has stalled because you have no Whirling Thought, this is the door you have not opened yet.</p>
<h2>Her actual job in a team is positional, not elemental</h2>
<p>The one lineup this site documents as tested puts her in the opening slot. Our <a href="/guides/best-team-comps/">best team comps guide</a> describes <strong>Cerese / Othello / Dana</strong> as the safest team for learning an unfamiliar boss, with Cerese and Dana as the two ranged heroes who keep the arena visible and Othello stepping in only for the close-range transition. The rotation starts with whichever of the two ranged heroes has the better position and the stronger current build, which means Cerese is not locked into opening — she shares that role with Dana rather than owning it.</p>
<p>The same page keeps her in the Dana-replacement variant too: <strong>Cerese / Othello / Reina</strong>, where she holds the ranged field role unchanged while Reina takes over part of Dana's rhythm with ground movement instead. Across both lineups her slot is the constant. That is a stronger recommendation than a tier grade, because it survives a roster change.</p>
<p>Build order in that comp follows from the rotation: the ranged hero who starts most encounters and stays active between Othello's entries is built first. If Cerese is the better-positioned opener on your save, she is your first investment in that team.</p>
<p>One more mention, softer: our <a href="/guides/bosses/">bosses page</a> records Cerese's Ice Storm being named for control against Hagen the Dark Necromancer — but explicitly as reader comments on a guide rather than the author's tested method. We are repeating the attribution along with the tip.</p>
<h2>The ailment question this site has not settled</h2>
<p>Here is the conflict, stated plainly rather than resolved. Our <a href="/heroes/all-heroes/">roster page</a> lists Cerese's applied ailment as <strong>"magic."</strong> The <a href="/guides/status-ailments-and-signals/">combat guide</a> attests nine ailments individually — Stun, Knockdown, Bleed, Burn, Shock, Freeze, Poison, Airborne, Break — and "magic" is not among them. Our <a href="/heroes/ailment-coverage/">ailment coverage page</a> therefore counts Cerese among the heroes carrying <strong>no recorded ailment at all</strong>.</p>
<p>Both readings are live on this site and neither has a first-party source behind it, because Hound13 has never published a hero-by-ailment chart. <strong>Unconfirmed: two conflicting records stand side by side.</strong> Either "magic" is a real ailment the combat guide's list of nine is missing, or it is descriptive text about her arcane-device kit that got filed in an ailment column. We are not picking.</p>
<p>The practical consequence matters more than the resolution. The comps guide describes her as feeding Othello's detonation windows as a fast Frost applicator — a <em>function</em>, not a labelled ailment. If you are building a chain around her, read her in-game skill text for the trigger rather than trusting any column on any site, including ours.</p>
<h2>Rankings, quest data and patches</h2>
<p>The two rankings our <a href="/heroes/tier-list/">tier list</a> tracks split hard on her: <strong>S on Pro Game Guides, B on games.gg.</strong> That page also flags the spelling trap — Cerese and Seris are the same hero, so a list that appears to rank both is ranking one hero twice. Pro Game Guides names her as one of two first picks for a beginner who wants damage with built-in control.</p>
<p>She is one of only five heroes with a Hero Quest achievement, per our <a href="/guides/achievements/">achievements page</a>, and hers has the <strong>highest global unlock rate of the five at 15.4%</strong> — consistent with a quest most players clear because the tower sits behind it.</p>
<p>Two patch entries touch her, both on our <a href="/guides/patch-notes-and-roadmap/">patch notes page</a>: <strong>1.0.6</strong> (31 July) gave her a summer costume and weapon skin alongside Astria and Reina, with no system changes, and <strong>1.0.9</strong> (13 August) included unenumerated Charlotte/Cerese/Roxy/Kalien bug fixes. Neither is a balance change. No patch through 1.0.10 names a Cerese tuning adjustment.</p>
<h2>What this page cannot tell you</h2>
<p>Her skill names, her Exclusive Karma, her gear set, her stat priorities and her Awakening effects are not documented on any page of this site. The <a href="/heroes/unlock-planner/">unlock planner</a> prices her at one Fate's Invitation like every other Exchange hero, and that is the extent of what the unlock economy can tell you about whether she is worth it.
