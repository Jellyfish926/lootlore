    Jellyfi is the editorial byline on LootWiki, responsible for researching, publishing and maintaining the American Plains Mudding guides on this site. The byline covers research, fact checks against official Roblox data and updates when the game changes. Every guide published under that byline is listed below.

    How these guides are researched

    American Plains Mudding is a Roblox game, so the most reliable facts are the ones the developer registers on Roblox itself: the game description, the eight badges, the 14 game pass records, the 11 developer products, the 60 event listings and the group page. Those are read directly from Roblox's public data and quoted word for word, emoji and capital letters included, with the date we checked them.

    Fan wikis, code sites and videos are used only as leads. If one of them is the only source for a fact, such as a code, a hidden vehicle location, a vehicle's speed or a command, that fact stays off our published pages until something official or our own check confirms it. That is why there is no codes page, no map page, no full commands list and no tier list yet.

    How vehicles are named

    Vehicles are named exactly as the developer names them: ATV, pickup, SUV, combine, tilt deck trailer and so on. The game's art is detailed, and some of it will remind players of real machines. We do not attach real-world makes or models to anything, in the text or in image descriptions, because the developer's own text does not.

    What counts as our opinion

    Buying orders, a first-session checklist and "check this in game" lists are editorial suggestions, not rules of the game, and each page says so where it matters. Ratios such as "per 100 welcome badges" and pass totals are our own arithmetic on official numbers. Anything we infer, such as the possible return of a holiday pass, is labelled as inferred.

    How to read the dates on a page

    "Published" is when the page first went live. "Last checked" is when the text was compared against its sources. Roblox numbers such as badge totals, visits, member counts and prices are snapshots from that day. This game has had an update listed for almost every Saturday, so a page checked on a Friday describes the previous week's version until we review it again.

    Found a mistake?

    If a price, name or menu no longer matches the game, use the Contact page and include the page link and a screenshot from the game. Screenshots of the whitelist option, the spawn menu, a hidden vehicle or the place where private server commands are typed are especially welcome, because those are gaps we could not fill from official data. The site-wide writing and correction rules are on the Editorial Policy page.
