    Get Your Driver's License! Cars: 18 Cars and Epic Tier

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-09

    Scope: Car count, tier wording and car-related products from the official game description and developer product descriptions on 2026-10-09; the 18 car names, the full tier list, drop odds and the location called The Loop are not published and are not covered; prices can change without notice

    Key points

    • The official description says you can "Win 1 of 18 cars, from rusty hatchbacks to secret supercars". No official source we could read names the 18 or says how a car is awarded.
    • One tier name is published: Epic. VIP Ticket 🎟️ (149 Robux) makes "your next car" Epic or better, which implies at least one tier above Epic.
    • Golden Supercar 🏆 costs 39 Robux and is described as "The best car in the game, yours to keep."
    • Car names, tier odds and the place called The Loop still have to be checked in the game.

    Get Your Driver's License! has 18 cars to win. The official description puts it as "Win 1 of 18 cars, from rusty hatchbacks to secret supercars". The 18 names, their odds and how a car is awarded are not published anywhere we could read. Two store products act on cars: VIP Ticket 🎟️ at 149 Robux and Golden Supercar 🏆 at 39.

    This is not a list of all 18 cars, and we would rather say so in the second paragraph than let you scroll for one. No official source we could read names them, and we have not yet collected them in the game. What follows is everything the developer has put in writing.

    All six rows were read from Roblox's records for the game on 9 October 2026. We found no seventh. We found no official wiki and no update notes, and the developer group has no description or shout.

    How do you win a car?

    The description does not say. It lists the car after three other features (take a number, pass the written test, drive the test course) but not how or when a car is awarded. To us "Win 1 of 18" reads like a draw from a pool, and "your next car" in the VIP Ticket 🎟️ description fits that reading. Neither phrase is a published rule.

    Whether you can win the same car twice, and whether a failed drive still pays out a car, are not stated. The driving test guide covers what is known about failing.

    How do car tiers work?

    We found only one tier name in official text, and it comes from a store listing. VIP Ticket 🎟️ promises a car that is "Epic or better". Two things are suggested by three words.

    • Cars appear to be sorted into tiers, with Epic as one of them.
    • "Or better" suggests at least one tier above Epic.

    The description names two kinds of car, "rusty hatchbacks" and "secret supercars", and its "from ... to ..." wording reads like a low end and a high end. It is tempting to guess that Secret is the name of the top tier. The description uses the word as an adjective, and we will not promote it to a tier name without seeing it in the game. The names of the tiers below Epic, how many cars sit in each one, and the odds of each were not in any source we read.

    What does VIP Ticket 🎟️ do?

    Its full description is "Ticket #001: your next car is Epic or better." It costs 149 Robux, which makes it the second most expensive of the 13 developer products after Kill All.

    Read the description closely before buying. It speaks of "your next car", in the singular. It is a developer product, which Roblox defines as "an item or ability that a user can purchase more than once", so a second ticket would cost another 149 Robux. And it guarantees a floor, not a result: Epic or better can still mean Epic.

    The name hints at the line. "Ticket #001" sounds like the first number at the counter, and the description opens with "Take a number". But the listing says nothing about your place in the line. We treat it as a car product only.

    What is Golden Supercar 🏆?

    A 39-Robux developer product whose description reads: "The best car in the game, yours to keep. Drive it on The Loop." Its store icon shows a gold, low-polygon supercar with a wedge-shaped nose and a rear wing.

    It was registered at 14:45 UTC on 30 September 2026, about eight and a half hours after the first batch of products. That makes it the first thing added to the store after launch.

    Three parts of that description are worth a second look.

    • "The best car in the game" is the developer's claim. We found no published stats for any car, so we cannot say what best means here: speed, handling or simply rarity.
    • "yours to keep" says this car stays with you. It does not say other cars are lost. We have not found any statement on whether cars you win are permanent.
    • "The Loop" is a named place. No other official text we read mentions it, so where it is and how you reach it are open questions.

    Whether Golden Supercar 🏆 is one of the 18 cars or a nineteenth is not stated either.

    Which car purchase is the better buy?

    On the listings alone, the comparison is lopsided. The reasoning is ours.

    If you want one strong car, Golden Supercar 🏆 is described as the best and costs 110 Robux less. VIP Ticket 🎟️ makes more sense if what you want is an Epic-or-better result on your next car. Neither is described as required: Get Your Driver's License! is free to play, and the description lists winning a car as a feature. We have not played a full run without paying. Other purchases are compared in the Robux shop guide and the game pass list.
