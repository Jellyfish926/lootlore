# One Tap Cases and Robux Shop Guides: What to Read

One Tap has 101 things on Roblox's store records: 4 game passes and 97 developer products. The passes are named for double money, double XP, double vote weight and case luck. The products are mostly cases, with currency packs, a product named Premium Battlepass, six products named Skip, Xp Boost products and limited-named items around them. These four guides price all of it.

One Tap prices run from 5 Robux to 10,000. This store comes with almost no official wording: the passes have a line of text on their icons, and the 97 products have no description at all. So the guides print names and prices exactly and are plain about what a name does not tell you.

The "more than once" column follows Roblox's own definitions of a pass and a developer product. The grouping of products is ours, and the last row includes four donation products that account for the 10,000 Robux top price.

## What should you read before spending anything?

Start with the basics. One Tap is free to play, and the weapon rules in the description make no mention of purchases. The [how-to-play guide](/one-tap/how-to-play/) sets those out, and the [rewards guide](/one-tap/rewards/) lists what can be earned by playing. Three points save the most Robux. Bigger case packs are almost never cheaper per case. Going by the tier counts in their names, Skip Three Tiers and Skip Ten Tiers cost about three and four times as much per tier as Skip One Tier and Skip Five Tiers. And the Xp Boost products cost the same per minute named at every size.

## What is not in these One Tap guides?

Case contents, drop odds and anything else the store records do not say. We have not tested purchases in the game, so nothing here is described from experience. No code is listed anywhere on this site, because none appears in the official sources we could read.

## Scope

One Tap prices were read on 10 October 2026 and can change without notice. Check the Robux amount on the Roblox purchase prompt before you confirm. A record marked for sale on Roblox is not proof that the in-game shop offers that product today. Suggestions about what to buy first are our opinion.
