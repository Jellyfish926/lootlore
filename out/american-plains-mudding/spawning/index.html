    American Plains Mudding Spawn Slots and Spawner Passes

    By Jellyfi · Last reviewed 2026-10-09 · Checked on version 2026-10-03 update

    Scope: Slot counts and pass effects as stated in five official game pass descriptions on 2026-10-09. The default split between vehicles and trailers is not stated and cannot be derived from those texts. Spawner locations, despawn rules and menus are not published and are not covered. Sums are our arithmetic.

    Key points

    • The Any Slot Spawner description refers to "the 4-8 slots owned": between four and eight, with the cap of eight reached by owning both the 4 Vehicle Spawner and 4 Trailer Spawner passes.
    • Slots are split into vehicle and trailer categories unless you own Any Slot Spawner (290 Robux), which lets anything go in any slot.
    • Premium Private Servers gives "2x Spawn Slots In Private Servers, Up To 16".
    • Eight flexible slots cost 1,070 Robux in passes: 490 + 290 + 290.

    In American Plains Mudding you own between four and eight spawn slots, according to the Any Slot Spawner pass description. The 4 Vehicle Spawner and 4 Trailer Spawner passes raise the count, Any Slot Spawner removes the split between vehicles and trailers, and Premium Private Servers doubles slots in private servers, up to 16.

    The game has no published rulebook, so this page is built from five pass descriptions read on 9 October 2026. If you are brand new, the how-to-play guide is the better first read. Where a number is stated outright we quote it. Where the texts leave a gap we say so.

    How many vehicles and trailers can you spawn?

    The clearest statement is in the Any Slot Spawner description: "anything can be spawned in any of the 4-8 slots owned. E.g., all slots could be filled with vehicles, with a cap of 8 if 4 trailer and 4 vehicle spawner gamepasses are owned."

    Put that beside the two spawner passes, each of which unlocks "the ability to spawn 4", and this is what the texts establish:

    SituationSlotsStated by the developer?
    Any playerBetween 4 and 8 ownedYes: "any of the 4-8 slots owned"
    Both spawner passes8: four vehicles and four trailersYes: "a cap of 8 if 4 trailer and 4 vehicle spawner gamepasses are owned"
    No spawner passNot stated; 4 is the lowest figure in the range, and the split is not givenNo
    One spawner passNot statedNo
    Any Slot Spawner addedSame slots, any mix of vehicles and trailersYes: "Removes trailer and vehicle slot categories."

    Only the range of 4 to 8 slots and the cap of 8 with both spawner passes are in the developer's words. The default split between vehicles and trailers is not stated, and the pass texts do not settle it: any split of four slots reaches eight once both categories are raised to four. We therefore give no slot count for a single spawner pass.

    What does each spawner pass say?

    PassRobuxOfficial description
    4 Vehicle Spawner490Unlock the ability to spawn 4 vehicles!
    4 Trailer Spawner290Unlock the ability to spawn 4 trailers!
    Any Slot Spawner290Removes trailer and vehicle slot categories. So anything can be spawned in any of the 4-8 slots owned. E.g., all slots could be filled with vehicles, with a cap of 8 if 4 trailer and 4 vehicle spawner gamepasses are owned. (Formerly Premium Rigs)
    Anywhere Trailer Spawner390Allows you to spawn trailers from anywhere!

    4 Vehicle Spawner costs 200 Robux more than 4 Trailer Spawner. Because the starting number in each category is not stated, we do not work out a price per extra slot. All four have gift versions at the same price; the game pass guide lists them.

    What does Any Slot Spawner change?

    It changes what a slot can hold, not how many you have. Without it, a slot belongs to a category: vehicle or trailer. With it, "anything can be spawned in any" slot. The developer's own example is filling every slot with vehicles.

    That makes it the pass for players who lean hard one way. If you never tow, Any Slot Spawner lets trailer slots hold vehicles instead (290 Robux against 490 for 4 Vehicle Spawner), but the pass text does not say how many slots you start with in each category.

    The note "(Formerly Premium Rigs)" tells you the pass was renamed. If an older video or guide mentions Premium Rigs, this is the pass it means. The record itself dates from 4 August 2022.

    Is Anywhere Trailer Spawner worth 390 Robux?

    It depends on how often you swap trailers. The description is one line: "Allows you to spawn trailers from anywhere!". That implies trailers otherwise have to be spawned at particular places, though the data does not say where those are.

    Two things to weigh. It adds no slots, so it does nothing for a player who is short of capacity. And it names trailers only: no pass on record lets you spawn vehicles from anywhere. At 390 Robux it costs more than either 290-Robux pass, so our suggestion is to buy it last.

    How do slots work in private servers?

    The Premium Private Servers pass, 195 Robux, lists this among its features: "2x Spawn Slots In Private Servers, Up To 16, All Admins In Your Server Have This Ability".

    Sixteen is twice the public cap of eight, so the two statements agree. The line also says the doubling extends to "All Admins In Your Server", which suggests the pass owner is not the only one who benefits. The same pass includes a command to give other players commands; the private server page covers it. Public servers hold 15 players, and nothing in the pass text changes slots there.
