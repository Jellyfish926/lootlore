    Key points

    • Start by throwing: the official description says every bounce gives +1 Skill, training and levels add distance, and farther zones pay more Wins.
    • Spend no Robux in your first session. On 9 October 2026 the cheapest product was Skill Multiplier [TIER 1] at 5 Robux and the two cheapest passes cost 25 Robux each.
    • On 9 October 2026 the game had no Roblox badges, so track progress by level, longest throw and world; the official event titles listed that day named Worlds 3 to 6.
    • Three guides cover the rest: how to play, updates and events, and the official community.

    What should you do in your first session?

    Our suggestion, using only what the developer has published. Throw, and watch Skill rise by one per bounce. Train when the game offers it, because levels are what make you throw farther. Try to reach a zone you have not reached before, since farther zones pay more Wins.

    When you can hatch a pet, do it and read the boost it shows. Rebirth when the game allows it, because the description ties Rebirth to growing stronger. Leave Robux alone until you know which part of the loop slows you down; the Robux shop guides are there for that moment.

    How does the free loop line up with the store?

    Each step of the loop has a free route in the description and paid items beside it in the store. The pairing below is our reading of item names, because no pass or product has a description. Prices are the official Robux figures on 9 October 2026.

    StepFree route, in the developer's wordsPaid items with a matching name (Robux)
    Skill"Every bounce gives you +1 Skill!"Skill Multiplier [TIER 1] (5)
    Distance"Train and level up to throw farther!"Koi Training Zone pass (99)
    Wins"Reach farther zones to earn more Wins!"Auto Wins pass (25), Wins Pack 1 (25)
    Pets"Collect pets for powerful boosts!"Rainbow Egg (45)
    Rebirth"Rebirth to grow stronger and beat your longest throw!"Skip Rebirth (35), Auto Rebirth pass (149)

    The description does not say what Skill or Wins are spent on. Stone prices, level thresholds and the Wins each zone pays are not shown in public data, so which in-game upgrade to buy first is not confirmed. The Skill and Wins boosts guide covers the paid side of both counters.

If you do want to spend, price is the only published fact to go on: when we read the store at 18:12 UTC on 9 October 2026, all 11 game passes and all 89 developer products had empty descriptions.     How do you track progress without badges?

    When we read Roblox's badge list on 9 October 2026 (UTC), +1 Stone Skipping had no Roblox badges, so there was no badge list to tick off. The markers that official material points to are your level, your longest throw and the world you have reached. Official art sets LEVEL 1 beside LEVEL 999, and the description ends its gameplay lines with "beat your longest throw".

    Worlds are dated only by official event titles: World 3 + New Content from 20 September 2026, WORLD 4 + UPDATE from 27 September and ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 5 on 3 October. How a world unlocks is not confirmed in any official source. The updates page keeps the dated timeline up to the World 5 event.

    Which events were scheduled for October 2026?

    When we read Roblox's official event listing at 19:44 UTC on 9 October 2026, it returned five events in total. Three had already ended: World 3 + New Content (18:00 UTC on 20 September 2026 to 18:00 UTC on 23 September 2026), WORLD 4 + UPDATE (16:00 UTC on 27 September 2026 to 16:00 UTC on 1 October 2026) and ADMIN ABUSE + WORLD 5 (16:00 UTC to 18:00 UTC on 3 October 2026). The other two had start times after that moment. WORLD 6 + ADMIN ABUSE was listed from 16:00 UTC on Saturday 10 October 2026 to 16:00 UTC on 11 October 2026, described in three lines: Admin Abuse, World 6 and New Features. Halloween Event was listed from 16:00 UTC on 17 October 2026 to 19:00 UTC on 1 November 2026, with no description.

    Going by creation dates (UTC), 78 of the 89 developer products listed at 18:12 UTC on 9 October 2026 existed by 2 October, and 11 were created after it. Eight are dated 3 October: Skill Pack 1 [TIER 4], Skill Pack 2 [TIER 4], Skill Pack 3 [TIER 4], +100 Pets Inventory Slots, Rainbow Trail, Void Trail, Skill Multiplier [TIER 11] and Skill Multiplier [TIER 12]. Three are dated 7 October: Witch Egg, Witch Egg x3 and Witch Egg x8.

    What do these guides not cover yet?

    There is no zone list, no pet tier list, no stone list and no rebirth calculator. The numbers those pages need are not published in any official source we can read, and we have not yet recorded them in game. The how to play guide ends with a table of what is still unknown, so you can see the gaps rather than trip over them.
