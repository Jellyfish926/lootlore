    Beast of Reincarnation Voice Actors & Cast

    Confirmed Japanese voice cast

    On June 10, 2026, Game Freak and publisher Fictions, Inc. revealed the Japanese voice cast of Beast of Reincarnation alongside a new gameplay trailer. Nine actors were announced — and it is a genuinely stacked lineup, pulling in some of the most recognizable voices working in Japanese games and anime today. So far, only the protagonist's role has been officially matched to an actor: Emma is voiced by Yui Ishikawa. The remaining eight names were confirmed as cast members, but Game Freak has not yet said who plays whom.

    That "not yet announced" column is normal for a Japanese release at this stage: studios there often list the ensemble first and let players discover the character pairings in the game itself, or drip-feed them in pre-launch character trailers. Expect the full role list to be nailed down in the credits when the game ships on August 4, 2026.

    Yui Ishikawa as Emma — why it's a perfect fit

    Emma is an 18-year-old Sealer traveling a Japan consumed by the corruption known as the Blight, accompanied by her dog companion Koo. According to the official character description shared with the cast reveal, she lives her life devoid of her past memories and emotions, and the journey with Koo is, in part, her path back toward understanding what feeling even is.

    If you wanted to cast one actress for "quiet, burdened, emotionally muted — but with an ocean underneath," Yui Ishikawa is the first name on the list. Her 2B in NieR:Automata built an entire performance out of restraint punctured by devastating cracks; her Violet Evergarden is literally a character learning emotions from zero. Emma's premise sits squarely in that lane, and the casting is one of the clearest signals of the tone Game Freak is aiming for with its first original AAA action RPG — a long way from the studio's Pokémon roots.

    The rest of the cast: voices you will recognize

    Even without confirmed roles, the supporting names tell you something about the production's ambitions. Akio Otsuka is gaming royalty — the gravelly baritone of Solid Snake and Batou, and a near-automatic pick for grizzled mentors, commanders, and hulking bosses. Akira Ishida has spent three decades voicing enigmatic, unsettlingly calm characters like Evangelion's Kaworu. Rina Sato and Yu Kobayashi bring lead-caliber range to whatever supporting roles they land, and Satoshi Mikami is a familiar, composed presence from Sailor Moon Crystal and countless drama dubs. Tetsuo Kanao rounds out the veterans.

    One fun coincidence: Sumire Morohoshi is best known for voicing a different Emma — the protagonist of The Promised Neverland. In Beast of Reincarnation she will be playing someone else entirely (Ishikawa has the Emma role here), but seeing two famous "Emma" actresses in the same credits is a nice wink for anime fans.

    Does Koo have a voice actor?

    Koo is a dog — a canine companion, not a talking mascot — so expect creature vocalizations rather than dialogue. No credited voice performer for Koo has been announced, and it is not yet confirmed whether the creature sounds will get a named credit at all. In gameplay terms Koo is far more than an emotional support animal: the June trailer emphasized how commanding Koo's techniques layers strategy on top of Emma's real-time sword combat, and our Koo guide breaks down everything shown so far.

    English dub status

    Yes — Beast of Reincarnation ships with two full audio tracks: Japanese and English. The Steam store page lists full audio support for both languages, so you can play the original Japanese performances with English subtitles, or switch to the English dub. As of late August 2026, however, the English cast has not been announced — no English actor names have appeared in any official material yet.

    Both audio options apply across the release: PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam and Microsoft Store), including the day-one Game Pass Ultimate version. Since the game supports Xbox Play Anywhere, your language settings travel with the same save between Xbox and PC.

    Japanese or English — which track should you play with?

    This matters more here than in most RPGs, because Beast of Reincarnation is a parry-heavy, SEKIRO-style action game. A practical way to decide:

    • Pick Japanese audio if you care about the confirmed star cast and tonal fit. The setting — an end-times Japan, region-guarding Nushi, gear like the Demon's Hat — was clearly written with these performances in mind, and Ishikawa's Emma is the marquee attraction.
    • Pick English audio if you would rather not read subtitles mid-fight. When you are watching a boss's shoulders for the next deflect window, story dialogue delivered in a language you understand without looking down is a real gameplay advantage.
    • Best of both: play the exploration-and-story stretches in Japanese, and nothing stops you from switching before a difficult Nushi. Voice language is a standard options-menu setting, so you can switch between Japanese and English from the options screen whenever you like.

    Cast announcement timeline

    • Reveal: Beast of Reincarnation was unveiled at an Xbox Developer Direct as Game Freak's original action RPG, built on Unreal Engine 5 and published by Fictions, Inc.
    • June 10, 2026: the nine-actor Japanese cast is announced alongside new gameplay footage; Yui Ishikawa is confirmed as Emma.
    • July 2026: English cast still unannounced; no demo is available.
    • August 4, 2026: launch on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC at $59.99 / 59,99 € / ¥8,980, day one on Game Pass Ultimate — full credits, including every English role, land with the game.

    FAQ

    Who voices Emma in Beast of Reincarnation?

    Yui Ishikawa voices Emma in Japanese — the actress behind 2B in NieR:Automata, Mikasa Ackerman in Attack on Titan, and Violet Evergarden. Her English counterpart has not been announced yet.

    Does Beast of Reincarnation have an English dub?

    Yes. The Steam page confirms full English and Japanese audio. Nine other languages — French, Italian, German, both Spanish variants, Brazilian Portuguese, Korean, and both Chinese scripts — are supported with subtitles and interface text only.

    Can I play with Japanese voices and English subtitles?

    Yes. With both audio tracks and full English text support, the classic "JP voices + EN subs" setup works out of the box.

    Who voices Koo?

    Nobody, as far as official materials show — Koo is a non-verbal dog companion, and no creature-vocalization credit has been announced.

    When will the remaining roles be revealed?

    Game Freak has matched only Emma to her actress so far. The other eight Japanese roles, and the entire English cast, are expected by launch on August 4, 2026 at the latest.
