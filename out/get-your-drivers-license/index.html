    About this guide

    The official description sums it up in one line: "Wait in line and get your Driver's License!" These guides are built from that description, the store listings the developer has registered on Roblox and the group's public record. We have not played the test course ourselves yet, and each page says where that matters.

    Where should a new driver start?

    • Cars: what is published about the 18 cars, the Epic tier, VIP Ticket 🎟️ and Golden Supercar 🏆.

    What does the official description list?

    Five features, in the order below. It is a feature list: the description does not say that every run goes through all five, or that every run wins a car. Each row links to the guide that covers it.

    A sixth line adds a role: "Be the Examiner and judge other players' driving".

    What does Robux buy?

    Two kinds of thing. The 5 game passes are one-time purchases from 9 to 1,200 Robux. Three of their descriptions name other players (Time Out, Gravity Gun 🔥 and Ban Hammer), one is a loud airhorn and one is an emoji pack. The 13 developer products cost 9 to 160 Robux. Their descriptions cover skipping the line, passing the written test, examiner time, cars and effects on other players. Start with the Cars & Robux Shop section, or go straight to the game pass list.

    What was added after launch?

    Why is there no codes page?

    Because we could not find a single code in an official source. On 9 October 2026 we read three official places we could access: the game description, the group description and the group shout. Social links on the game and the group could not be read without an account. The description has eight lines and none of them is a code. The Time Will Pass group has an empty description and no shout. If we see a code in an official place, a codes page for Get Your Driver's License! will follow.

    Who runs the game?

    Time Will Pass is a Roblox group created on 27 September 2026, two days before this game. It published five experiences between 27 and 29 September 2026. The developer page covers the group, its owner account, its other games and why we cannot confirm a Discord server.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who makes Get Your Driver's License!?
    The Roblox group Time Will Pass, created on 27 September 2026. It had about 800,000 members on 9 October 2026. Details are on the developer page.
    Is there a code?
    Not in any official source we could read on 9 October 2026. The game description has no code, and the group has no description or shout. We do not copy codes from other sites.
    How many cars are there?
    18, according to the official description: "Win 1 of 18 cars, from rusty hatchbacks to secret supercars". No official source we could read names them. See the car guide.
    How many players fit in one server?
    Up to 25, according to the Roblox game record we read on 9 October 2026.
    What is the cheapest thing in the store?
    Nine Robux. One game pass and five products cost that much, including Skip Line [SALE] and Skip Time [SALE]. The Robux shop guide lists all 13 products.
    Can you skip the line?
    Yes, with Robux. Skip Line [SALE] moves you up 5 places for 9 Robux and Skip To End goes straight to the driving test for 48. The line guide compares all of them.
